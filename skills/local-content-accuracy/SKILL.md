# Content accuracy review

Make the smallest confirmed accuracy corrections, with evidence that states what was covered and what remains unresolved. Read the current [project rules](../../PROJECT.md) and [workflow](../../docs/project/WORKFLOW.md); follow the assignment's scope, permissions and implementation/review/lifecycle ownership. A bounded repair may end with an uncommitted patch.

Distinguish a full audit from a repair. A full audit reads each requested unit completely in Chinese and English, including formal blocks, proofs, formulas, tables, examples and cited media. A repair rechecks affected claims and dependencies, reusing earlier evidence only after establishing correspondence to the current inputs. Neither a repair nor a passing build earns full-unit coverage. Use the current SUMMARY for the roster and keep historical coverage, media, output and review obligations distinct.

1. **Match the formal claim.** Check the current pin and configured branch, then compare every affected `lean` GID with the complete exact-pin frozen declaration: name, kind, module, assumptions, types, domains, quantifiers and conclusion. Theorem-like environments require `kind = theorem`. Keep proof explanations and media green boxes within the cited declaration's scope; manual derivations need attribution outside the verified boundary. The lock is tool-owned; route authorized pin changes through [trureturing-pin](../trureturing-pin/SKILL.md).
   **Verify backend evidence.** Read the exact-pin frozen state and the command’s implementation. Match the cited declaration in the `declarations` array. Publication badges and statistics use the official measurement `declarations[].axioms`. A historical row missing that field gives only the permitted upper bound of three standard axioms. `declarations[].strict_omega.axioms`, when present, records the strict measurement for the same declaration. Trace strict empty-kernel replay to the selected library roots and their actual import closure. Trace paired D5 evidence to the consumer builds, interface comparison, and per-backend measurements. For an axiom count, identify the precise roster and check every record on the stated backend. Match these qualifiers across editions and media, with exact-pin commands or source references. See the [backend evidence rule](../../PROJECT.md#4-三层认识论诚实契约).

2. **Check meaning independently.** Recompute material numerical claims and check physical protocol conditions and literature attribution. For interactive quantities, verify the selected curve over its actual supported control domain, including selector precedence; derive extrema independently of the producer and its test helpers. Keep Lean results, literature results, author interpretation and open questions visibly distinct. Record unresolved provenance as a limitation.
3. **Correct without rewriting.** Classify findings as confirmed, advisory or discarded; fix confirmed inaccuracies and necessary bilingual mismatches. A claim that is false or overstated is corrected to the strongest true statement the evidence supports, under the [project rule on correct and strongest statements](../../PROJECT.md): weakest hypotheses, strongest conclusion, exact constants and scope, never beyond the cited evidence; a correct sentence is not rewritten, only given its evidence. Preserve epigraphs, argument and voice using [style preservation](references/style-preservation.md), integrating any added or strengthened results into the owning thematic chapter's logical narrative flow rather than appending chronological theorem-proof logs, spawning stub subpages, or blindly appending chapters/subsections to the end of the book or its closing synthesis chapter (respecting `book.max_chapters`). Edit authoritative Chinese first, then natural English with the same assumptions and strength. Preserve chapter order, stable IDs/GIDs, environment and media sequences, and proof boundaries and closing sentences.
4. **Validate and hand off.** Use the applicable [review protocol](references/review-protocol.md) for conditional checks, evidence reuse and independent review. Validate affected outputs with builds and applicable automated checks first; inspect ordinary export pages only under the [book-export](../book-export/SKILL.md) visual-check triggers, within the assignment's execution limits. Report required checks left unrun. Identify the final candidate, changes, actual results, limits and remaining obligations in the handoff or PR discussion. List each substantive correction as original, corrected text, why it is true, and its evidence. The designated owner carries review, CI, merge and Pages through the project workflow; a repair worker hands these obligations back when they belong to the caller.
