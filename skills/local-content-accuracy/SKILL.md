# Content accuracy review

Make the smallest confirmed accuracy corrections, with evidence that states what was covered and what remains unresolved. Read the current [project rules](../../PROJECT.md) and [workflow](../../docs/project/WORKFLOW.md); follow the assignment's scope, permissions and implementation/review/lifecycle ownership. A bounded repair may end with an uncommitted patch.

Distinguish a full audit from a repair. A full audit reads each requested unit completely in Chinese and English, including formal blocks, proofs, formulas, tables, examples and cited media. A repair rechecks affected claims and dependencies, reusing earlier evidence only after establishing correspondence to the current inputs. Neither a repair nor a passing build earns full-unit coverage. Use the current SUMMARY for the roster and keep historical coverage, media, output and review obligations distinct.

1. **Match the formal claim.** Check the current pin and configured branch, then compare every affected `lean` GID with the complete exact-pin frozen declaration: name, kind, module, assumptions, types, domains, quantifiers and conclusion. Theorem-like environments require `kind = theorem`. Keep proof explanations and media green boxes within the cited declaration's scope; manual derivations need attribution outside the verified boundary. The lock is tool-owned; route authorized pin changes through [trureturing-pin](../trureturing-pin/SKILL.md).
2. **Check meaning independently.** Recompute material numerical claims and check physical protocol conditions and literature attribution. For interactive quantities, verify the selected curve over its actual supported control domain, including selector precedence; derive extrema independently of the producer and its test helpers. Keep Lean results, literature results, author interpretation and open questions visibly distinct. Record unresolved provenance as a limitation.
3. **Correct without rewriting.** Classify findings as confirmed, advisory or discarded; fix confirmed inaccuracies and necessary bilingual mismatches. Preserve epigraphs, argument and voice using [style preservation](references/style-preservation.md). Edit authoritative Chinese first, then natural English with the same assumptions and strength. Preserve chapter order, stable IDs/GIDs, environment and media sequences, and proof boundaries and closing sentences.
4. **Validate and hand off.** Use the applicable [review protocol](references/review-protocol.md) for conditional checks, evidence reuse and independent review. Validate affected outputs by actually reading them, within the assignment's execution limits; report required checks left unrun. Identify the final candidate, changes, actual results, limits and remaining obligations. The designated owner records coverage in the project ledger and carries review, CI, merge and Pages through the project workflow; a repair worker hands these obligations back when they belong to the caller.
