# 37.1 Ternion-Field Transformer (TFT): Purely Rational Rotor Dynamics, Non-Abelian Holonomy Attention, and Lie-Triple Curvature Gates on Parallel Concept-Field Bundles {#sec-ch39-ternion-field-transformer-and-rational-rotor-dynamics}

> *"Reconstructing the Transformer out of ternions ($\mathbb{T}_3 \equiv \Delta\mathcal{T}_\epsilon$) means dismantling flat vector space $\mathbb{R}^d$ along with its dense matrix multiplications (GEMM), `RMSNorm`, two-dimensional Abelian `RoPE`, `Softmax` exponential normalization, and $4d$-expanded feed-forward layers—rewriting the entire network as a purely rational spherical rotor dynamical system evolving on a parallel bundle of three-dimensional concept fields $(\mathbb{T}_3)^K$."*

Having liberated the minimal circular-arc ternion from Cartesian linearity into five lossless three-DOF nonlinear representations in [Chapter 32 §32.4](ch39-s21-nonlinear-ternion-representations-and-torsor-synthesis.md#sec-ch39-nonlinear-ternion-representations-and-torsor-synthesis) and established active self-halting discretion $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{self}}$ in [Chapter 36 §36.10](ch39-s07-self-halting-precision-and-spectral-sorting.md), we arrive at the engineering synthesis of Chapter 37: recasting modern neural sequence modeling from the ground up as the **Ternion-Field Transformer (TFT)** (the neural architecture design and adaptive early-exit heuristic are marked **`MODEL`**; regarding arithmetic scope, under the rational retraction $\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{rat}}$ the **entire forward rotor-block state-update core** invoked in our opening epigraph—$\star_{\mathcal{S}}$, `3D-NAPE`, bilateral and butterfly projections, Born–Heap attention, and Lie-triple curvature gates—uses purely rational four-operation arithmetic `+, -, ×, ÷`, whereas the optional Gibbs retraction $\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{sqrt}}$ and the Euclidean-norm halting readout $r_t^{(\ell)}$ in §37.1.2 Item 5 evaluate algebraic square roots; dual-denominator polynomial identities, the seam-invariant sine vector, conformal metric bounds, Jacobian pullbacks, and reference tensor checks are **`manual derivation` / `NUM`**; deep-learning baselines are **`Literature`**; and large-scale perplexity and hardware throughput benchmarks are demarcated as **`OPEN`** in [@prob-ch39-tft-scale-and-seams]).

Nearly every structural pathology of the standard flat Transformer stems from forcing curved relational inference onto homogeneous Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^d$: unconstrained dense `GEMM` layers let hidden norms drift exponentially, forcing repeated `RMSNorm` projections and severe activation outlier channels; 2D Rotary Position Embedding (`RoPE`) applies mutually commuting planar rotations, preventing position operators themselves from encoding path-ordered syntactic nesting; `Softmax` exponentials force weights to sum to $1$ even when no historical token matches, encouraging autoregressive heads under causal-mask asymmetry to park unmatched probability mass on initial tokens as empirically observed **attention sinks**; and flat linear layers lack intrinsic curvature interaction, forcing feed-forward blocks to spend nearly $70\%$ of model parameters on channel expansion (classical two-matrix FFNs expand to $4d$, while modern three-matrix gated `SwiGLU` uses hidden width $\frac{8}{3}d$ for $8d^2$ weights—or $12d^2$ weights at full $4d$ width). Restoring hidden states to a parallel bundle of three-dimensional ternion concept fields (echoing the 3D concept-field cap of [§32.1](ch39-s10-three-dimensional-field-cap-and-hilbert-ledger.md#sec-ch39-three-dimensional-field-cap-and-hilbert-ledger)) dissolves all five patches under a single rational circular-arc operator.

## 37.1.1 State Representation, the Dual-Denominator Arithmetic Core, and the Seam-Invariant Sine Vector {#sec-ch39-22-1}

In TFT, the hidden state of token $t$ is not a monolithic vector $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^d$, but a bundle of $K$ mutually orthogonal **three-dimensional ternion concept fields** on the projective adjoint quotient $\operatorname{Ad}(\mathbb{T}_3)^K \cong \mathrm{SO}(3)^K \cong \bigl(B_1(\mathbb{R}^3)/\!\sim\bigr)^K$ ($d \approx 3K$):

$$
\mathbf{\Sigma}_t \;=\; \Bigl(\boldsymbol{\sigma}_t^{(1)},\, \boldsymbol{\sigma}_t^{(2)},\, \dots,\, \boldsymbol{\sigma}_t^{(K)}\Bigr) \;\in\; \bigl(B_1(\mathbb{R}^3)/\!\sim\bigr)^K, \qquad \bigl\lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}_t^{(k)}\bigr\rVert \;\le\; 1,
$$

each stored in the **dual-chart Shadow-MRP coordinate** $\boldsymbol{\sigma} = \tan\frac{\theta}{4}\,\hat{\mathbf{u}} \in B_1(\mathbb{R}^3)$ of [§32.4.2](ch39-s21-nonlinear-ternion-representations-and-torsor-synthesis.md#sec-ch39-21-2) using strictly $3$ real numbers and no scalar axis $\mathbf{1}$ (on this adjoint quotient, $+\mathbf{1}$ and $-\mathbf{1}$ share $\boldsymbol{\sigma} = \mathbf{0}$, while antipodal boundary points $\boldsymbol{\sigma} \sim -\boldsymbol{\sigma}$ on $\lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rVert = 1$ represent the same half-turn $\theta = \pi$; if $4\pi$ spinor sheet resolution on $S^3$ is desired, one adjoins the $1\;\mathrm{bit}$ sheet bit $\epsilon_{\mathrm{sheet}}^{(k)} \in \{\pm 1\}$ of [§32.4.2](ch39-s21-nonlinear-ternion-representations-and-torsor-synthesis.md#sec-ch39-21-2)).

1. **Dual-Denominator Singularity-Free Ternion Multiplication ($\star_{\mathcal{S}}$) and Two Exact Polynomial Identities**:
   For any $\boldsymbol{\sigma}_A, \boldsymbol{\sigma}_B \in \mathbb{R}^3$, define the shared numerator $\mathbf{N}$, primary denominator $D_+$, and shadow denominator $D_-$ (`manual derivation`):

   $$
   \mathbf{N}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}_A, \boldsymbol{\sigma}_B) \;:=\; \bigl(1 - \lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}_A\rVert^2\bigr)\boldsymbol{\sigma}_B \;+\; \bigl(1 - \lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}_B\rVert^2\bigr)\boldsymbol{\sigma}_A \;+\; 2(\boldsymbol{\sigma}_A \times \boldsymbol{\sigma}_B),
   $$

   $$
   D_+(\boldsymbol{\sigma}_A, \boldsymbol{\sigma}_B) \;:=\; 1 + \lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}_A\rVert^2\lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}_B\rVert^2 - 2(\boldsymbol{\sigma}_A \cdot \boldsymbol{\sigma}_B), \qquad D_-(\boldsymbol{\sigma}_A, \boldsymbol{\sigma}_B) \;:=\; \lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}_A + \boldsymbol{\sigma}_B\rVert^2,
   $$

   $$
   \boldsymbol{\sigma}_A \star_{\mathcal{S}} \boldsymbol{\sigma}_B \;:=\; \begin{cases} +\dfrac{\mathbf{N}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}_A, \boldsymbol{\sigma}_B)}{D_+}, & D_+ \ge D_-, \\[8pt] -\dfrac{\mathbf{N}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}_A, \boldsymbol{\sigma}_B)}{D_-}, & D_+ < D_-. \end{cases}
   $$

   Direct polynomial expansion establishes two identities valid identically across all of $\mathbb{R}^3 \times \mathbb{R}^3$ (`manual derivation`):

   $$
   D_+ + D_- \;\equiv\; \bigl(1 + \lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}_A\rVert^2\bigr)\bigl(1 + \lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}_B\rVert^2\bigr) \;\ge\; 1, \qquad \bigl\lVert\mathbf{N}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}_A, \boldsymbol{\sigma}_B)\bigr\rVert^2 \;\equiv\; D_+\,D_-.
   $$

   Four exact properties follow immediately: first, the selected denominator satisfies the global floor $\max(D_+, D_-) \ge \frac{1}{2}(D_+ + D_-) \ge \mathbf{0.5}$, so **a single division never encounters a zero-denominator pole** while calling only $+, -, \times, \div$ (**zero transcendentals $\sin, \cos, \exp$**); second, $\lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}_A \star_{\mathcal{S}} \boldsymbol{\sigma}_B\rVert^2 = \frac{\min(D_+, D_-)}{\max(D_+, D_-)} \le 1$, **locking every output inside the unit ball $B_1(\mathbb{R}^3)$ without `RMSNorm`**; third, $\mathbf{0}$ is the exact two-sided identity on $\lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rVert \le 1$ and $-\boldsymbol{\sigma}$ is the exact global inverse ($(-\boldsymbol{\sigma})\star_{\mathcal{S}}\boldsymbol{\sigma} = \boldsymbol{\sigma}\star_{\mathcal{S}}(-\boldsymbol{\sigma}) = \mathbf{0}$); fourth, $\star_{\mathcal{S}}$ is associative everywhere on $\mathrm{SO}(3) \cong \mathbb{RP}^3$ and selects the shortest geodesic arc $\theta \le \pi$ (with literal coordinate equality $(\mathbf{a}\star_{\mathcal{S}}\mathbf{b})\star_{\mathcal{S}}\mathbf{c} = \mathbf{a}\star_{\mathcal{S}}(\mathbf{b}\star_{\mathcal{S}}\mathbf{c})$ whenever the final product avoids the measure-zero half-turn seam $D_+ = D_-$, even if an intermediate product lands on the seam).

2. **Seam-Invariant Rational Sine Vector $\boldsymbol{\xi}(\boldsymbol{\sigma})$ and Bounded Conformal Storage**:
   Because antipodal boundary representatives $\pm\hat{\mathbf{u}}$ on $\lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rVert = 1$ represent the same rotation, raw cross products in $\boldsymbol{\sigma}$ would flip sign across the seam. We therefore introduce the **seam-invariant rational sine vector** (matching the Cayley antisymmetric axis vector $\frac{1}{2}\operatorname{vee}(R(\boldsymbol{\sigma}) - R(\boldsymbol{\sigma})^\top) = \sin\theta\,\hat{\mathbf{u}}$ for $\theta = 4\arctan\lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rVert \in [0, \pi]$, `manual derivation`):

   $$
   \boldsymbol{\xi}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}) \;:=\; \frac{4\bigl(1 - \lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rVert^2\bigr)}{\bigl(1 + \lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rVert^2\bigr)^2}\,\boldsymbol{\sigma} \;=\; \sin\theta\,\hat{\mathbf{u}} \;\in\; B_1(\mathbb{R}^3), \qquad \boldsymbol{\xi}\!\left(-\frac{\boldsymbol{\sigma}}{\lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rVert^2}\right) \;\equiv\; \boldsymbol{\xi}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}), \qquad \boldsymbol{\xi}\big\rvert_{\lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rVert = 1} \;\equiv\; \mathbf{0}.
   $$

   It is $100\%$ invariant under shadow inversion and vanishes smoothly on the half-turn seam $\lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rVert = 1$, rendering all cross-field bridges and curvature gates single-valued on $B_1(\mathbb{R}^3)/\!\sim$! Simultaneously, differentiating $U(\boldsymbol{\sigma}) = \frac{(1 - \lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rVert^2)\mathbf{1} + 2\boldsymbol{\sigma}}{1 + \lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rVert^2} \in S^3$ yields a strictly conformally flat induced metric on $B_1(\mathbb{R}^3)$ with pointwise isotropic condition number $1$ (`manual derivation`):

   $$
   ds_{S^3}^2 \;=\; \lVert dU\rVert_{\mathbb{R}^4}^2 \;=\; \frac{1}{2}\operatorname{Tr}(dU^\dagger dU) \;=\; \frac{4\,\lVert d\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rVert^2}{\bigl(1 + \lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rVert^2\bigr)^2}, \qquad \lambda(\boldsymbol{\sigma}) \;:=\; \frac{ds_{S^3}}{\lVert d\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rVert} \;=\; \frac{2}{1 + \lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rVert^2} \;\in\; [1, 2].
   $$

   From center $\lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rVert = 0$ ($\lambda = 2$) to equator $\lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rVert = 1$ ($\lambda = 1$), the linear conformal length scale stays strictly within **$[1, 2]$** (max/min scale ratio $= 2$; metric scalar $\lambda^2 \in [1, 4]$; $\mathrm{SO}(3)$ rotation-angle scale $2\lambda \in [2, 4]$). Every inter-layer state and `KV-Cache` entry is strictly bounded inside $[-1, +1]^3$, eliminating unbounded norm outliers for scale-free fixed-point `INT8`/`INT4` state storage (empirical quantization sensitivity across antipodal seam flips $\pm\hat{\mathbf{u}}$ on $\lVert\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rVert = 1$ is tracked in [@prob-ch39-tft-scale-and-seams]).

## 37.1.2 Geometric Reconstruction of the Five Core Transformer Modules {#sec-ch39-22-2}

1. **Sequence Position Encoding: 3D Non-Abelian Path Holonomy (`3D-NAPE` Replacing 2D Abelian `RoPE`)**:
   Whereas 2D `RoPE` (Su et al. 2021/2024) applies mutually commuting rotations $R_m R_n = R_n R_m = R_{m+n}$ whose position operators encode only scalar offset $m - n$ without path-ordered curvature, `3D-NAPE` accumulates non-coplanar circular-arc rotors along each concept field:

   - **Constant-step regime (recovering `RoPE`)**: With constant step $\boldsymbol{\gamma}_0^{(k)}$, right recurrence $\boldsymbol{\Pi}_t^{(k)} = \boldsymbol{\Pi}_{t-1}^{(k)} \star_{\mathcal{S}} \boldsymbol{\gamma}_0^{(k)}$ ($\boldsymbol{\Pi}_0^{(k)} = \mathbf{0}$) paired with left inverse calibration $\widetilde{\boldsymbol{\sigma}}_{Q,t}^{(k)} = (-\boldsymbol{\Pi}_t^{(k)}) \star_{\mathcal{S}} \boldsymbol{\sigma}_{Q,t}^{(k)}$ and $\widetilde{\boldsymbol{\sigma}}_{K,s}^{(k)} = (-\boldsymbol{\Pi}_s^{(k)}) \star_{\mathcal{S}} \boldsymbol{\sigma}_{K,s}^{(k)}$ yields $\Pi_t \Pi_s^{-1} = (\gamma_0^{(k)})^{t-s}$, depending **strictly on relative offset $t - s$**;

4. **Feed-Forward Network: Fano-Inspired Cyclic Lie-Triple Curvature Gate (Replacing $\frac{8}{3}d$-Gated `SwiGLU` and $4d$ FFNs) and Two Retractions**:
   Motivated by the cyclic difference set $(0, 1, 3)$ of the Fano plane $\mathrm{PG}(2, 2)$ ([§32.3.2](ch39-s20-ternion-field-composition-and-hilbert-reprojection.md#sec-ch39-20-2)), we wire concept fields into triples $(k, m_1, m_2)$ (either $K/7$ disjoint Fano heptads when $7 \mid K$ as at $K = 84$ or $K = 1008$, or cyclic shifts $m_1 = (k+1)\bmod K,\; m_2 = (k+3)\bmod K$ at general $K$ such as $K = 1024$) and apply the **Lie triple curvature bracket** $\{\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b}, \mathbf{c}\} = (\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{c})\mathbf{b} - (\mathbf{b}\cdot\mathbf{c})\mathbf{a}$ to the seam-invariant sine vectors $\boldsymbol{\xi}_{t,\mathrm{mid}}^{(k)} := \boldsymbol{\xi}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}_{t,\mathrm{mid}}^{(k)})$ and $\mathbf{f}_t^{(k)} := \boldsymbol{\xi}\bigl(\boldsymbol{\sigma}_{t,\mathrm{mid}}^{(m_1)} \star_{\mathcal{S}} \boldsymbol{\sigma}_{t,\mathrm{mid}}^{(m_2)}\bigr)$:

   $$
   \boldsymbol{\Delta}_{\mathrm{FFN}}^{(k)} \;=\; \mathbf{w}_1^{(k)} \times \boldsymbol{\xi}_{t,\mathrm{mid}}^{(k)} \;+\; \Bigl\{\boldsymbol{\xi}_{t,\mathrm{mid}}^{(k)},\; \mathbf{w}_2^{(k)},\; \mathbf{f}_{t}^{(k)}\Bigr\}, \qquad \boldsymbol{\sigma}_{t,\mathrm{out}}^{(k)} \;=\; \boldsymbol{\sigma}_{t,\mathrm{mid}}^{(k)} \star_{\mathcal{S}} \mathcal{R}\bigl(\boldsymbol{\Delta}_{\mathrm{FFN}}^{(k)}\bigr).

Two exact retractions $\mathcal{R} : \mathbb{R}^3 \to B_1(\mathbb{R}^3)$ are available (`manual derivation`):
   - **Purely rational retraction** $\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{rat}}(\mathbf{u}) := \frac{\mathbf{u}}{1 + \lVert\mathbf{u}\rVert^2}$: uses only $+, \times, \div$ (**zero square roots, zero transcendentals**, with origin derivative $d\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{rat}}(\mathbf{0}) = I_3$), mapping into $B_{1/2}(\mathbb{R}^3)$ with maximum single-step rotation $4\arctan\frac{1}{2} \approx 106.26020471^\circ$;
