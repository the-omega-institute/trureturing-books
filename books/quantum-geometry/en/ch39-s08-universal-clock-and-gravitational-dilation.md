   $$
   c \;\equiv\; \ell_P \nu_0 \;=\; \frac{\ell_P}{t_P}.
   $$

## 33.7.2 Constant Master Clock $\nu_0 \equiv \mathrm{const}$ Versus Proper-Time Frame Rate $d\tau/dt$: Orthogonal Budget Splits for Kinematic and Static Radial Gravitational Dilation {#sec-ch39-08-2}

Picture two processors driven by the **exact same $5.0\;\mathrm{GHz}$ crystal oscillator**: one renders a single smooth sphere in an empty desert, needing only a handful of pairwise checks per frame and outputting thousands of frames per second; the other stands in a blizzard rendering a billion colliding ice crystals, and—to achieve the **exact same convergence tolerance $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{self}}$**—must process massive environmental cross-checks on every frame, causing its frame rate to drop sharply even though its $5.0\;\mathrm{GHz}$ hardware clock never slowed down! Placing this constant-frequency comparator into our Pythagorean budget equation yields both relativistic clock-slowing laws along one unified accounting chain (that the budget adds in squares is an input, not something the queueing picture derives—splitting slots linearly, as with the two chips, would give a linear factor of the $1 - \eta$ type):

1. **Special-relativistic kinematic dilation: cross-node spatial address hops crowd out self-halting bandwidth (`MODEL` + `manual derivation`)**.
   When observer $B$ traverses the spatial lattice at velocity $v$ (dimensionless speed ratio $\beta := v/c \in [0, 1]$) relative to a far-field rest frame $A$, each unit of coordinate time diverts comparison rate $\nu_{\mathrm{move}} = \nu_0(v/c) = \nu_0\beta$ into **spatial address-pointer updates** across cells (parameterized by auxiliary kinematic tilt $\sin\theta_{\mathrm{kin}} := \beta = v/c$). Under orthogonal sum-of-squares conservation (the squared budget is just the line element $c^2 dt^2 - d\mathbf{x}^2 = c^2 d\tau^2$ divided by $dt^2$, an input rather than a derivation, while across Lorentz boosts of rapidity $r = \operatorname{artanh}\beta$ the invariance of that line element uses the real identity $|\cosh r|^2 - |\sinh r|^2 = 1$ in [`bogoliubov_norm_conservation`](lean:D5/S3/Quantum/Bogoliubov/BogoliubovNormConservation.bogoliubov_norm_conservation), Conjunct 4 of [@thm-ch39-null-cone-speed-limit-and-pythagorean-budget]—recalling from [Chapter 17, §17.1](ch17-lorentz-geometry.md) [@ex-rapidity-three-fifths] that $\sin\theta_{\mathrm{kin}} = \beta$ is an algebraic budget reparameterization of $|\beta| \le 1$ rather than an identification of hyperbolic boosts with Euclidean rotations), the total squared clock budget stays constant:

   $$
   \begin{aligned}
   \nu_{\mathrm{move}}^2 + \nu_{\mathrm{self}}^2 \;=\; \nu_0^2 \qquad &\Longrightarrow \qquad \nu_{\mathrm{self}} \;=\; \nu_0\cos\theta_{\mathrm{kin}} \;=\; \nu_0\sqrt{1 - \frac{v^2}{c^2}}, \\

   \end{aligned}
   $$
