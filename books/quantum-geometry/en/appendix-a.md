# Appendix A Dictionary of Conventional vs. Geometric Quantum Terms {#appendix-a-dictionary-of-conventional-vs-geometric-quantum-terms}

Conventional quantum mechanics textbooks are written in the language of abstract Hilbert spaces, spectral decompositions of Hermitian operators, and complex matrix indices. While students quickly learn to manipulate Dirac ket notation $|\psi\rangle$ and commutators $[A, B]$, the underlying spatial directions, oriented bivector areas, spherical curvature defects, and topological obstructions often remain hidden behind algebraic formalism. This appendix provides a master translation dictionary across all ten parts (thirty-nine chapters), the Coda, and [Appendix D](appendix-d.md)–[Appendix E](appendix-e.md)—aligning **Conventional Physics / Mathematics Terms**, **Geometric Intuition Terms**, **Lean 4 Formal Objects / Locked `trureturing@dev` GIDs**, **One-Line Invariants**, and **Epistemic Status**.
