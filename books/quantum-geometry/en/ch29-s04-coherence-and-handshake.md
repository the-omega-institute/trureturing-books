> “In logic there is no ‘existence’ versus ‘non-existence’—only coherence and decoherence; so that the future interferes with the past is nothing strange at all: that is causality.”

In [the preceding section](ch29-s03-timeless-gram-causality.md#sec-coda-timeless-causality), unit-vector Gram rigidity ([@thm-ch29-gram-rigidity-and-pell]) showed how a timeless correlation web dissolves the mechanical domino picture of past pushing future. ## 29.4.1 Dissolving the Boolean Illusion of "Existence vs. Non-Existence": Orthogonal Hiding, Two-Path Cancellation, and Pinching Decoherence {#sec-ch29-handshake-boolean-illusion}

When a local detector reports `0`, Boolean habit declares that "nothing is there"; when it reports a definite click, it declares that an absolute classical entity has come into being. In fact, a bare zero reading by itself never identifies its underlying cause (an identically zero input also yields zero), and within controlled linear and operator models, phenomena loosely lumped together as "vanishing" or "freezing into a classical fact" correspond to three mathematically distinct operations (the component formulas below are elementary calculations, `manual derivation`; reading them as "no existence versus non-existence, only coherence and decoherence" is this book's interpretation, `MODEL`):

1. **Orthogonal Subspace Hiding (Vector Norm Intact)**: In the real plane, take the unit ruler $\mathbf{A} = (1, 0)$ and unit state vector $\mathbf{B} = (\cos\theta, \sin\theta)$, with visible subspace $M = \operatorname{span}(\mathbf{A})$. Splitting $\mathbf{B} = \mathbf{B}_\parallel + \mathbf{B}_\perp$ gives $\mathbf{B}_\parallel = (\cos\theta, 0)$ and $\mathbf{B}_\perp = (0, \sin\theta)$, obeying Pythagorean norm conservation $\|\mathbf{B}\|^2 = \|\mathbf{B}_\parallel\|^2 + \|\mathbf{B}_\perp\|^2 = 1$. At $\theta = \pi/2$, the reading along $\mathbf{A}$ vanishes ($\mathbf{A}\cdot\mathbf{B} = 0$, $\mathbf{B}_\parallel = \mathbf{0}$), yet $100\%$ of the squared vector norm resides intact in the orthogonal complement ($\|\mathbf{B}_\perp\|^2 = 1$), the squared norm of the area bivector is $\|\mathbf{A}\wedge\mathbf{B}\|^2 = \sin^2\theta = 1$, and the superposition norm squared is $\|\mathbf{A} + \mathbf{B}\|^2 = 2$—orthogonal hiding, not amplitude annihilation.
2. **Two-Path Scalar Anti-Phase Cancellation and Two-Port Conservation**: With nonnegative real beam magnitudes $a = \cos\xi, b = \sin\xi$ ($a^2 + b^2 = 1$) and total relative phase $\Delta \in [0, 2\pi]$, the raw single-port scalar intensity is

   $$
   I_{\mathrm{raw}} = \bigl|a + b e^{i\Delta}\bigr|^2 = a^2 + b^2 + 2ab\cos\Delta.
   $$

   At $\Delta = \pi$ (using $e^{i\pi} = -1$, whence $e^{iu} + e^{i(u+\pi)} = 0$), $I_{\mathrm{raw}} = (a - b)^2$, which **vanishes if and only if the two magnitudes are strictly equal, $a = b$**. Moreover, in a physical balanced unitary two-port recombiner $b_\pm = (a \pm b e^{i\Delta})/\sqrt{2}$, the port probabilities $p_+ = |b_+|^2 = \frac{1}{2}I_{\mathrm{raw}}$ and $p_- = |b_-|^2$ satisfy $p_+ + p_- = 1$ identically: at $a = b = 1/\sqrt{2}, \Delta = \pi$, the dark `+` port ($p_+ = 0$) is paired with a bright `-` port ($p_- = 1$), conserving total probability.
3. **Pinching Decoherence and "Settled Classical Records"**: For a normalized two-path state with coherence retention $\gamma \in [0, 1]$, define the path density family

   $$
   \rho_\gamma = \begin{pmatrix} a^2 & \gamma ab e^{-i\Delta} \\ \gamma ab e^{i\Delta} & b^2 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad p_\pm = \frac{1}{2}\bigl(1 \pm 2\gamma ab\cos\Delta\bigr).
   $$

   For every $\gamma \in [0, 1]$, $\rho_\gamma$ is positive semidefinite of trace $1$ with purity $\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_\gamma^2) = a^4 + b^4 + 2\gamma^2 a^2 b^2 = 1 - 2(1 - \gamma^2)a^2 b^2$. Under unread computational-basis pinching ($\gamma = 0$), the off-diagonal coherence entries vanish (discarding $2a^2 b^2$ of Hilbert–Schmidt mass relative to $\gamma = 1$), while the diagonal populations $a^2, b^2$ remain untouched and both output ports flatten to $p_\pm = 1/2$. One must distinguish **channel idempotence** from **matrix idempotence**: the unread pinching map obeys $\Pi_P(\Pi_P(\rho)) = \Pi_P(\rho)$ (repeating decoherence leaves a block-diagonal record unchanged), whereas the pinched mixed state itself generally does not satisfy $\rho_0^2 = \rho_0$ (for equal weights $\rho_0 = I/2$, and $(I/2)^2 = I/4 \neq I/2$).

## 29.4.2 Causality as a Two-Way Born Handshake and Common-Phase Fairness {#sec-ch29-handshake-born-reciprocity}

Why can a one-way complex overlap $\langle\phi|\psi\rangle$ never serve by itself as an objective causal weight? Under **independent** phase rotations $|\psi\rangle \mapsto e^{i\theta_\psi}|\psi\rangle$ and $|\phi\rangle \mapsto e^{i\theta_\phi}|\phi\rangle$ (note that a *common* rephasing $\theta_\psi = \theta_\phi$ already leaves $\langle\phi|\psi\rangle$ unchanged), direct calculation (`manual derivation`) gives

$$
\langle\phi\mid\psi\rangle \longmapsto e^{i(\theta_\psi - \theta_\phi)}\langle\phi\mid\psi\rangle, \qquad \langle\psi\mid\phi\rangle = \overline{\langle\phi\mid\psi\rangle} \longmapsto e^{-i(\theta_\psi - \theta_\phi)}\langle\psi\mid\phi\rangle.
$$

A one-way overlap carries the relative gauge factor $e^{i(\theta_\psi - \theta_\phi)}$ and is therefore not an invariant of the state rays; only multiplying the forward overlap $\langle\phi|\psi\rangle$ by its conjugate echo $\langle\psi|\phi\rangle = \overline{\langle\phi|\psi\rangle}$ cancels the relative phase and locks in the real, nonnegative, gauge-invariant, endpoint-symmetric Born handshake weight (`manual derivation`):

$$
w(\psi \leftrightarrow \phi) = \langle\phi\mid\psi\rangle\langle\psi\mid\phi\rangle = \bigl|\langle\phi\mid\psi\rangle\bigr|^2 = \bigl|\langle\psi\mid\phi\rangle\bigr|^2 = \operatorname{Tr}(P_\phi P_\psi) = \operatorname{Tr}(P_\psi P_\phi),
$$

together with the unit-vector rank-one projector sandwich $P_\psi P_\phi P_\psi = |\psi\rangle\langle\psi|\phi\rangle\langle\phi|\psi\rangle\langle\psi| = |\langle\phi|\psi\rangle|^2 P_\psi$ (when a unitary $U$ acts between preparation and test, the reversed experiment uses the adjoint $U^\dagger$, satisfying $|\langle\phi|U|\psi\rangle|^2 = |\langle\psi|U^\dagger|\phi\rangle|^2$, rather than claiming symmetry of a fixed forward propagator without taking its adjoint). At the level of common-$U(1)$ invariant polynomials, why do real nonnegative invariants of minimal nontrivial degree land squarely on quadratic Hermitian forms? The frozen theorem below certifies rank-one modulus-square reduction, Born conditional-ensemble pairing, and the common-phase polynomial invariant algebra:

## 29.4.3 Future–Past Interference: Stationary-Phase Bundles, Weak Values, Delayed-Choice Erasure No-Signaling, and Gram Endpoint Swap {#sec-ch29-handshake-future-past}

Once transition weights are understood as Hermitian pairings across initial and final boundaries, "interference between future and past" loses all mystique: apparent local irreversibility arises only because a partial-trace observer loses access to environmental and correlation sectors, whereas inside an undiscarded joint system unimodular phase records admit coherent reversal and future basis choices sort joint outcomes into complementary interference sub-ensembles without altering the unconditional past marginal. For environmental record reversal, local reduced irreversibility, bipartite correlation blind spots, and Hilbert-space Gram factorizations, the following four statements hold:

1. **Joint Coherent Reversal of Unimodular Multi-Copy Records** ([`joint_coherent_reversal`](lean:D5/S3/ObserverMemory/JointCoherentReversal.joint_coherent_reversal)): Over a finite decidable copy index type `Copy` (`[Fintype Copy] [DecidableEq Copy]`), let $\mathrm{records} : \mathrm{Copy} \to \mathrm{EnvironmentRecord}$, $\rho \in M_2(\mathbb{C})$, and $i, j \in \operatorname{Fin} 2$. Under the **unimodular overlap hypothesis** that every copy has unit-modulus overlap at $(i, j)$ ($\forall k,\, \overline{\gamma_k(i, j)}\,\gamma_k(i, j) = 1$, where $\gamma_k = \operatorname{recordOverlap}(\mathrm{records}(k))$), applying `reverseOn Finset.univ` across all copies restores the entry exactly:

   $$
   \operatorname{multiRecordChannel}\bigl(\operatorname{reverseOn}\,\mathrm{Finset.univ}\,\mathrm{records}\bigr)\bigl(\operatorname{multiRecordChannel}\,\mathrm{records}\,\rho\bigr)_{ij} = \rho_{ij}.
   $$

2. **Local Reduced Irreversibility Is an Access Defect** ([`reduced_irreversibility_is_access_defect`](lean:D5/S3/Quantum/Decoherence/ReducedRecordAccessDefect.reduced_irreversibility_is_access_defect)): For any two qubit matrices $\rho, \sigma \in M_2(\mathbb{C})$ with identical diagonals ($\forall i,\, \rho_{ii} = \sigma_{ii}$) and distinct off-diagonal coherence ($\exists i \neq j,\, \rho_{ij} \neq \sigma_{ij}$), $\mathrm{copyUnitary} \in \mathrm{U}(\operatorname{Fin} 2 \times \operatorname{Fin} 2, \mathbb{C})$ maps their blank-environment product states to distinct joint controlled-record states whose reduced qubit marginals after `traceEnvironment` coincide; hence no recovery map from the reduced qubit matrix alone can reconstruct both joint states, while global conjugate unitary evolution $\operatorname{unitaryEvolution}(\operatorname{star}\mathrm{copyUnitary})$ exactly restores both initial product states.
3. **Orthogonal Blind-Spot Decomposition of Local Marginals vs. Bipartite Correlations** ([`local_marginal_correlation_blind_spot`](lean:D5/S3/Quantum/Entanglement/LocalMarginalCorrelationBlindSpot.local_marginal_correlation_blind_spot)): For integers $m, n$ with $1 \le m$, $1 \le n$, and $1 < mn$, inside the real Hilbert space of bipartite Hermitian operators `BipartiteHermitian m n`, the trace-zero subspace `bipartiteTraceZero m n` (of real dimension $m^2 n^2 - 1$) splits as the Hilbert–Schmidt real-orthogonal direct sum of the local marginal sector of real dimension $(m^2 - 1) + (n^2 - 1)$ and the correlation sector of real dimension $(m^2 - 1)(n^2 - 1)$ (for two qubits $m = n = 2$: $6$ local dimensions versus $9$ correlation dimensions inside the $(4^2 - 1) = 15$-dimensional trace-zero Hermitian real space, ratio $9/15 = 3/5$); furthermore, the two-qubit rank-$1$ `bellDensity` and non-idempotent `classicalCorrelatedDensity` ($\rho_{\mathrm{cl}}^2 \neq \rho_{\mathrm{cl}}$) share identical marginals on both factors while remaining distinct joint states.
4. **Adjoint-Square Gram Realizability of Positive Continuous Operators** ([`gram_realizability`](lean:D5/S3/QuantumBounds/GramRealizability.gram_realizability)): On a complete complex inner-product space $V$, a continuous linear operator $Q : V \to_L[\mathbb{C}] V$ satisfies $0 \le Q$ if and only if there exists $O : V \to_L[\mathbb{C}] V$ such that

   $$
   Q = O^\dagger \circ_L O \quad \land \quad \forall x, y \in V,\; \langle Q x, y\rangle_{\mathbb{C}} = \langle O x, O y\rangle_{\mathbb{C}}.
   $$
