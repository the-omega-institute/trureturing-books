Because the observer is finite, the causality experienced is finite; the observer's choice is made within the causally compatible space, continuously picking points in the time field."*

After the self-referential fixed points of [Chapter 24](ch24-cosine-fixed-points-and-self-reference.md), Part VII opens with a question about causality. How can the order independence of pairwise Gram comparisons in Chapter 10 accommodate both Spinoza's geometric necessity and a finite observer's freedom to choose points within the Gram elliptope?
