# Chapter 25 Timeless Causality and the Gram Compatible Space: From the Pythagorean Pell Seam to Elliptic-Curve Tori {#timeless-causality-and-the-gram-compatible-space-from-the-pythagorean-pell-seam-to-elliptic-curve-tori}

> *"The essence of order-independence is that sequential before-and-after time does not exist at all ... and that is precisely why causality is necessary. Because the observer is finite, the causality experienced is finite; the observer's choice is made within the causally compatible space, continuously picking points in the time field."*

[Chapter 23](ch23-diophantine-definability.md) followed Pythagoras's “All Is Number” through the Pell seam to elliptic-curve tori, the route recalled by the first two panels of Figure 25. After the self-referential fixed points of [Chapter 24](ch24-cosine-fixed-points-and-self-reference.md), Part VII opens with a question about causality. How can the order independence of pairwise Gram comparisons in Chapter 10 accommodate both Spinoza's geometric necessity and a finite observer's freedom to choose points within the Gram elliptope?
