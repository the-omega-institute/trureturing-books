# Appendix J Finite-Environment Decoherence Histories and Saturation Obstructions {#sec-appendix-j}

In [Section 4.2](ch04-s02-quantum-noise.md), we presented phase damping, amplitude damping, and depolarizing noise geometrically as compressions and shifts of the Bloch ball, and in [§4.2.1](ch04-s02-s01-phase-and-environment.md) we traced step-by-step coherence decay under repeated records. Asking the sharper structural question—how far a strictly finite-dimensional environment register can faithfully realize multi-step decoherence histories before hitting saturation obstructions—requires an extended isometric-dilation and history-realization analysis. To keep [Chapter 4](ch04-decoherence.md) flowing directly from phase damping into amplitude damping and the maximally mixed dead center, that full technical development is placed here.
