# E.8 Third-Point Redundancy and Positivity of the Involution: Reducing H2 and H3 with the Arc-Body Calculus, and Restating the Inputs of H5–H7 {#sec-appe-third-point-redundancy}

The table in E.7 records `H2` as three parallel Layer-1 conditions: positive self-pairing, the elliptic negative square of a rank-$2$ bivector, and Gram positive semidefiniteness for any number of points. `H3` then adds parallelogram polarization. This section goes through the same accounts with the chord–sagitta decomposition of [§30.3](ch34-s30-arc-body-calculus-and-finite-stokes.md#sec-ch34-arc-body-calculus-and-finite-stokes). The outcome is that the four items are not independent. When the similarity readings are real scalars, the three items of `H2` follow from two more primitive conditions: **third-point redundancy** (comparing $x$ with $z$ is not changed by routing through a third point $y$) and **positivity of the involution** (the round trip of a nonzero difference is neither zero nor negative). The polarization item of `H3` follows from third-point redundancy together with one positive reading. E.8.2.1 adds a restatement of `H5` on a qubit: among probability rules that see only pairwise comparison, the Born rule is the one of minimal tomographic rank. E.8.2.2 and E.8.2.3 use the same notation to examine `H6` and `H7`: invertibility of the evolution, continuity in time, the generator and conditioning, and then the parameter count of composites and exchange statistics. These two subsections add no clause; they only recast the inputs in terms of compared objects and readings, and use models satisfying every clause to mark the boundaries. E.8.2.4 applies comparison to ensembles: when every object has an opposite, Gram positivity for any number of points is equivalent to "every ensemble has a nonnegative averaged similarity with itself"; `H5` on a qubit can be replaced by ensemble extensionality, which in the `MODEL` reading is Clause I applied to ensembles. E.8.3.1 reads the record of comparisons of one object along time: the rank of the record counts energy levels, an object whose ray sagitta stays away from zero shows that the state space is infinite-dimensional, which gives `H4` a criterion in terms of readings without deriving it; only the two channels together fix the Hamiltonian on the orbit (`H6`).

E.8.3.2–E.8.3.4 treat three inputs that the ledger still lists. E.8.3.2 applies the sagitta table of E.8.1.1 to comparisons between observers in $1+1$ dimensions: dropping the positivity condition does not yet select the Lorentz signature; two further statements are needed, "repeating the same comparison never reverses the order in time" and "the difference part is not square-zero", and for a single comparison the positivity condition and the first of these cannot hold together. E.8.3.3 gives exact identities and two-sided bounds for the entropy gain under a unit-preserving pinching. E.8.3.4 shows that for a quadratic action the finite-step path product equals the leading stationary-phase term exactly; for general actions only explicit one-dimensional cancellation bounds remain, and the continuum-time limit is a literature result or an input. E.8.3.5 returns to three items of `H4` and `H7` that are still listed as plain inputs (the central complex structure, the exchange sector and the choice of valuation), gives each an exact form, and uses models satisfying every clause to show that they remain inputs.

The boundary first. Both conditions are added on top of $\mathcal{A}_0$. What is proved here is "if they are added, then the rest follows"; nothing here claims they can be derived from Clauses I–III, and the countermodels of [@ex-appe-layer0-countermodels] show they cannot. The theorem boxes of this section all correspond to frozen theorems. Every other derivation is marked `manual derivation`, and classical results that are quoted are marked `Literature`.
