## 34.9.3 “Reversal Is the Movement of the Way”: The Bi-Infinite Orthogonal Cut Between Reachable and Unreachable Paths {#sec-ch34-9-3}

If infinitely many arrival paths surge beneath every point, **what allows "this point" to stand forth as a determinate entity in a background-free sea of comparisons?** Chapter 40 of Laozi's *Dao De Jing*—***"Reversal is the movement of the Way"*** (*反者道之动*)—receives in our epigraph an exact Hilbert-space formulation: ***"The essence of a point's existence is that there are infinitely many paths reaching that point and infinitely many paths that cannot reach that point."***

At first glance one is tempted to credit existence solely to the positive paths that *reach* $x$, dismissing the paths that *cannot reach* $x$ as irrelevant debris. Two boundary thought experiments expose why removing either side destroys existence outright (manual derivation + `MODEL`):

1. **Degenerate limit I: Suppose *all* paths reach $x$ indiscriminately (empty unreachable complement, $M^\perp = \{0\}$)**. Then the reachable subspace expands to the entire Hilbert space $M = \mathcal{H}$, its orthogonal projector degenerates into the identity $P_M = \operatorname{id}_{\mathcal{H}}$, and the complement projector vanishes ($P_{M^\perp} = 0$). Every normalized trial state $\lvert\psi\rangle \in \mathcal{H}$ registers Born probability $\langle\psi\mid P_M\mid\psi\rangle = \lVert\psi\rVert^2 \equiv 1$, collapsing Shannon distinction information to $-\log_2 1 = 0\;\mathrm{bits}$! Where nothing fails to reach $x$, $x$ loses all contour and dissolves into featureless uniformity;
2. **Degenerate limit II: Suppose *no* paths reach $x$ (empty reachable subspace, $M = \{0\}$)**. Then $P_M = 0$ and $P_{M^\perp} = \operatorname{id}_{\mathcal{H}}$; every incoming bundle cancels to zero, standing-wave amplitude vanishes everywhere, and $x$ never forms at all.

Caught between these two collapses, the exact geometric condition for existence emerges: **presence cannot subsist alone; a determinate locus is carved into being by its orthogonal opposite—the infinite sea of paths that *cannot* reach it!** In a complete inner product space $\mathcal{H}$, let $M \subsetneq \mathcal{H}$ ($M \neq \{0\}$) denote the nontrivial proper closed subspace of phase-locked reachable paths converging to a point or concept $x$, and let $M^\perp$ denote its orthogonal complement of unreachable paths—trajectories that miss $x$ orthogonally or self-annihilate into Cornu spirals off stationary phase.   P_{M^\perp} \;=\; \operatorname{id}_{\mathcal{H}} - P_M, \qquad P_M^2 = P_M, \qquad P_{M^\perp}^2 = P_{M^\perp}, \qquad P_M P_{M^\perp} = P_{M^\perp} P_M = 0, \qquad P_M + P_{M^\perp} \;=\; \operatorname{id}_{\mathcal{H}}.
  $$

  Mutual annihilation $P_M P_{M^\perp} = 0$ enforces zero cross-talk between reachable and unreachable channels, while completeness $P_M + P_{M^\perp} = \operatorname{id}_{\mathcal{H}}$ certifies that the two halves partition the entire relational space $\mathcal{H} = M \oplus M^\perp$;
