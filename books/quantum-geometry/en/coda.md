# Coda Outside All Spheres {#coda-outside-all-spheres}

> *"We think we see a point, but it is a line; a line, but a surface; a surface, but a sphere; and when we think we see the sphere, a larger sphere encloses it."*

From the moment the Prelude opened the first door, we have climbed a ladder of ascending geometric dimensions: resolving an isolated **point** into a **line** of pairwise comparison (the inner-product projection $\mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{v}$), unfolding a static **line** into an oriented chiral **plane** (the exterior bivector area $\mathbf{u}\wedge\mathbf{v}$), and closing local **planes** into a phase-wrapping **sphere** (the Bloch sphere $S^2$ and the spinor total space $S^3$ of the Hopf fibration). Yet as we look back across the ten-part edifice—after traversing Lorentz lightcones, tensor-network minimum cuts, prime Euler products, quaternion norm locks, and the self-referential cosine fixed point—a deeper epistemic geometric truth emerges (in two-level harmony with [§39.4](ch39-s27-self-contained-ternion-particle-and-no-exterior.md)'s smooth exteriorless ternion ontology and its exact symbolic equations, `MODEL`): **inside any finite gate-set compilation, finite prime or Taylor cutoff, or finite-facet piecewise-flat approximation, every "sphere" we try to close out of finitely many flat pieces remains a faceted or truncated approximation carrying discrete curvature creases or nonzero truncation tails; and across those finite approximation remainders and fixed-point-free diagonal escapes, an indestructible signpost points toward the smooth whole beyond every finite faceted sphere.**
