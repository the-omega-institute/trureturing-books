## 30.1.1 Geometric Optimization and Three-Face Anatomy of the Minimal Trihedron: From Climbing Orthogonal Side Walls to Cutting Along the Flat Interior Chord Plane {#sec-ch34-26-1}

$$
O = (0, 0, 0), \qquad \mathbf{B} = (0, 1, 0), \qquad \mathbf{A}(\epsilon) = (\sin\epsilon, \cos\epsilon, 0), \qquad \mathbf{C}(\epsilon) = (0, \cos\epsilon, \sin\epsilon).

Across this single cell $\Delta\mathcal{T}_\epsilon$ linking $\mathbf{A}(\epsilon)$ and $\mathbf{C}(\epsilon)$ sit **three geometric faces of sharply distinct curvature and dimension**, corresponding one-for-one to the three algorithmic regimes that carry shortest-path search from classical greedy queueing to cross-scale interior tunneling (`MODEL` + `manual derivation`):

   $$
   L_{\mathrm{walls}}(\epsilon) \;=\; \operatorname{arc}(\widehat{AB}) + \operatorname{arc}(\widehat{BC}) \;=\; \epsilon + \epsilon \;=\; 2\epsilon.
   $$

   Information-theoretically, any algorithm that insists on extracting all $n$ vertices in nondecreasing order of distance must distinguish among $n!$ permutations on a comparison tree and therefore incurs at least $\log_2(n!) = \Theta(n\log n)$ comparisons—paying the steep **$\Omega(n\log n)$ global sorting tax** even for vast thickets of side branches that never lie on any long-range bottleneck path!

1. **Fubini–Study geodesic angle triangle inequality for unit vectors in a complex inner-product space** ([`fs_angle_triangle`](lean:D5/S3/QuantumBounds/FubiniStudyRecordTime.fs_angle_triangle)): Let $E$ be a complex inner-product space (`[NormedAddCommGroup E] [InnerProductSpace ℂ E]`), and define for any $x, y \in E$ the phase-insensitive Fubini–Study angle $\operatorname{fsAngle}(x, y) := \arccos\lVert\langle x, y\rangle_{\mathbb{C}}\rVert \in \mathbb{R}$.    $$

::: {.proposition #prop-ch34-tropical-pivot-walk-distance lean="D5/S3/Combinatorics/Graph/Walks/TropicalSchurElimination.pivot_walks_and_distance" title="Single-Vertex Tropical Schur Walk Compression and Walk-Infimum Distance Preservation on Arbitrary Quivers"}
Let $V : \operatorname{Type}\;u$ be an arbitrary universe-polymorphic vertex type (assuming neither finiteness of $V$ nor a decidable-equality instance in the statement), equipped with the complete directed quiver $\operatorname{completeQuiver} V$ (whose hom-type between every pair of vertices is $\operatorname{PUnit}$, representing absent edges by $+\infty$ in the extended nonnegative reals $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}^\infty$). Given an edge-weight function $W : V \to V \to \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}^\infty$, write $\operatorname{Walk}(u, v) := \operatorname{Quiver.Path}(u, v)$ for finite directed walks, let $\operatorname{weight}(W, p) \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}^\infty$ sum edge weights along $p$ ($0$ on `.nil`), and define the walk-infimum distance $\operatorname{distance}(W, u, v) := \inf_{p : \operatorname{Walk}(u, v)} \operatorname{weight}(W, p)$ (without assuming attainment of a minimum-weight walk). Fix an eliminated vertex $b : V$ and define on the surviving pivot subtype $V_{\neq b} := \{x : V \mid x \neq b\}$ the single-vertex tropical $(\min, +)$ Schur update

$$
\operatorname{schur}(W, b)(a, z) \;:=\; \min\bigl(W(a.\mathrm{val}, z.\mathrm{val}),\; W(a.\mathrm{val}, b) + W(b, z.\mathrm{val})\bigr).
$$

Then for every pair of surviving vertices $a, z : V_{\neq b}$, all three properties hold ([`pivot_walks_and_distance`](lean:D5/S3/Combinatorics/Graph/Walks/TropicalSchurElimination.pivot_walks_and_distance)):

1. **Forward walk shortcutting**: For every walk $p : \operatorname{Walk}(a.\mathrm{val}, z.\mathrm{val})$ in $V$, there exists a reduced walk $q : \operatorname{Walk}(a, z)$ in $V_{\neq b}$ such that $\operatorname{weight}\bigl(\operatorname{schur}(W, b),\, q\bigr) \le \operatorname{weight}(W, p)$;
2. **Backward walk realizability**: For every reduced walk $q : \operatorname{Walk}(a, z)$ in $V_{\neq b}$, there exists a walk $p : \operatorname{Walk}(a.\mathrm{val}, z.\mathrm{val})$ in $V$ such that $\operatorname{weight}(W, p) = \operatorname{weight}\bigl(\operatorname{schur}(W, b),\, q\bigr)$;
3. **Exact preservation of walk-infimum distance**: $\operatorname{distance}\bigl(\operatorname{schur}(W, b),\, a,\, z\bigr) = \operatorname{distance}(W,\, a.\mathrm{val},\, z.\mathrm{val})$.
