## 29.11.3 The Maxwell–Laman 3D Rigidity Polynomial $\Delta E(N) = \frac{(N-3)(N-4)}{2}$: Isostatic Lock, Gravitational Deficit Angles, and Temporal Flips {#sec-ch34-10-3}

Consider any $N$-vertex closed triangulated spherical polyhedron assembled edge-to-edge from minimal triangular arc-bodies ($N \ge 3$). In any triangulation where every face carries $3$ darts and every undirected edge pairs $2$ opposite darts ($3F = 2E_{\mathrm{3D}}$), substituting into $N - E_{\mathrm{3D}} + F = 2$ solves immediately for the exact edge and face counts (manual derivation, valid for all $N \ge 3$, with $N = 3$ representing the two-sided triangular dihedron $F = 2, E = 3$):

$$
E_{\mathrm{3D}}(N) \;=\; 3N - 6, \qquad F(N) \;=\; 2N - 4.
$$

Now observe the remarkable coincidence: $E_{\mathrm{3D}}(N) = 3N - 6$ is identically equal to James Clerk Maxwell's (1864) **3D isostatic bar-and-joint rigidity count** ($3N$ translational coordinates of $N$ nodes in $\mathbb{R}^3$, minus the $3$ translations and $3$ rotations of the 3D Euclidean rigid-motion group $\mathrm{SE}(3)$, likewise equals $3N - 6$)! $$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta E(N) &\;:=\; \binom{N}{2} - E_{\mathrm{3D}}(N) \;=\; \frac{N(N - 1)}{2} - (3N - 6) \\
&\;=\; \frac{N^2 - 7N + 12}{2} \;\equiv\; \frac{(N - 3)(N - 4)}{2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
