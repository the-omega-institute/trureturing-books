$$
\bigl\lVert x_k - x_{k-1} \bigr\rVert \;=\; \bigl\lVert \mathcal{F}_{\mathbf{I}}(x_{k-1}) - x_{k-1} \bigr\rVert \;\le\; \varepsilon_{\mathrm{self}} \quad \leadsto \quad (\text{idempotent latch: }x^* \equiv x_k).

1. **Hilbert–Schmidt Pythagorean mass split under standard-basis qubit pinching** ([`pinching_hilbert_schmidt_pythagorean`](lean:D5/S3/QuantumChannels/PinchingProjection.pinching_hilbert_schmidt_pythagorean)): Writing $\mathrm{QubitMatrix} = M_2(\mathbb{C})$, $\operatorname{pinching}(\rho)_{ij} = \delta_{ij}\rho_{ij}$ ($i, j \in \operatorname{Fin} 2$), and $\operatorname{hilbertSchmidtInner}(A, B) := \operatorname{Tr}(A^{\mathrm{H}} B) \in \mathbb{C}$, for every $\rho \in \mathrm{QubitMatrix}$,

   $$
   \operatorname{hilbertSchmidtInner}(\rho, \rho) \;=\; \operatorname{hilbertSchmidtInner}\bigl(\operatorname{pinching}(\rho),\, \operatorname{pinching}(\rho)\bigr) + \operatorname{hilbertSchmidtInner}\bigl(\rho - \operatorname{pinching}(\rho),\, \rho - \operatorname{pinching}(\rho)\bigr);
   $$

2. **Exact off-diagonal coherence mass discarded by qubit pinching** ([`pinching_discarded_coherence_mass`](lean:D5/S3/QuantumChannels/PinchingProjection.pinching_discarded_coherence_mass)): For every $\rho \in \mathrm{QubitMatrix}$, the Hilbert–Schmidt self-inner product of the residual $\rho - \operatorname{pinching}(\rho)$ equals in $\mathbb{C}$ the sum of squared moduli of the two off-diagonal entries:

   $$
   \operatorname{hilbertSchmidtInner}\bigl(\rho - \operatorname{pinching}(\rho),\, \rho - \operatorname{pinching}(\rho)\bigr) \;=\; \bigl(\operatorname{Complex.normSq}(\rho_{01}) + \operatorname{Complex.normSq}(\rho_{10}) : \mathbb{C}\bigr);

3. **Minus-identity square of a skew-adjoint right-unitary matrix** ([`square_eq_neg_one_of_skewAdjoint_unitary`](lean:D5/S3/Quantum/Algebra/Unitary/BalancedUnitaryComplexStructure.square_eq_neg_one_of_skewAdjoint_unitary)): For any finite decidable index type $n$ (`[Fintype n] [DecidableEq n]`) and matrix $R \in M_n(\mathbb{C})$ satisfying both $R^{\mathrm{H}} = -R$ and $R R^{\mathrm{H}} = I_n$,

4. **Co-rank upper bound on strict inclusions of the finite observable Krylov tower** ([`observable_krylov_strict_growth_bound`](lean:D5/S3/ObserverMemory/Dynamics/ObservableKrylovGrowthBound.observable_krylov_strict_growth_bound)): Let $\mathbb{K}$ satisfy `[RCLike 𝕜]`, let $V, Y$ be finite-dimensional inner-product spaces over $\mathbb{K}$, and let $T : V \to_{\mathrm{l}}[\mathbb{K}] V$ and $C : V \to_{\mathrm{l}}[\mathbb{K}] Y$ be $\mathbb{K}$-linear maps. 5. **Permanent stability of the observable Krylov tower once two consecutive stages agree** ([`observable_krylov_once_stable_permanently`](lean:D5/S3/ObserverMemory/Dynamics/ObservableKrylovPermanentStability.observable_krylov_once_stable_permanently)): Under the finite-dimensional inner-product hypotheses of Clause 4, if $\operatorname{observableKrylov}(T, C, m) = \operatorname{observableKrylov}(T, C, m + 1)$ holds at stage $m \in \mathbb{N}$, then for every $r \in \mathbb{N}$,

6. **Rank-one Born modulus-square weight, trace pairing, and unread conditional mixture** ([`rank_one_born_pairing_weight`](lean:D5/S3/Observer/Conditioning/RankOneBornPairingWeight.rank_one_born_pairing_weight)): For finite types $n, \kappa$ with decidable equality on $n$ (`[Fintype n] [DecidableEq n] [Fintype kappa]`), let $P : \kappa \to M_n(\mathbb{C})$ satisfy `IsRecordMeasurement P` and let $\rho \in M_n(\mathbb{C})$ satisfy `rho.PosSemidef ∧ Matrix.trace rho = 1`. 7. **Stepwise recurrence and finite-horizon conservation for monitored unitary return** ([`monitored_return_conservation`](lean:D5/S3/QuantumChannels/ProjectionDiagnostics/MonitoredReturnConservation.monitored_return_conservation)): Let $E$ be a complete complex inner-product space, $U : E \simeq_{\mathrm{li}}[\mathbb{C}] E$ a complex linear isometric equivalence, and $v \in E$ a unit vector ($\lVert v\rVert = 1$).    s_0 \;=\; 1, \qquad \forall n \in \mathbb{N},\;\; p_{n+1} \;=\; s_n - s_{n+1}, \qquad \forall N \in \mathbb{N},\;\; \sum_{n=0}^{N-1} p_{n+1} + s_N \;=\; 1.
