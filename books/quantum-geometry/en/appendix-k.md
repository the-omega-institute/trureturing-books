# Appendix K Shor Finite-Period Sampling, Continued-Fraction Events, and Executable Order Certificates {#sec-appendix-k}

In [Section 15.4](ch15-s04-shor-order-reconstruction.md), we used the finite Poisson summation formula and phase-gear visualizations to explain exact dual combs when the period divides the register size and spectral leakage when it does not, moving directly into full worked hand calculations for Grover, QFT, and Shor in [§15.4.1](ch15-s04-s04-worked-algorithms.md). Bridging that geometric picture to exact counting-register marginals for non-dividing periods, continued-fraction observation events, and executable order-and-factor certificates involves a dense sequence of formal probability and number-theoretic theorems.
