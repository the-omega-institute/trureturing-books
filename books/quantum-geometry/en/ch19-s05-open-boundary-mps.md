# 19.5 Open-boundary matrix product states: three-site marginal recovery and intrinsic genericity {#sec-open-boundary-mps-recovery}

Whereas the width-three bridge graph bounds the entanglement rank transmissible across a fixed bottleneck cut, one-dimensional **homogeneous open-boundary matrix product states** (MPS) of bond dimension $2$ address the dual inverse problem: **when does a single contiguous three-site unnormalized marginal matrix uniquely determine the global many-body state up to a single global unit-modulus phase $z$ ($\|z\| = 1$)?**

Following the Lean formalization, write `Bond := Matrix (Fin 2) (Fin 2) ℂ` for $2\times 2$ virtual bond matrices and `Word n := Fin n → Fin 2` for length-$n$ qubit words. Given site matrices $a_0, a_1 \in \mathrm{Bond}$ and open-boundary vectors $b_0, b_1 \in \mathbb{C}^2$, the open-boundary MPS amplitude and state family are

$$
\operatorname{mpsState}(n, a_0, a_1, b_0, b_1)(w) := b_0 \cdot_{\mathrm{v}} \Bigl(\Bigl(\prod_{j \in \mathrm{Fin}\,n} a_{w(j)}\Bigr) *_{\mathrm{v}} b_1\Bigr), \qquad \operatorname{openBoundaryMPS}(n) := \bigl\{\psi \;\big|\; \exists a_0, a_1, b_0, b_1,\; \psi = \operatorname{mpsState}(n, a_0, a_1, b_0, b_1)\bigr\},
$$

where $\cdot_{\mathrm{v}}$ denotes the unconjugated bilinear `dotProduct`. Splitting a chain of length $k + m$ into its first $k$ sites and remaining $m$ sites defines the flattened matrix $\operatorname{amplitudeColumns}(k, m, \psi)_{u, b} := \psi(\operatorname{append}(u, b))$ and the **unnormalized** $k$-site prefix marginal

$$
\operatorname{rawPrefix}(k, m, \psi) := \operatorname{amplitudeColumns}(k, m, \psi)\,\bigl(\operatorname{amplitudeColumns}(k, m, \psi)\bigr)^* \in M_{\mathrm{Word}\,k}(\mathbb{C}).
$$

Two concrete hand checks anchor the definitions. First, on a $3$-site chain with commuting diagonal matrices $a_0 = I_2$, $a_1 = \operatorname{diag}(1, 2)$ and boundary vectors $b_0 = b_1 = (1, 1)^T$, the word $w = 010$ has amplitude $(1, 1)\cdot \operatorname{diag}(1, 2)(1, 1)^T = 1 + 2 = 3$; multiplying $\psi$ by $i$ leaves $\psi\psi^*$ and every $\operatorname{rawPrefix}$ unchanged since $i\overline{i} = 1$, illustrating why a global unit phase is invisible to unnormalized marginals. Second, at the minimal tail length $m = 2$ needed for interior windows (total chain length $3 + m = 5$, spanning $2^5 = 32$ complex amplitudes), $\operatorname{rawPrefix}(3, 2, \psi)$ is an $8\times 8$ Hermitian positive-semidefinite matrix, while leaving $p = 1$ site on the left and $q = 1$ site on the right ($p + q = 2$) produces the $8\times 8$ unnormalized interior marginal $\operatorname{rawInterior}(1, 3, 1, \psi)$ on the middle three sites $\{1, 2, 3\}$.

To isolate nondegenerate chains, define for any pair $A : \mathrm{Fin}\,2 \to \mathrm{Bond}$ the transfer map $\operatorname{transfer}(A)(X) := \sum_{i \in \mathrm{Fin}\,2} A_i X A_i^*$ and dual transfer map $\operatorname{dualTransfer}(A)(X) := \sum_{i \in \mathrm{Fin}\,2} A_i^* X A_i$. From a three-site slice $t : \mathrm{Word}\,3 \to \mathbb{C}$, minor and adjugate polynomials construct the rational canonical core $\operatorname{canonicalCore}(t) : \mathrm{Fin}\,2 \to \mathrm{Bond}$ guarded by the denominator polynomial `chartPoly : MvPolynomial (Word 3) ℂ`. A state $\psi : \mathrm{Word}\,(3 + m) \to \mathbb{C}$ satisfies `ObservableChart m ψ` when there exists a probe vector $v : \mathrm{Word}\,3 \to \mathbb{C}$ such that, setting $t := \operatorname{rawPrefix}(3, m, \psi) *_{\mathrm{v}} v$, both $\operatorname{eval}_t(\mathrm{chartPoly}) \neq 0$ and $\operatorname{transfer}(\operatorname{canonicalCore}(t))$ is injective on $M_2(\mathbb{C})$.

The diagonal example requires a distinction between the transfer maps of the given and reconstructed cores. Set $D=\operatorname{diag}(1,2)$. The transfer map $X\mapsto X+DXD$ of the given core $(I_2,D)$ multiplies the matrix units $E_{11},E_{12},E_{21},E_{22}$ by $2,3,3,5$, respectively, so it is invertible. The observable chart tests injectivity of $\operatorname{transfer}(\operatorname{canonicalCore}(t))$ and also requires $\operatorname{eval}_t(\mathrm{chartPoly})\ne0$. The amplitude depends only on the number of ones in the word, making the three-site state $\psi$ permutation symmetric. Every $t=(\psi\psi^*)v$ is proportional to $\psi$, so $t_{001}=t_{010}=t_{100}$. In the three-by-three determinant `minorPoly`, the first column is $(t_{000},t_{010},t_{100})^T$ and the third is $-(t_{000},t_{001},t_{010})^T$. These columns are negatives of each other, giving $\operatorname{eval}_t(\mathrm{chartPoly})=0$ for every $v$. The chart condition fails here. On this three-site chain, however, the first three sites comprise the entire state, and $\psi\psi^*$ determines $\psi$ up to a unit phase.

::: {.theorem #thm-mps-observable-phase-recovery title="MPS State Recovery up to Global Phase in an Observable Chart" lean="D5/S3/Quantum/TensorNetworks/OpenBoundaryMPSDensityRecovery.observable_chart_phase_recovery"}
Let $m \in \mathbb{N}$, and let $\psi, \varphi : \mathrm{Word}\,(3 + m) \to \mathbb{C}$ belong to $\operatorname{openBoundaryMPS}(3 + m)$ with $\operatorname{ObservableChart}(m, \psi)$. Then their unnormalized three-site prefix marginals agree if and only if $\varphi$ and $\psi$ differ by a unit-modulus complex phase:

$$
\operatorname{rawPrefix}(3, m, \varphi) = \operatorname{rawPrefix}(3, m, \psi) \iff \exists z \in \mathbb{C},\; \|z\| = 1 \;\land\; \varphi = z \cdot \psi.
$$

:::

::: proof
If $\varphi = z \cdot \psi$ with $\|z\| = 1$, then $\operatorname{amplitudeColumns}(3, m, \varphi) = z\,\operatorname{amplitudeColumns}(3, m, \psi)$, so $z\overline{z} = \|z\|^2 = 1$ cancels in $\operatorname{rawPrefix}(3, m, \varphi) = \operatorname{rawPrefix}(3, m, \psi)$. Conversely, assume $\operatorname{rawPrefix}(3, m, \varphi) = \operatorname{rawPrefix}(3, m, \psi)$ and choose $v$ witnessing $\operatorname{ObservableChart}(m, \psi)$. Both states produce the identical slice $t = \operatorname{rawPrefix}(3, m, \psi) *_{\mathrm{v}} v$ with $\operatorname{eval}_t(\mathrm{chartPoly}) \neq 0$ (avoiding zero-denominator rational junk values) and injective $\operatorname{transfer}(\operatorname{canonicalCore}(t))$. Using $t$, put both representations into the same gauge with a common canonical core and left boundary. Injectivity of the prefix map and of the $m$-fold iterated transfer map then gives equality of the right-boundary outer products. The right-boundary vectors differ by a unit phase, which carries through the amplitude formula to give $\varphi=z\psi$. The full proof is checked by the Lean kernel.
:::

Is such local recoverability rare or generic inside $\operatorname{openBoundaryMPS}(3 + m)$? Whenever $m \ge 2$ (total chain length $3 + m \ge 5$, accommodating at least $p \ge 1$ left boundary site and $q \ge 1$ right boundary site with $p + q = m$), tracing out the left $p$ sites and right $q$ sites defines the unnormalized interior three-site marginal $\operatorname{rawInterior}(p, 3, q, \cdot) \in M_{\mathrm{Word}\,3}(\mathbb{C})$. Writing $\mathrm{ObsIndex} := \mathrm{Word}\,3 \times \mathrm{Word}\,3$, the predicate `intrinsicInteriorRecovery m` asserts that there exists a nonzero real multivariable polynomial $R \in \mathrm{MvPolynomial}\,(\mathrm{ObsIndex} \oplus \mathrm{ObsIndex})\,\mathbb{R}$ (in the real and imaginary parts of $\operatorname{rawPrefix}(3, m, \psi)$) whose nonvanishing locus $\operatorname{actualPrincipalLocus}(m, R)$ inside the subtype $\operatorname{openBoundaryMPS}(3 + m)$ (equipped with the subspace topology) is **nonempty**, **open** (`IsOpen`), **dense** (`Dense`), and **invariant under unit-modulus phases**; furthermore, for every $\psi$ in $\operatorname{actualPrincipalLocus}(m, R)$, its $13$-coordinate real quotient invariant $x$ and canonical parameter $\theta = \operatorname{positiveRepresentative}(x)$ satisfy simultaneously: (i) $\sigma(x) > 0$ and $\psi = z \cdot \operatorname{state}(3 + m, \theta)$ for some $\|z\| = 1$; (ii) $\operatorname{TwoSiteSpanning}(\operatorname{core}(\theta))$ (the four products $A_i A_j$ span $M_2(\mathbb{C})$) and injectivity of $\operatorname{transfer}(\operatorname{core}(\theta))$; (iii) for every $k \ge 1$, the $k$-step iterated left and right $2\times 2$ boundary Gram matrices under $\operatorname{dualTransfer}(\operatorname{core}(\theta))$ and $\operatorname{transfer}(\operatorname{core}(\theta))$ are strictly positive definite (`PosDef`); and (iv) for every interior split $p \ge 1, q \ge 1, p + q = m$ (with index cast $\mathrm{cast}_h : \mathrm{Word}\,((p + 3) + q) \to \mathrm{Word}\,(3 + m)$) and every $\varphi \in \operatorname{openBoundaryMPS}(3 + m)$,

$$
\operatorname{rawInterior}(p, 3, q, \varphi \circ \mathrm{cast}_h) = \operatorname{rawInterior}(p, 3, q, \psi \circ \mathrm{cast}_h) \iff \exists z \in \mathbb{C},\; \|z\| = 1 \;\land\; \varphi = z \cdot \psi.
$$

::: {.theorem #thm-mps-generic-interior-recovery title="Intrinsic Genericity of Interior MPS Recovery" lean="D5/S3/Quantum/TensorNetworks/OpenBoundaryMPSInteriorGenericity.intrinsic_interior_recovery"}
For every natural number $m \in \mathbb{N}$ satisfying $2 \le m$ (total chain length $3 + m \ge 5$), $\operatorname{intrinsicInteriorRecovery}(m)$ holds: there exists a nonzero real polynomial $R \neq 0$ in the real and imaginary coordinates of the three-site prefix marginal whose principal nonvanishing locus in $\operatorname{openBoundaryMPS}(3 + m)$ is nonempty, open, dense, and unit-phase invariant, and on which every state admits canonical positive-representative reconstruction, two-site spanning, an injective transfer map, positive-definite iterated boundary Gram matrices, and state recovery up to a global unit phase inside $\operatorname{openBoundaryMPS}(3 + m)$ from any contiguous interior three-site unnormalized marginal ($p \ge 1, q \ge 1, p + q = m$).
:::

::: proof
Fix $m \ge 2$. The formalization packages two-site spanning, transfer injectivity, the quotient-chart positivity $\sigma(x) > 0$, and positive-definite boundary propagation into a single real polynomial $R$ evaluated on the real and imaginary entries of $\operatorname{rawPrefix}(3, m, \psi)$, and exhibits an explicit nondegenerate bond-dimension-$2$ witness to certify $R \neq 0$ and nonemptiness of $\operatorname{actualPrincipalLocus}(m, R)$. Continuity of $\psi \mapsto \operatorname{rawPrefix}(3, m, \psi)$ makes the nonvanishing locus open in the subspace topology, while real-analytic line perturbations from any parameter tuple toward the explicit witness have only isolated zeros and establish density in $\operatorname{openBoundaryMPS}(3 + m)$. At every $\psi$ in this locus, properties (ii) and (iii) ensure full-rank positive-definite left ($p \ge 1$) and right ($q \ge 1$) boundary Gram matrices, allowing $\operatorname{rawInterior}(p, 3, q, \psi \circ \mathrm{cast}_h)$ to determine the canonical gauge invariants and lock any $\varphi \in \operatorname{openBoundaryMPS}(3 + m)$ sharing that interior marginal to $z \cdot \psi$ with $\|z\| = 1$. The full proof is checked by the Lean kernel.
:::

Three scope guardrails govern [@thm-mps-observable-phase-recovery] and [@thm-mps-generic-interior-recovery]. First, $\operatorname{rawPrefix}$ and $\operatorname{rawInterior}$ are **unnormalized** reduced density matrices, equivalently sums of outer products of amplitude columns over the traced indices ($\psi$ is not assumed to have unit norm, and the marginal trace encodes $\|\psi\|^2$). Second, the observable chart and the principal locus give sufficient conditions for recovery. Outside them, uniqueness must be checked separately. For example, the five-site GHZ states $\lvert G_\pm\rangle=(\lvert 00000\rangle\pm\lvert 11111\rangle)/\sqrt{2}$ have bond matrices $E_{11},E_{22}$, left boundary $(1,1)$, and right boundary $(1,\pm1)^T/\sqrt{2}$. They are orthogonal, yet tracing out any two sites removes the cross terms and gives the same three-site density matrix. Third, "genericity" here means topological openness and density (`IsOpen` and `Dense`) in the subtype topology of $\operatorname{openBoundaryMPS}(3 + m)$, neither asserting "probability $1$" with respect to an unspecified measure nor claiming polynomial-time numerical robustness against finite-sample noise.

Across the width-three bridge network and one-dimensional homogeneous bond-dimension-$2$ open-boundary MPS chains, we have seen two results: under the ordered cone hypotheses, the bridge has maximal attainable rank $\min(ad, bc)$ ([@thm-bridge-quantum-maxflow-mincut]); on the observable chart, an unnormalized three-site prefix marginal determines the pure MPS state up to a single global unit phase ([@thm-mps-observable-phase-recovery]), while for chain lengths at least $5$ the same recovery holds on the principal locus from any contiguous interior three-site unnormalized marginal ([@thm-mps-generic-interior-recovery]). Rank saturation does not guarantee entropy saturation, as [@ex-bridge-two-five-flat-spectrum] shows exactly. Assuming in the model considered next that entanglement entropy has reached the bound given by the logarithm of the cut capacity remains a separate scenario assumption, and is not equivalent to thermal equilibrium.
