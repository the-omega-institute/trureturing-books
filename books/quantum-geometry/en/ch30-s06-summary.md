# Chapter Summary: The Orthogonal Convergence of Discrete Logic and Continuum Fluids {#sec-ch30-summary}

The chapter connects discrete logic, continuum fluids, and many-body partial-trace geometry through the author's aphorisms about the global static field, finite observers, “fields,” “lines,” and “quantum entanglement, P=NP, entropy.” Mathematically, static satisfying subspaces, complexity equalities with extra resources, restricted-model lower bounds, and the open standard $P\neq NP$ question have distinct boundaries. In the finite-graph coloring example, the Gallai–Hasse–Roy–Vitaver theorem gives an exact equivalence between $k$-colorability and the existence of an orientation whose directed paths have at most $k$ vertices ([@thm-ch30-gallai-roy]); it exchanges two existence witnesses without providing an algorithm or settling the general $P$ vs. $NP$ question. The galactic crossover table compares illustrative upper bounds ([@ex-ch30-galactic-crossover-scale]), not universal search lower bounds. The fluid discussion juxtaposes **Lamb's decomposition ([@rem-ch30-lamb-decomposition-literature]), the open unforced 3D global regularity conjecture ([@conj-coda-navier-stokes-regularity]), and the quadratic advection projection closure obstruction ([@thm-coda-quadratic-advection-no-closure])**, while the entanglement–entropy triad pairs exact two-body Pythagorean conservation $\|\mathbf{r}_A\|^2 + \tau = 1$ and Bell marginal blind spots ([@thm-local-correlation-sectors], [@lemma-bell-mixed-marginal]) with the Area- vs. Volume-Law bond-dimension and $\mathrm{MIP}^* = \mathrm{RE}$ literature ([@rem-ch30-entanglement-entropy-complexity-literature]). These results offer points of reference for the author's geometric interpretation, without establishing mathematical equivalence among the open problems. OpenAI's forced C / D resolution remains external literature ([@rem-ch30-navier-stokes-forced-literature]); the scaling calculation and dissipative comparison distinguish unbounded local speed, finite energy, and nonclosure ([@ex-ch30-core-energy-scaling], [@ex-ch30-dissipative-no-closure]).

Continuing across this orthogonal horizon, **[Chapter 31: The Essence of the Quantum Is Fairness](ch31-quantum-fairness.md)** and **[Chapter 32: Orthogonal Self-Referential Unified Field and Meta-Unification](ch32-unified-field-and-meta-unification.md)** unveil the fivefold abolition of geometric privilege, ergodic statistical holism, and cross-program meta-unification, before we enter the **[Coda: Outside All Spheres](coda.md)** to survey polyhedral angular-defect remainders (see Coda [@rem-coda-polyhedral-topology-literature]), the contracting fixed-point anchor, and the complete dependency map.
