Across the nine preceding parts and [Chapter 37](ch37-dreams-quantum-eraser-and-nonlocal-observer.md) of this monograph—from excising the privileged external "third point $C$" ([Chapter 2](ch02-born-rule.md)) and uncovering the timeless Gram compatibility crystal ([Chapter 29](ch29-timeless-causality-and-gram-space.md) [@thm-ch29-gram-rigidity-and-pell]), to establishing the fivefold constitution of quantum fairness ([Chapter 31](ch31-quantum-fairness.md) [@thm-ch31-seat-pythagorean-monogamy-fairness]), closing the orthogonal self-referential unified field ([Chapter 32 §32.4](ch32-s04-generative-laws.md#sec-ch32-generative-laws) [@thm-ch32-generative-self-reference-and-zeckendorf]), and unveiling waking-pinching versus dream-erasure address masks ([Chapter 37](ch37-dreams-quantum-eraser-and-nonlocal-observer.md))—one last mechanistic idol still lurks beneath ordinary intuition: we instinctively picture an "observer" as a heavy lump of matter trudging across an empty container called "space" while hauling a private warehouse of memories on its back.
