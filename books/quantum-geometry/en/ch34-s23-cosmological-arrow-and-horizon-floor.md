## 34.11.1 Bipartite Unitary Mutual-Information Balance and the Twin Initial Conditions of the Past Hypothesis {#sec-ch34-23-1}

Why can neither "local partial-trace truncation" ([§34.6](ch34-s17-entanglement-horizon-and-thermodynamics.md#sec-ch34-entanglement-horizon-and-thermodynamics)) nor "global unitary purity conservation" alone ever force a universal law of monotonic entropy increase? The answer sits directly inside the exact marginal-entropy-versus-mutual-information balance identity of bipartite unitary mechanics. Let a finite-dimensional composite system $A \times B$ evolve under an arbitrary joint unitary $U \in \mathrm{U}(A \times B)$ from initial state $\rho_0$ to final state $\rho_1 = U \rho_0 U^\dagger$. Because global von Neumann entropy is strictly invariant under unitary conjugation, $S_{AB}(\rho_1) = S_{AB}(\rho_0)$, subtracting the initial and final definitions of quantum mutual information $I(A{:}B) := S_A + S_B - S_{AB}$ yields the exact identity:

$$
\Delta S_A + \Delta S_B \;=\; \Delta I(A{:}B) \;=\; I_1(A{:}B) - I_0(A{:}B).
$$

Inspect the right-hand side: non-negativity of quantum relative entropy guarantees $I_1(A{:}B) \ge 0$ for every final state, yet nothing in unitary dynamics constrains $-I_0(A{:}B)$! Mathematically, `marginal_entropy_change_eq_mutual_information_change` does *not* prove that a positive entropy step makes $I_0 = 0$ or very low initial entropy logically necessary (special partially correlated states with $0 < I_0 < I_1$ or near-maximal marginals can still exhibit single-step $\Delta S_A, \Delta S_B > 0$); rather, it isolates **two exact sufficiency and headroom roles**, showing why cosmology introduces the **Past Hypothesis as an adopted boundary assumption** (`MODEL` / `OPEN`, [@prob-ch34-past-hypothesis-and-cosmological-constant]):

1. **Initial cross-region factorizability as a sufficient condition for nonnegative total marginal-entropy change ($I_0(A{:}B) = 0 \implies \Delta S_A + \Delta S_B = I_1 \ge 0$)**: Whenever $\rho_0 = \rho_{A,0} \otimes \sigma_{B,0}$ factors as a product, $I_0(A{:}B) = 0$ suffices to guarantee $\Delta S_A + \Delta S_B = I_1(A{:}B) \ge 0$ for *every* joint unitary $U$. Without boundary restrictions, pre-existing correlations with $I_0 > I_1$ allow unitary evolution to drive negative total marginal-entropy change ($\Delta S_A + \Delta S_B = I_1 - I_0 < 0$)!
2. **Unsaturated initial marginals as thermodynamic headroom ($S_{\alpha,0} < \ln d_\alpha$)**: Even under $I_0(A{:}B) = 0$, strict local entropy growth on $A$ requires positive local headroom $\ln d_A - S_{A,0} > 0$: if a single marginal is maximally mixed ($S_{A,0} = \ln d_A$), its entropy cannot **increase** ($\Delta S_A \le 0$), though a joint unitary *can* **decrease** it whenever the partner marginal is unsaturated (as `SWAP` on $(\mathbf{1}_2/2)\otimes\lvert 0\rangle\langle 0\rvert$ in Row (iv) below shows: $\Delta S_A = -\ln 2 < 0 < +\ln 2 = \Delta S_B$); only when **both** marginals of an initial product state are maximally mixed ($\rho_{AB} = \frac{\mathbf{1}_A}{d_A}\otimes\frac{\mathbf{1}_B}{d_B} = \frac{\mathbf{1}_{d_A d_B}}{d_A d_B}$) does global unitary invariance lock $\Delta S_A = \Delta S_B = 0$ under every joint unitary. Sustaining macroscopic irreversibility across cosmic epochs thus motivates adopting huge initial headroom $S_{\alpha,0} \ll \ln d_\alpha$ as a cosmological boundary postulate.
