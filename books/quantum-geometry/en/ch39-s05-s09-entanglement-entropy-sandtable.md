# 35.6.7 Cross-Cut Entanglement, Parry Horizon Entropy, and Closing the Eight-Concept Sandtable {#sec-ch39-p2e-sandtable-closing}

Continuing from the single-orbit scale comparison above, we now close the final two rings of the eight-concept sandtable. When an admissible $1^K$-free word is cut into left and right halves, the trailing run of ones $\tau(x)$ on the left and the leading run $\iota(y)$ on the right must obey the cross-cut compatibility condition $\tau(x) + \iota(y) < K$. This boundary rule is not by itself identical to quantum entanglement—two states with the same admissible-word support and computational-basis probabilities can still differ in entanglement—but under the direct-encoder contract it pins down the exact feasible Schmidt-rank rectangle $[1, R_0] \times [1, R_1]$ and page-entropy ceiling $\ln R_b$. The full formal proofs for direct-encoder ranks, local parent Hamiltonians, heat-bath chains, and uniform spectral gaps at fixed forbidden-word length are collected in [Appendix O, §O.1](ch39-s05-s07-cut-entanglement.md).

Moving from spatial cuts to long-range ergodic statistics and finite-horizon address collisions, the Parry stationary weights $\pi_K$ on the reset Markov chain govern the topological entropy $\log_2 \beta_K$ and induced-return entropy, while the finite-arena escape-pair count $E(S)$ tracks the residual ambiguity unresolved by a bounded observer. Epistemic boundaries remain strict here: the topological-entropy identification and Abramov's formula are literature results, the general fractal-horizon scaling law $S_{\mathrm{ent}} \propto R^{D_F}$ is a conceptual model (`MODEL`), and translation-invariant local gapped realizations together with general anisotropic $K \ge 4$ Pisot windows remain open ([@prob-ch39-p2e-fractal-horizon]). The full technical derivations—bilateral Parry sources, maximal entropy and return durations, Tribonacci Slater prefixes, noncontiguous cuts, direction complexes and ternary matroid extensions, substitution discrepancy, and open-chain spectral distance—are gathered in [Appendix O, §O.2](ch39-s05-s08-parry-law-and-entropy.md).
