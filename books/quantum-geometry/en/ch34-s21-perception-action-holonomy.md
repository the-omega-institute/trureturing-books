# 34.21 The Perception–Action Loop: Causal Intervention, Constrained Control, and Anchored Holonomy in the Gram Corridor {#sec-ch34-perception-action-holonomy}

> *An observer capable only of passively receiving round-trip echoes along a frozen axis $\mathbf{I}$ (`Observe / Read`) would be no more than a bolted-down film camera in the theater of the cosmos; only when a single settled record ([§34.20](ch34-s20-single-outcome-and-attractor-basins.md#sec-ch34-single-outcome-attractor-basins)) feeds back to steer the observer's probe along minimal circular arcs—or actively rewrite an external state—within the feasible Gram corridor $\Omega_M$ ([§34.18](ch34-s18-gram-window-and-consensus.md#sec-ch34-gram-window-and-consensus)) (`Act / Write`) do perception and action interlock through anchored phase holonomy into a living, **embodied self-referential observer**.*

In [§34.19](ch34-s19-roundtrip-projection-and-imaginary-blindspot.md#sec-ch34-roundtrip-projection-and-imaginary-blindspot) and [§34.20](ch34-s20-single-outcome-and-attractor-basins.md#sec-ch34-single-outcome-attractor-basins), we completed the **read half-loop (`Observe / Read`)** from the round-trip squared echo $\cos^2\theta$ to the stabilization of an individual classical record. Yet inside a self-contained relational universe, reading is never a one-way dead end: what distinguishes a genuine physical reorientation from a mere paper relabeling of Hilbert-space coordinates is that an embodied observer also commands the **write half-loop (`Act / Write`)**—rotating its own probe axis $\mathbf{I}_t$ relative to **held-fixed external anchors**, or dually applying controlled state-preparation channels relative to a fixed external analyzer.

Our goal in this section is to place passive observation and active causal intervention onto a single operator-trace and Gram-corridor ledger: first establishing a formal operational contract that separates five distinct read, write, and frame-change maps; next proving via exact $2\times 2$ qubit algebra how causal intervention parts ways with common frame conjugation, and deriving a closed-form generative cross-entropy selection benchmark across equal Fubini–Study control arcs; then demonstrating two distinct topological and dimensional obstructions imposed by the Gram memory window $\mathcal{W}_M$ on two-axis finite rotations; and finally classifying the gauge-invariant holonomy coordinates of the closed sensorimotor loop via anchored spanning-tree phase transport.

## 34.21.1 The Read–Write Dual Contract: Operational Criteria Separating Passive Observe / Read, Active Act / Write, and Common Frame Conjugation {#sec-ch34-21-1}

Why does actively rotating a laboratory polarizer produce observable physical consequences in a universe without an external absolute reference frame $C$, whereas changing orthonormal basis on paper changes nothing? Everything hinges on **whether at least one external anchor is held fixed rather than co-rotated**. At discrete control tick $t \in \mathbb{N}$, we represent an embodied observer's closed-loop configuration by the quadruple $(\rho_t, \mathbf{I}_t, \mathcal{W}_M, t)$—where $\rho_t$ is the target density state, $\mathbf{I}_t$ (with projector family $P(\mathbf{I}_t)$) is the observer's active probe axis, and $\mathcal{W}_M$ is the sliding Gram window retaining $M$ recent causal anchors ([§34.18](ch34-s18-gram-window-and-consensus.md#sec-ch34-gram-window-and-consensus)). The contract below pins down read–write trace duality and order noncommutation:

Defining causal intervention relative to a held-fixed external target state or held-fixed external analyzer yields the following closed-loop operational model and formal guardrails (`MODEL` + `LEAN ✓`):

1. **Closed-loop state tuple and Schrödinger–Heisenberg read–write trace duality (`MODEL` + `LEAN ✓`)**: At each tick $t$, the observer alternates two complementary couplings around $(\rho_t, \mathbf{I}_t, \mathcal{W}_M, t)$—in the **read phase (`Observe / Read`)**, holding $\mathbf{I}_t$ fixed to sample the Born law of $\rho_t$ and latch a conditioned record; in the **write phase (`Act / Write`)**, conditioning on stored records to choose a control action $u_t$ that either steers the apparatus axis $\mathbf{I}_t \mapsto \mathbf{I}_{t+1}$ against **held-fixed $\rho_t$** or drives a controlled channel $\rho_t \mapsto \mathcal{E}_{u_t}(\rho_t)$ against **held-fixed $P$**. The frozen theorem [`observer_pullback_trace_identity`](lean:D5/S3/Quantum/Dynamics/ObserverPullbackTraceIdentity.observer_pullback_trace_identity) (`LEAN ✓`) locks their exact algebraic duality: for any quantum channel $\mathcal{E}$ and Heisenberg adjoint $\mathcal{E}^\dagger$ satisfying single-step trace pairing $\operatorname{Tr}\bigl(\mathcal{E}(\rho)\cdot E\bigr) = \operatorname{Tr}\bigl(\rho\cdot \mathcal{E}^\dagger(E)\bigr)$, every horizon length $t \in \mathbb{N}$ obeys

   $$
   \operatorname{Tr}\Bigl(\mathcal{E}^t(\rho)\cdot E\Bigr) \;=\; \operatorname{Tr}\Bigl(\rho\cdot (\mathcal{E}^\dagger)^t(E)\Bigr).
   $$

   Pushing the target state forward $t$ steps against a fixed probe and pulling the observer's effect backward $t$ steps against a fixed target state are coordinate-free duals; **conjugating both state and probe simultaneously by the same unitary**, however, preserves every relative angle and performs zero physical action!
2. **Intrinsic order noncommutation of sequential belief revision and read–write updates (`LEAN ✓`)**: Once perceptual reading and interventional writing alternate, execution order matters strictly. In discrete concept dynamics, [`revision_conflict_noncommutation`](lean:D5/S3/ConceptDynamics/Revision/RevisionConflictNoncommutation.revision_conflict_noncommutation) (`LEAN ✓`) certifies the minimal prototype on `Fin 3 = {0, 1, 2}`: for prioritized intersection revision $\operatorname{revision}_P(A) := \text{if } (A \cap P \neq \emptyset) \text{ then } A \cap P \text{ else } P$ with initial belief $A = \{0\}$ and conflicting constraints $P = \{1, 2\},\; Q = \{0, 1\}$, we have $\operatorname{revision}_P(A) = \{1, 2\}$ whereas $\operatorname{revision}_Q(A) = \{0\}$, hence

   $$
   \operatorname{revision}_Q\bigl(\operatorname{revision}_P(A)\bigr) \;=\; \{1\} \;\neq\; \{1, 2\} \;=\; \operatorname{revision}_P\bigl(\operatorname{revision}_Q(A)\bigr).
   $$

   In the quantum loop, pinching before unitary actuation likewise differs strictly from unitary actuation before pinching because coherence is erased at different stages of the cycle.
3. **Operational five-row ledger separating read, write, and common frame maps (`MODEL`)**:

## 34.21.2 Causal Intervention vs. Passive Frame Change: Exact Qubit Calculus and Equal-Arc Generative Action Selection {#sec-ch34-21-2}

To turn the five rows of our operational ledger into unmistakable algebra, we work out two closed-form single-qubit benchmarks side by side: first tracking every sign under the $y$-axis rotation $R_y(\alpha)$ across state actuation, probe steering, common frame conjugation, maximally mixed reset, and read–write ordering; then showing how an internal generative reference distribution breaks symmetry cleanly between two candidate control arcs of **identical Fubini–Study geodesic length**:

::: {.example #ex-ch34-intervention-versus-frame title="Exact Qubit Sign Split Between Intervention and Frame Change, Reset Blind-Spot Breaking, Read–Write Noncommutation, and Equal-Arc Cross-Entropy Selection"}
We evaluate single-qubit perception–action coupling in two explicit parts (`manual derivation` + `MODEL`):

1. **$2\times 2$ matrix intervention vs. frame conjugation, maximally mixed unitary blindness, and read–write noncommutation (`manual derivation`)**:
   Take the pure equatorial target state $\rho = \frac{1}{2}(\mathbf{1} + \sigma_x) = \lvert +\rangle\langle +\rvert = \frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, the North-Pole projector $P = \frac{1}{2}(\mathbf{1} + \sigma_z) = \lvert 0\rangle\langle 0\rvert = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ (baseline overlap $\operatorname{Tr}(\rho P) = \frac{1}{2}$), and the $y$-axis unitary rotation

   $$
   U(\alpha) \;=\; R_y(\alpha) \;=\; \exp\Bigl(-i\frac{\alpha}{2}\sigma_y\Bigr) \;=\; \begin{pmatrix} \cos(\alpha/2) & -\sin(\alpha/2) \\ \sin(\alpha/2) & \cos(\alpha/2) \end{pmatrix}.
   $$

   Direct matrix multiplication gives $U(\alpha)\lvert +\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\bigl(\cos\frac{\alpha}{2} - \sin\frac{\alpha}{2},\; \sin\frac{\alpha}{2} + \cos\frac{\alpha}{2}\bigr)^\top$ and $U(\alpha)\lvert 0\rangle = \bigl(\cos\frac{\alpha}{2},\; \sin\frac{\alpha}{2}\bigr)^\top$, i.e. $U(\alpha)\sigma_x U(\alpha)^\dagger = \cos\alpha\,\sigma_x - \sin\alpha\,\sigma_z$ and $U(\alpha)\sigma_z U(\alpha)^\dagger = \cos\alpha\,\sigma_z + \sin\alpha\,\sigma_x$. Four exact operational consequences follow immediately:
   - **Forward state preparation matches Heisenberg pullback in sign, while forward apparatus rotation flips the sign**: Actuating the state $\rho \mapsto U(\alpha)\rho U(\alpha)^\dagger$ against fixed $P$ and pulling the effect back via $P \mapsto U(\alpha)^\dagger P U(\alpha) = \frac{1}{2}(\mathbf{1} + \cos\alpha\,\sigma_z - \sin\alpha\,\sigma_x)$ against fixed $\rho$ yield the exact same readout (instantiating [`observer_pullback_trace_identity`](lean:D5/S3/Quantum/Dynamics/ObserverPullbackTraceIdentity.observer_pullback_trace_identity)):

     $$
     \operatorname{Tr}\bigl(U(\alpha)\rho U(\alpha)^\dagger P\bigr) \;=\; \operatorname{Tr}\bigl(\rho\,U(\alpha)^\dagger P U(\alpha)\bigr) \;=\; \frac{1}{2}\Bigl(\cos\frac{\alpha}{2} - \sin\frac{\alpha}{2}\Bigr)^2 \;=\; \frac{1 - \sin\alpha}{2};
     $$

     conversely, holding $\rho$ fixed while physically rotating the apparatus forward to $U(\alpha) P U(\alpha)^\dagger$ (or dually preparing $U(\alpha)^\dagger \rho U(\alpha)$) yields the mirror-sign probability $\operatorname{Tr}(\rho\,U(\alpha) P U(\alpha)^\dagger) = \operatorname{Tr}(U(\alpha)^\dagger \rho U(\alpha) P) = \frac{1 + \sin\alpha}{2}$ (at $\alpha = \frac{\pi}{2}$, the former drops to $0$ while the latter saturates at $1$).
   - **Common frame conjugation cancels identically**: Co-rotating both $\rho$ and $P$ without a held-fixed external anchor cancels via $U(\alpha)^\dagger U(\alpha) = \mathbf{1}$:

     $$
     \operatorname{Tr}\Bigl(\bigl(U(\alpha)\rho U(\alpha)^\dagger\bigr)\bigl(U(\alpha) P U(\alpha)^\dagger\bigr)\Bigr) \;=\; \operatorname{Tr}(\rho P) \;=\; \frac{1}{2}, \qquad \forall \alpha \in \mathbb{R},
     $$

     leaving every observable probability strictly invariant!
   - **Unitary invariance of $\rho_{\mathrm{mix}} = \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{1}$ vs. dissipative reset `Write`**: At the maximally mixed Bloch center $\rho_{\mathrm{mix}} = \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{1}$, every unitary leaves the state unchanged ($U(\alpha)\frac{\mathbf{1}}{2}U(\alpha)^\dagger = \frac{\mathbf{1}}{2}$) and every apparatus rotation reads $\operatorname{Tr}(\frac{\mathbf{1}}{2} U(\alpha) P U(\alpha)^\dagger) = \frac{1}{2}$; only a non-unitary **state-preparation reset `Write` channel** $\mathcal{R}_0(\sigma) := \operatorname{Tr}(\sigma)\lvert 0\rangle\langle 0\rvert$ (exporting at least $k_{\mathrm{B}}T\ln 2$ of Landauer heat to a bath; see [§34.18](ch34-s18-gram-window-and-consensus.md#sec-ch34-gram-window-and-consensus)) breaks isotropic blindness and drives the readout to $\operatorname{Tr}(\mathcal{R}_0(\rho_{\mathrm{mix}}) P) = 1 \neq \frac{1}{2}$.
   - **Order noncommutation between `Read` and `Write`**: Let $\mathcal{P}_Z(\sigma) = P\sigma P + (\mathbf{1} - P)\sigma(\mathbf{1} - P)$ denote unread $Z$-pinching and $\mathcal{U}_\alpha(\sigma) = U(\alpha)\sigma U(\alpha)^\dagger$ unitary actuation. Executing `Read` before `Write` yields $\mathcal{P}_Z(\rho) = \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{1}$, erasing $\sigma_x$ coherence first so subsequent unitary control idles at $\operatorname{Tr}\bigl(\mathcal{U}_\alpha(\mathcal{P}_Z(\rho)) P\bigr) = \frac{1}{2}$; executing `Write` before `Read` rotates $\sigma_x$ coherence into the $\sigma_z$ axis prior to pinching, giving $\operatorname{Tr}\bigl(\mathcal{P}_Z(\mathcal{U}_\alpha(\rho)) P\bigr) = \frac{1 - \sin\alpha}{2}$ (e.g., $1 \neq \frac{1}{2}$ at $\alpha = -\frac{\pi}{2}$, echoing discrete belief revision [`revision_conflict_noncommutation`](lean:D5/S3/ConceptDynamics/Revision/RevisionConflictNoncommutation.revision_conflict_noncommutation)).

2. **Two-action generative-model comparison and step-by-step derivation of the $\frac{1}{4}\ln 3$ cross-entropy gap (`MODEL` + `manual derivation`)**:
   Hold the external target fixed at the [§34.20](ch34-s20-single-outcome-and-attractor-basins.md#sec-ch34-single-outcome-attractor-basins) $\theta = 30^\circ$ diagonal benchmark state $\rho_{\mathrm{ext}} = \operatorname{diag}(\frac{3}{4}, \frac{1}{4}) = \frac{1}{2}(\mathbf{1} + \frac{1}{2}\sigma_z)$ (longitudinal polarization $r_z = \frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{2}$), while the observer's internal generative model encodes a North-Pole homeostatic preference $p_{\mathrm{ref}} = (p_{\mathrm{ref}}(0),\, p_{\mathrm{ref}}(1)) = (\frac{3}{4}, \frac{1}{4})$. Starting from an equatorial probe orientation $\beta_0 = \frac{\pi}{2}$ ($\mathbf{I}_0 = \mathbf{e}_x$), the observer chooses between two symmetric $xz$-meridian steering actions $u_+ : \beta_0 \mapsto \beta_+ = \frac{\pi}{3}$ and $u_- : \beta_0 \mapsto \beta_- = \frac{2\pi}{3}$, where $\beta \in [0, \pi]$ denotes the Bloch polar angle of $P(\beta) = \frac{1}{2}(\mathbf{1} + \sin\beta\,\sigma_x + \cos\beta\,\sigma_z)$:
   - **Identical Fubini–Study control arc lengths**: Because the Fubini–Study geodesic distance between pure-state projectors equals half the Bloch great-circle angle, both candidate actions incur the exact same geometric control cost:

     $$
     d_{\mathrm{FS}}(\beta_0, \beta_+) \;=\; \frac{1}{2}\left\lvert\frac{\pi}{3} - \frac{\pi}{2}\right\rvert \;=\; \frac{\pi}{12} \;=\; \frac{1}{2}\left\lvert\frac{2\pi}{3} - \frac{\pi}{2}\right\rvert \;=\; d_{\mathrm{FS}}(\beta_0, \beta_-).
     $$

   - **Predicted outcome laws and the exact $\frac{1}{4}\ln 3$ cross-entropy gap**: Under probe angle $\beta$, measuring $\rho_{\mathrm{ext}}$ yields $q_\beta(0) = \operatorname{Tr}(\rho_{\mathrm{ext}} P(\beta)) = \frac{1 + r_z\cos\beta}{2}$ and $q_\beta(1) = 1 - q_\beta(0)$. Substituting $\cos\frac{\pi}{3} = +\frac{1}{2}$ and $\cos\frac{2\pi}{3} = -\frac{1}{2}$ gives

     $$
     q_{\pi/3} \;=\; \Bigl(\frac{1 + \frac{1}{4}}{2},\, \frac{1 - \frac{1}{4}}{2}\Bigr) \;=\; \Bigl(\frac{5}{8},\, \frac{3}{8}\Bigr), \qquad q_{2\pi/3} \;=\; \Bigl(\frac{1 - \frac{1}{4}}{2},\, \frac{1 + \frac{1}{4}}{2}\Bigr) \;=\; \Bigl(\frac{3}{8},\, \frac{5}{8}\Bigr).
     $$

     Evaluating the discrete cross-entropy $H_\times(q_\beta, p_{\mathrm{ref}}) := -\sum_{o \in \{0, 1\}} q_\beta(o)\ln p_{\mathrm{ref}}(o)$ against $p_{\mathrm{ref}} = (\frac{3}{4}, \frac{1}{4})$ step by step:

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     H_\times\bigl(q_{\pi/3}, p_{\mathrm{ref}}\bigr) &\;=\; -\frac{5}{8}\ln\frac{3}{4} - \frac{3}{8}\ln\frac{1}{4} \;=\; \ln 4 - \frac{5}{8}\ln 3, \\
     H_\times\bigl(q_{2\pi/3}, p_{\mathrm{ref}}\bigr) &\;=\; -\frac{3}{8}\ln\frac{3}{4} - \frac{5}{8}\ln\frac{1}{4} \;=\; \ln 4 - \frac{3}{8}\ln 3, \\
     \Delta H_\times \;:=\; H_\times\bigl(q_{2\pi/3}, p_{\mathrm{ref}}\bigr) - H_\times\bigl(q_{\pi/3}, p_{\mathrm{ref}}\bigr) &\;=\; \Bigl(\frac{5}{8} - \frac{3}{8}\Bigr)\ln 3 \;=\; \frac{1}{4}\ln 3 \;>\; 0.
     \end{aligned}
     $$

     Moreover, because $q_{\pi/3} = (\frac{5}{8}, \frac{3}{8})$ and $q_{2\pi/3} = (\frac{3}{8}, \frac{5}{8})$ are binary permutations of each other, their Shannon outcome entropies match identically, $H(q_{\pi/3}) = H(q_{2\pi/3})$, so the Kullback–Leibler divergence gap $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(q_{2\pi/3}\parallel p_{\mathrm{ref}}) - D_{\mathrm{KL}}(q_{\pi/3}\parallel p_{\mathrm{ref}})$ is likewise $\frac{1}{4}\ln 3$—breaking the $\frac{\pi}{12}$ geodesic tie unambiguously in favor of the northward steering action $u_+$ ($\beta_+ = \frac{\pi}{3}$)!

## 34.21.3 Two-Axis Finite Word Synthesis, Record-Time Lower Bounds, and Dual Topological Obstructions in the Gram Corridor {#sec-ch34-21-3}

Once a preferred target orientation is selected, can an embodied observer always steer its axis $\mathbf{I}_t$ smoothly along spherical circular arcs to reach it? 1. **Unconstrained two-axis finite word generation and moving-axis invariance on $\mathrm{SO}(3)$ (`LEAN ✓`)**:
   On three-dimensional Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^3$, [`two_axis_finite_word`](lean:D5/S3/Geometry/TwoAxisFiniteRotation.two_axis_finite_word) proves that whenever two control axes $\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b} \in \mathbb{R}^3$ are non-collinear (strict Cauchy–Schwarz inequality $\langle\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b}\rangle_{\mathbb{R}}^2 < \lVert\mathbf{a}\rVert^2 \lVert\mathbf{b}\rVert^2$), every proper rotation $R \in \mathrm{SO}(3)$ factors **exactly as a finite word** of circular rotations in $\operatorname{axisCircle}(\mathbf{a}) \cup \operatorname{axisCircle}(\mathbf{b})$—no infinite Lie–Trotter limit required; the companion theorem [`moving_axis_invariance`](lean:D5/S3/Geometry/TwoAxisFiniteRotation.moving_axis_invariance) proves that any binary geometric quantity preserved along local co-moving axes $\operatorname{axisCircle}(O\mathbf{a}) \cup \operatorname{axisCircle}(O\mathbf{b})$ across all $O \in \mathrm{SO}(3)$ is automatically invariant under global $\mathrm{SO}(3)$ rigid rotations.
2. **Fubini–Study record-time lower bound for orthogonal target states (`LEAN ✓`)**:
   Even along an unobstructed spherical arc, reorientation cannot occur instantaneously. In any complex inner-product space, [`record_time_lower_bound`](lean:D5/S3/QuantumBounds/FubiniStudyRecordTime.record_time_lower_bound) proves that if a unit reference state $a$ must stay within Fubini–Study reach of two mutually orthogonal record states $m_0 \perp m_1$ ($\langle m_0, m_1\rangle_{\mathbb{C}} = 0$, hence $d_{\mathrm{FS}}(m_0, m_1) = \frac{\pi}{2}$) under the explicit dual speed-bound hypotheses $d_{\mathrm{FS}}(a, m_0) \le \frac{E\tau}{\hbar}$ and $d_{\mathrm{FS}}(a, m_1) \le \frac{E\tau}{\hbar}$ ($E > 0,\; \hbar > 0$), then the spherical triangle inequality $\frac{\pi}{2} = d_{\mathrm{FS}}(m_0, m_1) \le d_{\mathrm{FS}}(m_0, a) + d_{\mathrm{FS}}(a, m_1) \le \frac{2E\tau}{\hbar}$ enforces the sharp time bound

   $$
   \tau \;\ge\; \frac{\pi\hbar}{4E}.
   $$

   (Note: both speed inequalities enter [`record_time_lower_bound`](lean:D5/S3/QuantumBounds/FubiniStudyRecordTime.record_time_lower_bound) as explicit premise hypotheses rather than being integrated from the time-dependent Schrödinger equation inside that module.)
3. **Two constrained reachability obstructions induced by retained Gram-window anchors (`manual derivation`)**:
   When steering must preserve prescribed overlaps with retained anchors in $\mathcal{W}_M$, positivity of the bordered Schur complement $\Delta_{\mathrm{Schur}} > 0$ alone does *not* guarantee continuous circular-arc reachability on $S^2 \subset \mathbb{R}^3$:
   - **Obstruction (a) — Retained-overlap disconnection on $S^2$ ($M = 2$)**: Retain two orthogonal equatorial anchors $\mathbf{v}_1 = \mathbf{e}_x,\; \mathbf{v}_2 = \mathbf{e}_y \in S^2 \subset \mathbb{R}^3$ ($G_2 = I_2$) and require zero overlap $\mathbf{c}_2 = (0, 0)^\top$.      $$
     \mathcal{F}_{S^2}(I_2, \mathbf{0}) \;=\; \bigl\{\mathbf{r} \in S^2 \;\big\vert\; \mathbf{r}\cdot\mathbf{e}_x = 0,\; \mathbf{r}\cdot\mathbf{e}_y = 0\bigr\} \;=\; \{+\mathbf{e}_z,\, -\mathbf{e}_z\},
     $$

     splits into two disconnected antipodal points! No continuous arc on $S^2$ can steer $+\mathbf{e}_z$ to $-\mathbf{e}_z$ without either relaxing exact equality into an inequality coherence-cap corridor $\Omega_2$ or sliding an old anchor out of memory.
   - **Obstruction (b) — Ambient-dimension realization obstruction ($M = 3$)**: Retain three orthonormal anchors $\mathbf{v}_1 = \mathbf{e}_1,\; \mathbf{v}_2 = \mathbf{e}_2,\; \mathbf{v}_3 = \mathbf{e}_3 \in S^2 \subset \mathbb{R}^3$ ($G_3 = I_3$) and demand orthogonal novelty $\mathbf{c}_3 = (0, 0, 0)^\top$.    - [`anchored_phase_classification`](lean:D5/S3/Observer/AgencyHolonomy/AnchoredPhaseClassification.anchored_phase_classification) (`LEAN ✓`) introduces a set of **held-fixed external anchors** $R \subseteq V$ containing the basepoint $r \in R$ ($\lvert R\rvert = k = a + 1$), restricts internal rephasings to $\operatorname{anchoredGroup}(R) = \{g : V \to \mathbb{T} \mid \forall v \in R,\; g(v) = 1\}$, and proves that the coordinate map $\chi : (E \to \mathbb{T}) \to \operatorname{Coordinates}(T, R, r)$ induces a topological group isomorphism

     $$
     (E \to \mathbb{T}) / N \;\simeq_{\mathrm{top},\,*} \; \operatorname{Coordinates}(T, R, r) \;\simeq_{\mathrm{top},\,*} \; \mathbb{T}^{m + k - n}, \qquad c + a \;=\; (m + 1 - n) + (k - 1) \;=\; m + k - n.
     $$

     This dimension formula fixes the exact coordinate count of the whole interaction graph's anchored $\mathrm{U}(1)$ gauge quotient (`LEAN ✓` `anchored_phase_classification`): the invariant quotient torus splits into $c = m - \lvert T\rvert = m + 1 - n$ fundamental cycle coordinates indexed by non-tree edges $e \notin T$ plus $a = k - 1$ open root-to-anchor coordinates connecting $r$ to $v \in R \setminus \{r\}$; interpreting a cycle coordinate as a **physical Berry phase** (under a geometric transport protocol) and an open coordinate as a **recorded intervention phase** (under an anchored endpoint-comparison protocol) is a physical `MODEL` assignment! 3. **Strict separation among three quantities—never conflating variational free energy, thermodynamic free energy, and formal `freezeDeposit`**:
   To prevent cross-disciplinary terminological drift, we keep three distinct mathematical objects strictly separate:
   - **Variational free energy** $F_{\mathrm{var}}(q, o; u) := D_{\mathrm{KL}}\bigl(q(s)\,\big\Vert\,p(s \mid o, u)\bigr) - \ln p(o \mid u)$ (`Literature result: Friston 2010` / `MODEL`): a **dimensionless information-theoretic functional** (measured in nats or bits) over internal beliefs $q(s)$, discrete outcome probabilities $p(o \mid u)$ (or a dimensionless likelihood ratio against a reference measure for continuous $o$), and controls $u$—exemplified by the $\frac{1}{4}\ln 3$ cross-entropy gap in [@ex-ch34-intervention-versus-frame]—carrying no Joule energy units by itself;
   - **Physical thermodynamic free energy** $F = U - T S_{\mathrm{phys}} = U - \beta^{-1} s$ (distinguishing dimensionless von Neumann entropy $s = -\operatorname{Tr}(\rho\ln\rho)$ from physical thermodynamic entropy $S_{\mathrm{phys}} = k_B s$ at inverse temperature $\beta = (k_B T)^{-1} > 0$, alongside Landauer heat dissipation $\beta Q \ge -\Delta s$ in [§34.17](ch34-s17-entanglement-horizon-and-thermodynamics.md#sec-ch34-entanglement-horizon-and-thermodynamics)–[§34.18](ch34-s18-gram-window-and-consensus.md#sec-ch34-gram-window-and-consensus)): an **energy-valued physical state function** coupled to a thermal bath, governing the minimal heat cost of non-unitary reset `Write` operations and anchor erasure;
   - **Formal decoherence `freezeDeposit`**: the real energy-valued algebraic scalar $\operatorname{freezeDeposit}(\beta, \Delta s, \Delta E_{\mathrm{pass}}) := \Delta E_{\mathrm{pass}} - \Delta s / \beta$ (with dimensionless $\Delta s$) defined in `D5/S3/QuantumChannels/DecoherenceFreeze`: for passive energy offset $\Delta E_{\mathrm{pass}} > 0$ and $\beta > 0$, the deposit is strictly positive when the inverse temperature $\beta$ exceeds $\beta_c := \Delta s / \Delta E_{\mathrm{pass}}$ ($\operatorname{freezeDeposit} > 0 \iff \beta > \beta_c$), and is *not* a Bayesian generative variational bound $F_{\mathrm{var}}(q, o; u)$.
