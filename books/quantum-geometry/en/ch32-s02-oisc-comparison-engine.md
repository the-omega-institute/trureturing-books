# 32.2 The Minimal Cosmic Comparison Machine: Single-Instruction-Set (OISC) Geometric Primitive {#sec-ch32-oisc-comparison-engine}

> "Perhaps the universe is not complex at all, just a computational system ceaselessly performing comparisons."

Having formulated the Zero-Background Axiom of Comparison $\mathcal{A}_0$ ([@def-ch32-axiom-of-comparison]) in [Section 32.1](ch32-s01-axiom-of-comparison.md#sec-ch32-axiom-of-comparison), let us immediately watch it execute at hardware level. Conventional physics appears labyrinthine because it splits reality in two: first positing a mute spacetime stage, then populating it with zoo upon zoo of particles, fields, and differential equations. Computer architecture, however, established a classic lesson in radical parsimony decades ago (Mavaddat & Parhami 1988, literature result; see [Section 32.3](ch32-s03-axioms-as-corollaries.md#sec-ch32-6-4) [@rem-ch32-comparison-axiom-literature]): a Turing-complete universal computer needs no sprawling instruction repertoire—a single atomic instruction such as `SUBLEQ` (*subtract and branch if less than or equal to zero*, i.e., **compare difference and turn**) suffices to synthesize every computable process. Such a machine is a **One-Instruction Set Computer (OISC)**.

Within our Layer-1 positive-definite Clifford realization (`MODEL`), cosmic hardware goes further than silicon: excising external clocks, global address buses, and the privileged "third point $C$," it runs one zero-background primitive—**pairwise orthogonal comparison $\mathrm{CMP}(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{B})$**. Whenever two unit directional states $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ meet, the Clifford geometric product splits their relation losslessly into Clause II's two orthogonal parity channels:

$$
\mathrm{CMP}(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{B}) \;\equiv\; \mathbf{A}\mathbf{B} \;=\; \underbrace{\mathbf{A}\cdot\mathbf{B}}_{\text{Scalar sameness (== / XNOR)}} \;+\; \underbrace{\mathbf{A}\wedge\mathbf{B}}_{\text{Bivector difference (!= / XOR)}} \;=\; \cos\theta \;+\; \sin\theta\,\hat{\mathbf{I}}.
$$

1. **Cyan Scalar "Sameness" Channel ($\mathbf{A}\cdot\mathbf{B} = \cos\theta$, continuous analogue of `XNOR` / equality `==`)**: Measures how far $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ align and agree. Projected onto classical ledgers, it registers as particle clicks, proper-time persistence, cache hits, and stable object identity. At $\mathbf{A} = \mathbf{B}$ ($\theta = 0$), $\mathrm{CMP}(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{A}) = 1 + 0$: positive self-normalization locks an idempotent 1D line.
2. **Magenta Bivector "Difference" Channel ($\mathbf{A}\wedge\mathbf{B} = \sin\theta\,\hat{\mathbf{I}}$, continuous analogue of `XOR` / inequality `!=`)**: Measures how far $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ diverge orthogonally, spanning the simple oriented 2D rotation plane $\hat{\mathbf{I}}$ ($\hat{\mathbf{I}}^2 = -1$) that turns one toward the other. Physically, it manifests as $90^\circ$ gate action, gauge phase tension, wave interference, and entanglement area. At $\mathbf{A} \perp \mathbf{B}$ ($\theta = \frac{\pi}{2}$), $\mathrm{CMP}(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{B}) = 0 + \hat{\mathbf{I}}$: pure difference becomes a quarter-turn generator.

Once we view the cosmos as ceaselessly executing $\mathrm{CMP}(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{B})$, six grand phenomena across physics, computation, and life unfold as facets of one comparison engine (this cross-domain synthesis is our `MODEL`, with mathematical-physics theorems and Lean anchors explicitly stratified):

- **(i) Quantum Probability and Heisenberg Uncertainty from Bidirectional Reciprocal Fairness**. With no external referee $C$ to privilege "comparing $\mathbf{A}$ against $\mathbf{B}$" over "comparing $\mathbf{B}$ against $\mathbf{A}$," an objective scalar ledger must combine both directions symmetrically. Because the difference channel reverses orientation ($\mathbf{B}\wedge\mathbf{A} = -\mathbf{A}\wedge\mathbf{B}$), reciprocal pairing multiplies a comparison by its reverse as in Clause III of $\mathcal{A}_0$:

  $$
  \mathrm{CMP}(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{B})\,\mathrm{CMP}(\mathbf{B}, \mathbf{A}) \;=\; (\cos\theta + \sin\theta\,\hat{\mathbf{I}})(\cos\theta - \sin\theta\,\hat{\mathbf{I}}) \;=\; \cos^2\theta + \sin^2\theta \;=\; 1.
  $$

  Cyan sameness weight $\cos^2\theta$ (Born probability skeleton) and magenta difference variance $\sin^2\theta$ split a conserved unit budget; folding away the sign and orientation of $\mathbf{A}\wedge\mathbf{B}$ breeds intrinsic quantum uncertainty without mechanical disturbance (anchored in finite dimensions by Conjunct 3 of [@thm-ch32-comparison-extensionality-and-lagrange], `lagrange_gram_identity`, `LEAN ✓`).

- **(ii) Three-Dimensional Space and Metric Distance from Positive-Semidefinite Similarity Embedding**. The universe does not start inside a pre-built $(x, y, z)$ container; it starts with a symmetric table of pairwise sameness overlaps $G_{ij} = \mathbf{A}_i\cdot\mathbf{A}_j$. By Schoenberg's positive-semidefinite embedding theorem (Schoenberg 1935, literature result; see [@rem-ch32-comparison-axiom-literature]), any Layer-1 similarity matrix satisfying $G \succeq 0$ embeds isometrically into Euclidean space with chordal metric $d_{ij} = \sqrt{2(1 - G_{ij})}$—"nearby" simply means high agreement across comparisons, while "far apart" means orthogonal divergence. One exact arithmetic fact deserves emphasis: when $8$ unit binary words in $\{\pm 1\}^8/\sqrt{8}$ are projected onto a rank-$3$ subspace by grouping the $8$ coordinate slots into three axes, because $3 \nmid 8$, equal three-way slot counts are impossible so slot-grouped binary words cannot form an equilateral cube; the **balanced `3-3-2` slot partition** chosen in the stage below (`BOX = [0,1,2,0,1,2,0,1]`) spans an **8-corner 3D rectangular box** (`三维八角盒`) with squared semi-axes $(\frac{3}{8}, \frac{3}{8}, \frac{2}{8})$.

To banish any romantic illusion that comparison or table lookup acts as a zero-cost magic oracle, `trureturing`'s computability library constructs explicit multi-stack finite-control machines (`FinTM2`) proving exact step-count bounds and lossless tape restoration for both **bitwise binary name comparison** and **streamed dictionary lookup**:

::: {.theorem #thm-ch32-paid-name-comparison-and-dictionary title="Exact Paid Step Conservation of Bitwise Name Comparison and Dictionary Scan" lean="D5/S0/Computability/BinaryNameComparison.name_compare_run D5/S0/Computability/BinaryNameDictionary.dictionary_lookup_run"}
Across the frozen binary-name-comparison and dictionary-lookup computability modules of `trureturing`, the following two paid step-bound and tape-restoration theorems hold simultaneously:

1. **Exact Step Bound and Lossless Restoration for Bitwise Name Comparison** ([`name_compare_run`](lean:D5/S0/Computability/BinaryNameComparison.name_compare_run)): For any two binary words $a, b \in \operatorname{List}\;\mathrm{Bool}$ (not necessarily of equal length), prior output stack $\mathrm{output}$, and context stack $\mathrm{frame}$, the six-stack machine `compareMachine` starting from $(\operatorname{some}\;\mathrm{compare},\, \langle\operatorname{none},\operatorname{none},\mathrm{true}\rangle,\, a,\, b,\, [],\, [],\, \mathrm{output},\, \mathrm{frame})$ halts within at most

   $$
   T_{\mathrm{cmp}}(a, b) \;\le\; 2\bigl(\lvert a\rvert + \lvert b\rvert\bigr) + 4
   $$

   steps in state $\operatorname{none}$, pushing the equality decision bit $\operatorname{decide}(a = b) :: \mathrm{output}$, emptying both scratch stacks, and restoring $a$, $b$, and $\mathrm{frame}$ intact;

2. **Exact Step Bound and Unary Index Readout for Streamed Dictionary Lookup** ([`dictionary_lookup_run`](lean:D5/S0/Computability/BinaryNameDictionary.dictionary_lookup_run)): For any list of binary names $\mathrm{names} \in \operatorname{List}(\operatorname{List}\;\mathrm{Bool})$, target word $\mathrm{query} \in \operatorname{List}\;\mathrm{Bool}$, and context stack $\mathrm{frame}$, writing $\operatorname{dictionaryStream}(\mathrm{names})$ for the delimiter-encoded dictionary stream, the machine `dictionaryMachine` halts from its initial lookup configuration within at most

   $$
   T_{\mathrm{dict}}(\mathrm{names}, \mathrm{query}) \;\le\; \bigl(2\lvert\mathrm{query}\rvert + 12\bigr)\lvert\mathrm{names}\rvert \;+\; 4\bigl\lvert\operatorname{dictionaryStream}(\mathrm{names})\bigr\rvert \;+\; 3
   $$

   steps, writing the hit bit and unary index $\operatorname{decide}(\mathrm{query} \in \mathrm{names}) :: \operatorname{replicate}(\mathrm{names.idxOf}(\mathrm{query}),\, \mathrm{true})$ onto the output stack while restoring $\mathrm{query}$, $\operatorname{dictionaryStream}(\mathrm{names})$, and $\mathrm{frame}$ without loss.
:::

::: proof
Conjunct 1 factors `compareMachine` into a forward bit-popping comparison pass and a backward stack-restoration pass: in `compare`, head bits of $a$ and $b$ are popped onto `savedLeft` and `savedRight` while updating the boolean match flag (if either word exhausts early, the match flag flips to `false` and the machine drains the remaining word), after which `restore` pours both scratch stacks back onto the left and right tapes in at most $2(\lvert a\rvert + \lvert b\rvert) + 4$ total transitions. Conjunct 2 inducts over `names` along `dictionaryStream`: each entry invokes the reversible bitwise comparator and appends a unary `true` tally mark prior to a match, then rewinds the full stream and query tape within $(2\lvert\mathrm{query}\rvert + 12)\lvert\mathrm{names}\rvert + 4\lvert\operatorname{dictionaryStream}(\mathrm{names})\rvert + 3$ steps. The full proof is checked by the Lean kernel.
:::
