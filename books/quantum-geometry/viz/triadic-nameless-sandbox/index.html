This is a numerical sandbox for geometric and philosophical models, not a proof.

The vertices obey Vₘ·Vₙ = c₀; the unit bisector Sᵢⱼ and common normal Aᵢⱼ are orthogonal, and Vₖ = α Sᵢⱼ + β Aᵢⱼ with α²+β² = 1. The three generations have 1, 4 and 10 cells: from each frontier midpoint a spherical tangent step δℓ = 0.42 φ⁻⁽ℓ⁻¹⁾ points away from the parent, with φ = (1+√5)/2. The cross-edge tangent normal is N = ±unit(Pₐ × Pᵦ), with its outward sign chosen from the parent centroid. The double cross product (Pₐ × Pᵦ) × M runs along the edge great circle and would give a degenerate child. This is a specified recursive model, without a claim of a nonoverlapping tessellation.

The weight ν²(3−2ν) continuously blends the sharp cell with the nameless surface R = 0.78[1+0.18 sin³θ cos(3(φ−π/6)−ω₁τ)+0.09 sin(2θ) cos(ω₂τ)]. The two frequencies have the golden ratio. At ν < 0.15 every stage label, screen, projection ray and vertex bead withdraws, leaving a hidden origin and an uncalibrated floating ring. “Unbounded” means continuing without a closed period; the radius remains bounded.
