理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

这一页把序曲的四重盲区画成可以亲手推的图：影子少一维，同一个位置可以藏着不同的运动。这些都是几何类比与数值演示，不是证明，也不是量子物理的推导。

  1. 以线为点。一根长度为 1 的射线固定不动，眼睛绕着它转。视网膜按平行投影画：视线与射线的夹角为 α 时，影子长 sin α。α = 0 时只剩一个点，射线本身并没有变短。
  2. 以面为线。上面是俯视的转盘，木桩在 (cos θ, sin θ)，逆时针匀速走；下面是贴着桌面的侧视。侧视里整个圆盘是一条线段，这条线段本身不动；在线上来回的是木桩的影子 x = cos θ。木桩的速度沿圆的切向，长度不变；它的影子只剩左右分量 −sin θ（以木桩速度为单位），在两端为 0。
  3. 同一个影子，相反的下一步。θ = 90° 与 θ = 270° 的两个木桩投到同一点 x = 0，影子的速度一个是 −1、一个是 +1（以木桩速度为单位）。只看线段上的位置，分不出这两种情形；把俯视的那一维补回来，差别就在那里。这只是丢了一维的几何事实，页面不声称任何经典隐变量模型能解释量子力学。
  4. 以球为面。固定极角 θ、只扫相位 φ，点走在纬圈上：纬圈所在的平面是 z = cos θ。cos θ 是带符号的高度，θ > 90° 时为负；平面到球心的距离是 |cos θ|。纬圈半径 sin θ，周长 2π sin θ。只有 θ = 90° 时 cos θ = 0，平面经过球心，纬圈才是赤道大圆。
  5. 球外之球。旧的单位圆留在原位，成为新球的赤道；新增的是与旧平面垂直的方向 ±e₃。点从旧圆抬向极点，离球心始终是 1：增加的是一个方向，不是把半径放大。图中画的是 R² ↪ R³ 的类比；在 R³ 里 ⋆₃I₃ = 1 是标量，要第四个方向必须先给出 R⁴ ⊃ R³。
  6. 放弃方向的平均。六个方向 ±x、±y、±z 两两对跖。权重从全在 +z 线性摊成每个 1/6，加权平均 m 的长度从 1 落到 0。这是等权混合，不是一次测量。
  7. 余弦蛛网。从 θ₀ = 1 出发，竖着走到曲线 y = cos θ，横着走到对角线 y = θ，读出的刻度成为下一个角。两条轴都是弧度数值。页面只画有限步；收敛、不动点的唯一性与超越性是文献结果，见序曲的注记，页面不证明它们，也不从浮点读数推断精确残差。

This page turns the four blind spots of the prelude into pictures you can push: a shadow loses a dimension, and the same position can hide different motions. All of it is geometric analogy and numerical illustration, not a proof and not a derivation of quantum physics.

  1. A line taken for a point. A ray of length 1 stays put while the eye goes round it. The retina is drawn as a parallel projection: when the line of sight makes the angle α with the ray, the image is sin α long. At α = 0 a single point is left, and the ray itself has not become shorter.
  2. A face taken for a line. Above, the turntable seen from the top, with the post at (cos θ, sin θ) going anticlockwise at a steady pace; below, the view along the table top. Edge-on, the whole disc is one segment, and the segment itself does not move; what swings along it is the shadow of the post, x = cos θ. The velocity of the post is tangent to the circle and keeps its length; its shadow keeps only the left–right part −sin θ (in units of the post speed), which is 0 at the two ends.
  3. Same shadow, opposite next step. The posts at θ = 90° and θ = 270° fall on the same point x = 0, and their shadows move at −1 and +1 (in units of the post speed). The position on the segment alone cannot tell the two apart; put back the dimension seen from the top and the difference is there. This is only the geometry of a lost dimension; the page claims no classical hidden-variable model of quantum mechanics.
  4. A sphere taken for a face. Fix the polar angle θ and sweep the phase φ alone: the point runs along a circle of latitude in the plane z = cos θ. Here cos θ is a signed height, negative once θ > 90°; the distance from the plane to the centre is |cos θ|. The circle has radius sin θ and length 2π sin θ. Only at θ = 90° is cos θ = 0: the plane passes through the centre and the latitude becomes the equator, a great circle.
  5. A sphere beyond the sphere. The old unit circle stays where it is and becomes the equator of a new sphere; what is added is the direction ±e₃, perpendicular to the old plane. The point rises from the old circle to a pole and stays at distance 1 from the centre: one more direction, not a bigger radius. The picture is the analogy R² ↪ R³; inside R³, ⋆₃I₃ = 1 is a scalar, and a fourth direction needs R⁴ ⊃ R³ to be given first.
  6. An average that gives up direction. The six directions ±x, ±y, ±z come in opposite pairs. As the weight spreads linearly from all on +z to 1/6 each, the length of the weighted mean m falls from 1 to 0. This is an equal-weight mixture, not a single measurement.
  7. The cosine cobweb. Start at θ₀ = 1, go up to the curve y = cos θ, across to the diagonal y = θ, and the reading becomes the next angle. Both axes are numbers in radians. The page draws finitely many steps; convergence, uniqueness and transcendence of the fixed point are literature results quoted in the remark of the prelude, which the page neither proves nor infers an exact residual from a floating-point reading.
