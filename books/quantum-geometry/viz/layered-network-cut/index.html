ARGUED跨过一道割的秩，不超过被割断各腿维数的乘积：每条键维数都是 χ 时，割断 k 条，秩至多 χᵏ。这是线性代数的直接论证，本书只在宽度三桥图上把它形式化；一般网络里，最大秩可以严格小于这个上界。The rank across a cut is at most the product of the dimensions of the legs it severs: with every bond of dimension χ, a cut through k legs allows rank at most χᵏ. This is a direct argument of linear algebra that the book formalizes only on the width-three bridge graph; in a general network the greatest rank can be strictly smaller than this bound.

理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

第 19 章说，研究者常借助 MERA 这样的层级张量网络，示意边界区域与网络深处截面之间的瓶颈关系。本页把这张网络画出来，让读者在边界上选一段，看把它切下来最少要割断几条腿。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 网络。底边 32 格，每格一条腿。往上一层先用方块（解纠缠器）把相邻两格搭在一起，再用三角（等距器）把两条腿并成一条；五层之后只剩顶上一个三角。每条键的维数都取同一个 χ。
  2. 割。在底边选一段 A。一道割是一组键：把它们剪断以后，A 的腿与其余底边的腿不再相连。页面用增广路求出最少的条数 k（图上的最大流等于最小割），品红的键是其中一组，青色是割内侧的张量。直接剪断 A 的每条腿总是一道割，所以 k ≤ L。
  3. 秩的上界。把整张网络看成从 A 的腿到其余腿的一个线性映射，它从割断的 k 条键上经过，所以秩至多 χᵏ。这只是上界：第 19 章强调，一般网络里最大秩可以小于最小割；只有宽度三桥图上，Lean 证明了秩的最大值不超过最小割，并在锥域内恰好相等。
  4. 对数增长。区间翻倍时，割往网络里多走一层，两侧大约各多割一条键：页面上 L 从 1 到 16，k 只从 1 长到 8 左右。这张网络的 527 段区间都满足 k ≤ 2·log₂L + 1（页面逐个算过）。在 MERA 类网络里把 k 读作 RT 极小曲面的“面积”，是 Swingle 等人的文献类比，未在本书形式化。
  5. 两段。A、B 两段时，页面直接求 A∪B 的最小割，再与 A、B 各自的割之和比较。最小割常见两种形状：两道各自的割；或一道包住 A、B 与中间空隙的大割，加上把空隙单独切开的一道小割。在 TWO 卡里，A 长到 7 格起，后一种更省。

Chapter 19 says that hierarchical tensor networks such as MERA are used to picture the bottleneck between a boundary region and a cross-section deep inside the network. This page draws such a network and lets the reader pick a stretch of the boundary, to see how few legs must be cut to separate it. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The network. Thirty-two boundary sites, one leg each. Each layer first joins neighbouring legs with a square (a disentangler), then merges two legs into one with a triangle (an isometry); after five layers a single triangle is left at the top. Every bond has the same dimension χ.
  2. The cut. Pick a stretch A of the boundary. A cut is a set of bonds whose removal disconnects the legs of A from the rest of the boundary. The page finds the least number k by augmenting paths (on a graph the maximal flow equals the minimal cut); the magenta bonds are one such set and the cyan tensors lie on the inner side. Cutting every leg of A is always a cut, so k ≤ L.
  3. The rank bound. Read the whole network as a linear map from the legs of A to the other legs: it passes through the k severed bonds, so its rank is at most χᵏ. This is only an upper bound: chapter 19 stresses that in a general network the greatest rank can be smaller than the minimal cut; only on the width-three bridge graph has Lean proved that the greatest rank does not exceed the minimal cut, with equality inside the cone.
  4. Logarithmic growth. When the interval doubles, the cut climbs one more layer into the network and cuts about one more bond on each side: as L runs from 1 to 16 on the page, k grows only from 1 to about 8. Every one of the 527 intervals of this network has k ≤ 2·log₂L + 1 (checked one by one on the page). Reading k as the area of an RT minimal surface in MERA-like networks is an analogy from the literature (Swingle and others), not formalized in this book.
  5. Two intervals. With two intervals A and B, the page finds the minimal cut of A∪B directly and compares it with the sum of the cuts of A and B. The minimal cut usually takes one of two shapes: two separate cuts; or one large cut around A, B and the gap between them, together with a small cut that separates the gap on its own. On the TWO card the second shape is cheaper once A reaches 7 sites.
