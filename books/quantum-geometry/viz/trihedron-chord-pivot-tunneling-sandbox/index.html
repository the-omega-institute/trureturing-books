这是数值沙盘，不是证明。每个数都由页面当场算出（NUM）；每条 Lean 定理只在依据栏写明的那一个实例上演示。

  1. 原胞。单位球上取 O = 0、B = (0, 1, 0)、A = (sin ε, cos ε, 0)、C = (0, cos ε, sin ε)，ε ∈ [10°, 90°]。两面紫色侧壁 OAB（z = 0）与 OBC（x = 0）互相垂直；斜面 OAC 是平的；外凸顶盖 △ABC 在球面上。A·B = B·C = cos ε，A·C = cos²ε。
  2. 三条路。双壁 A → B → C 长 2ε；顶盖测地弧 AC 长 arccos(cos²ε)；内割弦 AC 长 ‖A − C‖ = √(2 − 2cos²ε) = √2 sin ε。弦短于弧（弦长 2 sin(c/2) < c），弧短于双壁（FS 角三角不等式，取 fsAngle = arccos|⟨·,·⟩|）；页面在 [10°, 90°] 上逐度核对这三者的先后从不翻转。
  3. 月牙与鼓包。弦中点 M = (A + C)/2 的半径 rmid = √((1 + cos²ε)/2) = √(1 − ½ sin²ε)，比同方向的弧中点 Q 低 Δr = 1 − rmid ≈ ε²/4（小 ε）。平面 OAC 里，弧与弦之间的品红月牙就是平直内割削掉的鼓包。拉格朗日恒等式把面积与弦锁在一起：|A ∧ C|² = 1 − cos⁴ε = ‖A − C‖² · rmid²。顶盖的角超 Ω = 2 arctan(tan²(ε/2))。
  4. 热带舒尔消元。六点 A、C（边界）与 B、Q、M、O（内点）之间的最小—加权重：侧壁弧 A–B、B–C 各 ε，顶盖半弧 A–Q、Q–C 各 arccos(cos²ε)/2，半弦 A–M、M–C 各 (√2 sin ε)/2，半径 A–O、O–C 各 1，M–O 为 rmid。消去内点得 Weff(A, C) = min(WAC, minb WAb ⊗ Wbb* ⊗ WbC)：页面用克莱尼星与逐点消元各算一次，两者相同，取到弦 √2 sin ε。打开芝诺探针时只留下壁上的 B，得 2ε。消元只在 A–C 上补边，消元图是弦图；同一张图上的高斯核部分矩阵用团—分隔集公式补全，最小特征值为正。
  5. 枢轴锥。顶盖按谢尔宾斯基方式对分 D 次，得 N = 2^D 个叶（边界点 Λ⁰）。每 k 个相邻的点凝成一颗枢轴，放在它所辖区域的面积加权中心方向、半径 cos(θ/2)，θ 是该区域的角直径：这正是端点相隔 θ 的弦的中点深度。逐层得 ⌈n/k⌉ 个枢轴，直到一颗；它所辖的是整个顶盖，θ = arccos(cos²ε)，于是 r = rmid，与 M 同深。白线是叶图上从 A 端的叶出发的 Dijkstra 落定顺序。比较次数指标 n log₂ n、m + n log₂ n 与 m log2/3 n 只是主项，不含常数（m 为叶图的边数）。
  6. 共振中继与芝诺探针。沿弦取矩形势垒，E = 16、V₀ = 32（ħ = 2m = 1），k₀ = κ = 4，阿格蒙作用量 SE = κL。厚势垒（长 L = √2 sin ε）的透射由转移矩阵算出，约为 e−2κL 量级；把它拆成两段 L/2、中间放一个阱（中继枢轴），阱宽在一个法布里—珀罗周期 (0, π/k₀] 里调到共振，对称双势垒在共振处透射为 1。芝诺探针取 n = N 次投影排序 PI ρ PI，相干转移被压成 1 − cos²ⁿ(π/2n) ≈ π²/4n。

  1. The cell. On the unit sphere take O = 0, B = (0, 1, 0), A = (sin ε, cos ε, 0), C = (0, cos ε, sin ε), ε ∈ [10°, 90°]. The two violet walls OAB (z = 0) and OBC (x = 0) are perpendicular; the oblique face OAC is flat; the convex cap △ABC lies on the sphere. A·B = B·C = cos ε and A·C = cos²ε.
  2. Three routes. The walls A → B → C have length 2ε; the geodesic arc AC of the cap has length arccos(cos²ε); the interior chord AC has length ‖A − C‖ = √(2 − 2cos²ε) = √2 sin ε. The chord is shorter than the arc (a chord 2 sin(c/2) < c), the arc shorter than the walls (the FS-angle triangle inequality with fsAngle = arccos|⟨·,·⟩|); the page checks degree by degree over [10°, 90°] that the order never flips.
  3. Crescent and bulge. The chord midpoint M = (A + C)/2 has radius rmid = √((1 + cos²ε)/2) = √(1 − ½ sin²ε), lower than the arc midpoint Q in the same direction by Δr = 1 − rmid ≈ ε²/4 (small ε). In the plane OAC the magenta crescent between arc and chord is the bulge the flat interior cut shaves off. Lagrange's identity locks the area to the chord: |A ∧ C|² = 1 − cos⁴ε = ‖A − C‖² · rmid². The cap has excess Ω = 2 arctan(tan²(ε/2)).
  4. Tropical Schur elimination. Min-plus weights between the six points A, C (boundary) and B, Q, M, O (interior): wall arcs A–B, B–C of ε each, half cap arcs A–Q, Q–C of arccos(cos²ε)/2, half chords A–M, M–C of (√2 sin ε)/2, radii A–O, O–C of 1, and M–O of rmid. Eliminating the interior gives Weff(A, C) = min(WAC, minb WAb ⊗ Wbb* ⊗ WbC): the page computes it once with the Kleene star and once one vertex at a time, gets the same value, and it is the chord √2 sin ε. With the Zeno probe on only the wall vertex B is left, giving 2ε. Elimination fills only the edge A–C, so the elimination graph is chordal; on the same graph a Gaussian-kernel partial matrix is completed by the clique–separator formula, with a positive least eigenvalue.
  5. Pivot cone. The cap is bisected D times in the Sierpiński way into N = 2^D leaves (boundary points Λ⁰). Every k neighbouring points condense into a pivot, placed in the area-weighted mean direction of the region it gathers at radius cos(θ/2), θ the angular diameter of that region: the depth of the midpoint of a chord whose ends are θ apart. Level after level there are ⌈n/k⌉ pivots, down to one; it gathers the whole cap, θ = arccos(cos²ε), so r = rmid, as deep as M. The white thread is the Dijkstra settle order on the leaf graph from the leaf at A. The comparison indices n log₂ n, m + n log₂ n and m log2/3 n are leading terms without constants (m is the number of edges of the leaf graph).
  6. Resonant relay and Zeno probe. Along the chord take a rectangular barrier, E = 16, V₀ = 32 (ħ = 2m = 1), k₀ = κ = 4, Agmon action SE = κL. The transmission of the thick barrier (length L = √2 sin ε) comes from transfer matrices and is of order e−2κL; split it into two halves L/2 with a well (the relay pivot) between them, the well width tuned to resonance within one Fabry–Pérot period (0, π/k₀], and the symmetric double barrier transmits 1 at resonance. The Zeno probe takes n = N projective sorts PI ρ PI, which press the coherent transfer down to 1 − cos²ⁿ(π/2n) ≈ π²/4n.
