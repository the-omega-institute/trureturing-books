理论接口 · 双螺旋结构对应与勾股守恒对偶Theory interface · twin-spiral correspondence and Pythagorean conservation

为什么一维余弦迭代 θₖ₊₁ = cos θₖ 逼近 Dottie 不动点 θ* ≈ 0.7391 的过程，与黄金倒数连分数 xₖ₊₁ = 1/(1 + xₖ) 逼近 φ⁻¹ ≈ 0.6180 的过程一样在平面上卷出内旋螺旋？本页并置蛛网回旋方格（分段 90° 导引弧）、光滑等角极坐标螺旋模型与单位圆守恒对偶。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 负导数、等臂转向与分段导引弧。在各自的不动点处，(1/(1 + x))′|_{φ⁻¹} = −φ⁻² ≈ −0.3820 < 0（文献结果，未在本书形式化），而余弦迭代从 [0, 1] \ {θ*} 出发严格越过 θ*、误差比趋于 (cos θ)′|_{θ*} = −sin θ* = −√(1 − (θ*)²) ≈ −0.6736 < 0（第 33 章例 ex-ch33-mutual-cosine-monotonic-lock 的既有结果应用）。蛛网平面上从 Aₖ(xₖ, xₖ) 竖直走到 Bₖ(xₖ, f(xₖ))、再水平走到 Aₖ₊₁(f(xₖ), f(xₖ))，竖臂与横臂长度严格相等 sₖ = |f(xₖ) − xₖ|（单步两臂绕不动点转 180°）：当 x₀ < x* 时按“北 → 东 → 南 → 西”进入顺时针循环，当 x₀ > x* 时平移两拍按“南 → 西 → 北 → 东”进入同一循环（x₀ = x* 时 sₖ = 0）。每步围出嵌套正方形 Qₖ = [min(xₖ, xₖ₊₁), max(xₖ, xₖ₊₁)]² 及其分段 90° 圆弧导引线（在每个对角线顶点 Aₖ₊₁ 处切线恒正交，本身并不在曲线形状上收敛为光滑螺旋）；对角线顶点与方格边长按公比 λ 渐近等比收缩，极坐标舞台则以另一条独立的光滑等角对数螺旋 R(φ) = R₀e⁻ᵇᵠ（MODEL）对该离散等比收缩作连续插值。在克利福德几何诠释（MODEL）下，−1 = Î² 正是二维平面里两次同向 90° 正交旋转。
  2. 一维加法守恒与二维勾股守恒。平直一维自相似方程 x + x² = 1 给出 φ⁻¹ + φ⁻² = 0.618034 + 0.381966 = 1（每转 90° 按 √(φ⁻²) = φ⁻¹ ≈ 0.6180 收缩）；把单位尺弯成单位圆弧 cos θ* = θ* 后，正交收缩率变为 λ_θ = sin θ* ≈ 0.6736（比 0.6180 高出约 0.0556，即 5.56 个百分点、相对约 9.0% 的曲率修正），守恒律升维为勾股平方和 (θ*)² + sin²θ* = 0.546247 + 0.453753 = 1。
  3. 常向心径向切角与数值口径。对光滑极坐标等角对数螺旋 R(φ) = R₀e⁻ᵇᵠ（每转 90° 乘以 κ = e⁻ᵇπ/²），指向不动点中心的向心径向单位射线与前进切线的锐夹角恒为 α = arctan(1/b)（雅各布·伯努利 1692，文献结果）：理想闭合圆 κ = 1 对应 α = 90.00°；蛛网双步螺旋模型 κ = √(sin θ*) ≈ 0.8207（单折臂 90°，单步 Picard 迭代占两臂 180°）对应 α = 82.83°；θ* 正交螺旋 κ = sin θ* ≈ 0.6736 对应 α = 75.88°（较圆切线内偏 14.12°）；斐波那契黄金螺旋 κ = φ⁻¹ ≈ 0.6180 对应 α = 72.97°（内偏 17.03°）。常数与离散轨道采用 70 位定点整数实算，极坐标曲线与 α 采用 IEEE-754 双精度实算后按显示位数舍入。
  4. 统一场中的双螺旋分工（MODEL）。黄金比对任意有理数 q 满足赫尔维茨严格有理逼近下界（LEAN ✓；全 1 连分数在无理数中的渐近极值属 Hurwitz 1891 文献结果），支撑切投准晶与齐肯多夫编码的水平抗共振底盘；余弦迭代则在此舞台上将自指残差沿 0.6736 正交螺旋模型垂直收束到 θ*。
  5. 已验证的部分。θ* 的存在唯一性、φ⁻¹ 倒数分支正不动点充要判据、斐波那契—卡西尼 (−1)ⁿ⁺¹ 恒等式、黄金比赫尔维茨下界，以及反自伴幺正矩阵 R² = −I 均为 Lean 4 内核冻结定理（见右侧绿色框）。

Why does iterating θₖ₊₁ = cos θₖ toward Dottie's constant θ* ≈ 0.7391 wind into an inward plane spiral parallel to the golden continued-fraction iteration xₖ₊₁ = 1/(1 + xₖ) → φ⁻¹ ≈ 0.6180? This lab pairs cobweb whirling squares (piecewise 90° guides), smooth equiangular polar spiral models and the unit-circle conservation duality. It is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Negative slopes, equal arms and piecewise guides. At their fixed points, (1/(1 + x))′|_{φ⁻¹} = −φ⁻² ≈ −0.3820 < 0 (literature result, not formalized in this book), while from [0, 1] \ {θ*} the cosine iteration crosses θ* at every step with error ratio tending to (cos θ)′|_{θ*} = −sin θ* = −√(1 − (θ*)²) ≈ −0.6736 < 0 (application of existing results in Example ex-ch33-mutual-cosine-monotonic-lock of chapter 33). Moving vertically from Aₖ(xₖ, xₖ) to Bₖ(xₖ, f(xₖ)) and horizontally to Aₖ₊₁(f(xₖ), f(xₖ)) gives strictly equal arm lengths sₖ = |f(xₖ) − xₖ| (winding 180° per step): starting below x* enters the clockwise cycle at N → E → S → W, while starting above x* enters two quarter-turns shifted at S → W → N → E (with sₖ = 0 at x₀ = x*). Each step frames a square Qₖ = [min(xₖ, xₖ₊₁), max(xₖ, xₖ₊₁)]² with a piecewise 90° circular guide (retaining perpendicular tangents at every diagonal junction Aₖ₊₁ rather than converging in curve shape to a smooth spiral); the diagonal vertices and square side lengths contract asymptotically at geometric rate λ, while a separate smooth logarithmic spiral R(φ) = R₀e⁻ᵇᵠ (MODEL) on the polar stage continuously interpolates that discrete geometric contraction. In our Clifford model (MODEL), −1 = Î² is two same-handed 90° orthogonal turns in a 2D plane.
  2. 1D additive vs 2D Pythagorean conservation. Flat 1D self-similarity x + x² = 1 yields φ⁻¹ + φ⁻² = 0.618034 + 0.381966 = 1 (scaling by √(φ⁻²) = φ⁻¹ ≈ 0.6180 per quarter-turn); bending the unit ruler onto the unit circle via cos θ* = θ* shifts the orthogonal factor to λ_θ = sin θ* ≈ 0.6736 (higher by about 0.0556, i.e. 5.56 percentage points or roughly 9.0% relative curvature shift), governed by Pythagorean square conservation (θ*)² + sin²θ* = 0.546247 + 0.453753 = 1.
  3. Inward-radial tangent angle and precision. Along a smooth polar logarithmic spiral R(φ) = R₀e⁻ᵇᵠ with per-90° factor κ = e⁻ᵇπ/², the acute angle between the inward radial unit ray (pointing toward the fixed point) and the forward unit tangent is constant at α = arctan(1/b) (Jakob Bernoulli, 1692; literature result): 90.00° for the closed circle (κ = 1), 82.83° for the cobweb two-step spiral (κ = √(sin θ*) ≈ 0.8207 per 90° arm, 180° per Picard step), 75.88° for the θ* orthogonal spiral (κ = sin θ* ≈ 0.6736, inward tilt 14.12°), and 72.97° for the Fibonacci golden spiral (κ = φ⁻¹ ≈ 0.6180, inward tilt 17.03°). Constants and discrete iterates use 70-digit BigInt arithmetic; polar curves and α use IEEE-754 double precision rounded to displayed digits.
  4. Division of labor in the unified field (MODEL). Backed by the strict rational lower bound of the golden Hurwitz theorem (LEAN ✓; asymptotic extremality among all irrationals is Hurwitz 1891, literature), φ stages horizontal anti-resonant quasicrystals and Zeckendorf coding, while cos θ contracts self-referential residuals vertically along the 0.6736 spiral model to θ*.
  5. What is verified. Existence and uniqueness of θ*, the positive fixed-point characterization of φ⁻¹ under 1/(x + 1), Cassini's (−1)ⁿ⁺¹ identity, the golden Hurwitz lower bound, and R² = −I for skew-adjoint unitaries are kernel-verified Lean 4 theorems (the green boxes).
