理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

第 30 章的样本：平面流处处无旋，只在 2m 个点上各有一根涡丝，正负相间排在圆上，每个涡旋另带一项绕原点的进动。舞台画的是这些点本身，时间轴推动时间。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 静止。场 Fb,Ω 的第一项让涡旋绕原点进动，第二项是其余涡旋吹来的速度。偶数个涡旋只在半径 R = √(b/(4Ω)) 上两者相抵（这里 b = 2、Ω = ½，R = 1）；换一个半径，正负涡旋朝相反方向转开。
  2. 发散。从六边形起，平衡点的线性化有 2(m − 2) 维的发散方向，本征值是 λr = Ω(m − r)√(r² − 2)。沿 r = 2 的本征方向推 10⁻⁹，偏离按 eλt 长大；方程在平衡点处光滑，平衡却不稳定。
  3. 漂移。偶极与正方形的本征值全在虚轴上，零本征值却有一个二阶若尔当块：沿 c₁ 推 ε，线性化解 ε(c₁ + t c₀) 让整圈匀速转走，转角 εt。舞台上积分的是非线性方程，转角与 εt 的对照是数值观察。

A sample from Chapter 30: a plane flow with no vorticity anywhere except at 2m points, each pierced by a filament, alternating in sign around a circle, each vortex also precessing about the centre. The stage draws the points themselves; the rail moves time. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Rest. The first term of the field Fb,Ω makes each vortex precess about the centre, the second is the velocity the other vortices blow on it. An even ring balances the two only at the radius R = √(b/(4Ω)) (here b = 2, Ω = ½, R = 1); at another radius the two signs turn apart.
  2. Divergence. From the hexagon on, the linearization at the equilibrium has 2(m − 2) diverging directions, with eigenvalues λr = Ω(m − r)√(r² − 2). A push of 10⁻⁹ along an eigenvector of r = 2 grows like eλt; the equation is smooth at the equilibrium, and the equilibrium is unstable.
  3. Drift. The dipole and the square have a purely imaginary spectrum, but the zero eigenvalue carries a Jordan block of size two: a push ε along c₁ has the linearized solution ε(c₁ + t c₀), which turns the whole ring at a steady rate, through the angle εt. The stage integrates the nonlinear equation; the comparison of its angle with εt is a numerical observation.
