The squares of the coordinates of the point (dτ/dt, ηkin,eff, ηgrav) always add up to 1, so it can only move on the unit sphere: dτ/dt = √((1 − rs/r)(1 − β²)); the sphere is coloured by the share (dτ/dt)², cyan near the dτ/dt axis and magenta near the two load axes.   3. Tier 3 · trace ratio and overconstraint. One chiral Weyl generation: Tr T₃² = ½(1 + 3) = 2 and Tr Q² = 2(1 + 3 × 5/9) = 16/3, so the cyan column reaches exactly 3/8 of the silver one.
