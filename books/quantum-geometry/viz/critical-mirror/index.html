第二十二章 §22.4 说：ξ 的反射对称 ξ(1 − s) = ξ(s) 与共轭对称合起来，零点按 ρ、ρ̄、1 − ρ、1 − ρ̄ 四个一组出现，这四点两两重合的充要条件正是 Re ρ = ½。本页让读者拖动一个点 ρ，看它的四点轨道；再换到凯莱坐标 w = (s − 1)/s，临界线成了单位圆，s ↦ 1 − s̄ 成了关于圆的反演。本页是数值演示，不是证明，也不画“全部零点”。

  1. 镜子。左边是临界带 0 < Re s < 1（纵向压缩），虚线是 Re s = ½，叉是 ζ 的极点 s = 1。琥珀色的 ρ 与品红的三个伴组成矩形；δ = 0 时它们两两重合成青色的两点。
  2. 凯莱圆。右边 w = (s − 1)/s：临界线是单位圆，反射 s ↦ 1 − s 成为 w ↦ 1/w，共轭成为 w ↦ w̄，合起来 w ↦ 1/w̄ 是关于圆的反演。四个像两个在圆内、两个在圆外；|w|² − 1 = (1 − 2σ)/(σ² + t²)，所以越往高处，离线的点越贴近圆周，左下的放大镜把角度与 ln|w| 拉开来看。
  3. 四次式。F(s) = ((s − ½ − δ)² + γ²)((s − ½ + δ)² + γ²) 有同样的两种对称，零点恰是 ½ ± δ ± iγ。沿临界线 F(½ + it) = (δ² + γ² − t²)² + 4δ²t² 是实数：δ ≠ 0 时处处为正，γ ≥ δ 时最小值 4δ²γ²（在 t = ±√(γ² − δ²) 处），γ < δ 时最小值 (δ² + γ²)²（在 t = 0 处）；δ = 0 时零点 ½ ± iγ 成对落在线上（各为二重），F = (γ² − t²)² 非负，在 t = ±γ 归零。ξ(½ + it) 也是实数，却在 14 与 15 之间变号。对称只保证取实值，不保证变号。

Chapter 22 §22.4 says that the reflection symmetry ξ(1 − s) = ξ(s), together with conjugation, makes zeros come in fours, ρ, ρ̄, 1 − ρ, 1 − ρ̄, and that the four coincide in pairs exactly when Re ρ = ½. The page lets the reader drag a point ρ and watch its four-point orbit; in the Cayley coordinate w = (s − 1)/s the critical line becomes the unit circle and s ↦ 1 − s̄ becomes inversion in it. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof, and it does not draw "all the zeros".

  1. The mirror. On the left, the critical strip 0 < Re s < 1 (vertical scale compressed); the dashed line is Re s = ½ and the cross is the pole of ζ at s = 1. The amber ρ and its three magenta partners form a rectangle; at δ = 0 they merge in pairs into two cyan points.
  2. The Cayley circle. On the right, w = (s − 1)/s: the critical line is the unit circle, the reflection s ↦ 1 − s becomes w ↦ 1/w and conjugation becomes w ↦ w̄, so together w ↦ 1/w̄ is inversion in the circle. Two images lie inside and two outside; |w|² − 1 = (1 − 2σ)/(σ² + t²), so the higher up, the closer an off-line point comes to the circle, and the magnifier at the lower left stretches angle and ln|w| apart.
  3. The quartic. F(s) = ((s − ½ − δ)² + γ²)((s − ½ + δ)² + γ²) has the same two symmetries, and its zeros are exactly ½ ± δ ± iγ. Along the critical line F(½ + it) = (δ² + γ² − t²)² + 4δ²t² is real: for δ ≠ 0 it is positive everywhere, with minimum 4δ²γ² at t = ±√(γ² − δ²) when γ ≥ δ, and (δ² + γ²)² at t = 0 when γ < δ; at δ = 0 the zeros ½ ± iγ lie on the line in pairs (each double), and F = (γ² − t²)² is nonnegative and vanishes at t = ±γ. ξ(½ + it) is real too, yet it changes sign between 14 and 15. Symmetry guarantees real values, not sign changes.
