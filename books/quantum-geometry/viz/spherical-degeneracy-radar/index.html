LEAN ✓观察者迹拉回：信道满足单步迹对偶 Tr(Φ(ρ)·E) = Tr(ρ·Φ*(E)) 时，演化 t 步后的读数等于初始态去读拉回 t 次的效应。“拉回”场景只演示它的一个实例：单量子比特的幺正信道 ρ ↦ UρU†、t = 1；纯态 v 与投影效应 O 的迹读数是 (1 + O·v)/2，所以 O 读转过的 Rv 与拉回的 R⁻¹O 读原来的 v 是同一个数。已冻结：The observer trace pullback: when a channel satisfies the one-step trace duality Tr(Φ(ρ)·E) = Tr(ρ·Φ*(E)), the reading after t steps equals the initial state reading the effect pulled back t times. The Pullback scene shows only one instance of it: the unitary single-qubit channel ρ ↦ UρU† with t = 1; a pure state v and a projective effect O give the trace reading (1 + O·v)/2, so O reading the turned Rv and the pulled-back R⁻¹O reading the original v are one and the same number. LEAN ✓协变换位子：在任意环里，若 U·f = f′·U，则 U·f − f·U = (f′ − f)·U。页面在 Cl(3,0) 里取 U = 差旋子 D = R₂R̃₁、f = e₃：D e₃ = e₃ D，f′ = f，换位子为零；f = e₁ 时 f′ = e₂，换位子不为零（读数见“旋子”场景）。已冻结：The covariant commutator: in any ring, if U·f = f′·U then U·f − f·U = (f′ − f)·U. 舞台是单位球面 S² ⊂ ℝ³，标准正交基 e₁、e₂、e₃（e₃ 朝上）。琥珀色的点是单位观察者探针 O，它对一个状态 s 的读数是内积 O·s，画成从 s 落到观察者轴上的青色影子。本页是数值演示，不是证明；每个数都由页面在 Cl(3,0) 里当场算出（NUM）。

  1. 两步寻址（第三十四章例 1 与例 2 第 1 部分）。一次正交在子午面 e₃∧e₁ 里倾转 θ：v₁ = cos θ e₃ + sin θ e₁；两次正交再在赤道面 e₁∧e₂ 里拧过方位角 φ：v₂ = cos θ e₃ + sin θ (cos φ e₁ + sin φ e₂)。读数差 Δ(O) = O·v₂ − O·v₁ = sin θ [O₁(cos φ − 1) + O₂ sin φ]。
  2. 盲圈。固定 φ，Δ(O) = 0 的观察者是与 v₂ − v₁ 正交的那条大圆（青色虚线）。φ = π/2 时它是 O₁ = O₂ 的对称子午大圆；φ 扫过 (0, 2π)，这条大圆绕竖轴转过半圈、扫遍整颗球，所有盲圈的公共部分只剩 O = ±e₃，在那里两路读数都是 ±cos θ。
  3. 偏离极轴。观察者离北极 β、方位角 φO 时 Δ = sin θ sin β [cos(φ − φO) − cos φO]，随 φ 扫描张开的峰峰值是 2 |sin θ| sin β。
  4. 八分球面旋子（例 2 第 2 部分）。双明治作用 s ↦ R s R̃，R̃ 为逆序（eiej 的逆序是 −eiej）。一步：R₁ = exp(−π/4 e₁e₃) = (1 − e₁e₃)/√2，把 +e₁ 送到 +e₃。两步：先 e₁ → e₂，再左乘 e₂ → e₃，R₂ = ((1 − e₂e₃)/√2)((1 − e₁e₂)/√2) = (1 − e₁e₂ − e₂e₃ − e₁e₃)/2 = exp(−π/4 e₁e₂) R₁，也把 +e₁ 送到 +e₃。两者相差的差旋子 D = R₂R̃₁ = exp(−π/4 e₁e₂) 整个落在赤道面 e₁∧e₂ 里：它把随行的标架绕 e₃ 转 π/2（右下角的小盘）。
  5. 赤道测试态。s(α) = cos α e₁ + sin α e₂ 经两枚旋子得 s₁(α) = cos α e₃ + sin α e₂ 与 s₂(α) = cos α e₃ − sin α e₁。整条赤道的两个像是 e₂∧e₃ 面与 e₁∧e₃ 面里的两条大圆，只在 ±e₃ 相交：两者只在 sin α = 0 时重合。读数相等的条件是 O·(s₂ − s₁) = −sin α (O₁ + O₂) = 0；站在 e₂ 上读出 sin α 与 0，站在 e₃ 上两路都读 cos α。
  6. 转动标架。把初始赤道标架换成 e₁(γ) = cos γ e₁ + sin γ e₂、e₂(γ) = −sin γ e₁ + cos γ e₂，同样造两枚旋子：盲圈变成与 e₁(γ) + e₂(γ) 正交的大圆，随 γ 绕竖轴转动；对所有 γ 都读不出差别的只有 O = ±e₃。
  7. 冻结定理。公共单位法线 unit_perpendicular_pair、读出划分拓扑的不可分核 partition_inseparable_iff_kernel 、协变换位子 covariant_commutator_formula 与观察者迹拉回 observer_pullback_trace_identity 是第三十四章用 lean= 引用的定理（LEAN ✓）。它们是一般陈述，页面只演示它们在这几个例子里的样子；“盲区反演站位”本身是该章的模型诠释（MODEL），Berry—Pancharatnam 相位与虚拟 Z 门是该章转述的文献结果（ARGUED）。

The stage is the unit sphere S² ⊂ ℝ³ with the orthonormal basis e₁, e₂, e₃ (e₃ up). The amber point is a unit observer probe O; its reading of a state s is the inner product O·s, drawn as the cyan shadow that s casts on the observer’s axis. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof; every number is computed on the spot in Cl(3,0) (NUM).

  1. Two-step addressing (Chapter 34, Example 1 and part 1 of Example 2). The first orthogonal turn tilts by θ in the meridian plane e₃∧e₁: v₁ = cos θ e₃ + sin θ e₁; the second twists by the azimuth φ in the equatorial plane e₁∧e₂: v₂ = cos θ e₃ + sin θ (cos φ e₁ + sin φ e₂). The gap in the readings is Δ(O) = O·v₂ − O·v₁ = sin θ [O₁(cos φ − 1) + O₂ sin φ].
  2. The blind circle. For a fixed φ, the observers with Δ(O) = 0 form the great circle orthogonal to v₂ − v₁ (dashed cyan). At φ = π/2 it is the symmetric meridian O₁ = O₂; as φ runs over (0, 2π) the circle turns half a turn about the vertical and sweeps the whole sphere, and all the blind circles have only O = ±e₃ in common, where both readings are ±cos θ.
  3. Off the polar axis. For an observer at angle β from the North Pole and azimuth φO, Δ = sin θ sin β [cos(φ − φO) − cos φO], which opens, as φ sweeps, to a peak-to-peak spread of 2 |sin θ| sin β.
  4. Octant rotors (part 2 of Example 2). The sandwich action s ↦ R s R̃, with R̃ the reversion (the reverse of eiej is −eiej). One hop: R₁ = exp(−π/4 e₁e₃) = (1 − e₁e₃)/√2 takes +e₁ to +e₃. Two hops, first e₁ → e₂ and then, multiplied on the left, e₂ → e₃: R₂ = ((1 − e₂e₃)/√2)((1 − e₁e₂)/√2) = (1 − e₁e₂ − e₂e₃ − e₁e₃)/2 = exp(−π/4 e₁e₂) R₁, which also takes +e₁ to +e₃. The difference rotor D = R₂R̃₁ = exp(−π/4 e₁e₂) lies wholly in the equatorial plane e₁∧e₂: it turns the carried frame by π/2 about e₃ (the small dial at the lower right).
  5. An equator test state. s(α) = cos α e₁ + sin α e₂ becomes s₁(α) = cos α e₃ + sin α e₂ under the one-hop rotor and s₂(α) = cos α e₃ − sin α e₁ under the two-hop one. The images of the whole equator are two great circles, in the planes e₂∧e₃ and e₁∧e₃, meeting only at ±e₃: the two agree only where sin α = 0. The readings agree when O·(s₂ − s₁) = −sin α (O₁ + O₂) = 0; from e₂ one reads sin α and 0, from e₃ both read cos α.
  6. Turning the frame. Replace the initial equatorial frame by e₁(γ) = cos γ e₁ + sin γ e₂ and e₂(γ) = −sin γ e₁ + cos γ e₂ and build the two rotors the same way: the blind circle becomes the great circle orthogonal to e₁(γ) + e₂(γ) and turns about the vertical with γ; only O = ±e₃ cannot tell the results apart for every γ.
  7. Frozen theorems. The common unit normal unit_perpendicular_pair, the kernel of the readout partition topology partition_inseparable_iff_kernel, the covariant commutator covariant_commutator_formula and the observer trace pullback observer_pullback_trace_identity are theorems that Chapter 34 cites with lean= (LEAN ✓). They are general statements; the page only shows what they look like in these examples. “Inverting the observer’s position from the blind spots” is itself the chapter’s model interpretation (MODEL), and the Berry–Pancharatnam phase and the virtual Z gate are literature results the chapter reports (ARGUED).
