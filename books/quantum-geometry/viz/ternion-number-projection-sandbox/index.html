长除法每个新余数都在 0, 1, …, q − 1 中。余数一旦为 0，此后数字全为 0，小数展开终止。若余数始终非零，则至多 q − 1 种取值，由鸽巢原理必有余数重复，数字从它首次出现处起循环。Each new long-division remainder lies in 0, 1, …, q − 1. A zero remainder makes every later digit zero, so the decimal terminates. If the remainders stay nonzero, they take at most q − 1 values, so by the pigeonhole principle one repeats, and the digits recur from its first occurrence.
