  1. 弧体。取 eₐ = ½iₐ，则 [iₐ, iᵦ] = 2εₐᵦ𝒸 i𝒸，[eₐ, eᵦ] = εₐᵦ𝒸 e𝒸。对单位方向 û，Δ𝒯ε(θ, û) = exp(θ û·e) = cos(θ/2) 1 + sin(θ/2) û·(i, j, k) ∈ S³。紫色三叶雕体是它的示意，银白的弧画出 θ 从 0° 到 720°，琥珀手柄是当前点，青色一段是时间轴沿弧走过的路程。
  2. 平直死锁。“平直”台把四元数乘积正交投影回 span(1, i, j)，丢掉 ij = k。对任一实三维含幺双线性乘法取非标量 x，Lₓ 的实三次特征多项式至少有一个实根 λ₀，它的特征向量 v 给出 (x − λ₀1)v = 0，两个因子都不为零。180° 处罗德里格斯坐标 tan(θ/2) 无定义，品红柱顶的圆环标出发散；若改用 Z–Y–X 欧拉角，俯仰角到 ±90° 时首末两轴重合，那是另一种坐标退化。
  3. 余弦影与矢高。“ℍ”台的银白柱是 w = cos(θ/2)，青色环的半径是 ‖v‖ = |sin(θ/2)|，w² + ‖v‖² = 1；品红的一小段是矢高 1 − w。θ = 2θ* 时 w = cos θ* = θ* = 0.7391。
  4. 复切片与角盈。页面取 û = i cos α + j sin α，复投影 z = cos(θ/2) + i sin(θ/2) cos α，α = 0 时它是单位相位。切片台的球面三角形两个底点在赤道上的经度为 0 与 β，第三点在经度 β/2、纬度 α；β = 25° + 40°|sin(θ/2)| 只为让底边在全行程都看得见。角盈 Ω = 2 atan2(D, S)（球面度）；α 回到 0 时三条边落回同一条大圆，Ω = 0。
  5. 实迹与整圈。“ℝ”台的两把尺子分别量 ½Tr U = w 与空间旋转的迹 Tr R = 1 + 2cos θ = 4w² − 1。360° 时空间旋转复原（Tr R = +3），旋量却是 −1；720° 时两者一起复原。“ℤ/ℙ”台的两颗银珠计 n = ⌊θ / 360°⌋。
  6. 有理数。有理数滑块选 p/q：青色珠是长除的余数，余数回到出现过的值时小数开始循环；青色小环是不为 1 的 p 进因子，与 |x| 相乘恰为 1。
  7. 双透镜。“万数归弧”读法把四个台读成同一条弧的四个影子；“除环与同伦”读法把它们读成 Lₓ、ℍ 的范数、ℂ 的角盈、ℝ 的迹与 ℤ 的度。两种读法用的是同一组 θ、α 与 p/q。

Every number is computed on the page (NUM); each Lean theorem is shown only on the one instance named in the evidence panel, and where there is none the panel says the page does not show it; the three-lobed sculpture, the places of the stations and the prime knots are the reading of this book (MODEL).

  1. The arc body. Set eₐ = ½iₐ; then [iₐ, iᵦ] = 2εₐᵦ𝒸 i𝒸 and [eₐ, eᵦ] = εₐᵦ𝒸 e𝒸. For a unit direction û, Δ𝒯ε(θ, û) = exp(θ û·e) = cos(θ/2) 1 + sin(θ/2) û·(i, j, k) ∈ S³. The violet three-lobed sculpture depicts it, the silver arc draws θ from 0° to 720°, the amber handle is the current point, and the cyan stretch is the way the rail has travelled along the arc.
  2. The flat deadlock. The flat station projects the quaternion product orthogonally back onto span(1, i, j) and loses ij = k. For any unital bilinear product on real three-space, take a nonscalar x: the real cubic characteristic polynomial of Lₓ has a real root λ₀, and its eigenvector v gives (x − λ₀1)v = 0 with both factors nonzero. At 180° the Rodrigues coordinate tan(θ/2) is undefined and the ring on the magenta pillar marks the divergence; in Z–Y–X Euler angles, a pitch of ±90° aligns the first and last axes, which is another kind of coordinate collapse.
  3. Scalar shadow and sagitta. The silver column of the ℍ station is w = cos(θ/2), the radius of the cyan ring is ‖v‖ = |sin(θ/2)|, and w² + ‖v‖² = 1; the short magenta stretch is the sagitta 1 − w. At θ = 2θ*, w = cos θ* = θ* = 0.7391.
  4. Complex slice and excess. The page takes û = i cos α + j sin α, so the complex projection is z = cos(θ/2) + i sin(θ/2) cos α, a unit phase at α = 0. The spherical triangle of the slice station has its two base points on the equator at longitudes 0 and β and its third vertex at longitude β/2 and latitude α; β = 25° + 40°|sin(θ/2)| only keeps the base visible over the whole sweep. The excess is Ω = 2 atan2(D, S) steradians; when α returns to 0 the three sides fall onto one great circle and Ω = 0.
  5. Real trace and full turns. The two rulers of the ℝ station measure ½Tr U = w and the trace of the spatial rotation, Tr R = 1 + 2cos θ = 4w² − 1. At 360° the spatial rotation is back (Tr R = +3) while the spinor is −1; at 720° both are back together. The two silver beads of the ℤ/ℙ station count n = ⌊θ / 360°⌋.
  6. Rationals. The rational slider picks p/q: the cyan beads are the long-division remainders, and the decimal starts to recur when a remainder comes back; the small cyan rings are the p-adic factors other than 1, whose product with |x| is exactly 1.
  7. Two lenses. The all-numbers-from-arc reading reads the four stations as four shadows of one arc; the division-algebra and homotopy reading reads them as Lₓ, the norm of ℍ, the excess of ℂ, the trace of ℝ and the degree of ℤ. Both readings use the same θ, α and p/q.
