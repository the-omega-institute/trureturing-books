第 31 章把一串“不可能”读成同一件事：没有特权。本页把其中五本账画在同一颗布洛赫球上，每本账旁边是一根总额不动的柱子：读者推哪一边，另一边就让出同样的份额。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 席位。在球面上均匀撒点，任选一根轴为心、半角 α 的球冠，里面的点占 (1 − cos α)/2，与轴朝哪里无关；点的质心随点数增多趋于球心。两个纯态互相丈量：拿 ψ 量 φ 的权重 |⟨φ|ψ⟩|² 与拿 φ 量 ψ 的权重 |⟨ψ|φ⟩|² 相等，在球上就是 (1 + rψ·rφ)/2。
  2. 得失。箭头 r 沿测量轴的影子（青色的线）是可区分度 D = |z|，横向的一段（品红的面积）是相干 V = √(x² + y²)；D² + V² = r²，纯态时等于 1。线多一分，面积就少一分；混态的预算 r² 小于 1。
  3. 关系。沿 a|000⟩ + b|110⟩ + c|101⟩ + g|111⟩ 走过直积、贝尔、W 型与 GHZ 态。A 的布洛赫箭头只剩 rA = 2a² − 1 的竖直分量；总额 1 分成 A 自己留下的 ‖rA‖²（青色）、与 B、与 C 的二体纠缠 τAB、τAC（品红）和三体纠缠 τ₃（紫色）。贝尔态把全部预算给了 B，A 的箭头缩回球心，C 分不到。
  4. 复制。未知量子比特复制成 M 份时，每份的箭头缩成 η = (M + 2)/(3M) 倍，保真度 (2M + 1)/(3M) 趋于 2/3。只有彼此正交的一对（两极）能被同一台机器照抄。
  5. 遍历。两个态各自每步绕竖轴转过黄金角 2π(φ − 1)，出发相位不同。窗口是方位角的一段弧，占 2 − φ。两人落进窗口的频率都趋于 2 − φ，命中数与 T(2 − φ) 的偏差始终小于 1，频率误差按 1/T 下降；对照组的独立随机点偏差按 √T 增长。

Chapter 31 reads a string of impossibilities as one thing: no privilege. The page draws five of its ledgers on one Bloch sphere, each next to a bar whose total does not move: whatever the reader pushes on one side, the other side gives up the same share. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Seat. Throw points uniformly on the sphere and pick a cap of half-angle α around any axis: it holds (1 − cos α)/2 of the points wherever the axis points, and the centroid of the points tends to the centre as their number grows. Two pure states measure each other: the weight of measuring φ with ψ, |⟨φ|ψ⟩|², equals the weight of measuring ψ with φ, |⟨ψ|φ⟩|²; on the sphere it is (1 + rψ·rφ)/2.
  2. Gain. The shadow of the arrow r on the measurement axis (the cyan line) is the distinguishability D = |z|, the sideways part (the magenta area) is the coherence V = √(x² + y²); D² + V² = r², which is 1 for a pure state. More line means less area; a mixed state has a budget r² below 1.
  3. Bonds. Walk along a|000⟩ + b|110⟩ + c|101⟩ + g|111⟩ through product, Bell, W-type and GHZ states. The Bloch arrow of A keeps only its vertical part rA = 2a² − 1; the total 1 splits into what A keeps, ‖rA‖² (cyan), the bonds with B and with C, τAB and τAC (magenta), and the three-way tangle τ₃ (violet). The Bell state gives the whole budget to B: A's arrow shrinks to the centre and nothing is left for C.
  4. Copies. Copying an unknown qubit into M copies shrinks each copy's arrow by η = (M + 2)/(3M), with fidelity (2M + 1)/(3M) tending to 2/3. Only a pair of orthogonal states (the two poles) can be copied exactly by one machine.
  5. History. Two states each turn about the vertical axis by the golden angle 2π(φ − 1) per step, from different starting phases. The window is an arc of azimuths holding a share 2 − φ. Both shares of hits tend to 2 − φ, the discrepancy between the hits and T(2 − φ) stays below 1, and the error of the share falls like 1/T; independent random points, for comparison, drift like √T.
