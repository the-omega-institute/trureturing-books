理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

把泡利矩阵 σ₁, σ₂, σ₃ 当作三维空间的三根基矢量，单比特态 |ψ⟩ 就是单位球面上的一根布洛赫矢量 r = (⟨σ₁⟩, ⟨σ₂⟩, ⟨σ₃⟩)，单比特门就是旋子 R：它在球面上把 r 转到 R r R†，而在旋量 |ψ⟩ 上只走一半的角度。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 布洛赫矢量与玻恩权重。纯态 ρ = |ψ⟩⟨ψ| = (I + r·σ)/2 是厄米、迹为 1 且幂等的 2×2 矩阵，所以 ‖r‖ = 1：bloch_geometry_of_isHermitian（LEAN ✓）。在计算基上测量，P(0) = (1 + r_z)/2 = cos²(θ/2)、P(1) = sin²(θ/2)，两者之和是 tr ρ = 1：recordWeight_sum（LEAN ✓）。
  2. 泡利代数与体积元。X = σ₁、Z = σ₃ 自伴、平方为 I 且反对易：qubit_weyl_star（LEAN ✓）。页面精确算出 σ₁σ₂σ₃ = i·I₂：三根基矢量的乘积是体积元 I₃，它是 i 乘单位阵。I₃ 与每个单位双矢量 Bₙ = I₃ n̂ = i n̂·σ 都是反自伴的酉矩阵，因而平方为 −I：square_eq_neg_one_of_skewAdjoint_unitary（LEAN ✓）。
  3. 旋子与门。因为 Bₙ² = −1，欧拉公式给出 Rₙ(α) = exp(−(α/2)Bₙ) = cos(α/2) − Bₙ sin(α/2) = w − i b·σ，其中 w = cos(α/2)、b = sin(α/2) n̂。伴随作用 r ↦ R r R† 绕 n̂ 右手转 α。账本里每个门都是这样一枚旋子（与教科书矩阵差一个全局相位）：X、Y、Z 绕各自的轴转 π，H 绕 ĥ = (x̂ + ẑ)/√2 转 π，S、T 绕 ẑ 转 π/2、π/4，I 不转。输入行 A = e₃ 与 B = r₀ 是初态的两根矢量；从 #0 起，格子是当前布洛赫矢量 r 与累积旋子 R = Gₖ⋯G₁ 的四个分量 (w, b₁, b₂, b₃)。
  4. H·Z·H = X。由 qubit_weyl_star 的关系手算：H = (X + Z)/√2，HZH = (XZX + XZZ + ZZX + ZZZ)/2 = (−Z + X + X + Z)/2 = X。这一步是手算与页面的数值核对，不是所引的 Lean 定理。换成旋子，R_H R_Z R_H = (−i)³HZH = iX = −R_X：球面上是同一个转动，旋量却差一个 −1。预设“H·Z·H = X”的符号带因此以 −1 收尾。
  5. 双覆盖与符号带。R(2π) = cos π = −1：矢量转一整圈回到原处，旋量要转两圈才回到自身。左栏的探针旋子 Rₙ(α) 绕舞台右上所选的轴 n̂ 作用在当前步的态上，读数 tr(R) = 2cos(α/2) 在 α = 360° 时等于 −2。符号带记下每一步后累积旋子 R 落在哪一叶：⟨R⟩₀ = tr(R)/2 的符号，为 0 时取 (b₁, b₂, b₃) 第一个非零分量的符号。这是在 ±R 中选代表的约定。
  6. 两子几何积。取 A = e₃、B = r，A·B = r_z = cos θ 是青色投影线，‖A∧B‖ = sin θ 是紫色平行四边形；两者平方和为 1 是 lagrange_gram_identity（LEAN ✓）的特例。
  7. 相图。右栏逐格画出 θ ∈ [0°, 180°]（每 5°）× φ ∈ [0°, 360°]（每 10°）上所选的 ⟨σ⟩；琥珀色十字标出当前步的态，白圈是初态，细线是线路经过的态。点击相图选择初态。网格只是有限的检查，结论的一般性来自 Lean 定理。

Treat the Pauli matrices σ₁, σ₂, σ₃ as the three basis vectors of space: a qubit state |ψ⟩ is then a Bloch vector r = (⟨σ₁⟩, ⟨σ₂⟩, ⟨σ₃⟩) on the unit sphere, and a single-qubit gate is a rotor R that turns r into R r R† on the sphere while it turns the spinor |ψ⟩ through only half the angle. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Bloch vector and Born weights. A pure state ρ = |ψ⟩⟨ψ| = (I + r·σ)/2 is a Hermitian, trace-one, idempotent 2×2 matrix, so ‖r‖ = 1: bloch_geometry_of_isHermitian (LEAN ✓). Measured in the computational basis, P(0) = (1 + r_z)/2 = cos²(θ/2) and P(1) = sin²(θ/2), and they sum to tr ρ = 1: recordWeight_sum (LEAN ✓).
  2. Pauli algebra and the volume element. X = σ₁ and Z = σ₃ are self-adjoint, square to I and anticommute: qubit_weyl_star (LEAN ✓). The page computes σ₁σ₂σ₃ = i·I₂ exactly: the product of the three basis vectors is the volume element I₃, which is i times the identity. I₃ and every unit bivector Bₙ = I₃ n̂ = i n̂·σ are skew-adjoint unitary matrices and therefore square to −I: square_eq_neg_one_of_skewAdjoint_unitary (LEAN ✓).
  3. Rotors and gates. Since Bₙ² = −1, Euler's formula gives Rₙ(α) = exp(−(α/2)Bₙ) = cos(α/2) − Bₙ sin(α/2) = w − i b·σ with w = cos(α/2) and b = sin(α/2) n̂. The adjoint action r ↦ R r R† turns r right-handedly by α about n̂. Every gate of the ledger is such a rotor (up to a global phase of the textbook matrix): X, Y, Z turn by π about their axes, H by π about ĥ = (x̂ + ẑ)/√2, S and T by π/2 and π/4 about ẑ, and I does not turn. The input rows A = e₃ and B = r₀ are the two vectors of the initial state; from #0 on, the cells are the current Bloch vector r and the four components (w, b₁, b₂, b₃) of the accumulated rotor R = Gₖ⋯G₁.
  4. H·Z·H = X. By hand from the relations of qubit_weyl_star: H = (X + Z)/√2 and HZH = (XZX + XZZ + ZZX + ZZZ)/2 = (−Z + X + X + Z)/2 = X. This step is a hand computation and a numerical check on the page, not one of the cited Lean theorems. In rotors, R_H R_Z R_H = (−i)³HZH = iX = −R_X: the same turn of the sphere, but the spinor differs by −1, so the sign tape of the “H·Z·H = X” preset ends in −1.
  5. Double cover and the sign tape. R(2π) = cos π = −1: a vector comes back after one full turn, a spinor only after two. The probe rotor Rₙ(α) of the left column acts on the state of the current step about the axis n̂ chosen at the top right of the stage, and the readout tr(R) = 2cos(α/2) reaches −2 at α = 360°. The sign tape records which sheet the accumulated rotor R is on after each step: the sign of ⟨R⟩₀ = tr(R)/2, or, when that is 0, the sign of the first nonzero of (b₁, b₂, b₃). This is a convention that picks a representative of ±R.
  6. The pairwise geometric product. With A = e₃ and B = r, A·B = r_z = cos θ is the cyan projection and ‖A∧B‖ = sin θ is the violet parallelogram; that their squares sum to 1 is a case of lagrange_gram_identity (LEAN ✓).
  7. The phase map. The right column draws the chosen ⟨σ⟩ cell by cell over θ ∈ [0°, 180°] (every 5°) × φ ∈ [0°, 360°] (every 10°); the amber cross marks the state at the current step, the white ring the initial state, and the thin line the states the circuit passes through. Click the map to pick the initial state. The grid is a finite check only; the general statements come from the Lean theorems.
