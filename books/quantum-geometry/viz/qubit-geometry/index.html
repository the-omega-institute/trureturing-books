理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

单比特纯态是单位球面上的一根布洛赫矢量 B。拿另一根单位矢量 A 当量尺去比它，两者之间只有两样东西：共线的影子（线）和垂直撑开的面（面积）。几何积 AB = A·B + A∧B 把这两样东西写在一起。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 线与面积。A·B = cos θ 是 B 在 A 上的影子长度；|A∧B| = sin θ 是 A 与 B 垂直于 A 的分量撑开的矩形面积。舞台上的直角三角形以 B 为斜边：两条直角边就是线和面积，所以 线² + 面积² = 1，这是 lagrange_gram_identity（LEAN ✓）取 u = A、v = B 的特例。
  2. 线决定咔哒。沿 A 测量，得到 +A 的概率是 (1 + A·B)/2 = cos²(θ/2)，得到 −A 的概率是 sin²(θ/2)；两者之和为 1：recordWeight_sum（LEAN ✓）。纯态的布洛赫矢量长度为 1：bloch_geometry_of_isHermitian（LEAN ✓）。
  3. 面积决定条纹。把 ±A 两条路重新合束，条纹的明暗对比（可见度）等于 B 垂直于 A 的分量长度 sin θ，条纹位置由绕 A 的方位角决定。线越长，哪条路越确定，条纹越淡；面积越大则相反。这是示意（MODEL）。第六章对纯态记录标记（归一化的纯态环境记录态）给出同形的已冻结定理 D² + V² = 1：pure_record_distinguishability_coherence_complementarity（LEAN ✓；那里的 V 是两个记录态内积的模，D = √(1 − V²)）。一般混合环境下的 Englert 不等式是文献结果，未在本书形式化。
  4. 门是旋子。X = σ₁、Z = σ₃ 自伴、平方为 I 且反对易：qubit_weyl_star（LEAN ✓）。单位双矢量 Bₙ = i n̂·σ 是反自伴的酉矩阵，平方为 −I：square_eq_neg_one_of_skewAdjoint_unitary（LEAN ✓）。于是旋子 Rₙ(α) = cos(α/2) − Bₙ sin(α/2) = w − b·(iσ)，其中 w = cos(α/2)、b = sin(α/2) n̂；它把布洛赫矢量绕 n̂ 右手转 α：r ↦ R r R†。本页把 X、Y、Z 画成绕各自的轴转 180°，H 绕 ĥ = (x̂ + ẑ)/√2 转 180°，S、T 绕 ẑ 转 90°、45°，与教科书矩阵只差一个全局相位（约定，不是定理）。
  5. 矢量一圈，旋子两圈。旋子只走一半的角度：α = 360° 时矢量回到原处，R = cos 180° = −1；α = 720° 时 R = +1。H·Z·H 与 X 在球面上是同一个转动，旋子却是 R_H R_Z R_H = −R_X。页面在 ±R 中用标量部 w 的符号（w = 0 时取 b 的第一个非零分量的符号）标出当前落在哪一叶，这是选代表的约定，叶 +1 不等于旋子是 +1。B 回到出发点也不说明整段线路是恒等：B 在北极时加一个 S 门，B 不动，旋子却是 (w, b) = (√½, 0, 0, √½)，不是 ±1；只有整段转动的旋子矢量部为零时，页面才说旋子回到 ±1。H·Z·H = X 可由上面的泡利关系手算，页面只做数值核对。

A pure qubit state is a Bloch vector B on the unit sphere. Hold another unit vector A against it as a ruler and there are exactly two things between them: the collinear shadow (a line) and the perpendicular face they span (an area). The geometric product AB = A·B + A∧B writes the two together. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Line and area. A·B = cos θ is the length of the shadow of B on A; |A∧B| = sin θ is the area of the rectangle spanned by A and the part of B perpendicular to A. The right triangle on the stage has B as its hypotenuse and the line and the area as its legs, so line² + area² = 1: the case u = A, v = B of lagrange_gram_identity (LEAN ✓).
  2. The line sets the clicks. Measured along A, the outcome +A has probability (1 + A·B)/2 = cos²(θ/2) and −A has sin²(θ/2); they sum to 1: recordWeight_sum (LEAN ✓). The Bloch vector of a pure state has length 1: bloch_geometry_of_isHermitian (LEAN ✓).
  3. The area sets the fringes. Recombine the two paths ±A and the contrast of the fringes (the visibility) is the length sin θ of the part of B perpendicular to A, while their position is the azimuth about A. The longer the line, the more certain the path and the fainter the fringes; the larger the area, the opposite. This is a schematic (MODEL). For pure environment record states (normalized pure record vectors) Chapter 6 has a frozen theorem of the same shape, D² + V² = 1: pure_record_distinguishability_coherence_complementarity (LEAN ✓; there V is the modulus of the inner product of the two record states and D = √(1 − V²)). Englert's inequality for general mixed environments is a literature result, not formalized in this book.
  4. Gates are rotors. X = σ₁ and Z = σ₃ are self-adjoint, square to I and anticommute: qubit_weyl_star (LEAN ✓). The unit bivector Bₙ = i n̂·σ is a skew-adjoint unitary matrix and squares to −I: square_eq_neg_one_of_skewAdjoint_unitary (LEAN ✓). Hence the rotor Rₙ(α) = cos(α/2) − Bₙ sin(α/2) = w − b·(iσ) with w = cos(α/2) and b = sin(α/2) n̂; it turns the Bloch vector right-handedly by α about n̂: r ↦ R r R†. The page draws X, Y, Z as 180° turns about their axes, H as a 180° turn about ĥ = (x̂ + ẑ)/√2, and S, T as 90° and 45° turns about ẑ, which differ from the textbook matrices by a global phase (a convention, not a theorem).
  5. One turn for the vector, two for the rotor. The rotor moves through half the angle: at α = 360° the vector is back and R = cos 180° = −1; at α = 720°, R = +1. H·Z·H and X are the same turn of the sphere, yet the rotors satisfy R_H R_Z R_H = −R_X. Of the pair ±R the page marks the current sheet by the sign of the scalar part w (or, when w = 0, of the first nonzero component of b): a convention for picking a representative, so sheet +1 does not mean the rotor is +1. Nor does B coming back mean the whole circuit is the identity: with B at the north pole an S gate leaves B where it is, yet its rotor is (w, b) = (√½, 0, 0, √½), not ±1; the page says the rotor is back at ±1 only when the vector part of the whole rotor vanishes. H·Z·H = X follows by hand from the Pauli relations above; the page only checks it numerically.
