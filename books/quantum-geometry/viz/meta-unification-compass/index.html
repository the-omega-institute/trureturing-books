第三十二章下半篇的两个画面：一只诊断立方体，一座四元数黄金桥。前者是本书的诠释示意，后者的每个数都在 ℤ[φ] 里精确算出；本页是计算与诠释示意，不是证明。

  1. 罗盘。三根轴照 §32.3 的定义：θ 是代数阶数（0 端是一维内积的点线，1 端是二维外积的面积），η 是几何分辨率（0 端是可以无限细分的光滑流形，1 端是有限门的多面体晶格），λ 是观察者的位置（0 端是球外全知的点 C，1 端是场内有限记忆的观察者）。每根轴分三段：§32.4 总表在“它摸到的”“它残留的外部点 C”两栏里点明偏向哪一端，就落在那一端的三分之一里；没点明或两端都有，就落在中间三分之一里，几家挤在同一格时在格内错开。坐标只表示落在哪一段，段内的位置没有意义。四大阵营照 §32.4 配对：弦论与圈量子引力，AdS/CFT 与扭量—振幅多面体，非对易几何与 E₈ 晶格同 Wolfram 超图与因果集，朗兰兹纲领与同伦类型论同熵引力、主动推理与惠勒自激回路。这些位置都是本书的比较诊断坐标（MODEL），不贬损任何学科，也不替任何学科的结论背书；本书没有形式化地取代哪一家，“本书的位置”只是本书对自己的诠释。§32.4 给各阵营附上的冻结定理见正文，本页不复述。
  2. 二元二十面体群 2I。±1、±i、±j、±k（8 个），(±1 ± i ± j ± k)/2（16 个），以及 (0, ±1, ±φ⁻¹, ±φ)/2 的偶置换（96 个），共 120 个单位四元数，其中前 24 个是 Hurwitz 单位。页面在 ℤ[φ] 里精确核对它们对乘法封闭。投影去掉实部 w：同一个 |w| 的元素落在同一层球面上，层的大小依次是 1、12、20、12、30、12、20、12、1；实部为负的一半落在与实部为正的一半相同的位置上，画成空心圈。第一段（按实部一层层画出 2I）里，页面把每一层的位置连成凸包：12 个位置围成正二十面体，20 个围成正十二面体，30 个围成截半二十面体（顶点、棱、面的个数由页面从这些位置算出）；选中元素所在的那一层画成实体，其余各层只画出棱。
  3. E₈。2I 张成艾柯西安环 𝓘。约化范数 Nrd q = q q̄ = a + bφ（a、b ∈ ℤ），Q(q) = 2(a + b) = (2φ/√5)‖q‖² + (2φ⁻¹/√5)‖qσ‖²，σ 把 φ 换成 1 − φ。Q = 2 的元素恰有 240 个：2I 的 120 个（Nrd = 1）与 φ⁻¹·2I 的 120 个（Nrd = 2 − φ）。页面核对：240 个根两两的 B(x, y) = (Q(x + y) − Q(x) − Q(y))/2 都是 −2…2 的整数，每个根恰有 56 个 B = 1 的邻居，𝓘 的一组 ℤ-基的格拉姆行列式为 1。舞台按带权坐标 (√(2φ/√5) q, √(2φ⁻¹/√5) qσ) 画，所以 ‖∥‖² + ‖⊥‖² 就是 Q。

Two pictures from the second half of Chapter 32: a diagnostic cube and a golden quaternion bridge. The first is an interpretive sketch of this book; every number in the second is computed exactly in ℤ[φ]. The page is computation and interpretive sketch, not a proof.

  1. Compass. The three axes follow §32.3: θ is algebraic grade (0 is the points and lines of the one-dimensional inner product, 1 the area of the two-dimensional outer product), η is geometric resolution (0 a smooth manifold that can be divided without end, 1 the polytope lattice of a finite number of gates), and λ is where the observer stands (0 the all-seeing point C outside the sphere, 1 an observer with finite memory inside the field). Each axis has three bands: when the §32.4 table says, in its columns “what it touched” and “the external point C it keeps”, which end a programme leans to, it sits in that end's third; when the table names neither end or both, it sits in the middle third, and several programmes sharing a cell are spread out inside it. A coordinate says only which band; the place inside a band means nothing. The four camps are paired as in §32.4: string theory with loop quantum gravity; AdS/CFT with twistors and the amplituhedron; noncommutative geometry and the E₈ lattice with Wolfram hypergraphs and causal sets; the Langlands programme and homotopy type theory with entropic gravity, active inference and Wheeler's self-excited circuit. All these places are this book's comparative diagnostic coordinates (MODEL); they belittle no field and vouch for no field's conclusions, the book does not formally replace any programme, and “the book's place” is only its reading of itself. The frozen theorems §32.4 attaches to each camp are in the chapter; this page does not repeat them.
  2. The binary icosahedral group 2I. ±1, ±i, ±j, ±k (8), (±1 ± i ± j ± k)/2 (16), and the even permutations of (0, ±1, ±φ⁻¹, ±φ)/2 (96): 120 unit quaternions, the first 24 of them the Hurwitz units. The page checks exactly in ℤ[φ] that they are closed under multiplication. The projection drops the real part w: elements with the same |w| lie on one spherical shell, and the shells hold 1, 12, 20, 12, 30, 12, 20, 12 and 1 elements; the half with negative real part lands on the same places as the half with positive real part and is drawn as open rings. In the first leg (2I drawn shell by shell) the page joins the places of each shell into their convex hull: 12 places enclose an icosahedron, 20 a dodecahedron, 30 an icosidodecahedron (the numbers of vertices, edges and faces are computed by the page from the places); the shell of the selected element is drawn solid and the other shells by their edges.
  3. E₈. 2I spans the icosian ring 𝓘. The reduced norm is Nrd q = q q̄ = a + bφ (a, b ∈ ℤ) and Q(q) = 2(a + b) = (2φ/√5)‖q‖² + (2φ⁻¹/√5)‖qσ‖², where σ replaces φ by 1 − φ. Exactly 240 elements have Q = 2: the 120 of 2I (Nrd = 1) and the 120 of φ⁻¹·2I (Nrd = 2 − φ). The page checks that B(x, y) = (Q(x + y) − Q(x) − Q(y))/2 between any two of the 240 roots is an integer from −2 to 2, that each root has exactly 56 neighbours with B = 1, and that a ℤ-basis of 𝓘 has Gram determinant 1. The stage uses the weighted coordinates (√(2φ/√5) q, √(2φ⁻¹/√5) qσ), so ‖∥‖² + ‖⊥‖² is Q itself.
  4. The pure part and E∥ ⊕ E⊥. Sixty roots have real part zero (30 units and 30 golden shrunken copies). The pure submodule Im 𝓘 has rank 6, so as an additive group it is ℤ⁶; the page also computes that with Q its Gram determinant is 4 and its shortest vectors are exactly these 60: it is the lattice D₆ (the half of ℤ⁶ with even coordinate sum), not ℤ⁶ with the standard inner product. The last leg turns the plane of projection from E∥ to E⊥: the 30 units fall from the outer shell to the inner one, the golden copies rise from the inner shell to the outer one, and ‖∥‖² + ‖⊥‖² stays 2 for every one of them.
