理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

N 项里有一个标记项，初始态是均匀叠加 |s⟩，它落在目标上的概率 p = 1/N。把 |s⟩ 分成目标方向 W 与其余各项的均匀叠加 B，|s⟩ = sin θ·W + cos θ·B，sin θ = 1/√N。整个搜索都发生在 W、B 张成的平面里；舞台上竖轴是 W，横轴是 B。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 第一面镜子 I − 2P。它把 W 方向反号、B 方向不动：箭头关于横轴（失败轴）照一次镜子。动画里箭头尖沿竖直的虚线滑到横轴另一侧。
  2. 第二面镜子 2|s⟩⟨s| − I。它保留 |s⟩ 方向、把与 |s⟩ 垂直的方向反号：箭头关于青色虚线（|s⟩ 那条线）再照一次。
  3. 两面镜子合成转动。两条镜线夹角 θ，先后照两次等于转 2θ（紫色的弧）：与横轴成 α 角的箭头先到 −α，再到 2θ − (−α) = α + 2θ。从 θ 出发，第 n 步后停在 (2n + 1)θ，这正是冻结定理里的 sin((2n + 1)θ)·W + cos((2n + 1)θ)·B。
  4. 影子。箭头在竖轴上的影子（青色）是成功振幅 sin((2n + 1)θ)，它的平方是这一步测到标记项的概率；横轴上的品红是还留在失败方向上的振幅。右边的柱子是每一步的成功概率，琥珀色的框是当前这一步。
  5. 整数步与过冲。步数只能取整数，(2n + 1)θ 一格一格地跳。N = 4 时 θ = 30°，一步到 90°，成功率 1；N = 8 时 θ ≈ 20.70°，一步 25/32 = 0.78125，两步 121/128 = 0.9453125，第三步转到约 144.9°，掉到约 0.33。N 大时 θ ≈ 1/√N，最好的 n 在 π√N/4 附近，下面的小图把逐个比较得到的最佳步数与 π√N/4 画在一起。

Among N items one is marked, and the initial state is the uniform superposition |s⟩, which finds the target with probability p = 1/N. Split |s⟩ into the target direction W and the uniform superposition B of all other items: |s⟩ = sin θ·W + cos θ·B with sin θ = 1/√N. The whole search happens in the plane of W and B; on the stage the vertical axis is W and the horizontal axis is B. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The first mirror I − 2P. It flips the W direction and keeps B: the arrow is mirrored in the horizontal (failure) axis. In the animation the tip slides down the vertical dashed line to the other side of the axis.
  2. The second mirror 2|s⟩⟨s| − I. It keeps the |s⟩ direction and flips the direction perpendicular to it: the arrow is mirrored in the cyan dashed line, the line of |s⟩.
  3. Two mirrors make a turn. The two mirror lines meet at the angle θ, and two reflections in a row turn by 2θ (the violet arc): an arrow at the angle α from the horizontal axis goes to −α and then to 2θ − (−α) = α + 2θ. Starting from θ, after n steps it sits at (2n + 1)θ, which is the frozen theorem's sin((2n + 1)θ)·W + cos((2n + 1)θ)·B.
  4. Shadows. The arrow's shadow on the vertical axis (cyan) is the success amplitude sin((2n + 1)θ), and its square is the probability of finding the marked item after this step; the magenta on the horizontal axis is the amplitude still left in the failure direction. The bars on the right are the success probabilities step by step, with the amber frame on the current step.
  5. Whole steps and overshoot. The step count is a whole number, so (2n + 1)θ jumps. For N = 4, θ = 30° and one step reaches 90°, success 1; for N = 8, θ ≈ 20.70°, one step gives 25/32 = 0.78125, two steps 121/128 = 0.9453125, and the third turns to about 144.9° and drops to about 0.33. For large N, θ ≈ 1/√N and the best n is near π√N/4; the small plot draws the best step count, found by comparing every n, against π√N/4.
