理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

第十四章的三比特位翻转重复码：V|0⟩ = |000⟩，V|1⟩ = |111⟩，逻辑态 α|0⟩ + β|1⟩ 编成 α|000⟩ + β|111⟩。两道校验 g₁ = Z₁Z₂、g₂ = Z₂Z₃ 只问相邻两个比特一不一致，从不问 α、β。八个基态按两道校验的读数分成四间两两正交的房。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 翻一个比特，灯指出它。Xk 与跨在第 k 个比特上的校验反对易：g₁ 管第 1、2 个，g₂ 管第 2、3 个。亮法 (−1,+1)、(−1,−1)、(+1,−1) 分别指向第 1、2、3 个比特，与例题第二步的表一致。灯亮不亮，由泡利字乘法吐出的交叉相位决定：pauli_word_mul（LEAN ✓）；模 2 辛奇偶的读法是正文由它推出的。
  2. 错误把态整个搬进另一间房。四间房是 Sj = EjV（E ∈ {I, X₁, X₂, X₃}），两两正交。一个 X 错误把 |000⟩、|111⟩ 两根柱子一起搬进同一间房，α、β 原样跟着走，别的房一点不沾。
  3. 拼回原态。解码映射 Σi Si*(·)Si 把每间房里的东西拉回逻辑空间，房与房之间的交叉项全被切掉：对任意 σ、ρ，结果是 Tr(σ)·ρ：orthogonal_syndrome_recovery（LEAN ✓）。“σ 混合”取例题第四步的 σ = diag(½, ¼, ⅛, ⅛)：四个分支的柱子按权重分宽窄，回到码空间后并排拼成一整根。
  4. 翻两个，灯指错。X₁X₂ 的灯与 X₃ 相同，解码器翻回第 3 个比特，合起来是 X₁X₂X₃：逻辑翻转，|000⟩ 与 |111⟩ 的柱子对调。正文说这套码只纠至多一个 X 翻转。
  5. 翻相位，灯不亮。Z₁ 与 g₁、g₂ 都对易，两盏灯都不亮，态留在码空间里，可 |111⟩ 的柱子翻到了轴下：|+L⟩ 变成正交的 |−L⟩。纠任意单比特错误要更大的码（五比特码、Steane 码、Shor 码），这是文献结果，未在本书形式化。

Chapter 14's three-qubit bit-flip repetition code: V|0⟩ = |000⟩, V|1⟩ = |111⟩, so the logical state α|0⟩ + β|1⟩ is encoded as α|000⟩ + β|111⟩. The two checks g₁ = Z₁Z₂ and g₂ = Z₂Z₃ only ask whether neighbouring qubits agree, never about α or β. By the readings of the two checks the eight basis states fall into four pairwise orthogonal rooms. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Flip one qubit and the lights point at it. Xk anticommutes with the checks over qubit k: g₁ covers qubits 1 and 2, g₂ covers 2 and 3. The patterns (−1,+1), (−1,−1), (+1,−1) point at qubits 1, 2, 3, as in the table of step 2 of the example. Whether a light goes on is decided by the cross phase that the product of Pauli words throws out: pauli_word_mul (LEAN ✓); reading it as a symplectic parity mod 2 is the chapter's consequence of it.
  2. The error moves the state, whole, into another room. The four rooms are Sj = EjV (E ∈ {I, X₁, X₂, X₃}), pairwise orthogonal. An X error carries both bars, |000⟩ and |111⟩, into one room together; α and β ride along unchanged, and no other room gets anything.
  3. Adding up to the state again. The decoding map Σi Si*(·)Si pulls the contents of every room back to the logical space and cuts away every cross term between rooms: for every σ and ρ the result is Tr(σ)·ρ: orthogonal_syndrome_recovery (LEAN ✓). “σ mixture” takes σ = diag(½, ¼, ⅛, ⅛) from step 4 of the example: the bars of the four branches are as wide as their weights, and back in the code space they line up side by side into one whole bar.
  4. Flip two and the lights point wrong. X₁X₂ lights the same checks as X₃, so the decoder flips qubit 3 back, and together that is X₁X₂X₃: a logical flip, with the bars of |000⟩ and |111⟩ swapped. The chapter says that this code corrects at most one X flip.
  5. Flip a phase and no light goes on. Z₁ commutes with g₁ and g₂, both lights stay dark and the state stays in the code space, yet the bar of |111⟩ has flipped below the axis: |+L⟩ has become the orthogonal |−L⟩. Correcting arbitrary single-qubit errors needs larger codes (five-qubit, Steane, Shor), a literature result not formalized in this book.
