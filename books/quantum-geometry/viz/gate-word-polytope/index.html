理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

舞台是布洛赫球。从北极 ez 出发，长度不超过 L 的 {H, T} 门字（含空字）把它送到有限个点 𝒮L（青色），时间轴是 L。青色的面是它们的欧氏凸包 PL = conv(𝒮L)；琥珀色是目标方向，品红是它到 𝒮L 的缺口。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 可达点。按正文的转动 RH(x, y, z) = (z, −y, x) 与 RT(x, y, z) = ((x − y)/√2, (x + y)/√2, z)，逐层得到 𝒮L = 𝒮L−1 ∪ RH(𝒮L−1) ∪ RT(𝒮L−1)。点数依次是 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 19, …，远小于上界 2L+1 − 1（H² = I、北极是 T 的不动点等关系造成碰撞）。每个点标出它第一次出现时的门字，按算子乘积书写，最右边的门先作用。
  2. 多面体。L = 0, 1, 2 时 PL 是单点、线段、三角形；L = 3 第一次是立体，五个顶点都满足 x ≥ 0、z ≥ 0，球心不在里面；L = 4 时球心恰在一个面上；从 L = 5 起球心在内部，这时把棱沿半径投到球面就是一张大圆弧网格（测地多面体）。PL 本身的面始终是平直的弦面。
  3. 缺口。目标方向 n 的支撑高度 h = maxs ∈ 𝒮L n·s；n 到最近可达点的角度是 arccos h，平直多面体与球皮之间的径向间隙是 1 − h。最坏方向的角度 εL = maxn arccos h 就是正文的覆盖半径：凸包每个面的外法向是一个不含任何点的球冠中心，εL 取其中最大的一个。εL 随 L 不增，而 𝒮L 有限，所以 εL > 0 对一切有限 L 成立。
  4. 与连续两轴定理的分别。连续轴圆上的有限字可以精确到达任意旋转：two_axis_finite_word（LEAN ✓）；它不给字长，也不能推出离散门集 {H, T} 的任何性质。离散门集的渐近字长 O(logc(1/ε)) 是 Solovay–Kitaev 定理，文献结果，未在本书形式化。单态的覆盖缺口会放过绕初态的转动，不能代替整个门的编译误差。
  5. 与割圆的分工。sphere-escape 的割圆卡讲平面里的内接多边形与圆之间的缝；本页讲有限门字在球面上的落点集与离目标的最近距离。

书中：有限门字与三维割圆多面体 · L ≤ 3 的五顶点验算 · Solovay–Kitaev 文献注记

The stage is the Bloch sphere. From the north pole ez, the {H, T} words of length at most L (the empty word included) reach finitely many points 𝒮L (cyan), and the rail is L. The cyan faces are their Euclidean convex hull PL = conv(𝒮L); amber is the target direction and magenta its gap to 𝒮L. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Reachable points. With the text's rotations RH(x, y, z) = (z, −y, x) and RT(x, y, z) = ((x − y)/√2, (x + y)/√2, z), layer by layer, 𝒮L = 𝒮L−1 ∪ RH(𝒮L−1) ∪ RT(𝒮L−1). The counts are 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 19, …, far below the bound 2L+1 − 1 (relations such as H² = I and the pole being fixed by T make words collide). Each point carries the word with which it first appears, written as an operator product: the rightmost gate acts first.
  2. The polytope. For L = 0, 1, 2, PL is a point, a segment and a triangle; at L = 3 it is solid for the first time, its five vertices all with x ≥ 0 and z ≥ 0, so the centre is not inside; at L = 4 the centre lies exactly on a face; from L = 5 on the centre is inside, and projecting the edges radially onto the sphere gives a mesh of great-circle arcs (a geodesic polytope). The faces of PL itself are always flat chords.
  3. The hole. The support height of a target direction n is h = maxs ∈ 𝒮L n·s; the angle from n to the nearest reachable point is arccos h, and the radial gap between the flat polytope and the sphere is 1 − h. The angle of the worst direction, εL = maxn arccos h, is the covering radius of the text: the outer normal of each face of the hull is the centre of a cap that holds no point, and εL is the largest of them. εL does not increase with L, and 𝒮L is finite, so εL > 0 for every finite L.
  4. Not the continuous two-axis theorem. Finite words on continuous axis circles reach every rotation exactly: two_axis_finite_word (LEAN ✓); it gives no word length and implies nothing about the discrete gate set {H, T}. The asymptotic word length O(logc(1/ε)) of a discrete gate set is the Solovay–Kitaev theorem, a literature result not formalized in this book. The covering hole of one state lets rotations about that state through, so it does not stand for the compilation error of a whole gate.
  5. Not the polygon of Liu Hui. The cards of sphere-escape on cutting the circle show the gap between an inscribed polygon and the circle in the plane; this page shows where finite gate words land on the sphere and how close they come to a target.
