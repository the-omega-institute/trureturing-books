理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

舞台上方是实轴 1 < s ≤ 4 上的两条曲线：墨色的 ζ(s) 与青色的、只用前 K 个素数的乘积 ∏(1 − p⁻ˢ)⁻¹；两者之间是品红的缺口。右边的放大镜把当前 s 处的缺口按整数拆开。下方是整数 1 到 96：青色的格子出现在乘积的展开里（素因子全在清单上），品红的格子不在。时间轴把素数一个个加进清单。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 依据的章节。第二十二章 · 22.1 一根素数轴上的等比级数、欧拉乘积与前三个素数的有限筛。
  2. 已验证的定理。Re(s) > 1 上全体素数的欧拉乘积等于 ζ(s)：riemann_zeta_euler_product；狄利克雷级数等于欧拉乘积：riemann_zeta_dirichlet_sum_eq_euler_product；s = 1 处的留数为 1：riemann_zeta_residue_one（均为 LEAN ✓）。Lean 的陈述对复数 s 成立；页面只取实数 s。
  3. 页面算的是什么。ζ(s) 由欧拉—麦克劳林求和算出（前 11 项加 7 个伯努利修正），乘积逐个素数相乘。放大镜里自下而上依次是品红格子里的整数 n 的 n⁻ˢ，最底下一段是下一个素数 pK+1⁻ˢ，余下的整数合成最上面的浅色一段。主图的纵轴按“值 − 1”取对数刻度，好让 s = 2 到 4 之间的曲线不挤在一起；刻度上写的是值本身。“差不到 1% 要几个素数”逐个素数累乘，直到乘积达到 ζ(s) 的 99%；超出 2 000 000 以内的 148 933 个素数时只标“超出”。这些都是数值（NUM）。
  4. 文献结果与开放问题。余项等于最大素因子大于 pK 的整数之和、在实数 s > 1 上大于 pK+1⁻ˢ，以及 ζ(2) = π²/6，是文献结果，未在本书形式化（ARGUED）。在临界带 0 < Re(s) < 1 里欧拉乘积不再收敛，零点是否都在 Re(s) = 1/2 上（黎曼猜想）仍是开放问题；本页不画临界带。

The top of the stage shows two curves over the real axis 1 < s ≤ 4: the ink curve ζ(s) and the cyan product ∏(1 − p⁻ˢ)⁻¹ over the first K primes only, with the magenta gap between them. The magnifier on the right takes the gap at the current s apart, integer by integer. Below are the integers 1 to 96: a cyan cell appears in the product's expansion (all its prime factors are on the list), a magenta one does not. The rail puts the primes on the list one at a time.   1. The chapter behind it. Chapter 22 · 22.1, the geometric series along one prime axis, the Euler product and the finite sieve of the first three primes.
  2. Verified theorems. On Re(s) > 1 the Euler product over all primes equals ζ(s): riemann_zeta_euler_product; the Dirichlet series equals the Euler product: riemann_zeta_dirichlet_sum_eq_euler_product; the residue at s = 1 is 1: riemann_zeta_residue_one (all LEAN ✓). Lean states them for complex s; the page takes real s only.
  3. What the page computes. ζ(s) comes from Euler–Maclaurin summation (eleven terms and seven Bernoulli corrections); the product is multiplied out prime by prime. From the bottom up, the magnifier stacks n⁻ˢ for the integers n of the magenta cells; the lowest slice is the next prime, pK+1⁻ˢ, and the remaining integers make up the pale slice on top. The main plot's vertical axis is logarithmic in “value − 1”, so that the curves between s = 2 and 4 do not crowd together; the ticks show the values themselves. “Primes needed for 1%” multiplies prime by prime until the product reaches 99% of ζ(s); beyond the 148 933 primes below 2 000 000 the page only says “beyond”. All of this is numerical (NUM).
  4. Literature and open problems. That the remainder is the sum over the integers whose largest prime factor exceeds pK, that it exceeds pK+1⁻ˢ for real s > 1, and that ζ(2) = π²/6 are literature results, not formalized in this book (ARGUED). In the critical strip 0 < Re(s) < 1 the Euler product no longer converges, and whether every zero there lies on Re(s) = 1/2 (the Riemann hypothesis) is still open; the page does not draw the critical strip.

开放问题Open problem

OPEN黎曼猜想：临界带 0 < Re(s) < 1 内 ζ 的每个零点都在 Re(s) = 1/2 上。本书把它写成猜想，不附 Lean 引用；本页只画欧拉乘积收敛的实轴 s > 1。The Riemann hypothesis: every zero of ζ in the critical strip 0 < Re(s) < 1 lies on Re(s) = 1/2. The book states it as a conjecture, without a Lean reference; this page draws only the real axis s > 1, where the Euler product converges.
