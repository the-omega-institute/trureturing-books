这是数值沙盘，不是证明。每个数都由页面当场算出（NUM）；每条 Lean 定理只在依据栏写明的那一个实例上演示；把四种力读成同一块弧是本书的模型（MODEL）。

  1. 极小圆弧体。N、A = Ry(ε)N、B = Rx(−ε)N 围成两腰为 ε 的直角球面三角形，从球心 O 张成一块受光的小弧体。ε = 45° 时角超 Ω(ε) = 2 arctan(tan²(ε/2)) = 19.47°，格拉姆行列式 det G(N, A, B) = sin⁴ε = 0.25。它的 1 条弧、2 维墙、3 维体在模型里分别对应 U(1)、SU(2)、SU(3)。
  2. U(1)：盲面里的相位。相位 eîqφ 只在与可见方向正交的盲面 î 里转动；两次 U(1) 转动可交换，[U₁, U₂] = 0。+q 与 −q 的相位相乘为 1，包住两者的高斯面上通量之和为 0：中性。能标取 1 MeV 时页面读出低能极限 α(0) = 1/137.036，不在 MZ 以下跑动耦合。
  3. SU(2)：双覆盖。U(θ) = cos(θ/2) − i sin(θ/2) n·σ：U(2π) = −I，U(4π) = +I。弱作用由 mW = 80.377 GeV 屏蔽：探针距离 r = ħc/E 处汤川因子 e−mWr = e−mW/E，E = 100 GeV 时为 0.448；屏蔽长度 λW = ħc/mW = 2.455 × 10⁻³ fm。每个有质量矢量 2 个横向极化加 1 个吃进的戈德斯通模，2 + 1 = 3。单圈 α₂(100 GeV) = 0.03374（α₂(MZ) = 0.0338）。
  4. SU(3)：色锁与弦。色三轴 (eR, eG, eB) 在 U ∈ SU(3) 下转动，det U = 1，所以三轴撑开的体积恒为 1。页面画实转动 SO(3) ⊂ SU(3)，另核对一个带相位的 SU(3) 元素。把一个夸克拉开，色通量收成一根管，V(r) = σr，σ = 0.18 GeV² = 0.912 GeV/fm，r = 1.20 fm 时 V = 1.09 GeV。模型把高能端的胞口径 ε(E) 取成 Ω(ε(E)) = Ω(45°)·ln(1 GeV/Λ)/ln(E/Λ)（Λ = 0.2 GeV，E ≥ 1 GeV），Ω(ε) → 0 示意渐近自由。
  5. 雷杰铰链与钟载折射。N = 6 个点两两相连的 K₆ 有 15 根杆，比三维刚性所需 3N − 6 = 12 多 ΔE(6) = 3；ΔE(N) = (N − 3)(N − 4)/2 只在 N = 3、4 为零。四个正四面体绕一条公共铰链，二面角之和 4 arccos(1/3) = 282.12°，留下角亏 δh = 77.88°。模型的钟载 η(r) = η₀r₀/r 让 dτ/dt = √(1 − η)，慢速波包按 a = −c²∇ln(dτ/dt) 被弯向铰链；+q 与 −q 的玻恩载荷都是 cos²θ，只相加。只用钟率的折射给出的是牛顿极限；广义相对论对光的偏折是它的两倍。
  6. 普朗克合流。αG(E) = (E/EP)²：电子质量处 1.75 × 10⁻⁴⁵，α(0)/αG(me) = 4.17 × 10⁴²，EP 处为 1。单圈三条规范曲线两两相交于 1.0 × 10¹³、2.4 × 10¹⁴、1.0 × 10¹⁷ GeV，交点处 α = 0.021–0.025（10⁻² 量级），彼此最近处仍差 Δα−1 = 3.68：只近交，不汇于一点。αG 在 1018.2–1018.3 GeV 穿过这条规范带。

This is a numerical sandbox, not a proof. Every number is computed on the page (NUM); each Lean theorem is shown only on the one instance named in the evidence panel; reading the four forces as one arc is the book's model (MODEL).

  1. The minimal arc-trihedron. N, A = Ry(ε)N and B = Rx(−ε)N bound a right spherical triangle with legs ε, and from the centre O they span a small lit arc body. At ε = 45° its excess is Ω(ε) = 2 arctan(tan²(ε/2)) = 19.47° and the Gram determinant is det G(N, A, B) = sin⁴ε = 0.25. In the model its 1 arc, 2D wall and 3D body stand for U(1), SU(2) and SU(3).
  2. U(1): phase in the blind plane. The phase eîqφ turns only in the blind plane î orthogonal to the visible direction; two U(1) turns commute, [U₁, U₂] = 0. The phases of +q and −q multiply to 1, and the flux through a Gauss surface around both adds to 0: neutral. At 1 MeV the page reads the low-energy limit α(0) = 1/137.036 and does not run couplings below MZ.
  3. SU(2): the double cover. U(θ) = cos(θ/2) − i sin(θ/2) n·σ: U(2π) = −I, U(4π) = +I. The weak force is screened by mW = 80.377 GeV: at the probe distance r = ħc/E the Yukawa factor is e−mWr = e−mW/E, 0.448 at E = 100 GeV; the screening length is λW = ħc/mW = 2.455 × 10⁻³ fm. Each massive vector has 2 transverse polarizations plus 1 eaten Goldstone mode, 2 + 1 = 3. One loop gives α₂(100 GeV) = 0.03374 (α₂(MZ) = 0.0338).
  4. SU(3): colour lock and string. The colour triad (eR, eG, eB) turns under U ∈ SU(3) with det U = 1, so the volume it spans stays 1. The page draws a real turn in SO(3) ⊂ SU(3) and also checks an element of SU(3) with phases. Pull one quark away and the colour flux gathers into a tube, V(r) = σr with σ = 0.18 GeV² = 0.912 GeV/fm: V = 1.09 GeV at r = 1.20 fm. The model takes the cell aperture at high energy as Ω(ε(E)) = Ω(45°)·ln(1 GeV/Λ)/ln(E/Λ) (Λ = 0.2 GeV, E ≥ 1 GeV), so that Ω(ε) → 0 pictures asymptotic freedom.
  5. Regge hinge and clock-load refraction. K₆, all pairs of N = 6 points joined, has 15 bars, ΔE(6) = 3 more than the 3N − 6 = 12 that rigidity in three dimensions needs; ΔE(N) = (N − 3)(N − 4)/2 vanishes only at N = 3, 4. Four regular tetrahedra round one shared hinge have dihedral angles adding up to 4 arccos(1/3) = 282.12° and leave the deficit δh = 77.88°. The model's clock load η(r) = η₀r₀/r gives dτ/dt = √(1 − η), and a slow packet is bent towards the hinge by a = −c²∇ln(dτ/dt); the Born loads of +q and −q are both cos²θ and only add. Refraction by the clock rate alone gives the Newtonian limit; general relativity bends light twice as much.
  6. Planck convergence. αG(E) = (E/EP)²: 1.75 × 10⁻⁴⁵ at the electron mass, α(0)/αG(me) = 4.17 × 10⁴², and 1 at EP. The three one-loop gauge curves cross pairwise at 1.0 × 10¹³, 2.4 × 10¹⁴ and 1.0 × 10¹⁷ GeV with α = 0.021–0.025 at the crossings (of order 10⁻²), and where they come closest they still differ by Δα−1 = 3.68: they come close and do not meet at one point. αG crosses this gauge band at 1018.2–1018.3 GeV.
