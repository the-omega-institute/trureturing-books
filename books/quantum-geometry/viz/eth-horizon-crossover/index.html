理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

第 30 章用一组跨越宇宙尺度的数字说明：一个渐近意义上更好的上界，可以在一切物理上可及的规模上都还没有赢。本页把这组数字画成曲线，并画出正文拿来对照的两条已验证定理。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 指数率。运行时间写成 2^{r(n)·n}，r(n) 是每个变量付出的指数。PPSZ 的 r ≈ 0.386 是常数；示意曲线 r(n) = c·log₂log₂n/√log₂n 趋于零，但降得极慢：c = 1 时，n = 2¹⁶ 处它才降回 1（与穷举相同），n = 2²⁶⁶（宇宙原子数）处是 0.494，要到 n ≈ 2⁵⁵⁸ 才与 0.386 相交。常数 c 决定一切：c 小于约 0.78 时交叉点落在 2²⁶⁶ 以内，c 小于约 0.364 时曲线处处低于 0.386。
  2. 桥图。两个节点各带三个矩阵：M₀, M₁, M₂ 是 a×b 的，N₀, N₁, N₂ 是 d×c 的，流矩阵 flow = Σᵣ Mᵣ ⊗ Nᵣ 有 a·d 行、b·c 列。把 M、N 各放在输入或输出一侧，四种放法给出整张图的四道割：行数 a·d、列数 b·c、割断腿 a、三条桥与腿 c 的 3·a·c，以及割断腿 b、三条桥与腿 d 的 3·b·d；秩不超过其中每一道。Lean 证明了秩的最大值不超过前三道的最小值 QMinCut = min(3ac, ad, bc)，并且在锥域 a² + b² ≤ 3ab、c² + d² ≤ 3cd 内恰好等于 min(a·d, b·c)；第四道 3·b·d 的上界是初等的矩阵分解，不在冻结定理里，a ≤ b 且 c ≤ d 时它不会更小。舞台上是一次随机整数抽样，给出的是这一次的秩，可能低于最大秩：流矩阵下方的奇异值在它之后归零。
  3. 模态。一条非负、总和为 T 的谱里，不小于 θ 的项不可能超过 T/θ 个，否则它们的和就超过 T。舞台上品红的竖墙立在 T/θ 处，青色的柱子永远到不了墙的右边。

Chapter 30 uses a set of numbers on the scale of the universe to make one point: a bound that is better asymptotically can still be losing at every size that is physically within reach. This page draws those numbers as a curve, and draws the two verified theorems the text sets beside them. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The exponent rate. Write the running time as 2^{r(n)·n}, where r(n) is the exponent paid per variable. For PPSZ r ≈ 0.386 is a constant; the schematic curve r(n) = c·log₂log₂n/√log₂n tends to zero, but very slowly: for c = 1 it is back at 1 (the rate of brute force) only at n = 2¹⁶, it is 0.494 at n = 2²⁶⁶ (the atoms of the universe), and it meets 0.386 only at n ≈ 2⁵⁵⁸. The constant c decides everything: below about 0.78 the crossover falls within 2²⁶⁶, and below about 0.364 the curve lies under 0.386 everywhere.
  2. The bridge graph. Two nodes carry three matrices each: M₀, M₁, M₂ are a×b and N₀, N₁, N₂ are d×c, and the flow matrix flow = Σᵣ Mᵣ ⊗ Nᵣ has a·d rows and b·c columns. Putting M and N each on the input or the output side gives the four cuts of the whole graph: the a·d rows, the b·c columns, 3·a·c through leg a, the three bridges and leg c, and 3·b·d through leg b, the three bridges and leg d; the rank exceeds none of them. Lean proves that the greatest rank never exceeds the least of the first three, QMinCut = min(3ac, ad, bc), and that inside the cone a² + b² ≤ 3ab, c² + d² ≤ 3cd it is exactly min(a·d, b·c); the bound by the fourth, 3·b·d, is an elementary matrix factorization outside the frozen theorem, and when a ≤ b and c ≤ d it is never smaller. The stage shows one random integer draw and the rank of that draw, which may fall below the greatest rank: the singular values under the flow matrix vanish after it.
  3. The modes. In a nonnegative spectrum of total T, no more than T/θ terms can reach θ, or their sum would exceed T. On the stage a magenta wall stands at T/θ, and the cyan bars never get to its right.
