理论接口 · 黎曼路径积分与素数阶梯Theory interface · the Riemann path integral and the prime staircase

这是数值沙盘，不是证明。每个数都由页面当场算出（NUM）；每条 Lean 定理只在依据栏写明的那一个实例上演示。

  1. 舞台。地面是复平面 ℂ（实部向右、虚部向里），原点 O 在中央。整数 n 的箭头 n−s = n−σe−it ln n 首尾相接，第 n 个顶点 Sn = Σk≤n k−s 抬到高度 ln n（作用量，由 n 的分解算出）。俯瞰就是经典的部分和螺旋；侧看就是作用量的刻度。
  2. 延拓就是螺旋的轴。0 < σ < 1 时部分和发散，但欧拉–麦克劳林给出 SN − ζ(s) ≈ N1−s/(1 − s) + N−s/2：链顶绕着过 ζ(s) 的竖轴转，半径 N1−σ/|1 − s| 越来越大。琥珀色的轴与地上的琥珀珠就是 ζ(s)，由交错 η 级数算出（Lean 担保部分和收敛到 (1 − 21−s)ζ(s)，页面用 Borwein 权重加速），链顶的墨色虚线是余项 ζ(s) − SN。σ > 1 时链自己收敛，欧拉乘积给出同一点。
  3. 双壳。地上围着 O 的青色环半径 p−σ（p = 2, 3, 5, 7），品红环半径 p−(1−σ)，是 s 与 1 − s 两侧同一素数的箭头长度。σ = ½ 时两两重合为 p−1/2；σ = 0.62 时 2−0.62 = 0.6507、2−0.38 = 0.7684。这是本书的读法（MODEL）。
  4. 余维数。ξ(s) = ½ s(s − 1)π−s/2Γ(s/2)ζ(s) 满足 ξ(1 − s) = ξ(s) 与 ξ(1 − s̄) = conj ξ(s)，所以 ξ(½ + it) 是实数：沿临界线，零点是实函数的变号点。离开临界线要实部、虚部同时为零。这解释了零点为什么“容易”落在线上，但不证明它们都在线上：四次式 F 有同样的对称，零点却全在线外。
  5. 素数阶梯。显式公式 ψ₀(x) = x − Σρ xρ/ρ − ln 2π − ½ ln(1 − x−2)：每对零点 ½ ± iγₖ 贡献一支驻波 −2Re(xρ/ρ)，振幅 2√x/|ρ|。紫色曲面最后面一行只有主项，每向前一行多叠一对零点；最新的一行是琥珀色，紧贴在它前面的是青色的精确阶梯；前方地面上品红的残差 Σk≤K(−2Re(xρₖ/ρₖ)) 逼近青色锯齿 ψ₀(x) − 主项。条带小图量每个整数 n 处在 [n − ½, n + ½] 上的升高：精确值是 Λ(n)，素数幂处为 ln p，6、10、12 处为 0。

This is a numerical sandbox, not a proof. Every number is computed on the page (NUM); each Lean theorem is shown only on the one instance named in the evidence panel.

  1. The stage. The floor is the complex plane ℂ (real part to the right, imaginary part inwards), with the origin O in the middle. The arrows n−s = n−σe−it ln n of the integers are chained head to tail, and the n-th vertex Sn = Σk≤n k−s is lifted to the height ln n (the action, computed from the factorization of n). From above it is the classical spiral of partial sums; from the side, the scale of the actions.
  2. The continuation is the axis of the spiral. For 0 < σ < 1 the partial sums diverge, but Euler–Maclaurin gives SN − ζ(s) ≈ N1−s/(1 − s) + N−s/2: the top of the chain turns round the vertical axis through ζ(s) with a growing radius N1−σ/|1 − s|. The amber axis and the amber bead on the floor are ζ(s), computed from the alternating η series (Lean guarantees that its partial sums converge to (1 − 21−s)ζ(s); the page accelerates them with Borwein weights), and the dashed ink line at the top is the remainder ζ(s) − SN. For σ > 1 the chain converges by itself and the Euler product gives the same point.
  3. The two shells. Around O on the floor the cyan rings have radius p−σ (p = 2, 3, 5, 7) and the magenta rings p−(1−σ): the arrow lengths of the same prime on the side of s and of 1 − s. At σ = ½ they coincide in pairs at p−1/2; at σ = 0.62, 2−0.62 = 0.6507 and 2−0.38 = 0.7684. This is the reading of this book (MODEL).
  4. Codimension. ξ(s) = ½ s(s − 1)π−s/2Γ(s/2)ζ(s) satisfies ξ(1 − s) = ξ(s) and ξ(1 − s̄) = conj ξ(s), so ξ(½ + it) is real: along the critical line a zero is a sign change of a real function. Off the line the real and imaginary parts must vanish together. This explains why zeros fall on the line “easily”; it does not prove that all of them do: the quartic F has the same symmetries and all its zeros off the line.
  5. The prime staircase. The explicit formula ψ₀(x) = x − Σρ xρ/ρ − ln 2π − ½ ln(1 − x−2): each pair of zeros ½ ± iγₖ contributes a standing wave −2Re(xρ/ρ) of amplitude 2√x/|ρ|. The back row of the violet sheet is the main term alone, and each row towards the front adds one more pair of zeros; the newest row is amber with the exact cyan staircase just in front of it, and on the floor in front the magenta residual Σk≤K(−2Re(xρₖ/ρₖ)) approaches the cyan sawtooth ψ₀(x) − main term. The strip measures the rise across [n − ½, n + ½] at each integer n: exactly Λ(n), that is ln p at prime powers and 0 at 6, 10 and 12.
