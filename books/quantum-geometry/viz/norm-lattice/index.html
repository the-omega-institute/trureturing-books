舞台是两张整数格与它们的范数等值线。艾森斯坦格：整数对 (x, y) 画在复平面上的 x + y·eiπ/3，范数 x² + xy + y² 是到原点距离的平方，等值线是圆；换成整数坐标 (P, Q) = (x + (t + 1)y, y) 去读，同一批点、同一批圆就成了斜椭圆，二次型成了 Qform(t, P, Q)。黄金整数：g = a + bφ 画在 (g, ḡ) 平面上，范数 g·ḡ 是以原点和 g 为对角的矩形的有向面积，等值线是双曲线。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 依据的章节。第二十三章 · 二次范数与迹投影与艾森斯坦范数剪切不变性与黄金佩尔恒等式，例题 t = 2 与 (Lₙ, Fₙ) 的手算。
  2. 已验证的定理。剪切归约 Qform_eq_eisNorm、判别式恒为 −3 Qform_discriminant、值域相等 Qform_range_eq_eisNorm、黄金佩尔恒等式 pell_form_eq_four_mul_norm（均为 LEAN ✓）。
  3. 页面算的是什么。格子的面积：以 (P, Q) 的两根基矢 u₁ = 1、u₂ = −(t + ½) + i·√3/2 在复平面上张成的平行四边形面积是 √3/2，不随 t 变；Qform 的三个系数正是这两根基矢的内积 (|u₁|², 2u₁·u₂, |u₂|²)，判别式等于 −4 × 面积²，所以是 −3。乘 φ 把 (a, b) 变成 (b, a + b)，在 (g, ḡ) 平面上把 g 拉长 φ 倍、把 ḡ 缩成 −1/φ 倍：矩形翻到另一侧，面积的大小不变。从 1 出发乘 n 次，(2a + b, b) 依次是 (Lₙ, Fₙ)。这些由页面数值核对（NUM）。
  4. 示意与简化。非整数 t 的中间帧与两种坐标之间的过渡只是动画（MODEL）。用四元数范数在 ℚ 中定义 ℤ 是文献结果，未在本书形式化（ARGUED）；页面不涉及它。

The stage shows two integer lattices and the level curves of their norms. The Eisenstein lattice: an integer pair (x, y) is drawn in the complex plane at x + y·eiπ/3, its norm x² + xy + y² is the squared distance to the origin, and the level curves are circles; read in the integer coordinates (P, Q) = (x + (t + 1)y, y), the same points and the same circles become slanted ellipses, and the form becomes Qform(t, P, Q). The golden integers: g = a + bφ is drawn in the (g, ḡ) plane, its norm g·ḡ is the signed area of the rectangle with corners at the origin and at g, and the level curves are hyperbolas. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The chapter behind it. Chapter 23 · Quadratic norms and trace projections and Shear invariance of the Eisenstein norm and the golden Pell identity, with the worked example for t = 2 and (Lₙ, Fₙ).
  2. Verified theorems. The shear reduction Qform_eq_eisNorm, the discriminant −3 Qform_discriminant, the equal ranges Qform_range_eq_eisNorm and the golden Pell identity pell_form_eq_four_mul_norm (all LEAN ✓).
  3. What the page computes. The cell: the two basis vectors of (P, Q), u₁ = 1 and u₂ = −(t + ½) + i·√3/2, span a parallelogram of area √3/2 in the complex plane, whatever t is; the three coefficients of Qform are their inner products (|u₁|², 2u₁·u₂, |u₂|²), and the discriminant is −4 × area², hence −3. Multiplying by φ sends (a, b) to (b, a + b); in the (g, ḡ) plane it stretches g by φ and turns ḡ into −ḡ/φ, so the rectangle flips to the other side and keeps the size of its area. Starting from 1, n multiplications give (2a + b, b) = (Lₙ, Fₙ). The page checks these numerically (NUM).
  4. Models and simplifications. Frames with t between integers and the passage between the two coordinate systems are animation only (MODEL). Defining ℤ in ℚ with quaternion norms is a literature result, not formalized in this book (ARGUED); the page does not touch it.
