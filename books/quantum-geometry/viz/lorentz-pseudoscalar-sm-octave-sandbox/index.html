  1. 体的平方。立方体角上从同一点出发的三条棱 e₁、e₂、e₃ 两两正交。I₃ = e₁e₂e₃ 把线 e₃ 送到与它正交的面 e₁₂，再送回 −e₃：eI₃θe₃ = cos θ·e₃ + sin θ·e₁₂，θ = π 时为 −e₃。用泡利矩阵代表，I₃ = σ₁σ₂σ₃ = i·1，I₃² = −1。页面让 θ 随固有时前进：θ = π·cτ，cτ = ct·√(1 − β²)。
  2. 预算。每一步坐标时间 c dt 是一份预算：花在空间位移上的是 dx = β c dt，余下的是固有时 c dτ，(c dτ)² = c²dt² − dx²。舞台上的穹顶半径就是 c dt：位移尖端正上方的穹顶高度恰为 c dτ，到表盘边缘 β = 1 时为零，那里是光。若把 β 写成旁矢 p = 1 + βê，p p = 1 − β²；把多重矢量 I₃ + βê 平方，标量部是 −1 + β² = −(dτ/dt)²。
  3. 1·3·3·1。立方体角上过同一顶点的元素：1 个点、3 条棱、3 个面、1 个体，正是外代数 Λ⁰…Λ³ 的维数，合为 8。本书把 U(1)×SU(2)×SU(3) 的 1—2—3 读作这四阶在三维封顶（MODEL）；页面只显示维数，不声称由此推出规范群。
  4. 三色成体。三种颜色是三条两两正交的单位方向 r、g、b，它们的楔积是体，格拉姆行列式 det G₃ = 1；第三色与 g 重合时体塌成面，det G₃ = 0。若尔当—维格纳费米子的反对易让交换两色差一个负号，这是楔积的符号规则。
  5. 新的一点。体封成质子 p⁺ = uud（电荷 2/3 + 2/3 − 1/3 = +1），中子 n = udd 电荷为 0。两个核子之间剩下的是 π 介子的汤川场：穿过半径 r 球面的通量占库仑值的 (1 + r/λ)e−r/λ，λπ = ħ/(mπc) = 1.414 fm，在质子半径 rp = 0.8409 fm 处约为 0.880。

书中：第 34 章 §34.14 · 洛伦兹伪标量与标准模型八度

This is a numerical illustration, not a proof: every number is computed on the spot by the page (NUM), each Lean theorem is shown only on the instance written below, literature results are marked ARGUED and the book's interpretation MODEL.

  1. The square of the volume. The three edges e₁, e₂, e₃ leaving one corner of the cube are mutually orthogonal. I₃ = e₁e₂e₃ sends the line e₃ to the orthogonal plane e₁₂ and then back to −e₃: eI₃θe₃ = cos θ·e₃ + sin θ·e₁₂, which is −e₃ at θ = π. With Pauli matrices, I₃ = σ₁σ₂σ₃ = i·1 and I₃² = −1. The page lets θ advance with proper time: θ = π·cτ, cτ = ct·√(1 − β²).
  2. The budget. Each step of coordinate time c dt is a budget: what goes into the drift is dx = β c dt, what remains is the proper time c dτ, (c dτ)² = c²dt² − dx². The dome on the stage has radius c dt: right above the tip of the drift its height is exactly c dτ, and at the rim of the dial, β = 1, it is zero: that is light. Writing β as the paravector p = 1 + βê gives p p = 1 − β²; squaring the multivector I₃ + βê gives the scalar part −1 + β² = −(dτ/dt)².
  3. 1·3·3·1. The elements of the cube through one corner: 1 point, 3 edges, 3 faces, 1 volume, exactly the dimensions of the exterior algebra Λ⁰…Λ³, eight in all. The book reads the 1—2—3 of U(1)×SU(2)×SU(3) as these four grades capped in three dimensions (MODEL); the page shows the dimensions and does not claim that the gauge groups follow from them.
  4. Three colors make a volume. The three colors are three mutually orthogonal unit directions r, g, b; their wedge is a volume with Gram determinant det G₃ = 1. When the third color coincides with g the volume collapses into a plane and det G₃ = 0. The anticommutation of Jordan–Wigner fermions makes the swap of two colors cost a minus sign, the sign rule of the wedge.
  5. A new point. The volume seals into the proton p⁺ = uud (charge 2/3 + 2/3 − 1/3 = +1); the neutron n = udd has charge 0. What is left between two nucleons is the Yukawa field of the pion: the flux through a sphere of radius r is (1 + r/λ)e−r/λ of the Coulomb value, with λπ = ħ/(mπc) = 1.414 fm, about 0.880 at the proton radius rp = 0.8409 fm.
