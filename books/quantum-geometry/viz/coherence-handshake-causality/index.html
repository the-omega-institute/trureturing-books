LEAN ✓正交补投影恒等式：完备内积空间里闭子空间 M 的投影与正交补的投影满足 P_{M⊥} = I − P_M，两者都幂等，彼此相乘为零，相加为恒等。所以 B 在 A 线上的影子为零时，补空间的投影就是整个 B。定理不谈面积，也不谈存在与否。已冻结：Orthogonal-complement projections: for a closed subspace M of a complete inner-product space, P_{M⊥} = I − P_M, both are idempotent, their products vanish and their sum is the identity. So when the shadow of B on the line of A is zero, the complementary projection is all of B. The theorem says nothing about area or about existence. Frozen: orthogonal_complement_projection_identities
LEAN ✓拉格朗日—格拉姆恒等式：对有限指标上的实数 u、v，(Σuᵢ²)(Σvᵢ²) − (Σuᵢvᵢ)² = ½ΣᵢΣⱼ(uᵢvⱼ − uⱼvᵢ)²，不要求归一。取平面单位向量 A、B，就是 (A·B)² + (A∧B)² = 1：线的平方加面积的平方。它是 2 × 2 格拉姆余项，不是三阶行列式公式。已冻结：The Lagrange–Gram identity: for real u, v over a finite index set, (Σuᵢ²)(Σvᵢ²) − (Σuᵢvᵢ)² = ½ΣᵢΣⱼ(uᵢvⱼ − uⱼvᵢ)², with no normalization needed. For unit planar A, B it reads (A·B)² + (A∧B)² = 1: line squared plus area squared. It is the 2 × 2 Gram remainder, not the 3 × 3 determinant formula. Frozen: lagrange_gram_identity
LEAN ✓有限记录捏合的幂等性：有限、完备、两两正交的自伴记录族 P 给出的未读映射满足 unreadState P ∘ unreadState P = unreadState P。这是信道的性质；捏过一次再捏，什么也不变。它不说输出的密度矩阵自乘不变：等权时 Π(ρ) = I/2，而 (I/2)² ≠ I/2。已冻结：Idempotence of finite record pinching: a finite, complete, pairwise orthogonal self-adjoint record family P gives an unread map with unreadState P ∘ unreadState P = unreadState P. This is a property of the channel: pinching twice changes nothing more. It does not say the output density matrix squares to itself: at equal weights Π(ρ) = I/2, and (I/2)² ≠ I/2. Frozen: finite_record_pinching_idempotent
LEAN ✓捏合的希尔伯特—施密特勾股：对任意复 2 × 2 矩阵 ρ 与标准基捏合 Π，⟨ρ, ρ⟩ = ⟨Πρ, Πρ⟩ + ⟨ρ − Πρ, ρ − Πρ⟩。ρ 是密度矩阵时，纯度 Tr ρ² 正好分成留下的对角质量与被捏掉的相干质量。它不说被捏掉的相干能在本地找回。已冻结：Hilbert–Schmidt Pythagoras for pinching: for every complex 2 × 2 matrix ρ and the standard-basis pinching Π, ⟨ρ, ρ⟩ = ⟨Πρ, Πρ⟩ + ⟨ρ − Πρ, ρ − Πρ⟩.   1. Three zeros. A = (1, 0), B = (cos θ, sin θ), M = span(A): B∥ = (cos θ, 0), B⊥ = (0, sin θ), ‖B∥‖² + ‖B⊥‖² = 1. At θ = 90°, A·B = 0, all of the squared length of B lies in the complement, and A∧B = sin θ squares to 1; orthogonal unit vectors have ‖A + B‖² = 2, which is no cancellation. Arms a = cos ξ, b = sin ξ through a balanced recombiner: b± = (a ± b e^{iΔ})/√2, p₊ + p₋ = 1; at Δ = π, p₊ = (a − b)²/2, zero only when a = b. At γ = 0 both ports give ½. These are three different operations: a zero alone cannot name its cause, since an empty input, orthogonality and cancellation all read zero.
  2. The handshake. |ψ⟩ = e^{iχ_ψ}(1, 0), |φ⟩ = e^{iχ_φ}(cos(θ/2), sin(θ/2)), r = cos(θ/2).
