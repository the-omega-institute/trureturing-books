理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

两颗量子比特处在贝尔态 |Φ⁺⟩ = (|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2。Alice 与 Bob 各在 (Z, X) 平面里摆两个测量方向，角度从 Z 轴量起（盘上向上是 Z，向右是 X）。页面上的每个数都实时算出；本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 四个余弦。同平面两个方向 a、b 的关联是 E = ⟨Φ⁺|(a·σ) ⊗ (b·σ)|Φ⁺⟩ = cos(a − b)（第十三章 §贝尔态的手算）。CHSH 值 ⟨S⟩ = E₀₀ + E₀₁ + E₁₀ − E₁₁。量尺上青色的四段就是这四项，从 0 依次往上（或往下）叠，最后停在琥珀色的横线上。
  2. 两块面积。Alice 的两个方向张开 α，Bob 的张开 β；品红的菱形是两支箭头（缩到 0.55）张成的平行四边形，面积正比于 |sin α| 与 |sin β|。由 Landau 恒等式 S² = 4I − [A₀, A₁] ⊗ [B₀, B₁] 与泡利乘法，换位子项的谱是 ±N，N = 4 |sin α sin β|（手工推导）。0 < N < 4 时，冻结命题把 S 的谱关进 ±√(4 + N)、±√(4 − N)；N = 0 时 S² = 4I；N = 4 时手算得谱 {±2√2, 0}。品红的刻度画在 ±√(4 + N)。
  3. 转动 Bob。时间轴把 Bob 的一对方向整体转动 φ。把四项写成 Re(e^{iφ} Z̄)，Z = e^{ia₀} + e^{i(a₀+β)} + e^{ia₁} − e^{i(a₁+β)}，|Z|² = 4 + 4 sin α sin β：转一整圈，琥珀色的横线的最高与最低正好是 ±√(4 + 4 sin α sin β)。sin α 与 sin β 同号时，它正好碰到外侧的品红刻度 ±√(4 + N)；异号时只碰到内侧的点线 ±√(4 − N)（页面的数值核对；它说的只是贝尔态上的同平面方向）。
  4. 天花板。|sin α sin β| ≤ 1，所以品红的刻度最高到 √8 = 2√2：两侧都张成直角时。这时还要 Bob 的十字架转 45°，四个夹角成为 45°、45°、45° 与 135°，⟨S⟩ = 4/√2 = 2√2。冻结推论说这组固定方向在所有双比特态上最多就是 2√2；冻结定理取 d = 2（手工推导）说任何有限维的四个厄米对合观测量都超不过它。
  5. 隐方向。经典的对手：每对粒子带一个隐方向 λ，Alice 输出 sgn(a·λ)，Bob 输出 sgn(b·λ)（值为 0 时记作 +1）。页面取 λ = 0.5°, 1.5°, …, 359.5°，权重各 1/360。每个隐方向上的 CHSH 值都是 ±2（第十三章十六张应答表），环上青色的刻度是 +2，品红的是 −2；平均值就是这个经典模型的 CHSH 值。它是一个有限确定性纤维模型，冻结定理保证它出不了 [−2, 2]。两侧都张成直角时，Bob 的十字架在 0° 到 90° 之间怎么转，360 个隐方向都给 +2，平均值钉在 2。
  6. 弧与折线。横轴是两方向的夹角（0° 到 180°）。青色的弧是 cos，暗色的折线是隐方向模型的关联，夹角为整数度时恰为 1 − 2θ/π。四个夹角各落一对点；品红的竖段是弧高出（或低于）折线的部分。在 0°、90°、180° 两者重合；在 45° 与 135°，四段之差加起来是 2√2 − 2 ≈ 0.828。这条折线只属于这个玩具模型。

Two qubits are in the Bell state |Φ⁺⟩ = (|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2. Alice and Bob each set two measurement directions in the (Z, X) plane, with angles measured from the Z axis (up on the dial is Z, right is X). Every number on the page is computed live; the page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Four cosines. Two coplanar directions a and b have the correlation E = ⟨Φ⁺|(a·σ) ⊗ (b·σ)|Φ⁺⟩ = cos(a − b) (worked by hand in Chapter 13, on Bell states). The CHSH value is ⟨S⟩ = E₀₀ + E₀₁ + E₁₀ − E₁₁. The four cyan steps on the gauge are these four terms, stacked up (or down) from 0, ending at the amber bar.
  2. Two areas. Alice's two directions open an angle α and Bob's open β; each magenta rhombus is the parallelogram of two arrows (scaled to 0.55), with area proportional to |sin α| and |sin β|. From Landau's identity S² = 4I − [A₀, A₁] ⊗ [B₀, B₁] and Pauli multiplication, the commutator term has spectrum ±N with N = 4 |sin α sin β| (a manual step). For 0 < N < 4 the frozen proposition confines the spectrum of S to ±√(4 + N), ±√(4 − N); at N = 0, S² = 4I; at N = 4 a hand calculation gives the spectrum {±2√2, 0}. The magenta marks sit at ±√(4 + N).
  3. Turning Bob. The rail turns Bob's pair as a whole by φ. Writing the four terms as Re(e^{iφ} Z̄) with Z = e^{ia₀} + e^{i(a₀+β)} + e^{ia₁} − e^{i(a₁+β)} gives |Z|² = 4 + 4 sin α sin β: over a full turn the amber bar reaches exactly ±√(4 + 4 sin α sin β). When sin α and sin β have the same sign it touches the outer magenta marks ±√(4 + N); when they differ it touches only the inner dotted marks ±√(4 − N) (a numerical check by the page; it concerns coplanar directions on the Bell state only).
  4. The ceiling. |sin α sin β| ≤ 1, so the magenta mark reaches at most √8 = 2√2, when both sides open a right angle. Bob's cross must then also be turned by 45°: the four angles become 45°, 45°, 45° and 135°, and ⟨S⟩ = 4/√2 = 2√2. The frozen corollary says this fixed set of directions gives at most 2√2 over all two-qubit states; the frozen theorem with d = 2 (a manual step) says no four Hermitian involutions in finite dimension do better.
  5. Hidden directions. The classical opponent: each pair carries a hidden direction λ; Alice outputs sgn(a·λ) and Bob sgn(b·λ) (a value of 0 counts as +1). The page takes λ = 0.5°, 1.5°, …, 359.5°, each with weight 1/360. On every hidden direction the CHSH value is ±2 (the sixteen answer tables of Chapter 13); cyan ticks on the ring are +2, magenta ones −2, and their mean is the CHSH value of this classical model. It is a finite deterministic fiber model, so the frozen theorem keeps it inside [−2, 2]. With both sides at right angles, however Bob's cross turns between 0° and 90°, all 360 hidden directions give +2 and the mean stays pinned at 2.
  6. Arc and line. The horizontal axis is the angle between two directions (0° to 180°). The cyan arc is cos; the dark polyline is the correlation of the hidden-direction model, exactly 1 − 2θ/π at whole degrees. Each of the four angles drops a pair of points, and the magenta segment is where the arc clears (or dips below) the line. They meet at 0°, 90° and 180°; at 45° and 135° the four gaps add up to 2√2 − 2 ≈ 0.828. The polyline belongs to this toy model only.
