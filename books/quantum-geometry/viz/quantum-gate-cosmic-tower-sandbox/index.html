本页是数值与模型演示，不是证明：每个数都由页面当场算出（NUM），每条 Lean 定理只在下面写明的那个实例上演示。

  1. 换位子。Ux(ε) = cos(ε/2) − i sin(ε/2) X，Uy(ε) 同理。C = Ux†Uy†UxUy 的迹 tr C/2 = 1 − 2 sin⁴(ε/2)，所以 C 是绕 n ∝ (−sin(ε/2), sin(ε/2), cos(ε/2)) 转 Φ = 2 arccos(1 − 2 sin⁴(ε/2)) 的旋转。从北极 N 出发依次走 Uy、Ux、Uy†、Ux† 四段弧，终点差了一截：这是品红的缺口。
  2. 极小圆弧体。N、A = Uy(ε)N、B = Ux(ε)N 围成直角球面三角形 ΔTε，两条腿都是 ε，角超 Ω = 2 arctan(tan²(ε/2))。小 ε 时 ½Φ ≈ Ω ≈ ε²/2：换位子的旋量相位与三角形的面积同阶。det G(N, A, B) = sin⁴ε。
  3. Segre 环面。纠缠门 exp(−i(η/2)(XY + YX)/2) 把 |00⟩ 送到 cos(η/2)|00⟩ + sin(η/2)|11⟩：共生度 |sin η|、单比特线性熵 ½ sin²η、CHSH 最大值 2√(1 + sin²η)。实振幅的两比特态构成 S³，乘积态 det c = 0 是一张环面；球极投影后，态从环面出发，沿品红的楔方向走向贝尔态的圆。
  4. 1—3—3—1 四拍。三面角有 1 个顶点、3 条棱、3 面墙、1 块体，对应 Λ⁰、Λ¹、Λ²、Λ³ 的维数 1、3、3、1。模型把每层的体收成上一层的点（Λ³ ≡ Λ⁰ℓ+1），六层自下而上：L0 原语比较、L1 量子门、L2 粒子、L3 场与波、L4 时空、L5 觉知。
  5. 4π 回环。莫比乌斯带上搬运的法向走一圈变号，走两圈复原：U(2π) = −I，U(4π) = +I。模型让 L5 经这条回环回到 L0 的比较原语。余弦不动点 θ* = cos θ* ≈ 0.7391 是 L5 的自指读数。

  2. The minimal-arc trihedron. N, A = Uy(ε)N and B = Ux(ε)N bound the right spherical triangle ΔTε with both legs ε and excess Ω = 2 arctan(tan²(ε/2)). For small ε, ½Φ ≈ Ω ≈ ε²/2: the spinor phase of the commutator is of the order of the area of the triangle. det G(N, A, B) = sin⁴ε.
  3. The Segre torus. The entangling gate exp(−i(η/2)(XY + YX)/2) takes |00⟩ to cos(η/2)|00⟩ + sin(η/2)|11⟩: concurrence |sin η|, one-qubit linear entropy ½ sin²η, CHSH maximum 2√(1 + sin²η). Real two-qubit states form S³ and the product states det c = 0 a torus in it; after stereographic projection the state leaves the torus along the magenta wedge direction towards the circle of the Bell state.
  4. The 1—3—3—1 beats. A trihedron has 1 apex, 3 edges, 3 walls and 1 solid, matching the dimensions 1, 3, 3, 1 of Λ⁰, Λ¹, Λ², Λ³. The model closes the volume of each tier into the point of the next (Λ³ ≡ Λ⁰ℓ+1), six tiers from the bottom: L0 comparison primitive, L1 quantum gate, L2 particle, L3 field and wave, L4 spacetime, L5 awareness.
  5. The 4π return. A normal carried round the Möbius band changes sign after one turn and comes back after two: U(2π) = −I, U(4π) = +I. The model lets L5 return along this loop to the comparison primitive of L0. The cosine fixed point θ* = cos θ* ≈ 0.7391 is the self-referential reading of L5.
