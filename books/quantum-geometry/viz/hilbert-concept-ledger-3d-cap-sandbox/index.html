理论接口 · 三维概念场账本与封顶Theory interface · the 3D concept-field ledger and its cap

这是数值沙盘，不是证明。每个数都由页面当场算出（NUM）；每条 Lean 定理只在依据栏写明的那一个实例上演示；“三维封顶”是本书的诠释模型。

  1. 混编与分箱。五个概念各有三个坐标，合起来是 ℝ¹⁵ 里的一个单位向量 ψ = ⊕α √wα uα，块范数平方之和 Σwα = 1。把 ℝ¹⁵ 当成一个同质空间，它的转动 so(15) 有 15 · 14 / 2 = 105 个生成元；按概念分箱，只剩五份 so(3)，共 5 × 3 = 15 个。多出来的 105 − 15 = 90 = 9 · C(5, 2) 个生成元把一个概念的轴转进另一个概念（“把空间转成颜色”），没有物理意义：页面把 15 个基向量两两配对，逐对数出块内与跨块。k = 3 时是 36 − 9 = 27。
  2. 空间与颜色的联合账本。每颗三维概念球取一个二能级系统的布洛赫球（m = n = 2）。两体态 ρ 的无迹部分有 m²n² − 1 = 15 个数：A 的局域 3 个、B 的局域 3 个、相关表 Tij = tr(ρ σi ⊗ σj) 9 个。时间轴取 ρ(λ) = (1 − λ/2)|Φ+⟩⟨Φ+| + (λ/2)|Φ−⟩⟨Φ−|：λ = 0 是 Bell 密度，λ = 1 是经典相关的 (|00⟩⟨00| + |11⟩⟨11|)/2。整条路上两个局域布洛赫向量都是 0，相关表却是 diag(1 − λ, −(1 − λ), 1)；部分转置的最小本征值 (λ − 1)/2 在 λ < 1 时为负。颜色球里 RGB 正交基的格拉姆行列式是 1；三条高斯视锥曲线的 L² 内积给出 LMS 斜交框，行列式约 0.235，仍大于零。
  3. 运动的三颗球。物体是单位球第一卦限的弧面体（三面四分之一圆盘墙与球面顶盖）。运动 x ↦ R(λθn) diag(eλh) x + λt，取 t = (0.4, 0.36, 0.16)、θ = 120°、n = (1, 1, 1)/√3、h = (ln 1.5, ln 1.25, ln 0.8)。平移、转动向量、对数主应变各在自己的三维球里走直线；页面对 F = RU 做极分解，从 FᵀF 的本征值读回 h。缩放的另一种三维读法是 sl(2, ℝ)：基 H、E、F 的基灵型本征值为 8、4、−4，符号 (2, 1)。
  4. 场的封顶。d 维里梯度（1-形式）有 C(d, 1) = d 个分量，旋度（2-形式）有 C(d, 2) 个，差为 d(d − 3)/2，在 d ≥ 1 上只在 d = 3 为零。霍奇星号把 2-形式送到 (d − 2)-形式：d = 3 时落在一根轴上（紫线连到青色箭头），d = 4 时落回另一个 2-形式，六个分成 3 + 3（so(4) ≅ su(2) ⊕ su(2)），d = 2 时是一个数，d = 5 时是一个 3-形式。d = 3 时四元数 ij = k、e1 × e2 = e3，纯态的布洛赫向量长 1。
  5. 停机。模塔 wk = (1 − q)qk − 1，q = 0.65，Σwk = 1，永不自己结束。自停机精度 εself 取最小的 K 使尾巴 Σk > K wk = qK ≤ ε：ε = 0.05 时 K = 7，保留 95.10%，折叠 4.90%。另一种数法（Lean 定理里的那一种）数 wk ≥ ε 的模，得 5 个，不超过 1/ε = 20。两种数法都给出有限。

书中：§32.1 三维概念场封顶与希尔伯特账本

This is a numerical sandbox, not a proof. Every number is computed on the page (NUM); each Lean theorem is shown only on the one instance named in the evidence panel; “the 3D cap” is the book’s interpretive model.

  1. Pooling and binning. Five concepts with three coordinates each make one unit vector ψ = ⊕α √wα uα in ℝ¹⁵, whose squared block norms add up to Σwα = 1. Read ℝ¹⁵ as one homogeneous space and its rotations so(15) have 15 · 14 / 2 = 105 generators; bin by concept and five copies of so(3) remain, 5 × 3 = 15 of them. The extra 105 − 15 = 90 = 9 · C(5, 2) generators turn an axis of one concept into another concept (“rotate space into color”) and mean nothing physical: the page pairs the 15 basis vectors two by two and counts in-block and cross-block pairs. For k = 3 it is 36 − 9 = 27.
  2. The joint ledger of space and color. Each 3D concept ball is taken as the Bloch ball of a two-level system (m = n = 2). The traceless part of a two-party state ρ has m²n² − 1 = 15 numbers: 3 local to A, 3 local to B and the correlation table Tij = tr(ρ σi ⊗ σj), 9 of them. The rail takes ρ(λ) = (1 − λ/2)|Φ+⟩⟨Φ+| + (λ/2)|Φ−⟩⟨Φ−|: λ = 0 is the Bell density, λ = 1 the classically correlated (|00⟩⟨00| + |11⟩⟨11|)/2. All along, both local Bloch vectors are 0 while the correlation table is diag(1 − λ, −(1 − λ), 1); the least eigenvalue of the partial transpose, (λ − 1)/2, is negative for λ < 1. The orthonormal RGB frame of the color ball has Gram determinant 1; the L² inner products of three Gaussian cone curves give the skewed LMS frame, with determinant about 0.235, still positive.
  3. Three balls of motion. The body is the arc solid of the first octant of the unit sphere (three quarter-disc walls and a spherical cap). The motion is x ↦ R(λθn) diag(eλh) x + λt with t = (0.4, 0.36, 0.16), θ = 120°, n = (1, 1, 1)/√3 and h = (ln 1.5, ln 1.25, ln 0.8). The translation, the rotation vector and the log principal strains each run along a straight line in their own 3D ball; the page polar-decomposes F = RU and reads h back from the eigenvalues of FᵀF. Another 3D reading of scale is sl(2, ℝ): the Killing form of the basis H, E, F has eigenvalues 8, 4, −4, signature (2, 1).
  4. The cap of fields. In d dimensions a gradient (1-form) has C(d, 1) = d components and a curl (2-form) C(d, 2); the difference d(d − 3)/2 vanishes on d ≥ 1 only at d = 3. The Hodge star sends a 2-form to a (d − 2)-form: at d = 3 it lands on an axis (a violet line to a cyan arrow), at d = 4 on another 2-form, splitting the six into 3 + 3 (so(4) ≅ su(2) ⊕ su(2)), at d = 2 on a number and at d = 5 on a 3-form. At d = 3 the quaternions give ij = k and e1 × e2 = e3, and a pure state has a Bloch vector of length 1.
  5. Halting. The tower wk = (1 − q)qk − 1, q = 0.65, has Σwk = 1 and never ends by itself. The self-halting precision εself takes the least K whose tail Σk > K wk = qK is ≤ ε: at ε = 0.05, K = 7, keeping 95.10% and folding 4.90%. The other count (the one in the Lean theorem) counts the modes with wk ≥ ε: 5 of them, at most 1/ε = 20. Both counts are finite.
