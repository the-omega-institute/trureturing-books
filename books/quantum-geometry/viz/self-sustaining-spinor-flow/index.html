舞台是实三维几何代数 Cl(3,0)：单位球里画旋量 ψ 织出的两样东西，球外一圈单侧光带画 ψ 自己的正负号。本页是数值演示，不是证明；每个数都由页面在 Cl(3,0) 里当场算出（NUM）。

  1. 旋量与密度。偶多向量 ψ = a + b e₂e₃ + c e₃e₁ + d e₁e₂，反序 ψ̃ 把双向量变号。ρ = ψψ̃ = a² + b² + c² + d² ≥ 0，R = ψ/√ρ 满足 RR̃ = 1（第三十九章例 ex-ch39-even-clifford-spinor-bilinear-fierz 第 1 步）。
  2. 两个双线性量。流向量 J = ψe₃ψ̃ = ρ R e₃ R̃，长度 ρ（青色的箭头）；自旋双向量 S = ψe₁e₂ψ̃ = ρ (Re₁R̃)(Re₂R̃)，画成边长 √ρ、由 Re₁R̃ 与 Re₂R̃ 张成的品红正方形，面积 ρ，小弧标出它的转向。由结合律 J² = ψe₃(ψ̃ψ)e₃ψ̃ = ρ²，S² = ρ ψ(e₁e₂)²ψ̃ = −ρ²：这就是 J² = −S² = ρ²（例第 2、3 步）。在三维里 e₁e₂ = I e₃（I = e₁e₂e₃ 与一切元素对易），所以 S = I J：自旋面恰是与 J 正交的那块面。
  3. 转动。取一个单位双向量 B（e₂e₃、e₃e₁ 或 e₁e₂），令 ψ(t) = R(t) ψ₀，R(t) = cos(t/2) − sin(t/2) B。J(t) = R(t) J₀ R̃(t) 绕紫色的轴 n（B 的对偶）转过角 t；t = 360° 时 J、S 都回到出发时的样子，ψ(360°) = −ψ₀，t = 720° 时 ψ = +ψ₀（例第 4 步）。
  4. 单侧光带与小旗。球外的光带以 n 为轴绕一圈；在 J 所在的方位插一面紫色小旗，方向 cos(t/2) n + sin(t/2) r（r 是这一方位上指向外面的单位向量），即 ψψ̃₀/ρ = cos(t/2) − sin(t/2) B 的两个分量。光带的横向在一圈里只转半圈，是一条单侧的莫比乌斯带：小旗走完一圈到了另一面（与出发时相反），走完两圈才回到原面。旗尖走过的路是光带的边，边要绕两圈才闭合。

The stage is the real geometric algebra Cl(3,0): inside the unit sphere, the two things a spinor ψ weaves; outside it, a one-sided band round the sphere that shows the sign of ψ itself. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof; every number is computed on the spot in Cl(3,0) (NUM).

  1. Spinor and density. An even multivector ψ = a + b e₂e₃ + c e₃e₁ + d e₁e₂; the reversion ψ̃ flips the sign of the bivectors. ρ = ψψ̃ = a² + b² + c² + d² ≥ 0, and R = ψ/√ρ has RR̃ = 1 (Chapter 39, step 1 of Example ex-ch39-even-clifford-spinor-bilinear-fierz).
  2. Two bilinears. The current vector J = ψe₃ψ̃ = ρ R e₃ R̃ has length ρ (the cyan arrow); the spin bivector S = ψe₁e₂ψ̃ = ρ (Re₁R̃)(Re₂R̃) is drawn as the magenta square of side √ρ spanned by Re₁R̃ and Re₂R̃, area ρ, with a small arc for its sense. By associativity J² = ψe₃(ψ̃ψ)e₃ψ̃ = ρ² and S² = ρ ψ(e₁e₂)²ψ̃ = −ρ²: this is J² = −S² = ρ² (steps 2 and 3). In three dimensions e₁e₂ = I e₃, with I = e₁e₂e₃ commuting with everything, so S = I J: the spin plane is exactly the plane orthogonal to J.
  3. The turn. Take a unit bivector B (e₂e₃, e₃e₁ or e₁e₂) and set ψ(t) = R(t) ψ₀ with R(t) = cos(t/2) − sin(t/2) B. J(t) = R(t) J₀ R̃(t) turns through t about the violet axis n (the dual of B); at t = 360° J and S look as they did at the start while ψ(360°) = −ψ₀, and at t = 720° ψ = +ψ₀ (step 4).
  4. The one-sided band and the flag. The band outside goes once round the axis n. At the bearing of J stands a violet flag pointing along cos(t/2) n + sin(t/2) r, with r the outward unit vector at that bearing: the two components of ψψ̃₀/ρ = cos(t/2) − sin(t/2) B. Across one round the band’s width turns only half a turn, so it is a one-sided Möbius band: after one round the flag is on the other side (the reverse of where it started), after two it is back. The path of the flag’s tip is the band’s edge, which closes only after going round twice.
  5. Frozen theorems. The squaring double cover of the circle has no continuous global section, no_continuous_global_section, and every path has a unique history-dependent lift, circle_double_cover_history_lift: the two theorems Chapter 39 cites with lean= in thm-ch39-double-cover-no-section-and-720-lift (LEAN ✓). They are about the circle map z ↦ z²; on the page the point downstairs is eit (the angle J has turned through) and upstairs it is eit/2 (the half angle of the flag).
  6. Literature and interpretation. The Fierz identity (with JμJμ = σ² + ω² for a Dirac spinor) and the double cover Spin(3) ≅ SU(2) → SO(3) are literature results reported in the remark rem-ch39-cartan-penrose-hestenes-fierz-literature (ARGUED), not formalized in this book; “a substrate-free self-referential spinor flow” and “the observer is the ontological universe” are the chapter’s model interpretation (MODEL), and the page does not claim them as theorems or physical results.
