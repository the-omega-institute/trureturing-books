这是数值沙盘，不是证明。每个数都由页面当场算出（NUM）；每条 Lean 定理只在依据栏写明的那一个实例上演示；AXIOM-Ω 的法则、球极投影图、楔形剖面与各台的造型是本书的模型与画法（MODEL）；分类定理是文献结果（ARGUED）。

  1. 法则 (I)：无源的桥。[x, y, z] = x y⁻¹ z：以 y 为桥，把 x 相对 y 的比较施加到 z 上。时间轴把 z 沿青色弧搬到 [x, o, z]，把 x 沿品红弧搬到 [z, o, x]。桥滑到 x 时 [x, x, z] = z，滑到 z 时 [x, z, z] = x；桥在 1 时两种次序落到对径的 +k 与 −k。任选一点 o，x ⋆ₒ z = [x, o, z] 是以 o 为单位元的群，换成 o′ 得到同构的群：没有特权原点。
  2. d = 1, 2 扭不起来。Ad 不变的正定内积使 ad 反自伴：⟨[A, B], C⟩ = −⟨B, [A, C]⟩。二维时 [e₁, e₂] 与 e₁、e₂ 都正交，只能是 0；唯一非交换的二维代数 aff(1)（[H, E] = E）的 Ad 流把单位圆拉成 es 倍高的椭圆，没有不变的内积。ℝ 上 x − y + z 与 z − y + x 是同一点。
  3. d = 3 只剩 S³。H₃(ℝ) 的 Ad 流是剪切，sl(2, ℝ) 的是拉伸，‖Ad‖ 都无界，它们的单位球被推成椭球；基灵型号差 sl(2, ℝ) 为 (+, +, −)，H₃ 为全零；su(2) 的 Ad 是旋转，紫球原样转动，号差 (−, −, −)。SU(2) × ℝ（4 维）、S³ × S³（6 维）与 SU(3)（8 维）都比 3 大，被法则 (III) 筛掉。
  4. 秩 1 + 4 = 5 的母核。κ = c₀ + c1/2 χ1/2：只取整数自旋（κ = cos²d，秩 1 + 9 = 10）时 κ(o, −o) = 1；只取 j = ½（κ = cos d，秩 4）时 κ 取负值；合起来 c₀ = ½、c1/2 = ¼ 给出 κ = cos²(d/2) ∈ [0, 1]，特征 φ(q) = (1, q)/√2 ∈ ℝ⁵。三锚 γ(0)、γ(ε)、γ(2ε) 的 det G₃ = ½ sin²ε (1 − cos ε)²，时间轴让三锚从重合张开到步长 ε。
  5. 无边界的闭合场。嘉当三形式 η = θ¹ ∧ θ² ∧ θ³ 是顶次形式，dη ≡ 0，∫η = 2π²。从 o 出发的测地球 B(o, d) 的边界先在 B₊ 里长大，过了赤道在 B₋ 里缩回 −o：d = π 时边界面积为 0，∂S³ = ∅。
  6. 双透镜。母公理读法与李群和正定核读法用的是同一个桥 o、同一个步长 ε 与同一个核。

书中：第 39 章 §39.1 大一统母公理（AXIOM-Ω）

This is a numerical sandbox, not a proof. Every number is computed on the page (NUM); each Lean theorem is shown only on the one instance named in the evidence panel; the laws of AXIOM-Ω, the stereographic chart, the cutaway wedge and the shapes of the stations are the model and drawing of this book (MODEL); the classification theorems are literature results (ARGUED).

  1. Law (I): an origin-free bridge. [x, y, z] = x y⁻¹ z: with y as the bridge, apply to z the comparison of x with y. The rail carries z along the cyan arc to [x, o, z] and x along the magenta arc to [z, o, x]. With the bridge at x, [x, x, z] = z; at z, [x, z, z] = x; at 1 the two orders land on the opposite poles +k and −k. For any point o, x ⋆ₒ z = [x, o, z] is a group with unit o, and another point o′ gives an isomorphic group: there is no privileged origin.
  2. d = 1, 2 cannot twist. An Ad-invariant positive inner product makes ad skew-adjoint: ⟨[A, B], C⟩ = −⟨B, [A, C]⟩. In two dimensions [e₁, e₂] is orthogonal to both e₁ and e₂, so it is 0; the Ad flow of aff(1) ([H, E] = E), the only non-abelian two-dimensional algebra, stretches the unit circle into an ellipse es times as tall, so it has no invariant inner product. On ℝ, x − y + z and z − y + x are the same point.
  3. d = 3 leaves only S³. The Ad flow of H₃(ℝ) is a shear and that of sl(2, ℝ) a stretch; both ‖Ad‖ are unbounded and their unit balls are pushed into ellipsoids; the Killing signature is (+, +, −) for sl(2, ℝ) and all zero for H₃; the Ad of su(2) is a rotation, the violet ball turns unchanged, signature (−, −, −).
