理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

在实单位球面 Sd−1 ⊂ ℝd 上按旋转不变的均匀测度取点 x，看它在一根固定测量轴上的坐标 x₁。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 舞台。左边是球面过测量轴的截面：圆上的每个点就是一个随机点，放在高度 x₁ 处（离赤道的距离），左右随机。右边把 x₁ 的分布横着画，与圆共用同一根竖直轴：曲线是精确密度，灰色的条是这 1500 个点的直方图。赤道带 |x₁| < ε 里的点是青色，带外的是品红。
  2. 精确分布。x₁ 的密度正比于 (1 − x²)(d−3)/2；等价地，x₁² 服从 Beta(1/2, (d − 1)/2)。d = 3 时密度是常数 1/2：普通球面上点的高度均匀分布（阿基米德）。页面用不完全贝塔函数精确算出带内外的份额，并在对数下计算，所以 d = 2²⁰ 时也能给出约 3 × 10−2291 这样的数。
  3. 宽度。各坐标对称，x₁² + … + xd² = 1，所以 E[x₁²] = 1/d，典型宽度 1/√d：量子比特每多一个，宽度缩小为 1/√2。小图在对数坐标上画出宽度与带外份额随 d 的变化。
  4. 马尔可夫上界。Pr(|x₁| ≥ ε) ≤ E[x₁²]/ε² = 1/(dε²)。它总在实际份额之上，而且宽松得多：d = 128、ε = 0.2 时界是 0.195，实际是 0.023；二十个量子比特、ε = 0.1 时界是 9.54 × 10−5（第十二章的例子），实际约 3 × 10−2291。
  5. 边界。实振幅球面只是受限的状态模型（一般 n 比特复态在 S2n+1−1 上）。赤道集中是贫瘠高原的几何背景，但随机电路梯度的指数衰减还依赖电路分布、2-设计与可观测量的局域性等条件（文献结果，未在本书形式化）；页面不画梯度。

Pick a point x of the real unit sphere Sd−1 ⊂ ℝd under the rotation-invariant uniform measure and look at its coordinate x₁ along one fixed measured axis. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The stage. On the left is the section of the sphere through the measured axis: every dot on the circle is one random point, placed at height x₁ (its distance from the equator) on a random side. On the right the distribution of x₁ is drawn sideways on the same vertical axis: the curve is the exact density, the grey bars the histogram of the 1500 points. Points in the equator belt |x₁| < ε are cyan, points outside it magenta.
  2. The exact distribution. x₁ has density proportional to (1 − x²)(d−3)/2; equivalently x₁² follows Beta(1/2, (d − 1)/2). At d = 3 the density is the constant 1/2: on the ordinary sphere the heights of points are spread evenly (Archimedes). The page computes the shares inside and outside the belt exactly from the incomplete beta function, in logarithms, so even at d = 2²⁰ it can report a number like 3 × 10−2291.
  3. The width. By symmetry and x₁² + … + xd² = 1, E[x₁²] = 1/d and the typical width is 1/√d: every extra qubit narrows it by a factor 1/√2. The small plot shows the width and the outside share against d on logarithmic axes.
  4. The Markov bound. Pr(|x₁| ≥ ε) ≤ E[x₁²]/ε² = 1/(dε²). It always lies above the actual share and is far from tight: at d = 128 and ε = 0.2 the bound is 0.195 against an actual 0.023; at twenty qubits and ε = 0.1 the bound is 9.54 × 10−5 (the example of Chapter 12) against an actual 3 × 10−2291 or so.
  5. Limits. The real-amplitude sphere is a restricted state model (general complex n-qubit states lie on S2n+1−1). Equator concentration is the geometric background of barren plateaus, but the exponential decay of random-circuit gradients also needs conditions on the circuit distribution, 2-designs and the locality of the observable (literature results, not formalized in this book); the page draws no gradients.
