左边是一张密度矩阵：第 i 行第 j 列那一格的方块面积是 |ρᵢⱼ|，方块里的短线指向它的相位，虚线框是它出发时的大小。对角是每个位置的权重（青色），非对角是位置之间的相干。右边是这张矩阵在一面屏上留下的亮度：青色虚线只由对角给出，青色填色是锚点块里的相干添上的条纹，品红填色是其余相干添上的条纹。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 锚点。全局纯态 Ψ 落在四个位置上，x₀ 是躯体。锚点是一组投影 {Pₖ}，ρ(λ) = (1 − λ)|Ψ⟩⟨Ψ| + λ Σₖ PₖΨΨ*Pₖ：跨块的格乘上 1 − λ，块里的格与对角不动。它对一切 λ 都是迹为 1、厄米、半正定的矩阵（两个密度矩阵的凸组合）。λ = 1 时跨块全为零，再捏一次什么也不变：这是第 37 章 Lean 已验证的捏合幂等与不动点判据。“逐个位置”时 λ = 1 的熵等于删去相干的相对熵（Lean 已验证的熵产恒等式，从熵为 0 的纯态出发）；“躯体 | 其余”的块捏合与 0 < λ < 1 的熵是页面数值。
  2. 卸下锚点。λ 从 1 降到 0.1，跨块相干的模长回到原来的 0.9 倍，条纹回来；对角——每个位置的权重——始终不动。“梦境卸下躯体先验”是本书的诠释（MODEL）。
  3. 记录。两条路 x₀、x₁ 的等幅叠加，ρ₀₁ = 1/2。每份环境记录给地址 0 写 (1, 0)，给地址 1 写 (|c|e^{iφ}, √(1 − |c|²))，重叠 c = |c|e^{−iφ}；写入一份让 ρ₀₁ 乘 c，逆转一份乘 c̄。|c| = 1、φ = 90° 时正是 Lean 证书里的相位记录：1/2 → −i/2 → −1/2 → −i/2 → 1/2，只逆转一份停在 −i/2。复原定理的前提是每份记录 c̄·c = 1；|c| < 1 时两份都逆转只剩 |c|⁴ 倍，|c| = 0 时为零——这是页面按同一公式的数值，不是那条定理的推论。屏上条纹的对比度是 2|ρ₀₁|，位置随 ρ₀₁ 的相位移动。
  4. 醒来。梦中的态取“逐个位置”的 ρ(λ)。读出投影 P₀ = |x₀⟩⟨x₀| 只留躯体那一格，遗忘的权重 1 − Tr(P₀ρ) = 1 − |⟨x₀|Ψ⟩|²：P₀ 与锚点的块对易，捏合不改对角，所以它与 λ 无关。把这个数读成“醒来忘掉的梦”是本书的诠释（MODEL）。
  5. 不是什么。页面不模拟大脑，也不演示第 37 章的黄金切投与德洛内定理；量子擦除实验与睡眠生理是文献背景（ARGUED）。

On the left is a density matrix: the square in row i, column j has area |ρᵢⱼ|, the short needle inside points to its phase, and the dashed frame is its size at the start. The diagonal is the weight of each place (cyan); the off-diagonal entries are the coherence between places. On the right is the brightness this matrix leaves on a screen: the dashed cyan curve comes from the diagonal alone, the cyan fill marks the fringes added by the coherence inside an anchor block, the magenta fill those added by the rest of the coherence. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Anchors. The global pure state Ψ lies on four places, x₀ being the body. The anchors are a family of projections {Pₖ}, and ρ(λ) = (1 − λ)|Ψ⟩⟨Ψ| + λ Σₖ PₖΨΨ*Pₖ: cross-block entries are multiplied by 1 − λ, entries inside a block and the diagonal do not move. For every λ it is a matrix of trace 1, Hermitian and positive semidefinite (a convex combination of two density matrices). At λ = 1 every cross-block entry is zero and pinching again changes nothing: pinching idempotence and its fixed-point criterion, verified in Lean in chapter 37. For “every place”, the entropy at λ = 1 equals the relative entropy of the deleted coherence (the entropy-production identity verified in Lean, starting from a pure state of entropy 0); the block pinching “body | the rest” and the entropies for 0 < λ < 1 are numbers of the page.
  2. Unpinning. As λ drops from 1 to 0.1, the cross-block coherence comes back to 0.9 times its original modulus and the fringes return; the diagonal, the weight of each place, never moves. “A dream unloads the bodily prior” is this book's interpretation (MODEL).
  3. Records. An equal superposition of two paths x₀, x₁, with ρ₀₁ = 1/2. Each environment record writes (1, 0) for address 0 and (|c|e^{iφ}, √(1 − |c|²)) for address 1, so its overlap is c = |c|e^{−iφ}; writing a copy multiplies ρ₀₁ by c, reversing one multiplies it by c̄. With |c| = 1 and φ = 90° this is the phase record of the Lean certificate: 1/2 → −i/2 → −1/2 → −i/2 → 1/2, and reversing one copy only stops at −i/2. The restoration theorem assumes c̄·c = 1 for every copy; for |c| < 1 reversing both copies leaves |c|⁴ times the start, and at |c| = 0 nothing: numbers of the page from the same formula, not consequences of that theorem. The fringe contrast on the screen is 2|ρ₀₁|, and the fringes shift with the phase of ρ₀₁.
  4. Waking. The dream state is ρ(λ) anchored at “every place”. The readout projection P₀ = |x₀⟩⟨x₀| keeps only the body cell, and the forgotten weight is 1 − Tr(P₀ρ) = 1 − |⟨x₀|Ψ⟩|²: P₀ commutes with the anchor blocks and pinching leaves the diagonal alone, so it does not depend on λ. Reading this number as “the dream forgotten on waking” is this book's interpretation (MODEL).
  5. What it is not. The page does not simulate a brain, nor does it show the golden cut-and-project and Delone theorems of chapter 37; the quantum-eraser experiments and sleep physiology are literature background (ARGUED).
