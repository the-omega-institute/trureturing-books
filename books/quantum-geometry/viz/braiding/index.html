理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

几颗粒子排在平面 (e₁, e₂) 里，时间朝上，每颗走出一条世界线。σk 让第 k、k + 1 颗绕它们的中点逆时针转半圈（从上往下看），左边那颗从前面过；σk⁻¹ 顺时针，右边那颗从前面过。“前面”“后面”就是第三个方向。每股绳子带一个颜色（ℤ/3 的元素），从底端往上传。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 颜色怎样过交叉。σk 把相邻两个颜色 (ck, ck+1) 换成 (ck ⋆ ck+1, ck)：前面那股带着原色走，后面那股被它改色；σk⁻¹ 把 (u, v) 换成 (v, v ⋆⁻¹ u)。两步互逆：反着再交换一次，颜色原样回来。默认的规则是 Fox 三染色 x ⋆ y = 2x − y（模 3）：两色相同不变，两色不同变成第三色。
  2. 同向交换两次，回不来。σ₁² 的两条世界线互相绕了整整一圈，颜色 (0, 1) 到顶端成了 (1, 2)。在平面里这一圈解不开；粒子若能离开平面，借第三个空间方向可以把这一圈抬过去解开（辫群与对称群之别，文献结果，未在本书形式化）。
  3. 三股的两种顺序。σ₁σ₂σ₁ 与 σ₂σ₁σ₂ 的世界线不一样，顶端的颜色却总相同：crossing_yang_baxter（LEAN ✓，对任意架成立）。
  4. 首尾接上，数回得来的着色。把第 k 个顶端接回第 k 个底端，颜色必须原样回来：闭合着色就是着色传输的不动点。三染色下 σ₁ 有 3 个，σ₁³ 有 9 个。在最右边添一股、只与原来最后一股交叉一次，新股只是把末位颜色抄一遍，个数不变；换一处剪开（共轭）也不变：有限幂等架的闭合着色数在 Markov 等价下不变，quandle_coloring_markov_invariant（LEAN ✓）。3 ≠ 9，所以 σ₁ 与 σ₁³ 不是 Markov 等价的；把它读成“三叶结不是平凡结”，要靠 Artin、Alexander、Markov 三条文献定理。
  5. 幂等公理省不得。换成 x ⋆ y = y + 1：它是架，却不满足 x ⋆ x = x。一股的平凡编织有 3 个闭合着色，添一股之后一个也没有。

A few particles sit in the plane (e₁, e₂) with time going up, each tracing a world line. σk turns particles k and k + 1 half a turn anticlockwise (seen from above) about their midpoint, the left one passing in front; σk⁻¹ turns them clockwise, the right one passing in front. “In front” and “behind” are the third direction. Each strand carries a color (an element of ℤ/3) from the bottom up. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. How colors pass a crossing. σk replaces the neighbouring colors (ck, ck+1) by (ck ⋆ ck+1, ck): the strand in front keeps its color, the one behind is recolored by it; σk⁻¹ replaces (u, v) by (v, v ⋆⁻¹ u). The two steps are inverse: exchanging back the other way returns the colors. The default rule is Fox's three-coloring x ⋆ y = 2x − y (mod 3): equal colors stay, two different colors give the third.
  2. Two exchanges the same way do not come back. In σ₁² the two world lines wind a full turn around each other, and the colors (0, 1) arrive at the top as (1, 2). In the plane this turn cannot be undone; if the particles could leave the plane, the third spatial direction would let the loop be lifted over and undone (the braid group versus the symmetric group, a literature result not formalized in this book).
  3. The two orders on three strands. σ₁σ₂σ₁ and σ₂σ₁σ₂ have different world lines, yet the colors at the top always agree: crossing_yang_baxter (LEAN ✓, for every rack).
  4. Close up and count what comes back. Join the k-th top end to the k-th bottom end, and the colors must come back as they were: closed colorings are the fixed points of the transport. With three colors σ₁ has 3 of them and σ₁³ has 9. Add a strand on the right that crosses the old last strand once, and the new strand merely copies the last color, so the count stays; cutting the loop elsewhere (conjugation) keeps it too: for a finite quandle the closed-coloring count is invariant under Markov equivalence, quandle_coloring_markov_invariant (LEAN ✓). 3 ≠ 9, so σ₁ and σ₁³ are not Markov equivalent; reading that as “the trefoil is not the unknot” takes the three literature theorems of Artin, Alexander and Markov.
  5. The idempotence axiom cannot be dropped. Switch to x ⋆ y = y + 1: a rack, but x ⋆ x ≠ x. The trivial one-strand braid has 3 closed colorings, and after adding a strand there are none.
