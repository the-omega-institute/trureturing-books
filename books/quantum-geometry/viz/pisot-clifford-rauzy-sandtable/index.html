  1. 齐肯多夫与 K-波那契。长为 Q、没有 K 个连续 1 的字恰有 dbonacci K (Q + 2) 个；K = 2 时是斐波那契数，这些字正是二进制齐肯多夫自动机读得完的字。字树把会凑满 K 个 1 的枝剪掉（品红 ×）。K = 2 时内部坐标 Σ εⱼψʲ（ψ = 1 − φ）落在黄金窗口 [−1, φ] 里。
  2. 皮索根。PK(X) = XK − (XK−1 + … + 1) 在 (1, 2) 里有唯一的根 βK，2 − βK = βK−K，K → ∞ 时 βK → 2；其余 K − 1 个复根都在开单位圆盘里。β₂ = φ ≈ 1.618034，β₃ ≈ 1.839287，β₄ ≈ 1.927562。
  3. p 进余数树。第 k 层的结点是模 pᵏ 的余数类，往下一层按低位数字分成 p 支。两个整数 x ≠ y 在前 vp(x − y) 层走同一条路，在第 vp(x − y) + 1 层第一次分开。有限视界 H 的距离 dH = H − 一致层数满足强三角不等式；它不是指数形式的 p 进度量，也不自动与劳齐动力系统共轭。
  4. 劳齐分形。K = 3，取 X³ − X² − X − 1 在上半平面的根 α ≈ −0.419643 + 0.606291i（|α| = β₃−1/2 ≈ 0.737353，arg α ≈ 124.69°）。R₃ 是所有有限合法和 Σ εⱼαʲ 的闭包，等于三支收缩 f₀(w) = αw、f₁₀(w) = 1 + α²w、f₁₁₀(w) = 1 + α + α³w 的像之并，内部非空，边界面积为零。页面上的点云只是有限深度的样本；平铺、连通、同胚于圆盘都不是这里的结论。

Chapter 39 puts eight concepts on one sandtable: the same 0/1 word ε with no K ones in a row, written as the polynomial e(X) = Σ εⱼXʲ, is an expanded real number at the Pisot root βK and a contracted complex number at a conjugate root α. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof; the lines drawn between the eight concepts are an interpretive model of this book, and only the green boxes with Lean links are verified theorems, each vouching for its own statement only.

  1. Zeckendorf and K-Bonacci. There are exactly dbonacci K (Q + 2) words of length Q with no K ones in a row; at K = 2 these are Fibonacci numbers, and the words are exactly those the binary Zeckendorf automaton runs through. The word tree prunes every branch that would complete K ones (magenta ×). At K = 2 the internal coordinate Σ εⱼψʲ (ψ = 1 − φ) lies in the golden window [−1, φ].
  2. The Pisot root. PK(X) = XK − (XK−1 + … + 1) has a unique root βK in (1, 2), with 2 − βK = βK−K and βK → 2 as K → ∞; the other K − 1 complex roots all lie in the open unit disc. β₂ = φ ≈ 1.618034, β₃ ≈ 1.839287, β₄ ≈ 1.927562.
  3. The p-adic residue tree. The nodes at level k are the residue classes mod pᵏ, and each splits into p by the next low digit. Two integers x ≠ y share the path for the first vp(x − y) levels and part for the first time at level vp(x − y) + 1. The finite-horizon distance dH = H − (levels in agreement) satisfies the strong triangle inequality; it is not the exponential p-adic metric, and it is not automatically conjugate to the Rauzy dynamics.
  4. The Rauzy fractal. K = 3, with the root α ≈ −0.419643 + 0.606291i of X³ − X² − X − 1 in the upper half-plane (|α| = β₃−1/2 ≈ 0.737353, arg α ≈ 124.69°). R₃ is the closure of all finite legal sums Σ εⱼαʲ; it equals the union of the images of the three contractions f₀(w) = αw, f₁₀(w) = 1 + α²w and f₁₁₀(w) = 1 + α + α³w, has nonempty interior and a boundary of zero area. The point cloud on the page is a sample of finite depth only; tiling, connectedness and being homeomorphic to a disc are not conclusions here.
  5. The Clifford sandtable. The vertical axis E∥ is e(β)·β−Q and the horizontal plane E⊥ is e(α). The same word is expanded vertically and contracted in the plane. These are algebraic coordinates declared by the page; the Clifford reading is a model.
  6. Self-reference. θn+1 = cos θn contracts to θ* ≈ 0.739085 (by about sin θ* ≈ 0.674 per step); αⁿ spirals in round the origin by the ratio |α| ≈ 0.737353. The two numbers differ by about 0.2344%, a numerical closeness and not an identity.
  7. Encoding across a cut. A word x of length mX on the left and a word y of length mY on the right join legally if and only if the number τ(x) of ones at the end of x plus the number ι(y) at the start of y is less than K. The page rank caps are R₀ = min(cmX,0, ⌊cmY,0/M₀⌋) and R₁ = min(cmX,1, mins (as + ⌊ns/M₁⌋)). For N = 2, K = 3, mX = 4, mY = 5 they are R₀ = 6 and R₁ = 5, and rank 3 is just one of the allowed choices.
  8. Entropy and escape. The Parry parameter p = βK⁻¹ satisfies p + … + pK = 1, and the law of the reset chain is stationary. The set E(S) of ordered pairs a catalog S cannot separate shrinks as observations are added; taking observation i away raises the escape rate by |Ui|/[q(q − 1)], which is positive if and only if |Ui| > 0. The topological entropy log₂ βK and Abramov’s formula are literature results; the fractal horizon area law is a model.
