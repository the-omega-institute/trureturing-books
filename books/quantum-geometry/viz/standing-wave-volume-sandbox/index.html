理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

舞台是单位球 S² ⊂ ℝ³ 上的极小球面扇形 ΔT_ε 与汇到同一点的比较路径。本页是数值演示，不是证明：每个数都由页面当场算出（NUM），定理只在下面写明的那一个实例上演示。

  1. 扇形。A = (1, 0, 0)、B = (cos ε, sin ε, 0)、C = (cos ε, 0, sin ε)：A 处两条直角边的弧长都是 ε。立体角 Ω = 2 arctan(tan²(ε/2))，扇形体积 Ω/3，弦四面体 OABC 的体积 sin²ε/6。
  2. 多路一点。M 条比较路径从 A 出发，汇到同一点 x；第 k 条带着相位 kδ 到达（k = 0, …, M − 1，δ = n·Δγ）。平均 Ψ_M = (1/M) Σ eikδ，|Ψ_M|² = (1/M²) Σa,b cos((a − b)δ)：全部路径对 Γ_a ∘ Γ_b⁻¹ 的回路相位 γ_B = (a − b)δ 的余弦平均。页面把两种算法分别算出。
  3. 规范与回路。把路径写成多重图（根 A、M 个途经点、终点 x）：边 2M 条、点 M + 2 个，基本回路 2M + 1 − (M + 2) = M − 1 个。终点处的规范相位 θ 让所有到达相位一起转（selected_tree_transport）；规范不变的只有回路相位（anchored_phase_classification），所以 |Ψ_M| 不随 θ 变。
  4. 闭环的相位。闭合回路的相位是沿途内积之积的辐角（bargmann_path_product）。三角回路 A → B → C → A 的巴格曼不变量 Tr(P_A P_B P_C) = (1 + A·B + B·C + C·A)/4 + i·A·(B × C)/4（triple_bargmann_bloch）：虚部 sin²ε/4 是弦四面体体积的 3/2 倍，辐角等于 Ω/2（文献结果）。
  5. 虚空。黄金步长 Δγ = 2π(√5 − 1)/2 与整圈不可公度，到达相位按哈尔测度铺满圆周，Ψ_M → 0（continuous_average_tendsto_haar）。闭式 |Ψ_M| = |sin(Mδ/2)/(M sin(δ/2))| ≤ 1/(M |sin(δ/2)|)。
  6. 回声与筛子。沿闭合回路绕 M 圈，每圈带回步长 δ。δ 是 2π 的整数倍时全部同相，|Ψ_M|² = 1；其余处随 M 增大被压低，梳状的峰落在 Δγ = 2πk/n。Z/12Z 里 H = {0, 3, 6, 9}、H⊥ = {0, 4, 8}：Σk∈H⊥ e2πikx/12 在 x ∈ H 时为 3，否则为 0（sum_annihilator_character），恰是 3·Ψ₃(2πx/3)。
  7. 驻波。Ψ_M = ei(M−1)δ/2·r，r = sin(Mδ/2)/(M sin(δ/2)) 是实数（phase_locked_overlap_is_rotated_real）：合成振幅锁在一条直线上伸缩。
  8. 体积的涌现（模型）。扇形按重心三分、径向三层切成 27 个胞，扇形外另有 36 个背景胞，各胞的权重 w = 立体角 × (ρ₁³ − ρ₀³)/3，扇形内权重之和正好是 Ω/3。扇形内的点取滑块的步长，扇形外的点取与整圈不可公度的步长 Δγj。涌现体积 V = Σ|Ψ_M|²w：M 增大时背景抵消，V 收拢到 Ω/3。
  9. 依据。页面引用七条已冻结的 Lean 定理（LEAN ✓）：selected_tree_transport、anchored_phase_classification、bargmann_path_product、triple_bargmann_bloch、continuous_average_tendsto_haar、sum_annihilator_character、phase_locked_overlap_is_rotated_real。它们是一般陈述，页面只演示它们在这里的样子；立体角公式、扇形体积、几何相位与玻尔–索末菲条件是文献结果（ARGUED）；等距相位、候选格点、背景步长与“体积从驻波中涌现”是模型（MODEL）。尚未解决的问题不在页面上声称。

书中：第三十四章 · 相位积分驻波成体与三维体积涌现

The stage is the minimal spherical sector ΔT_ε of the unit sphere S² ⊂ ℝ³ and comparison paths converging on one point. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof: every number is computed on the spot (NUM), and each theorem is shown only on the one instance written below.

  1. The sector. A = (1, 0, 0), B = (cos ε, sin ε, 0), C = (cos ε, 0, sin ε): the two right-angle legs at A both have arc length ε. The solid angle is Ω = 2 arctan(tan²(ε/2)), the sector volume Ω/3, and the chord tetrahedron OABC has volume sin²ε/6.
  2. Many paths, one point. M comparison paths leave A and converge on one point x; path k arrives with phase kδ (k = 0, …, M − 1, δ = n·Δγ). The average is Ψ_M = (1/M) Σ eikδ, and |Ψ_M|² = (1/M²) Σa,b cos((a − b)δ): the mean cosine of the loop phases γ_B = (a − b)δ of all pairs of paths Γ_a ∘ Γ_b⁻¹. The page computes both ways separately.
  3. Gauge and loops. Written as a multigraph (root A, M waypoints, end point x) the paths have 2M edges and M + 2 vertices, hence 2M + 1 − (M + 2) = M − 1 basic loops. The gauge phase θ at the end point turns every arrival phase together (selected_tree_transport); only the loop phases are gauge invariant (anchored_phase_classification), so |Ψ_M| does not change with θ.
  4. The phase of a closed loop. The phase of a closed loop is the argument of the product of the overlaps along it (bargmann_path_product). For the triangle loop A → B → C → A, the Bargmann invariant is Tr(P_A P_B P_C) = (1 + A·B + B·C + C·A)/4 + i·A·(B × C)/4 (triple_bargmann_bloch): the imaginary part sin²ε/4 is 3/2 times the volume of the chord tetrahedron, and the argument equals Ω/2 (a literature result).
  5. The void. The golden step Δγ = 2π(√5 − 1)/2 is incommensurate with a full turn: the arrival phases fill the circle by Haar measure and Ψ_M → 0 (continuous_average_tendsto_haar). In closed form |Ψ_M| = |sin(Mδ/2)/(M sin(δ/2))| ≤ 1/(M |sin(δ/2)|).
  6. Echoes and the sieve. Going M times round a closed loop brings back the step δ each turn. When δ is a whole multiple of 2π every echo agrees and |Ψ_M|² = 1; elsewhere the echoes are pressed down as M grows, and the peaks of the comb sit at Δγ = 2πk/n. In Z/12Z, H = {0, 3, 6, 9} and H⊥ = {0, 4, 8}: Σk∈H⊥ e2πikx/12 is 3 for x ∈ H and 0 otherwise (sum_annihilator_character), which is exactly 3·Ψ₃(2πx/3).
  7. The standing wave. Ψ_M = ei(M−1)δ/2·r with r = sin(Mδ/2)/(M sin(δ/2)) real (phase_locked_overlap_is_rotated_real): the resultant amplitude stretches along one locked line.
  8. Emergent volume (a model). The sector is cut barycentrically in three and radially in three shells into 27 cells, with 36 background cells around it; each cell weighs w = solid angle × (ρ₁³ − ρ₀³)/3, and the weights inside the sector add up to exactly Ω/3. Points inside the sector take the slider’s step, points outside take a step Δγj incommensurate with a full turn. The emergent volume is V = Σ|Ψ_M|²w: as M grows the background cancels and V settles on Ω/3.
  9. Evidence. The page cites seven frozen Lean theorems (LEAN ✓): selected_tree_transport, anchored_phase_classification, bargmann_path_product, triple_bargmann_bloch, continuous_average_tendsto_haar, sum_annihilator_character and phase_locked_overlap_is_rotated_real. They are general statements; the page only shows what they look like here. The solid-angle formula, the sector volume, the geometric phase and the Bohr–Sommerfeld condition are literature results (ARGUED); the evenly spaced phases, the candidate lattice, the background steps and “volume emerges from the standing wave” are models (MODEL). The page claims no unsolved problem.
