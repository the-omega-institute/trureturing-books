  1. 均匀的本体。125 个银白格点排成 5 × 5 × 5 的立方晶格，间距 a。每个格点与观察者寄存器里的一个比特配成贝尔对 (|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2，整体是这些对的积，是纯态，S(Ψ) = 0；可每个格点对另一半求偏迹都得同一个 ½·1：Tr ρ = 1，相邻格点之差为 0。只看格点自己，看不出任何方向、任何花样。热积态 ⊗½·1 的边缘一模一样，熵却是 125 ln 2，条件化之后也读不出条纹。
  2. 观察者的切面。本体格的记录方向沿 z 以周期 a 转动；观察者的格是它绕 x 轴转过 θ 的副本。切面 Σ 取两者的角平分面，所以它倾斜 θ/2，两组波矢之差 Δk 落在面内，|Δk| = (4π/a) sin(θ/2)。切面上每一点读的是两只半角旋量的玻恩配对 Islice = |⟨χont|χobs⟩|² = cos²ΔΦ = (1 + cos(Δk·x − φ))/2：青色是峰（→ 1），紫色是盲区（→ 0），Islice + I⊥ = 1。条纹周期 LM = 2π/|Δk| = a/(2 sin(θ/2))：θ = 36° 时 1.618a，θ = 1.05° 时 54.568a。
  3. 两枚透镜。同一组参数下，本体透镜按无条件的边缘读，切面处处 Tr(½·1·P) = ½，平而无纹；切面透镜经观察者寄存器条件化，读出条纹。花纹不在格点里，在关联里。

This is a numerical sandbox, not a proof. Every number is computed on the page (NUM); each Lean theorem is shown only on the one instance named in the evidence panel; the drawing of the nodes, the slice and the dark ring is the reading of this book (MODEL).

  1. A uniform ontology. 125 silver nodes form a 5 × 5 × 5 cubic lattice of spacing a. Each node is paired with a qubit of the observer register into the Bell pair (|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2, and the whole is the product of these pairs, a pure state with S(Ψ) = 0; yet tracing out the other half at any node gives the same ½·1: Tr ρ = 1, and neighbouring nodes differ by 0. The nodes by themselves show no direction and no pattern. The thermal product ⊗½·1 has exactly the same marginals, but entropy 125 ln 2, and no fringes appear after conditioning.
  2. The observer slice. The record direction of the field lattice turns along z with period a; the observer lattice is a copy turned by θ about the x axis. The slice Σ is the bisector plane of the two, so it leans by θ/2, and the difference of the wave vectors Δk lies in it, |Δk| = (4π/a) sin(θ/2). Every point of the slice reads the Born pairing of two half-angle spinors, Islice = |⟨χont|χobs⟩|² = cos²ΔΦ = (1 + cos(Δk·x − φ))/2: cyan marks the peaks (→ 1), violet the blind zones (→ 0), and Islice + I⊥ = 1. The fringe period is LM = 2π/|Δk| = a/(2 sin(θ/2)): 1.618a at θ = 36° and 54.568a at θ = 1.05°.
  3. Two lenses. With the same parameters, the ontology lens reads the unconditioned marginals, Tr(½·1·P) = ½ everywhere on the slice, flat and without fringes; the slice lens conditions on the observer register and reads the fringes. The pattern is not in the nodes; it is in the correlations.
  4. The sweep. As φ runs once round, the fringes glide by one period along Δk, with pattern speed vM = ω/|Δk|; not one of the 125 nodes moves, ‖Δx‖ = 0. Moving where the patterns meet carries nothing and sends no signal; dynamical group velocities still obey vg ≤ c.
  5. The orthogonal dark ring. The magenta ring stands perpendicular to the slice and, in the record basis of the slice, at 90° to the record axis: pinching keeps a diagonal mass of ½ and discards a coherence mass of ½, so the contrast the slice reads is IEM(90°) = 0. Unseen, it still draws on the clock budget: dτ/dt = √(1 − ηslice² − ηdark²).
  6. The aperture. Average the readings of W consecutive cells along Δk: the phase step per cell is α = 2 sin(θ/2), the irrational (√5 − 1)/2 at θ = 36°, and the average tends to ½ in the manner of Weyl and Haar. The contrast |DW| = |sin(Wπα)/(W sin(πα))| does not fall cell by cell: for α not an integer (θ < 60° on this page) it oscillates beneath the decaying envelope 1/(W sin πα) towards 0, at θ = 36° 0.1582 for W = 3, back up to 0.2672 for W = 4 and 0.0486 for W = 20; at θ = 60°, α = 1, every cell is one full period on, and the contrast stays 1.
