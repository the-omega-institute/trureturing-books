这是数值沙盘，不是证明。每个数都由页面当场算出（NUM）；每条 Lean 定理只在依据栏写明的那一个实例上演示。

  1. 一对与它的施密特角。每条弧是一对比特的纯态 |ψ(θ)⟩ = cos θ|00⟩ + sin θ|11⟩，0 ≤ θ ≤ π/4。视界只看见其中一个比特时，它的边缘是 ρA = diag(cos²θ, sin²θ)：纯度 cos⁴θ + sin⁴θ，熵 SA = −cos²θ ln cos²θ − sin²θ ln sin²θ（0 ln 0 = 0），比特数 SA/ln 2，到 I/2 的相对熵 ln 2 − SA。θ = 0 是积态 |00⟩（SA = 0），θ = π/4 是贝尔态（SA = 1 比特）。
  2. 十二比特的切口。六对 (1, 12)、(2, 11)、…、(6, 7) 彼此独立且各自是纯态，十二比特的整体仍是纯态，Sglobal = 0。视界罩住 A = {1, …, NA} 时，恰有 min(NA, 12 − NA) 对横跨边界，其余每对或者整对在内、或者整对在外，对 A 的熵没有贡献，所以 S(A) = min(NA, 12 − NA) · SA(θ)：θ = 45° 时依次为 0、2、4、6、4、2、0 比特，在 NA = 6 处达到 6 比特，NA = 12 时回到 0。这是特意安排的配对模型，不是随机纯态的哈尔平均（Page 1993），相邻配对会给出别的切口。
  3. 谱匹配温度。取 H = diag(0, ΔE)，令吉布斯态 e−βH/Z 的谱等于 ρA：βΔE = ln(cos²θ / sin²θ) = 2 ln cot θ，kBTeff/ΔE = 1/(2 ln cot θ)。θ = 0 时 βΔE = +∞、温度为 0（极限）；θ = 45° 时 βΔE = 0、温度为 +∞（极限）；θ = 30° 时 kBTeff/ΔE = 0.910239。这只是一种吉布斯参数化，不认定任何物理热浴的温度。
  4. 模流与第一定律。模哈密顿量 KA = −ln ρA = βH + ln Z。模流 e−isKA 让 |0⟩⟨1| 带上相位 eis·2 ln cot θ，ρA 与 SA 本身不动——时间轴上紫色相位珠在品红切口上绕圈，而条带小图里熵是一条平线。沿 θ 变化，dSA = βΔE · d(sin²θ)，dE = ΔE · d(sin²θ)，于是 dE = kBTeff dSA（纠缠第一定律）。把 Teff 代进安鲁公式 T = ħa/(2πckB) 得到等效加速度 aU = 2π(kBTeff/ΔE) · ΔE c/ħ，θ = 30° 时是 5.719202 ΔE c/ħ——这只是换算。富田—竹崎、比索尼亚诺—威奇曼与安鲁效应本身只作为文献背景（依据栏 ARGUED）。
  5. 朗道尔热。把一个比特从最大混合复位到 |0⟩，热浴在温度 T 时至少放热 kBT ln 2；页面示意地取 T = Teff，给出 (kBTeff/ΔE) ln 2 个 ΔE，θ = 30° 时是 0.630930 ΔE。依据栏的 Lean 恒等式在理想复位 I = 0、D = 0 上演示：βQ = −ΔSsys = SA。
  6. 余弦不动点。θ* = cos θ* = 0.73908513 rad = 42.346459° 落在 0° 与 45° 之间：cos²θ* = 0.546247、sin²θ* = 0.453753，序偏 cos 2θ* = 9.25%，SA = 0.993820 比特，kBTeff/ΔE = 5.390324。42.35° 只是四舍五入的近似（差 0.003541°）。把它读成冻石与热寂之间的“活的稳态”是本书的诠释，页面不断言 θ* 在物理上被选中。

书中：§34.17 受限不妄我、跨视界纠缠与热力学涌现

This is a numerical sandbox, not a proof. Every number is computed on the page (NUM); each Lean theorem is shown only on the one instance named in the evidence panel.

  1. A pair and its Schmidt angle. Each arc is the pure state of two qubits |ψ(θ)⟩ = cos θ|00⟩ + sin θ|11⟩, 0 ≤ θ ≤ π/4. When the horizon sees only one of its qubits, the marginal is ρA = diag(cos²θ, sin²θ): purity cos⁴θ + sin⁴θ, entropy SA = −cos²θ ln cos²θ − sin²θ ln sin²θ (0 ln 0 = 0), SA/ln 2 bits, and relative entropy ln 2 − SA to I/2. θ = 0 is the product state |00⟩ (SA = 0), θ = π/4 a Bell state (SA = 1 bit).
  2. Cutting twelve qubits. The six pairs (1, 12), (2, 11), …, (6, 7) are independent and each is pure, so all twelve qubits are in a pure state, Sglobal = 0. With the horizon over A = {1, …, NA}, exactly min(NA, 12 − NA) pairs straddle the boundary; every other pair lies wholly inside or wholly outside and adds nothing to the entropy of A, so S(A) = min(NA, 12 − NA) · SA(θ): at θ = 45° it runs 0, 2, 4, 6, 4, 2, 0 bits, reaching 6 bits at NA = 6 and returning to 0 at NA = 12. This is an explicitly arranged pair model, not the Haar average over random pure states (Page 1993); adjacent pairs would give other cuts.
  3. Spectrum-matched temperature. Take H = diag(0, ΔE) and match the spectrum of the Gibbs state e−βH/Z to ρA: βΔE = ln(cos²θ / sin²θ) = 2 ln cot θ and kBTeff/ΔE = 1/(2 ln cot θ). At θ = 0, βΔE = +∞ and the temperature is 0 (a limit); at θ = 45°, βΔE = 0 and the temperature is +∞ (a limit); at θ = 30°, kBTeff/ΔE = 0.910239. This is only a Gibbs parametrization; it identifies no physical bath temperature.
  4. Modular flow and the first law. The modular Hamiltonian is KA = −ln ρA = βH + ln Z. The modular flow e−isKA gives |0⟩⟨1| the phase eis·2 ln cot θ and leaves ρA and SA themselves unchanged: along the rail the violet phase beads circle the magenta cuts while the entropy in the strip plot stays flat. Along θ, dSA = βΔE · d(sin²θ) and dE = ΔE · d(sin²θ), so dE = kBTeff dSA (the entanglement first law). Putting Teff into the Unruh formula T = ħa/(2πckB) gives the equivalent acceleration aU = 2π(kBTeff/ΔE) · ΔE c/ħ, 5.719202 ΔE c/ħ at θ = 30° — a conversion only. Tomita–Takesaki theory, the Bisognano–Wichmann theorem and the Unruh effect themselves are literature background only (ARGUED in the evidence panel).
  5. Landauer heat. Resetting one maximally mixed bit to |0⟩ with a bath at temperature T releases at least kBT ln 2 of heat; the page schematically takes T = Teff, which gives (kBTeff/ΔE) ln 2 in units of ΔE, 0.630930 ΔE at θ = 30°. The Lean identity of the evidence panel is shown on the ideal reset I = 0, D = 0: βQ = −ΔSsys = SA.
  6. The cosine fixed point. θ* = cos θ* = 0.73908513 rad = 42.346459° lies between 0° and 45°: cos²θ* = 0.546247, sin²θ* = 0.453753, order bias cos 2θ* = 9.25%, SA = 0.993820 bits and kBTeff/ΔE = 5.390324. 42.35° is only a rounded value (off by 0.003541°). Reading it as a living steady state between the frozen stone and heat death is the interpretation of this book; the page does not claim that θ* is physically selected.
