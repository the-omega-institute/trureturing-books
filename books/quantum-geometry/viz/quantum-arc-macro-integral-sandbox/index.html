极小圆弧与宏观积分Minimal arcs and the macroscopic integral

这是数值演示与示意模型，不是证明。右栏九条 Lean 验证各自只担保它的冻结陈述；把它们拼成“微弧缝合宏观物理”的叙事属于模型解读。

弧与矢高：起点 A、终点 B 相隔 ε，切线长 sin ε，弧比切线鼓出 1 − cos ε = 2 sin²(ε/2)。费曼—伊东模式 ε = Θ/√N：N(1 − cos ε) → Θ²/2（Θ = 1 时 N = 1…256 全部大于 0.45）；牛顿模式 ε = Θ/N：N(1 − cos ε) ≈ Θ²/(2N) → 0。伊东链画成三角锯齿：偶数顶点在脊线上，奇数顶点交替偏出 a = arccos(cos ε / cos(Θ/N))，每段恰为 ε。

路径束：41 条路径 λₘ(s) = ηₘ·4s(1 − s)，ηₘ = 0.4Θ(m − 20)/20，在 (ψ, λ) 经纬坐标里从 A = (−Θ/2, 0) 拱到 B = (Θ/2, 0)。相位 Φₘ = k[L(ηₘ) − L(0) − 2βΩ(ηₘ)/Θ]，k = 30/Θ，L 是球面长度，Ω 是与测地线之间的带号面积，由沿路径再沿测地线闭合的 Bargmann 旋量内积连乘的辐角给出（Ω = −2 arg Π）。β = 0 时 Φ 是 η 的偶函数、Ω 是奇函数，±Ω 成对相消，驻相点 η* = 0；β ≠ 0 时 η* 与 β 同号。

斯托克斯核对：同一块球面小片，∮A 由边界多边形的转角按高斯—博内算出，∬F 由扇形三角剖分的立体角相加算出，Bargmann 连乘给第三条路线，三者互不调用。熵税：从 |0⟩ 出发，每步绕 y 轴转 ε 再在 z 基底捏合，z 分量乘以 cos ε。

This is a numerical illustration and an illustrative model, not a proof. Each of the nine Lean results in the side panel supports only its own frozen statement; stitching them into a story of micro-arcs building macroscopic physics is a model reading.

Arc and sagitta: start A and end B are ε apart; the tangent has length sin ε and the arc bulges past it by 1 − cos ε = 2 sin²(ε/2). Feynman–Itô mode ε = Θ/√N: N(1 − cos ε) → Θ²/2 (above 0.45 for every N = 1…256 at Θ = 1); Newton mode ε = Θ/N: N(1 − cos ε) ≈ Θ²/(2N) → 0. The Itô chain is drawn as a triangular sawtooth: even vertices on the spine, odd vertices alternately offset by a = arccos(cos ε / cos(Θ/N)), so that every step is exactly ε.

Path bundle: 41 paths λₘ(s) = ηₘ·4s(1 − s), ηₘ = 0.4Θ(m − 20)/20, arch in (ψ, λ) coordinates from A = (−Θ/2, 0) to B = (Θ/2, 0). Phase Φₘ = k[L(ηₘ) − L(0) − 2βΩ(ηₘ)/Θ] with k = 30/Θ, L the spherical length and Ω the signed area to the geodesic, read from the argument of the Bargmann product of spinor overlaps along the path closed by the geodesic (Ω = −2 arg Π). At β = 0, Φ is even in η and Ω is odd, the ±Ω pairs cancel and the stationary point is η* = 0; for β ≠ 0, η* has the sign of β.

Stokes check: for the same spherical patch, ∮A comes from the turning angles of the boundary polygon by Gauss–Bonnet and ∬F from the solid angles of a fan triangulation, and the Bargmann product gives a third route; none of them calls another. Entropy tax: from |0⟩, each step turns by ε about the y axis and pinches in the z basis, multiplying the z component by cos ε.
