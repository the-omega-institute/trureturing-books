理论接口 · 格拉姆窗口与共识Theory interface · Gram window and consensus

这是数值沙盘，不是证明。每个数都由页面当场算出（NUM）；每条 Lean 定理只在依据栏写明的那一个实例上演示。

  1. 窗口与相干帽。观察者记得最近的 M 条记录 v₁（最新）… vM（最旧），取黄金角螺旋：vj 的极角 12° + 6°(j − 1)、方位角 (j − 1)·π(3 − √5)（模型选择）。每条记录只允许下一步方向 x 落在相干帽 x·vj ≥ cos αcoh 里，走廊 ΩM 是 M 顶帽的交集。时间轴是记得多深：两个整数之间，最旧那顶帽从刚好包住上一条走廊的半角缓缓收到 αcoh（过渡画面，MODEL）；整数处窗口精确。
  2. 面积。|ΩM| 由高斯—博内公式精确算出：求出两两边界小圆的可行交点，留下落在其余各帽里的边界弧，面积 = 2πχ − Σ h Δt − Σ 顶点外角，h = cos αcoh，Δt 是弧绕帽轴转过的角，外角 = arccos((vi·vj − h²)/(1 − h²))。αcoh = 60° 时 |Ω₁| = π = 3.141593 sr，|Ω₃| = 1.802272 sr（球面的 14.3420%），|Ω₉| = 0.054604 sr（0.4345%），|Ω₁₀| = 0。页面另以斐波那契点求积核对。
  3. 舒尔补。加边格拉姆矩阵 GM+1 = [[GM, c], [cᵀ, 1]]，cj = vj·x；舒尔补 Δ = 1 − cᵀGM⁺c 等于 x 在记录张成空间之外那部分的平方长度 ‖PS⊥x‖²，且 det GM+1 = det GM · Δ。M = 2 时 Δ = sin²δ，δ 是 x 离开虚线大圆 span(v₁, v₂) 的角；M ≥ 3 时 v₁、v₂、v₃ 张满 ℝ³，Δ = 0（M > 3 时 GM 奇异，用伪逆）。等相关族 G = (2/3)I + (1/3)11ᵀ、c = ½1：Q = 3M/(4(M + 2))，Δ = (M + 8)/(4(M + 2)) 始终大于 1/4，永不过约束；正交族 G = IM、c = ½1：Δ = 1 − M/4，M = 4 时恰为 0，M = 5 时为 −1/4，不可实现。这两族要 M + 1 维以上的空间，不画在 S² 上。
  4. 遗忘。遗忘最旧的记录 vM 就撤掉它那顶帽，走廊重开 qM = ln(|ΩM−1|/|ΩM|)。αcoh = 60° 时 q₉ = 1.338960 nats = 1.931710 bits，而且 q₁ + … + q₉ = ln(4π/|Ω₉|)。不在走廊边界上的帽是冗余的，撤掉它 q = 0。把 qM 读成以 kBT 为单位的热量下界，需要均匀可达体积的记忆模型与等温重置（MODEL）；单凭几何收缩不能断言耗散了热量。
  5. 两位观察者。B 的记录 wj = Ry(η)vj，两人同用一个 αcoh；共识是 2M 个不等式的交集 ΩA ∩ ΩB。η = 10° 时，M = 3 的共识是 1.570105 sr（A 走廊的 87.1181%），M = 9 时只剩 0.010419 sr（19.0813%）；η = 35° 时 M = 9 的共识为空，回到 M = 3 又有 1.000488 sr。块格拉姆 K = WWᵀ（W 是 2M × 3 的坐标矩阵）恒半正定且秩 ≤ 3，所以 2M > 3 时 λmin(K) = 0 精确成立；K 奇异并不意味着共识为空。

书中：§34.18 格拉姆窗口、舒尔补可行走廊与多观察者共识

This is a numerical sandbox, not a proof. Every number is computed on the page (NUM); each Lean theorem is shown only on the one instance named in the evidence panel.

  1. Window and coherence caps. The observer keeps its M most recent records v₁ (newest) … vM (oldest), a golden-angle spiral: vj at colatitude 12° + 6°(j − 1) and azimuth (j − 1)·π(3 − √5) (a model choice). Each record lets the next direction x lie only in its coherence cap x·vj ≥ cos αcoh, and the corridor ΩM is the intersection of the M caps. The rail is how deep the memory reaches: between two integers the oldest cap eases from the half-angle that just holds the previous corridor down to αcoh (a transition picture, MODEL); at integers the window is exact.
  2. Area. |ΩM| is exact by Gauss–Bonnet: find the feasible crossings of the boundary circles, keep the boundary arcs that lie in every other cap, and area = 2πχ − Σ h Δt − Σ exterior angles, with h = cos αcoh, Δt the angle of an arc about its cap axis and exterior angle arccos((vi·vj − h²)/(1 − h²)). At αcoh = 60°, |Ω₁| = π = 3.141593 sr, |Ω₃| = 1.802272 sr (14.3420% of the sphere), |Ω₉| = 0.054604 sr (0.4345%) and |Ω₁₀| = 0. The page cross-checks with Fibonacci-point quadrature.
  3. Schur margin. The bordered Gram matrix GM+1 = [[GM, c], [cᵀ, 1]] has cj = vj·x; its Schur margin Δ = 1 − cᵀGM⁺c is the squared length ‖PS⊥x‖² of the part of x outside the span of the records, and det GM+1 = det GM · Δ. For M = 2, Δ = sin²δ with δ the angle of x off the dashed great circle span(v₁, v₂); for M ≥ 3, v₁, v₂, v₃ span ℝ³ and Δ = 0 (for M > 3, GM is singular and the pseudoinverse is used). Equicorrelated family G = (2/3)I + (1/3)11ᵀ, c = ½1: Q = 3M/(4(M + 2)) and Δ = (M + 8)/(4(M + 2)) stays above 1/4, never over-constrained; orthonormal family G = IM, c = ½1: Δ = 1 − M/4, exactly 0 at M = 4 and −1/4 at M = 5, which cannot be realized. These two families need more than M dimensions and are not drawn on S².
  4. Forgetting. Forgetting the oldest record vM releases its cap, and the corridor reopens by qM = ln(|ΩM−1|/|ΩM|). At αcoh = 60°, q₉ = 1.338960 nats = 1.931710 bits, and q₁ + … + q₉ = ln(4π/|Ω₉|). A cap that is not on the boundary of the corridor is redundant: releasing it gives q = 0. Reading qM as a lower bound on heat in units of kBT needs a memory model of uniform accessible volume and an isothermal reset (MODEL); the geometric shrinking alone does not establish that any heat was dissipated.
  5. Two observers. The records of B are wj = Ry(η)vj, with the same αcoh; their consensus is the intersection ΩA ∩ ΩB of 2M inequalities. At η = 10° the consensus is 1.570105 sr for M = 3 (87.1181% of the corridor of A) and only 0.010419 sr for M = 9 (19.0813%); at η = 35° the consensus is empty for M = 9 and back to 1.000488 sr for M = 3. The block Gram matrix K = WWᵀ (W the 2M × 3 matrix of coordinates) is positive semidefinite of rank ≤ 3, so λmin(K) = 0 holds exactly when 2M > 3; a singular K does not mean an empty consensus.
