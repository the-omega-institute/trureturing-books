舞台是单位比较球面 S² ⊂ ℝ³ 上的一块直角三面角原胞 ΔT_ε。本页是数值演示，不是证明：每个数都由页面当场算出（NUM），定理只在下面写明的那一个实例上演示。

  1. 原胞。O = (0, 0, 0)，转点 B = e₂，A(ε) = (sin ε, cos ε, 0) 在墙 e₂∧e₁ 里，C(ε) = (0, cos ε, sin ε) 在墙 e₂∧e₃ 里。三面平墙 OBA、OBC、OAC 加一块外凸的球面帽 △ABC 围成三面角原胞；A∧B∧C = sin²ε e₁e₂e₃。
  2. 正交一次（沿棱看）。沿 OB 正投影到 e₁∧e₃ 平面，O 与 B 落在同一点，两面墙变成长 sin ε 的两条腿，A⊥ = (sin ε, 0)、C⊥ = (0, sin ε)。一次的弧 AC 满足 cos c_ε = A·C = cos²ε，c_ε = arccos(cos²ε) = √2 ε (1 − ε²/12 + O(ε⁴)) < √2 ε：平直的勾股只在一阶成立。
  3. 正交两次（沿墙走）。先在墙 e₁∧e₂ 里从 A 走到 B，在 B 处拐 β = 90°，再在墙 e₂∧e₃ 里走到 C。两枚墙旋子 U₁₂(ε) = cos(ε/2) − sin(ε/2) e₁e₂、U₂₃(ε) = cos(ε/2) − sin(ε/2) e₂e₃ 之积 U₂₃U₁₂ = cos²(ε/2) − sin(ε/2)cos(ε/2)(e₁e₂ + e₂e₃) − sin²(ε/2) e₁e₃，四个分量的平方和为 (cos²(ε/2) + sin²(ε/2))² = 1。次序对调只改变 e₁e₃ 的符号：U₂₃U₁₂ − U₁₂U₂₃ = −2 sin²(ε/2) e₁e₃。
  4. 球面帽与平直削面。平直削面 △ABC 的三个内角和为 180°，B 处 cos β_flat = sin²(ε/2)，β_flat < 90°。球面帽上 β = 90°、α = γ = arctan(sec ε) = π/4 + ε²/4 + O(ε⁴) > 45°，角超 Ω(ε) = α + β + γ − π = 2 arctan(tan²(ε/2)) = ε²/2 + ε⁴/12 + O(ε⁶) > 0。
  5. 两件要分开的事。（a）精确：沿测地三角形 A → B → C → A 平行移动，箭头转过 Ω(ε)；三枚边旋子之积 U_CA U₂₃ U₁₂ = exp(−(Ω/2) A⋆)，自旋 1/2 的相位是 −Ω/2，对 (0, π/2] 里每个 ε 都成立。（b）近似：只走两面坐标墙的 U₂₃U₁₂ 里，e₁e₃ 分量 −sin²(ε/2) ≈ −ε²/4 与 −Ω/2 ≈ −ε²/4 只在最低阶 O(ε²) 一致；ε 变大两者分开（ε = 48° 时 −0.165 对 −0.196，ε = 90° 时 −0.5 对 −π/4 ≈ −0.785）。在八分球极限 ε = 90°，三条边都落在坐标面里，三枚坐标旋子之积正好是 exp(−(π/4) e₂e₃)，Ω = π/2。
  6. 格拉姆体积砖。G(ΔT_ε) = [[1, cos ε, cos²ε], [cos ε, 1, cos ε], [cos²ε, cos ε, 1]]，det G = (1 − cos²ε)² = sin⁴ε > 0，平行六面体体积 √det G = sin²ε（四面体 sin²ε / 6，球扇形 Ω/3）。共面两步 A_cop = (sin ε, cos ε, 0)、B、C_cop = (−sin ε, cos ε, 0) 的 A_cop·C_cop = cos 2ε，det G 恒为 0：困在一个圆里，没有体积。
  7. 冻结定理。页面引用六条已冻结的 Lean 定理（LEAN ✓）：unit_perpendicular_pair、two_axis_finite_word、partition_inseparable_iff_kernel、covariant_commutator_formula、lagrange_gram_identity、finite_layer_projection_escape。它们是一般陈述，页面只演示它们在这块原胞上的样子；球面三角、吉拉尔、高斯—博内、贝里相位、旋子代数与 Regge 单纯形是文献结果（ARGUED），“两个投影”的说法与 ε_min 是模型（MODEL）。

The stage is one right-angled trihedron cell ΔT_ε on the unit comparison sphere S² ⊂ ℝ³. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof: every number is computed on the spot (NUM), and each theorem is shown only on the one instance written below.

  1. The cell. O = (0, 0, 0), the pivot B = e₂, A(ε) = (sin ε, cos ε, 0) in the wall e₂∧e₁ and C(ε) = (0, cos ε, sin ε) in the wall e₂∧e₃. Three flat walls OBA, OBC, OAC and an outward-bulging spherical cap △ABC bound the trihedron cell; A∧B∧C = sin²ε e₁e₂e₃.
  2. Orthogonal once (down the ridge). Project along OB onto the plane e₁∧e₃: O and B land on one point, the two walls become two legs of length sin ε, A⊥ = (sin ε, 0) and C⊥ = (0, sin ε). The once arc AC has cos c_ε = A·C = cos²ε, c_ε = arccos(cos²ε) = √2 ε (1 − ε²/12 + O(ε⁴)) < √2 ε: flat Pythagoras holds only to first order.
  3. Orthogonal twice (along the walls). First walk from A to B in the wall e₁∧e₂, turn β = 90° at B, then walk to C in the wall e₂∧e₃. The two wall rotors U₁₂(ε) = cos(ε/2) − sin(ε/2) e₁e₂ and U₂₃(ε) = cos(ε/2) − sin(ε/2) e₂e₃ multiply to U₂₃U₁₂ = cos²(ε/2) − sin(ε/2)cos(ε/2)(e₁e₂ + e₂e₃) − sin²(ε/2) e₁e₃, whose four parts have squares summing to (cos²(ε/2) + sin²(ε/2))² = 1. Swapping the order only flips the sign of the e₁e₃ part: U₂₃U₁₂ − U₁₂U₂₃ = −2 sin²(ε/2) e₁e₃.
  4. The cap and the flat cut. The flat cut △ABC has interior angles summing to 180°, with cos β_flat = sin²(ε/2) at B, so β_flat < 90°. On the spherical cap β = 90° and α = γ = arctan(sec ε) = π/4 + ε²/4 + O(ε⁴) > 45°, so the excess is Ω(ε) = α + β + γ − π = 2 arctan(tan²(ε/2)) = ε²/2 + ε⁴/12 + O(ε⁶) > 0.
  5. Two things to keep apart. (a) Exact: carried once round the geodesic triangle A → B → C → A, an arrow turns by Ω(ε); the product of the three edge rotors is U_CA U₂₃ U₁₂ = exp(−(Ω/2) A⋆), and the spin-½ phase is −Ω/2, for every ε in (0, π/2]. (b) Approximate: in U₂₃U₁₂, which walks only the two coordinate walls, the e₁e₃ part −sin²(ε/2) ≈ −ε²/4 agrees with −Ω/2 ≈ −ε²/4 only at the lowest order O(ε²); as ε grows they part (−0.165 against −0.196 at ε = 48°, −0.5 against −π/4 ≈ −0.785 at ε = 90°). At the octant limit ε = 90° all three edges lie in coordinate planes, and the product of the three coordinate rotors is exactly exp(−(π/4) e₂e₃), with Ω = π/2.
  6. The Gram volume brick. G(ΔT_ε) = [[1, cos ε, cos²ε], [cos ε, 1, cos ε], [cos²ε, cos ε, 1]], det G = (1 − cos²ε)² = sin⁴ε > 0, parallelepiped volume √det G = sin²ε (tetrahedron sin²ε / 6, spherical sector Ω/3). The coplanar two-step A_cop = (sin ε, cos ε, 0), B, C_cop = (−sin ε, cos ε, 0) has A_cop·C_cop = cos 2ε and det G = 0 identically: trapped in one circle, with no volume.
  7. Frozen theorems.
