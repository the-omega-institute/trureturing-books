希帕索斯发现，整数的比 m/n 永远追不上 √2；但追的过程留下一道不灭的裂缝：m² − 2n² = ±1。本页把这道 ±1 接缝推广到任意无平方因子的 d ≤ 37，再把它接到素数在高斯整数 ℤ[i] 中的分裂与同余数椭圆曲线 Ed: y² = x³ − d²x 上。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 佩尔账本。√d 的连分数 [a₀; a₁, a₂, …] 由整数递推 aᵢ = ⌊(a₀ + Pᵢ)/Qᵢ⌋、Pᵢ₊₁ = aᵢQᵢ − Pᵢ、Qᵢ₊₁ = (d − Pᵢ₊₁²)/Qᵢ 得到，渐近分数 mᵢ/nᵢ 按 mᵢ = aᵢmᵢ₋₁ + mᵢ₋₂ 逐行生成；种子行 #0 是平凡解 1² − d·0² = +1。每行的范数 Δ = m² − d n² 等于 ±Qᵢ₊₁，是精确整数。d = 2 时这正是泰昂的边径数 1/1、3/2、7/5、17/12……，范数在 −1 与 +1 之间交替。
  2. 一维与二维。舞台“Pell ±1”左侧把 d nₖ² 个单位格逐行填进 mₖ × mₖ 的正方形：Δ = +1 时恰好剩一格，Δ = −1 时恰好溢出一格。右侧是 (n, m) 平面上的双曲线 m² − d n² = ±1 与渐近线 m = √d·n：渐近分数沿双曲线奔向渐近线，一维误差 |m/n − √d| = |Δ|/(n(m + n√d)) 趋于 0，二维面积差 |Δ| 却不会小于 1。
  3. 手性热图。右栏逐格画出 m² − d n² 的符号：青色是 +1，品红是 −1，其余格只按符号淡淡着色。琥珀色十字与方框标出当前渐近分数；点击热图会选中最近的渐近步。页面核对网格内每个 ±1 格都是 √d 的渐近分数——这是文献结果的有限检查。
  4. 素数的面与线。p ≡ 1 (mod 4) 时 −1 是模 p 的平方，p = a² + b² = (a + bi)(a − bi) 在 ℤ[i] 中分裂（“面素数”）；p ≡ 3 (mod 4) 时 x² ≡ −1 无解，p 保持惰性（“线素数”）；2 = −i(1 + i)² 分歧。舞台“素数”左侧是半径 √p 的圆与格点，右侧是模 p 的剩余钟。取 u = (a, b)、v = (a, −b) 代入 lagrange_gram_identity（LEAN ✓）得到 p² = (a² − b²)² + (2ab)²：例如 13 = 3² + 2² 给出 5² + 12² = 13²。两条对角线 a ± bi 服从 inner_product_norm_parallelogram_law（LEAN ✓）。“面”“线”与“手性”是阐释性的命名。
  5. 同余数曲线。d 是同余数，当且仅当存在面积为 d 的有理直角三角形 (a, b, c)。曲线 Ed 上任一 y ≠ 0 的有理点 (x, y) 给出 a = |(x² − d²)/y|、b = |2dx/y|、c = |(x² + d²)/y|。d ≤ 37 中的同余数是 5、6、7、13、14、15、21、22、23、29、30、31、34、37：页面为每一个存了一个有理点，并在有理数中精确核对 a² + b² = c² 与 ab/2 = d。其余 d 的 Tunnell 计数 A(d) ≠ 2B(d)，按 Tunnell 定理（文献结果）不是同余数。
  6. 哈塞与弗罗贝尼乌斯余弦。在 𝔽ₚ 上逐点数出 #Ed(𝔽ₚ)，aₚ = p + 1 − #Ed(𝔽ₚ)。p ∤ 2d 时哈塞定理给出 |aₚ| ≤ 2√p，于是 cos θₚ = aₚ/(2√p) ∈ [−1, 1]；p | 2d 是坏约化素数，哈塞界不适用。Ed 有复乘：p ≡ 3 (mod 4) 时 aₚ = 0（θₚ = 90°），p = a² + b² 时 |aₚ| = 2|a|，弗罗贝尼乌斯角就是高斯直角三角形的角。把 θₚ 读作单比特态 ψ = cos θₚ|0⟩ + sin θₚ|1⟩ 是页面的模型，记录权重由 rank_one_pure_state_modulus_square_reduction（LEAN ✓）给出，纯度分解由 complementary_context_probability_pythagoras（LEAN ✓）给出。
  7. 交换 d ↔ p。当 d 是素数且 p ≤ 37 时，“交换”让素数本身当佩尔根数：p = 13 换成 d = 13 后，第 5 步出现 18² − 13·5² = −1；p = 7 换成 d = 7 后，范数 −1 从不出现，只有 8² − 7·3² = +1。这正是“素数 p ≡ 1 (mod 4) 时 m² − p n² = −1 可解”（勒让德，文献结果）。

Hippasus found that no ratio of integers m/n ever reaches √2, yet the chase leaves a crack that never closes: m² − 2n² = ±1. The page extends this ±1 seam to every squarefree d ≤ 37 and connects it to the splitting of primes in the Gaussian integers ℤ[i] and to the congruent-number curves Ed: y² = x³ − d²x. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The Pell ledger. The continued fraction [a₀; a₁, a₂, …] of √d comes from the integer recurrence aᵢ = ⌊(a₀ + Pᵢ)/Qᵢ⌋, Pᵢ₊₁ = aᵢQᵢ − Pᵢ, Qᵢ₊₁ = (d − Pᵢ₊₁²)/Qᵢ, and the convergents mᵢ/nᵢ follow row by row from mᵢ = aᵢmᵢ₋₁ + mᵢ₋₂; the seed row #0 is the trivial solution 1² − d·0² = +1. The norm Δ = m² − d n² of each row equals ±Qᵢ₊₁, an exact integer. For d = 2 these are Theon's side and diagonal numbers 1/1, 3/2, 7/5, 17/12, …, whose norms alternate between −1 and +1.
  2. 1D against 2D. On the stage “Pell ±1” the left picture fills the mₖ × mₖ square with d nₖ² unit cells row by row: for Δ = +1 exactly one cell is left over, for Δ = −1 exactly one spills out. The right picture is the (n, m) plane with the hyperbolas m² − d n² = ±1 and the asymptote m = √d·n: the convergents run along the hyperbolas towards the asymptote, the 1D error |m/n − √d| = |Δ|/(n(m + n√d)) goes to 0, while the 2D area gap |Δ| never drops below 1.
  3. The chirality map. The side panel draws the sign of m² − d n² cell by cell: cyan is +1, magenta −1, every other cell is only faintly tinted by its sign. The amber cross and box mark the current convergent; clicking the map picks the nearest convergent step. The page checks that every ±1 cell of the grid is a convergent of √d, a finite check of a literature result.
  4. Plane and line primes. For p ≡ 1 (mod 4), −1 is a square mod p and p = a² + b² = (a + bi)(a − bi) splits in ℤ[i] (a “plane prime”); for p ≡ 3 (mod 4), x² ≡ −1 has no root and p stays inert (a “line prime”); 2 = −i(1 + i)² ramifies. On the stage “Primes” the left picture is the circle of radius √p among the lattice points and the right picture the residue clock mod p. Putting u = (a, b) and v = (a, −b) into lagrange_gram_identity (LEAN ✓) gives p² = (a² − b²)² + (2ab)²: 13 = 3² + 2², for instance, gives 5² + 12² = 13². The two diagonals a ± bi obey inner_product_norm_parallelogram_law (LEAN ✓). “Plane”, “line” and “chirality” are interpretive names.
  5. Congruent-number curves. d is a congruent number exactly when a rational right triangle (a, b, c) has area d. Every rational point (x, y) with y ≠ 0 on Ed gives a = |(x² − d²)/y|, b = |2dx/y|, c = |(x² + d²)/y|. The congruent numbers d ≤ 37 are 5, 6, 7, 13, 14, 15, 21, 22, 23, 29, 30, 31, 34, 37: the page stores one rational point for each and checks a² + b² = c² and ab/2 = d exactly in rationals. Every other d has Tunnell counts A(d) ≠ 2B(d), so by Tunnell's theorem (a literature result) it is not congruent.
  6. Hasse and the Frobenius cosine. The page counts #Ed(𝔽ₚ) point by point and sets aₚ = p + 1 − #Ed(𝔽ₚ). For p ∤ 2d Hasse's theorem gives |aₚ| ≤ 2√p, so cos θₚ = aₚ/(2√p) lies in [−1, 1]; p | 2d is a prime of bad reduction where Hasse's bound does not apply. Ed has complex multiplication: aₚ = 0 (θₚ = 90°) for p ≡ 3 (mod 4), and |aₚ| = 2|a| for p = a² + b², so the Frobenius angle is the angle of the Gaussian right triangle. Reading θₚ as the qubit state ψ = cos θₚ|0⟩ + sin θₚ|1⟩ is the page's model; the record weights come from rank_one_pure_state_modulus_square_reduction (LEAN ✓) and the purity split from complementary_context_probability_pythagoras (LEAN ✓).
  7. Swap d ↔ p. When d is prime and p ≤ 37, “Swap” makes the prime itself the Pell radicand: after p = 13 becomes d = 13, step 5 shows 18² − 13·5² = −1; after p = 7 becomes d = 7, the norm −1 never appears, only 8² − 7·3² = +1. This is the statement that m² − p n² = −1 is solvable for every prime p ≡ 1 (mod 4) (Legendre, a literature result).
