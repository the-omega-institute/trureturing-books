第 29 章从勾股定理出发：直角边不能直接相加，平方之后才守恒。希帕索斯发现 √2 不是分数，整数格点只能贴近这条线，m² 与 2n² 之间永远差着 ±1。本页把这道接缝画出来，再沿着它走到素数的两族与一条椭圆曲线。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 佩尔接缝。√d 的渐近分数 m/n 交替落在直线 m = √d·n 的两侧。沿直线量，误差 |m/n − √d| 很快趋于零；按面积量，m² − d·n² 是非零整数，绝对值至少为 1。右边的两条色带是 m² 与 d·n² 的格子数，放大镜里看得到它们永远差着整数格。差值的符号一正一负地翻转；连分数周期为奇数（如 d = 2、5、13）时能取到 −1，周期为偶数（如 d = 3、7）时只能取到 +1。
  2. 线素数与面素数。在高斯整数平面上画半径 √p 的圆。p ≡ 1 (mod 4) 时圆上有格点 (a, b)，p = a² + b² = (a + bi)(a − bi)，把 a + bi 平方得到整数直角三角形 (|a² − b²|, 2ab, p)；p ≡ 3 (mod 4) 时圆上没有格点。这与“−1 是不是模 p 的平方”是同一个问题。
  3. 模 p 数点。曲线 y² = x³ − x 在 𝔽ₚ 上的点数与 p + 1 的差是 aₚ，哈塞定理把它限制在 ±2√p 之内，于是 aₚ/(2√p) 是一个余弦。线素数上 aₚ = 0；面素数 p = a² + b²（a 奇）上 |aₚ| = 2|a|，这个余弦的绝对值正是高斯直角三角形的邻边比斜边。换成没有复乘的曲线 y² = x³ − x + 1，余弦值的分布趋于半圆律。

Chapter 29 starts from Pythagoras: the legs of a right triangle do not add, their squares do. Hippasus found that √2 is not a fraction, so lattice points can only hug that line, and m² and 2n² always differ by ±1. The page draws this seam and follows it to the two families of primes and to an elliptic curve. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The Pell seam. The convergents m/n of √d fall on alternating sides of the line m = √d·n. Measured along the line, the error |m/n − √d| quickly goes to zero; measured as area, m² − d·n² is a nonzero integer, at least 1 in absolute value. The two bands on the right are the cell counts m² and d·n², and the magnifier shows that they always differ by whole cells. The sign of the difference flips back and forth; when the period of the continued fraction is odd (d = 2, 5, 13) the value −1 occurs, and when it is even (d = 3, 7) only +1 does.
  2. Line primes and plane primes. Draw the circle of radius √p in the plane of Gaussian integers. For p ≡ 1 (mod 4) it passes through a lattice point (a, b), p = a² + b² = (a + bi)(a − bi), and squaring a + bi gives the integer right triangle (|a² − b²|, 2ab, p); for p ≡ 3 (mod 4) the circle misses every lattice point. This is the same question as whether −1 is a square mod p.
  3. Points mod p. The number of points of y² = x³ − x over 𝔽ₚ differs from p + 1 by aₚ; Hasse's theorem keeps it within ±2√p, so aₚ/(2√p) is a cosine. On line primes aₚ = 0; on a plane prime p = a² + b² (a odd) |aₚ| = 2|a|, and the absolute value of that cosine is exactly adjacent over hypotenuse in the Gaussian right triangle. For a curve without complex multiplication, y² = x³ − x + 1, the cosines tend to the semicircle law.
