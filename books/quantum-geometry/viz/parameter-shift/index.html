理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

一个量子比特，布洛赫向量 r₀，经过一扇门 Ry(θ) = e−iθY/2 后测沿单位轴 m 的泡利算符，期望值 f(θ) = m·r(θ)。页面上的每个数都实时算出；本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 曲线。Ry(θ) 把 r₀ 绕 Y 轴转 θ，所以 f(θ) = c + a cos θ + b sin θ，只含常数项与一倍角谐波；令 R = √(a² + b²)、θ₀ = atan2(b, a)，就是 f = c + R cos(θ − θ₀)。圆里的箭头长 R、与竖直方向夹角 θ − θ₀，它的高度就是 f：箭头在测量轴上的影子。第十二章的例子是 r₀ = m = (0, 0, 1)，f = cos θ；“倾斜”一例取 r₀ = (0.48, 0.60, 0.64)、m = (0.80, 0.36, 0.48)，c = 0.216、a = 0.6912、b = 0.2816。
  2. 左右各 90°。两个青色的点在 θ ± 90°。因为 cos(φ + 90°) = −sin φ，f(θ + 90°) − c 恰好等于斜率 f′(θ) = −R sin(θ − θ₀)，f(θ − 90°) − c 是它的相反数；两值之差的一半就是斜率，常数 c 消掉。这是参数位移律（文献结果，未在本书形式化），它要求 θ 只出现在一扇对合泡利门里。
  3. 切线。虚线切线的斜率不是由两点公式算的：页面把 ρθ = Uρ₀U*、P = (I + m·σ)/2 写成 2 × 2 复矩阵，按冻结命题的对易子公式 p′ = Re(i Tr(ρθ(HP − PH)))、H = Y/2 算出 f′ = 2p′。量尺上青色与切线重合，是两种算法给出同一个数。
  4. 小步长差分。品红的弦连接 θ ± h 两点，斜率 [f(θ + h) − f(θ − h)]/(2h)。对这样的曲线它等于 f′(θ)·sin h / h：h = 30° 时短 4.5%，h 越小越准，但从不精确。
  5. 采样。真实硬件只能测有限发，每发 ±1，均值的方差是 (1 − f²)/N。两点公式的散布是 √[(1 − f₊²) + (1 − f₋²)] / (2√N)，小步长差分要再除以 h（以弧度计）：h = 6°、N = 100、θ = 60° 时，两者理论宽度是 0.035 与 0.58。小图画出截断误差随 h 变小、噪声随 h 变大。

One qubit with Bloch vector r₀ passes one gate Ry(θ) = e−iθY/2, and the Pauli operator along a unit axis m is measured: the expectation value is f(θ) = m·r(θ). Every number on the page is computed live; the page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The curve. Ry(θ) turns r₀ by θ about the Y axis, so f(θ) = c + a cos θ + b sin θ: a constant and the first harmonic only. With R = √(a² + b²) and θ₀ = atan2(b, a) this is f = c + R cos(θ − θ₀). The arrow in the dial has length R and makes the angle θ − θ₀ with the vertical; its height is f, the arrow's shadow on the measured axis. The example of Chapter 12 is r₀ = m = (0, 0, 1), f = cos θ; the tilted case takes r₀ = (0.48, 0.60, 0.64) and m = (0.80, 0.36, 0.48), so c = 0.216, a = 0.6912 and b = 0.2816.
  2. A quarter turn each way. The two cyan points sit at θ ± 90°. Since cos(φ + 90°) = −sin φ, f(θ + 90°) − c is exactly the slope f′(θ) = −R sin(θ − θ₀) and f(θ − 90°) − c is its negative; half their difference is the slope, and the constant c drops out. This is the parameter-shift rule (a literature result, not formalized in this book); it needs θ to appear in a single involutory Pauli gate.
  3. The tangent. The slope of the dashed tangent does not come from the two-point formula: the page writes ρθ = Uρ₀U* and P = (I + m·σ)/2 as 2 × 2 complex matrices and evaluates the commutator formula of the frozen proposition, p′ = Re(i Tr(ρθ(HP − PH))) with H = Y/2, to get f′ = 2p′. On the gauge the cyan mark sits on the tangent's line: two computations give the same number.
  4. The small step. The magenta chord joins the points at θ ± h; its slope is [f(θ + h) − f(θ − h)]/(2h). For such a curve this equals f′(θ)·sin h / h: 4.5% short at h = 30°, better as h shrinks, never exact.
  5. Shots. Hardware only takes finitely many shots, each ±1, and the variance of their mean is (1 − f²)/N. The two-point formula scatters by √[(1 − f₊²) + (1 − f₋²)] / (2√N), and the small-step difference is further divided by h (in radians): at h = 6°, N = 100 and θ = 60° the predicted widths are 0.035 and 0.58. The small plot shows the truncation error falling and the noise rising as h shrinks.
