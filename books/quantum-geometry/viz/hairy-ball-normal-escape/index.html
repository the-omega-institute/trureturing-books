ARGUED庞加莱—霍普夫定理：闭曲面上只有孤立零点的连续切向量场，零点指标之和等于欧拉示性数 χ；球面 χ(S²) = 2，所以至少有一个零点（毛球定理），环面 χ(T²) = 0。笛卡尔定理：与球面同胚的多面体，顶点角亏之和为 2πχ = 4π。文献结果，未在本书形式化。Poincaré–Hopf: for a continuous tangent field with isolated zeros on a closed surface, the indices of the zeros add up to the Euler characteristic χ; the sphere has χ(S²) = 2, so some zero is unavoidable (the hairy ball theorem), and the torus has χ(T²) = 0. Descartes: for a polyhedron homeomorphic to the sphere the vertex angle defects add up to 2πχ = 4π. Literature results, not formalized in this book.
MODEL把箭头沿外法向抬起、在三维空间里不再为零，读作“跳出切空间、借球外之球安顿奇点”，是本书的诠释。抬起后的箭头已不是切向量，这不是毛球定理的反例；从两个 +1 到一个 +2 的那一族场，是为画面选定的一条路。Reading the arrows lifted along the outward normal, no longer vanishing in space, as “leaving the tangent space and settling the singular point in a sphere beyond the sphere” is this book's interpretation. Lifted arrows are no longer tangent vectors, so this is no counterexample to the hairy ball theorem; the family of fields that takes two +1 zeros to one +2 is one road chosen for the picture.

理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

舞台是单位球面 S² 与环面 T²，上面铺着连续的箭头场。品红的环圈出箭头为零的点（发旋），旁边写着它的指标：沿零点一小圈走一周，箭头跟着转了几圈。琥珀色只给当前的点、当前的零点位置或当前的抬升量。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 纬向场。v(x, y, z) = (−y, x, 0)，处处与球面相切（x·v = 0），长度 √(x² + y²) = sin θ，只在南北两极为零，两极的指标各为 +1，和为 2。第十章 berry-loop 的 comb 卡画的是同类的场（竖直方向的影子），这里不再重复，只把它当起点。
  2. 合并。用立体投影 u = (x + iy)/(1 − z) 把平面上的场 f(u) 拉回球面：f(u) = eiψ(a − u)/(1 + a)，a = tan(πt/2)，ψ = −(π/2)(1 − t)。t = 0 时它就是纬向场；第二个零点在 u = a，沿 +X 一侧的经线从南极升到北极；t = 1 时 f ≡ 1，拉回的正是偶极切场 w(x, y, z) = (((1 − z)² − x² + y²)/2, −xy, x(1 − z))：x·w = 0，‖w‖ = 1 − z，只在北极为零，指标 +2。这一族是为画面选定的路（MODEL），每一步的指标都由页面数出（NUM），和始终是 2。
  3. 环面。r(θ, φ) = ((R + r cos θ) cos φ, (R + r cos θ) sin φ, r sin θ)，R > r > 0。沿 φ 方向的单位切场 êφ = (−sin φ, cos φ, 0) 处处长度为 1，没有零点；网格的 V − E + F = 0 = χ(T²)。
  4. 角亏。正二十面体每个面切成 ν² 个三角形，再把顶点推到球面上（测地穹顶），连成平的三角面。顶点角亏 δv = 2π − Σ 面角；页面逐个顶点算出，ν = 1 到 6 的总和都是 4π = 720°（NUM；笛卡尔定理是文献结果，ARGUED）。终章的静态图 coda-defect-spread 用横条画同一组数，这里把它们放回球面上。
  5. 法向抬升。w̃λ(x) = (1 − λ) w(x) + λ (1 + z)/2 · x。切向部分与法向部分互相垂直，所以 ‖w̃λ‖² = (1 − λ)²(1 − z)² + λ²(1 + z)²/4；0 < λ < 1 时它在整个球面上都大于零，‖w̃1(N)‖ = 1。λ = 1 时切向部分全没了，箭头全指向外，长度 (1 + z)/2 在南极又为零：立起来的箭头已不是切向量，毛球定理管不着它，但它也不是“白来的”。把抬升读作跳出切空间是诠释（MODEL）。
  6. 已验证的定理。第二十一章用 lean= 引用的 complete_diagonal_not_continuous（LEAN ✓）说：满射的解码目录与无不动点的翻转造出的相对语义对角线，在潜坐标的划分拓扑下不连续。它讲的是概念读出的拓扑，不是球面上的向量场；庞加莱—霍普夫定理与毛球定理在本书只是文献结果（ARGUED）。

The stage is the unit sphere S² and the torus T², covered with continuous fields of arrows. Magenta rings circle the points where the arrows vanish (whorls), each labelled with its index: how many times the arrow turns as you walk once round a small loop about the zero. Amber is kept for the current point, the current position of a zero or the current amount of lift. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The zonal field. v(x, y, z) = (−y, x, 0) is tangent everywhere (x·v = 0), has length √(x² + y²) = sin θ and vanishes only at the two poles, each of index +1, adding up to 2. The comb card of berry-loop in Chapter 10 draws a field of the same kind (the shadow of the vertical); it is not repeated here, only used as the starting point.
  2. Merging. Stereographic projection u = (x + iy)/(1 − z) pulls a plane field f(u) back to the sphere: f(u) = eiψ(a − u)/(1 + a), a = tan(πt/2), ψ = −(π/2)(1 − t). At t = 0 it is the zonal field; the second zero sits at u = a and climbs the meridian on the +X side from the South Pole to the North Pole; at t = 1, f ≡ 1 and the pull-back is exactly the dipole field w(x, y, z) = (((1 − z)² − x² + y²)/2, −xy, x(1 − z)): x·w = 0, ‖w‖ = 1 − z, vanishing only at the North Pole with index +2. The family is one road chosen for the picture (MODEL); every index along it is counted by the page (NUM), and they always add up to 2.
  3. The torus. r(θ, φ) = ((R + r cos θ) cos φ, (R + r cos θ) sin φ, r sin θ) with R > r > 0. The unit field along φ, êφ = (−sin φ, cos φ, 0), has length 1 everywhere and no zero; the mesh has V − E + F = 0 = χ(T²).
  4. Defect. Each face of the icosahedron is cut into ν² triangles and the vertices pushed out onto the sphere (a geodesic dome) and joined by flat triangles. The vertex defect δv = 2π − Σ face angles is computed vertex by vertex; for ν = 1 to 6 the total is 4π = 720° every time (NUM; Descartes' theorem is a literature result, ARGUED). The end chapter's figure coda-defect-spread draws the same numbers as bars; here they sit back on the sphere.
  5. Normal lift. w̃λ(x) = (1 − λ) w(x) + λ (1 + z)/2 · x. The tangent and normal parts are perpendicular, so ‖w̃λ‖² = (1 − λ)²(1 − z)² + λ²(1 + z)²/4; for 0 < λ < 1 it is positive over the whole sphere, and ‖w̃1(N)‖ = 1. At λ = 1 the tangent part is gone, every arrow points straight out, and its length (1 + z)/2 vanishes at the South Pole: lifted arrows are no longer tangent vectors, so the hairy ball theorem does not govern them, but the lift is not free either. Reading the lift as leaving the tangent space is an interpretation (MODEL).
  6. Verified theorem. complete_diagonal_not_continuous (LEAN ✓), cited with lean= in Chapter 21, says that the relative semantic diagonal built from a surjective decoder catalogue and a fixed-point-free twist is not continuous for the partition topology of the latent coordinate. It is about the topology of concept readouts, not about vector fields on the sphere; Poincaré–Hopf and the hairy ball theorem are literature results in this book (ARGUED).
