第三十二章上半篇的三个画面：一个圆、一颗球上的回路、一张球面网络。页面上的每个数都实时算出；本页是数值演示与诠释示意，不是证明。

  1. 时钟。横轴 t、竖轴 x，白线是光 t = x。快度 η 把 (1, 0) 推到双曲线 t² − x² = 1 上的 (cosh η, sinh η)；这条世界线穿过竖线 t = 1 的高度是 tanh η，水平移到单位圆上，落点的角就是 θ = gd(η) = arctan(sinh η)。于是 sin θ = tanh η（品红的高，速度 v/c），cos θ = sech η = 1/γ（青色的线，固有时率 dτ/dt）。η → ∞ 时世界线贴上光线，圆上的点贴到 θ = 90°，青色的线缩成零。紫色的弧长 θ 与青色的 cos θ 只在 θ* ≈ 0.739085 相等，那时品红的高 sin θ* ≈ 0.6736 正是迭代 θ ↦ cos θ 在不动点处的收缩率。（文献结果，未在本书形式化；“线/面积/视界”的读法是本书的诠释。）
  2. 回路。R(n, ε) = cos(ε/2) − sin(ε/2) In 是绕单位轴 n 转 ε 的旋子，In 是与 n 垂直的单位二向量；页面用四元数 [w, b] 记它，U = w − (b₁e₂₃ + b₂e₃₁ + b₃e₁₂)。时间轴依次施加 R₂⁻¹、R₁⁻¹、R₂、R₁，所以走完一圈得到 U = R₁R₂R₁⁻¹R₂⁻¹，琥珀色的点是一根参考箭头的尖端。两个旋子在同一个平面（都绕 e₃）时它们对易，U = 1，箭头回到原处；在两个平面（绕 e₁、绕 e₂）时 U ≠ 1：剩下一个转角 φ = 2 arccos|w| 的转动，品红的弧是箭头没合上的缺口。ε 小时 φ ≈ ε²（按弧度），二向量余项主要落在 e₁e₂ 平面。把这个余项读作规范曲率（杨—米尔斯 F = [D, D]）属文献背景，读作相互作用的源属本书的诠释模型（MODEL）。
  3. 视界。正二十面体内接于单位球，一个顶点在北极（观察者）。每个顶点有 5 个等边三角形，角之和 300°，缺 60°；12 个顶点一共缺 720° = 4π。时间轴把视界从北极往下推到极角 λ：A 是视界以内的顶点，割边 |∂A| 是一端在 A、一端在外的边。λ = 90° 时 A 是北极加上一圈 5 个顶点，割断 10 条边。若每条边携带 D 维指标，任何这样的张量网络态跨这道割的施密特秩 ≤ D|∂A|（线性代数，文献结果）。第三十二章冻结的是另一个网络（桥图）上的最大流 = 最小割定理；本页不是它的实例。

Three pictures from the first half of Chapter 32: a circle, a loop on a sphere, a network on a sphere. Every number on the page is computed live; the page is a numerical illustration and an interpretive sketch, not a proof.

  1. Clock. t runs across, x up, and the white line is light, t = x. The rapidity η carries (1, 0) to (cosh η, sinh η) on the hyperbola t² − x² = 1; this worldline crosses the vertical line t = 1 at height tanh η, and moved across to the unit circle that height lands at the angle θ = gd(η) = arctan(sinh η). So sin θ = tanh η (the magenta height, the speed v/c) and cos θ = sech η = 1/γ (the cyan line, the proper-time rate dτ/dt). As η → ∞ the worldline hugs the light line, the point on the circle reaches θ = 90° and the cyan line shrinks to nothing. The violet arc length θ equals the cyan cos θ only at θ* ≈ 0.739085, and there the magenta height sin θ* ≈ 0.6736 is exactly the contraction rate of the iteration θ ↦ cos θ at its fixed point. (Literature results, not formalized in this book; reading them as “line, area, horizon” is this book's interpretation.)
  2. Loop. R(n, ε) = cos(ε/2) − sin(ε/2) In is the rotor turning by ε about the unit axis n, with In the unit bivector perpendicular to n; the page keeps it as a quaternion [w, b], U = w − (b₁e₂₃ + b₂e₃₁ + b₃e₁₂). The rail applies R₂⁻¹, R₁⁻¹, R₂ and R₁ in turn, so one round gives U = R₁R₂R₁⁻¹R₂⁻¹; the amber point is the tip of a reference arrow. When the two rotors share a plane (both about e₃) they commute, U = 1, and the arrow comes home; in two planes (about e₁ and about e₂) U ≠ 1: a turn by φ = 2 arccos|w| is left over, and the magenta arc is the gap the arrow fails to close. For small ε, φ ≈ ε² (in radians) and the bivector remainder lies mostly in the e₁e₂ plane. Reading this remainder as gauge curvature (Yang–Mills F = [D, D]) is literature background; reading it as the source of an interaction is this book's interpretive model (MODEL).
  3. Horizon. A regular icosahedron sits in the unit sphere with one vertex at the north pole (the observer). Each vertex has 5 equilateral triangles whose angles add to 300°, so 60° is missing; the 12 vertices miss 720° = 4π in all. The rail pushes the horizon down from the north pole to the polar angle λ: A is the set of vertices inside it and the cut |∂A| counts the edges with one end in A and one outside. At λ = 90°, A is the pole and a ring of 5 vertices, and 10 edges are cut. If each edge carries a D-dimensional index, any such tensor-network state has Schmidt rank ≤ D|∂A| across the cut (linear algebra, a literature result). What Chapter 32 freezes is the max-flow = min-cut theorem on a different network, the bridge graph; this page is not an instance of it.
