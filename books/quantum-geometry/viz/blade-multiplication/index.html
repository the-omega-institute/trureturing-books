理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

三维欧氏克利福德代数 Cl(3,0) 有 8 块基刀片，每块记成一个 3 位掩码 m：第 0、1、2 位分别表示 e₁、e₂、e₃ 是否出席（e₁₂ 是 e₁e₂ 的简写，I₃ = e₁e₂e₃）。舞台左边是它们的乘法表，右边把选中的一格拆开：左因子与右因子拼成一串，逐个交换排成 e₁ e₂ e₃ 的顺序，每交换一次变一次号；排好后相邻的同号一对按 eᵢ² = +1 抵掉。剩下的方向正是两个掩码的异或。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 依据的章节。第二十五章 · 三比特刀片掩码、逆序符号与两比特辛泡利字，例题 M = 2 的四个泡利词乘法表 与 3 比特刀片异或乘法、伪标量平方与布洛赫算符平方核验。
  2. 交换次数。左掩码里每个为 1 的第 i 位，都要越过右掩码里下标比它小的每个 1 位：inv = Σᵢ [左的第 i 位]·popcount(右 & (2ⁱ − 1))，符号为 (−1)^inv。舞台上每一次交换画成两条交叉的品红线，数交叉就是数 inv。
  3. 泡利字。泡利字场景只用两位：第 0 位记 X，第 1 位记 Z，掩码 3 是 XZ = −iY。注意这里第 1 位是 Z，不是 8 块刀片场景里 e₂ ↦ Y 的那一位；两种编码共用同一条位运算规则。此时 inv = z左·x右，正是合成律的指数 bc（LEAN ✓，下方声明地图）。
  4. 向量自乘。v = 3e₁ + 4e₂ + 12e₃ 的九项里，对角三项的掩码异或为 0，给出 9 + 16 + 144 = 169；交叉项 eᵢeⱼ 与 eⱼeᵢ 一正一负，成对抵消。与布洛赫算符 B(3, 4, 12) 的矩阵平方 169·I 一致。
  5. 不在页面上的。Cl(3,0) ≅ M₂(ℂ)、泡利群的辛表示与 Gottesman–Knill 定理是文献结果，未在本书形式化（ARGUED）。

The three-dimensional Euclidean Clifford algebra Cl(3,0) has 8 basis blades, each written as a 3-bit mask m: bits 0, 1, 2 say whether e₁, e₂, e₃ are present (e₁₂ is short for e₁e₂, and I₃ = e₁e₂e₃). The left of the stage is their multiplication table; the right takes the chosen cell apart: the left and right factors are joined into one string and swapped one pair at a time into the order e₁ e₂ e₃, each swap flipping the sign; once sorted, each adjacent equal pair cancels by eᵢ² = +1. The directions left over are exactly the XOR of the two masks. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The chapter behind it. Chapter 25 · Three-bit blade masks, inversion parity, and two-bit symplectic Pauli words, with the worked examples the multiplication table of the four Pauli words for M = 2 and three-bit XOR products, the pseudoscalar square and the Bloch vector square.
  2. The number of swaps. Each set bit i of the left mask must move past every set bit of the right mask with a smaller index: inv = Σᵢ [bit i of left]·popcount(right & (2ⁱ − 1)), and the sign is (−1)^inv. On the stage every swap is drawn as two crossing magenta lines, so counting crossings is counting inv.
  3. Pauli words. The Pauli scene uses two bits only: bit 0 is X, bit 1 is Z, and mask 3 is XZ = −iY. Bit 1 here is Z, not the e₂ ↦ Y of the eight-blade scene; the two encodings share the same bit rule. Then inv = z_left·x_right, which is the exponent bc of the composition law (LEAN ✓, declaration map below).
  4. A vector squared. Of the nine terms of v = 3e₁ + 4e₂ + 12e₃ squared, the three diagonal ones XOR to the mask 0 and give 9 + 16 + 144 = 169; the cross terms eᵢeⱼ and eⱼeᵢ come with opposite signs and cancel in pairs. This agrees with the matrix square 169·I of the Bloch operator B(3, 4, 12).
  5. Not on the page. Cl(3,0) ≅ M₂(ℂ), the symplectic representation of the Pauli group and the Gottesman–Knill theorem are literature results, not formalized in this book (ARGUED).
