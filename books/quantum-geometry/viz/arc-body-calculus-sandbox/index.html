ARC//CALCULUS

极小圆弧体微积分重塑 · 每一步都是一枚模长为 1 的小旋子：横向切弦 c、轴向矢高 v，(1 − v)² + c² = 1 永远成立。竖直处斜率爆掉，旋子照转；复步不做相减；粗糙度降到 1/2，矢高总和锁到 ½σ₀²T；有限网格上内棱正反相消，斯托克斯一步不差。Minimal arc-body calculus · every step is a small rotor of modulus 1: a transverse chord c, an axial sagitta v, and (1 − v)² + c² = 1 always. The slope blows up at the vertical while the rotor keeps turning; the complex step subtracts nothing; at roughness 1/2 the sagitta sum locks onto ½σ₀²T; on a finite mesh the interior arcs cancel in pairs and Stokes holds exactly.

理论接口 · 极小圆弧体微积分Theory interface · minimal arc-body calculus

这是数值沙盘，不是证明。每个数都由页面当场算出（NUM）；每条 Lean 定理只在依据栏写明的那一个实例上演示；文献结果标 ARGUED，诠释与画法标 MODEL，开放问题标 OPEN。

  1. 旋子与切线。沿滑板坡走一步 ε，切向转过 φ = κε。这一步落在密切圆上：横向切弦 c = sin φ、轴向矢高 v = 2 sin²(φ/2)，(1 − v)² + c² = cos²φ + sin²φ = 1，旋子 D̂ε = cos(φ/2) − Î sin(φ/2) 的模长恒为 1。平直的斜率 dy/dx = tan θ 在 θ = 90° 处爆掉、过了竖直还变号；旋子只认转角，照转不误。矢高要按 2 sin²(φ/2) 算：φ 小于 10⁻⁸ 时 cos φ 在双精度里就是 1，1 − cos φ 相减归零。
  2. 复步。f(x) = ex sin x，x₀ = 1。向 90° 盲面迈一步 x₀ + iε：实轴上 Im f ≡ 0，所以 Im f(x₀ + iε)/ε 里没有两个大数相减，从 ε ≈ 10⁻⁸ 一直到 10⁻²⁰ 都准到机器精度。前向差分 (f(x₀ + ε) − f(x₀))/ε 在 ε ≈ 10⁻⁸ 附近最好，再往下被舍入吃掉；ε 小于 1.1×10⁻¹⁶ 时 x₀ + ε 就是 x₀，差分是 0，相对误差 100%。
  3. 矢高相变。N = T/ε 步，每步转 σ₀εα（σ₀ = 1，T = 1）：Σsag = N·2 sin²(σ₀εα/2) ≈ ½σ₀²Tε2α−1。α = 1 是牛顿—莱布尼茨与李—特罗特一端（ε = 10⁻³ 时约 0.0005）；1/2 < α < 1 时 ε → 0 矢高消失（Young 区）；α = 1/2 时锁到 ½σ₀²T = 0.5，这一笔就是伊藤修正；α < 1/2 发散，要用粗糙路径的迭代面积。球上画的是一条随机旋子路径：每步沿随机方向转 φ，平均高度 E[z] = (1 − v)N 被矢高一点点拉下来。
  4. 有限网格斯托克斯。上半球分成 M 个以 P 为中心的球面三角。联络 𝒜+ = 2 sin²(θ/2) dφ + dg 在北极正则，曲率 d𝒜 = sin θ dθ ∧ dφ 就是面积元，所以每个三角 ∮∂ΔTk 𝒜 = Ω(ΔTk)（角盈）。相邻三角的公共弧各走一次、方向相反，+Φ 与 −Φ 相消；剩下的只有赤道，环流 2π = Σ Ω。没有离散化误差：M = 4 也一样。
  5. 闭壳 d² = 0。四面体（M = 4）、八面体（8）与它们各细分一次（16、32）投到球面，都是 V − E + F = 2 的闭壳。每个面上 curl(grad φ) = 0；所有面的 curl A 加起来是 0（每条边两侧各走一次）；壳没有外圈，边界的边界是空的。全部顶点上的上边缘 δ(δA) 也处处为 0。

This is a numerical sandbox, not a proof. Every number is computed on the page (NUM); each Lean theorem is shown only on the one instance named in the evidence panel; literature results are marked ARGUED, readings and drawing choices MODEL, open problems OPEN.

  1. Rotor and tangent. One step ε along the skate ramp turns the tangent by φ = κε. The step lies on the osculating circle: a transverse chord c = sin φ and an axial sagitta v = 2 sin²(φ/2), with (1 − v)² + c² = cos²φ + sin²φ = 1, and the rotor D̂ε = cos(φ/2) − Î sin(φ/2) always has modulus 1. The flat slope dy/dx = tan θ blows up at θ = 90° and changes sign past the vertical; the rotor only knows the turn and keeps turning. The sagitta must be computed as 2 sin²(φ/2): below φ = 10⁻⁸, cos φ is exactly 1 in double precision and 1 − cos φ cancels to zero.
  2. Complex step. f(x) = ex sin x at x₀ = 1. One step into the 90° blind sheet, x₀ + iε: on the real axis Im f ≡ 0, so Im f(x₀ + iε)/ε subtracts no two large numbers and stays exact to machine precision from ε ≈ 10⁻⁸ all the way to 10⁻²⁰. The forward difference (f(x₀ + ε) − f(x₀))/ε is best near ε ≈ 10⁻⁸ and is eaten by rounding below; for ε below 1.1×10⁻¹⁶, x₀ + ε is x₀, the difference is 0 and the relative error 100%.
  3. The sagitta phase transition. N = T/ε steps, each turning σ₀εα (σ₀ = 1, T = 1): Σsag = N·2 sin²(σ₀εα/2) ≈ ½σ₀²Tε2α−1. α = 1 is the Newton–Leibniz and Lie–Trotter end (about 0.0005 at ε = 10⁻³); for 1/2 < α < 1 the sagitta vanishes as ε → 0 (the Young regime); at α = 1/2 it locks onto ½σ₀²T = 0.5, and this term is the Itô correction; below 1/2 it diverges and needs the iterated areas of rough paths. The sphere carries one random rotor path: each step turns by φ in a random direction, and the mean height E[z] = (1 − v)N is pulled down by the sagitta, a little at each step.
  4. Finite-mesh Stokes. The upper hemisphere is cut into M spherical triangles around a centre P. The connection 𝒜+ = 2 sin²(θ/2) dφ + dg is regular at the North Pole and its curvature d𝒜 = sin θ dθ ∧ dφ is the area element, so ∮∂ΔTk 𝒜 = Ω(ΔTk) (the angular excess) for every triangle. Each shared arc of two neighbours is run once in each direction, +Φ against −Φ; only the equator is left, with circulation 2π = Σ Ω. There is no discretization error: M = 4 is as exact as M = 32.
  5. Closed shell, d² = 0. The tetrahedron (M = 4), the octahedron (8) and one subdivision of each (16, 32), projected to the sphere, are closed shells with V − E + F = 2. On every face curl(grad φ) = 0; the curls of A over all faces add up to 0 (each edge is run once from each side); the shell has no rim, and the boundary of the boundary is empty. The coboundary δ(δA) on all vertices vanishes everywhere as well.
