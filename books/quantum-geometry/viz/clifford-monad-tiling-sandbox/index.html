  1. 单元。A = (1, 0, 0)，B = (cos ε, sin ε, 0)，C = (cos ε, 0, sin ε)。从原点 O 出发的三面平墙 OAB、OBC、OCA 加一块外凸的球面帽 △ABC 围成一个扇形体。
  2. 四个阶。Λ⁰：相融 A·B = A·C = cos ε；Λ¹：弧长 ε，单位态在 B 处的切向梯度 |A − (A·B)B| = sin ε；Λ²：球面帽的角超 Ω = 2 arctan(tan²(ε/2))；Λ³：扇形体积 Ω/3（平直四面体 OABC 的体积是 sin²ε/6）。在 N = 1 的单元里，每个阶都只数到 1 个对象：1⁰ = 1¹ = 1² = 1³ = 1。
  3. 相位锁定。拉格朗日—格拉姆给出 cos²ε + sin²ε = 1；巴格曼迹给出 Δ₃ = Tr(P_A P_C P_B) 的虚部 −¼ sin²ε 与辐角 −Ω/2（自旋 ½ 的贝里相位）；投影概率流给出 A 的玻恩权重的变化率 −½ sin ε。三者由同一个 B 驱动。
  4. 霍奇与斯托克斯。⋆(A∧B) = A × B = sin ε e₃ 与 A、B 都正交，长度等于 A∧B 张成的平行四边形面积；⋆(A∧B∧C) = det[A, B, C] = sin²ε 是一个标量。页面在一个不含零点的切标架下沿每条测地边搬运标架，得到连接量 δΓ；沿边界求和 ∮∂Σ = −Ω，与各小三角形的巴格曼角超之和 −ΣΩᵢ 是两种独立算法。
  5. 多胞抵消。N = k² 时把 ΔT_ε 按重心格剖成 k² 个测地小三角形：有向边 3k² 条，内部边成对反向（3k(k − 1) 条）抵消，剩 3k 条外沿；内部净环流为 0，小角超之和等于 Ω。N = 27 时取 3 层径向壳 × 3 × 3 个测地四边形的体胞（参数立方的 3 × 3 × 3 剖分）：有向面 162 个，内部 108 个成对抵消，剩 54 个边界面，边界面的边界为零（∂∂ = 0），V − E + F − C = 1。

  7. 冻结定理。页面引用六条已冻结的 Lean 定理（LEAN ✓）：lagrange_gram_identity、unit_perpendicular_pair、triple_bargmann_bloch、projection_probability_flow、face_cycle_exact、tetra_defects_incidence_repair_and_exactness。它们是一般陈述，页面只演示它们在这些单元上的样子；吉拉尔、立体角公式、高斯—博内、斯托克斯、贝里相位、∂∂ = 0 与霍奇对偶是文献结果（ARGUED）。

书中：第二十九章 · 极小圆弧体与多胞抵消

The stage is the minimal arc cell ΔT_ε on the unit sphere S² ⊂ ℝ³ and its subdivisions. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof: every number is computed on the spot (NUM), and each frozen theorem is shown only on the instance written below.

  1. The cell. A = (1, 0, 0), B = (cos ε, sin ε, 0), C = (cos ε, 0, sin ε). Three flat walls OAB, OBC, OCA from the origin O and an outward-bulging spherical cap △ABC bound a sector.
  2. Four grades. Λ⁰: the compatibility A·B = A·C = cos ε; Λ¹: the arc ε and the tangent gradient of the unit state at B, |A − (A·B)B| = sin ε; Λ²: the excess of the cap, Ω = 2 arctan(tan²(ε/2)); Λ³: the sector volume Ω/3 (the flat tetrahedron OABC holds sin²ε/6). In the cell N = 1 each grade counts exactly one object: 1⁰ = 1¹ = 1² = 1³ = 1.
  3. Phase lock. Lagrange–Gram gives cos²ε + sin²ε = 1; the Bargmann trace gives Δ₃ = Tr(P_A P_C P_B) with imaginary part −¼ sin²ε and argument −Ω/2 (the spin-½ Berry phase); the projection probability flow gives the rate −½ sin ε at which the Born weight of A changes. One B drives all three.
  4. Hodge and Stokes. ⋆(A∧B) = A × B = sin ε e₃ is orthogonal to A and B, and its length is the area of the parallelogram spanned by A∧B; ⋆(A∧B∧C) = det[A, B, C] = sin²ε is a scalar. In a tangent frame without zeros the page carries the frame along each geodesic edge to get the connection δΓ; the sum along the boundary, ∮∂Σ = −Ω, and the sum −ΣΩᵢ of the Bargmann excesses of the small triangles are two independent computations.
  5. Multi-cell cancellation. For N = k² the cell is cut along its barycentric lattice into k² geodesic triangles: 3k² directed edges, of which the inner ones come in opposite pairs (3k(k − 1) of them) and cancel, leaving 3k rim edges; the inner net circulation is 0 and the small excesses add up to Ω. For N = 27 the page takes 3 radial shells × 3 × 3 geodesic quadrilaterals (the 3 × 3 × 3 subdivision of a parameter cube): 162 directed faces, 108 inner ones cancelling in pairs, 54 boundary faces whose boundary is zero (∂∂ = 0), and V − E + F − C = 1.
  6. The macroscopic sieve. At λ = 1 only one node (N⁰), a spine through the 3 shells (N1/3), 9 outer faces (N2/3) and 27 cells of volume (N¹) remain; at N = 1 the four curves meet in one point (1, 1). This graded reading is a model (MODEL).
  7. Frozen theorems. The page cites six frozen Lean theorems (LEAN ✓): lagrange_gram_identity, unit_perpendicular_pair, triple_bargmann_bloch, projection_probability_flow, face_cycle_exact and tetra_defects_incidence_repair_and_exactness. They are general statements; the page only shows what they look like on these cells. Girard, the solid-angle formula, Gauss–Bonnet, Stokes, the Berry phase, ∂∂ = 0 and Hodge duality are literature results (ARGUED).
