舞台是单位开圆盘，白色的圆周是第三十六章的“纯光视界” θ = 90°。页面上的每个数都实时算出；本页是数值演示与诠释模型，不是证明。

  1. 阶梯。θ* 是 cos θ = θ 的根（≈ 0.739085），η₀ = gd⁻¹(θ*) = ln tan(θ*/2 + π/4) ≈ 0.817326。第 k 级 ηk = η₀ + kΔη。Beltrami–Klein 圆盘里它在半径 r = tanh ηk = sin θk 处，庞加莱圆盘里在 ρ = tanh(ηk/2) = tan(θk/2) 处，ρ = r/(1 + √(1 − r²))。两种画法里固有时率都是 dτ/dt = √(1 − r²) = (1 − ρ²)/(1 + ρ²) = sech ηk。过每一级画一条与射线垂直的测地线：Klein 里是直弦，庞加莱里是与圆周正交的圆弧；相邻两条的双曲距离都是 Δη。
  2. 缝。到边界还差 1 − r = 2/(e2η + 1)（Klein）或 1 − ρ = 2/(eη + 1)（庞加莱）：对每个有限的 k 都大于 0，按指数缩小。放大镜按 10 的整数次幂放大，把这道品红的缝一直放大到看得见。
  3. 角度尺。换成外部角度尺：每步把 θ 加上同样的 Δθ = θ₁ − θ*（与阶梯的第一步相同）。有限步后 θ 越过 90°，点落到圆周上（页面把这一步记成恰在 90°），然后回到圆心 θ = 0 重新走：这是本章的“化光—擦表—重来”模型（MODEL）。小图画出 ρ = ½[[1 + c, s], [s, 1 − c]] 的捏合账：对角里的轴向偏置 ½c²（青色）与被丢掉的相干 ½s²（品红），两者之和恒为 ½；撞上边界那一步青色归零。
  4. 折叠。第一次擦表之后，步号 n 与 n + p 的角度相同，p 是一圈的步数。轮子把每一圈画成一根辐条；折叠把所有辐条转到同一个方向，每个位置上叠着同一个角度：目标“角度”在“步号模 p”这条读出的每条纤维上取常值，于是经它分解。
  5. 分式。w = (s − 1)/s = 1 − 1/s 把竖直线 Re s = c（c > 1/2）送成圆心 1 − 1/(2c)、半径 1/(2c) 的圆，都在单位圆里面、都在 w = 1 处与它相切；c 越接近 1/2，像圆越贴近单位圆；Re s = 1/2 的像就是单位圆，而 w = 1 本身不是任何有限 s 的像。

The stage is the open unit disk; the white rim is Chapter 36's “horizon of pure light”, θ = 90°. Every number on the page is computed live; the page is a numerical illustration and an interpretive model, not a proof.

  1. Staircase. θ* is the root of cos θ = θ (≈ 0.739085) and η₀ = gd⁻¹(θ*) = ln tan(θ*/2 + π/4) ≈ 0.817326. Step k has ηk = η₀ + kΔη. In the Beltrami–Klein disk it sits at radius r = tanh ηk = sin θk, in the Poincaré disk at ρ = tanh(ηk/2) = tan(θk/2), with ρ = r/(1 + √(1 − r²)). In both drawings the proper-time rate is dτ/dt = √(1 − r²) = (1 − ρ²)/(1 + ρ²) = sech ηk. Through each step runs the geodesic perpendicular to the ray: a straight chord in Klein, an arc orthogonal to the rim in Poincaré; neighbouring ones are Δη apart in hyperbolic distance.
  2. The gap. What is left to the rim is 1 − r = 2/(e2η + 1) (Klein) or 1 − ρ = 2/(eη + 1) (Poincaré): positive for every finite k, and shrinking exponentially. The magnifier zooms by integer powers of 10 and keeps this magenta gap visible.
  3. Angle ruler. Switch to the outside angle ruler: each step adds the same Δθ = θ₁ − θ* to θ (the staircase's own first step). After finitely many steps θ passes 90° and the point lands on the rim (the page records that step as exactly 90°), then falls back to the centre θ = 0 and walks again: the chapter's model of turning into light, wiping the table and starting over (MODEL). The strip plots the pinching ledger of ρ = ½[[1 + c, s], [s, 1 − c]]: the axial bias ½c² kept on the diagonal (cyan) and the discarded coherence ½s² (magenta), which always add up to ½; at the step on the rim the cyan part is zero.
  4. Folding. After the first wipe, steps n and n + p have the same angle, where p is the number of steps in a round. The wheel draws each round as a spoke; folding turns all spokes to one direction, and every position carries one and the same angle: the target “angle” is constant on every fibre of the readout “step number mod p”, so it factors through it.
  5. Fractional map. w = (s − 1)/s = 1 − 1/s sends the vertical line Re s = c (c > 1/2) to the circle with centre 1 − 1/(2c) and radius 1/(2c); all of them lie inside the unit circle and touch it at w = 1; the closer c is to 1/2, the closer the image hugs the unit circle; the image of Re s = 1/2 is the unit circle itself, while w = 1 is the image of no finite s.
