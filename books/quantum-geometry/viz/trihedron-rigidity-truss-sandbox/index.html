理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

舞台是单位比较球面 S² ⊂ ℝ³ 上的 N 个概念 C₁, …, CN（3 ≤ N ≤ 8）与它们之间的比较弧。本页是数值演示，不是证明：每个数都由页面当场算出（NUM），每条定理只在下面写明的那一个实例上演示。

  1. 布局。C₁ 是枢纽；C₂, …, C₇ 依次绕 C₁ 排在弧距 ε 处，相邻两根辐条相隔等边球面三角形的顶角 A(ε) = arccos(c/(1 + c))，c = cos ε，所以每个扇形 C₁CkCk+1 都是边长 ε 的极小弧面体 ΔTε；C₈ 是 C₁ 关于弧 C₄C₅ 的镜像，补上第六块。整体只做一次刚性转动，让概念团对准镜头。
  2. 计数。全员两两比较有 E(KN) = N(N − 1)/2 条弧；三维里 N 个自由节点的桁架刚好刚性需要 3N − 6 根杆（麦克斯韦计数，文献结果）。差 ΔE = (N − 3)(N − 4)/2 只在 N = 3、4 为零：三个与四个概念时，“全都比一遍”恰好等于“刚好够刚”；N ≥ 5 多出的比较是冗余的，它们必须彼此相容。每个新概念 Ck 先用离它最近的三个旧概念定住（青色），其余比较为冗余（品红），合计 3N − 6 条定位比较。
  3. 砍掉对角。只留一圈 CN（边都是 ε）时 E = N，ΔE = 6 − 2N；N = 4 时 ΔE = −2。软模数按三维计数为 3N − 6 − N，贴在球面上为 N − 3：四边形有一个剪切自由度。页面让链上的内角随推力变化，最后一个节点由两圆相交闭合，每条边长保持到 10⁻¹² 以内；菱形的两条对角余弦始终满足 (1 + c₁₃)(1 + c₂₄) = 4 cos²ε，这是四个单位向量的格拉姆表秩不超过 3 的结果。补回两条对角（K₄），c₁₃、c₂₄ 被定住，ε = 60° 时停在 (0.5, −1/3)：这块四面体由两个共边的 ΔT60° 组成。注意边长全为 60° 的 K₄ 在 S² 上不存在：它的格拉姆表秩为 4。
  4. 不可下塌。三个概念的格拉姆行列式 det G₃ = 1 + 2c³ − 3c² = (1 − c)²(1 + 2c) 在 0 < ε < 120° 时为正，三者线性无关；球面三角形的角超 Ω = 3A(ε) − π > 0，而平直弦三角形内角和恰为 π、角超为 0，塌成线段时 det G₃ = 0。
  5. 棱上的曲率。ε = 60° 时 A = arccos(1/3)，恰是正四面体的二面角：五块 ΔT60° 绕 C₁ 合不拢，留下 2π − 5 arccos(1/3) ≈ 7.356° 的缝，与五个正四面体绕一条棱留下的缝相同。雷杰离散引力把这种缝读成集中在棱上的曲率（文献结果）；页面的“雷杰辉光” ΔE·(2π − 5 arccos(1/3)) 是示意量（MODEL），它只是把冗余比较数换成角度，不是测量值。
  6. 上链缺陷。取 F(i, j, k) 为三角形 CiCjCk 的带号立体角 2 atan2(Ci·(Cj × Ck), 1 + Ci·Cj + Cj·Ck + Ck·Ci)。概念都在一个开半球里时，四面体上边界处处为零，F 是边上链的上边界；某个四元组的四面体把球心包进去时，上边界是 ±4π，缺陷数随之出现。
  7. 冻结定理。依据栏的八条 Lean 定理都是一般陈述，页面只演示它们在这个布局上的样子：exists_unitary_mul_eq_of_conjTranspose_mul_eq、bloch_geometry_of_isHermitian、three_anchor_gram_det_formula、three_anchor_gram_det_pos_iff、fs_angle_triangle、two_axis_finite_word、face_cycle_exact、tetra_defects_incidence_repair_and_exactness。麦克斯韦计数、亨内贝格加点、吉拉尔定理与雷杰离散引力是文献结果（ARGUED），“概念—比较桁架”的说法、扰动、应力与辉光是模型（MODEL）。

The stage shows N concepts C₁, …, CN (3 ≤ N ≤ 8) on the unit comparison sphere S² ⊂ ℝ³ and the comparison arcs between them. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof: every number is computed on the spot (NUM), and each theorem is shown only on the one instance written below.

  1. The layout. C₁ is the hub; C₂, …, C₇ sit round it at arc distance ε, consecutive spokes separated by the angle of the equilateral spherical triangle, A(ε) = arccos(c/(1 + c)) with c = cos ε, so each sector C₁CkCk+1 is a minimal arc-body ΔTε of side ε; C₈ is the mirror image of C₁ across the arc C₄C₅ and adds a sixth one. A single rigid turn points the cluster at the camera.
  2. The count. Comparing all pairs gives E(KN) = N(N − 1)/2 arcs; a truss of N free nodes in three dimensions needs exactly 3N − 6 bars to be rigid (Maxwell counting, a literature result). The difference ΔE = (N − 3)(N − 4)/2 vanishes only at N = 3, 4: with three or four concepts, “compare everything” is exactly “just rigid”; from N = 5 on the extra comparisons are redundant and must agree with each other. Each new concept Ck is pinned first by its three nearest older concepts (cyan); its other comparisons are redundant (magenta); the pinning comparisons number 3N − 6.
  3. Cutting the diagonals. Keeping only the ring CN (all sides ε) gives E = N and ΔE = 6 − 2N, which is −2 at N = 4. The floppy modes number 3N − 6 − N by the three-dimensional count and N − 3 on the sphere: the four-ring has one shear. The page lets the interior angles along the chain follow the push and closes the last node on two circles, keeping every side length within 10⁻¹²; the two diagonal cosines of the rhombus always satisfy (1 + c₁₃)(1 + c₂₄) = 4 cos²ε, because the Gram table of four unit vectors has rank at most 3. Putting both diagonals back (K₄) fixes c₁₃ and c₂₄; at ε = 60° they rest at (0.5, −1/3): this tetrahedron is two ΔT60° sharing an edge. Note that a K₄ with all six arcs equal to 60° does not exist on S²: its Gram table has rank 4.
  4. No collapse. The Gram determinant of three concepts, det G₃ = 1 + 2c³ − 3c² = (1 − c)²(1 + 2c), is positive for 0 < ε < 120°, so the three are linearly independent; the spherical triangle has excess Ω = 3A(ε) − π > 0, whereas the flat chord triangle has angle sum exactly π and no excess, and a collapse onto a segment has det G₃ = 0.
  5. Curvature on the hinges. At ε = 60°, A = arccos(1/3), exactly the dihedral angle of the regular tetrahedron: five ΔT60° round C₁ do not close and leave a gap 2π − 5 arccos(1/3) ≈ 7.356°, the same gap five regular tetrahedra leave round an edge. Regge calculus reads such gaps as curvature concentrated on hinges (a literature result); the page's “Regge glow” ΔE·(2π − 5 arccos(1/3)) is a schematic quantity (MODEL) that only turns the number of redundant comparisons into an angle; it is not a measurement.
  6. Cochain defects. Take F(i, j, k) to be the signed solid angle of the triangle CiCjCk, 2 atan2(Ci·(Cj × Ck), 1 + Ci·Cj + Cj·Ck + Ck·Ci). While the concepts lie in one open hemisphere the tetrahedral coboundary vanishes everywhere and F is the coboundary of an edge cochain; once the tetrahedron of a quadruple encloses the centre, its coboundary is ±4π and defects appear.
  7. Frozen theorems. The eight Lean theorems of the evidence panel are general statements; the page only shows what they look like on this layout: exists_unitary_mul_eq_of_conjTranspose_mul_eq, bloch_geometry_of_isHermitian, three_anchor_gram_det_formula, three_anchor_gram_det_pos_iff, fs_angle_triangle, two_axis_finite_word, face_cycle_exact, tetra_defects_incidence_repair_and_exactness. Maxwell counting, Henneberg vertex addition, Girard's theorem and Regge calculus are literature results (ARGUED); the “concept–comparison truss” reading, the push, the stress and the glow are models (MODEL).
