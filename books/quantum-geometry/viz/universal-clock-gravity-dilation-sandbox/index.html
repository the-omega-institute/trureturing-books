LEAN ✓零锥刚性：实二次型 S 在闵可夫斯基零锥 t² = |w|² 上处处为零，则 S = S(1, 0)·(t² − |w|²)，对任意空间维数 d 成立。页面取 d = 2，在零锥上取 12 个方向，解出在这些方向上都为零的二次型：解空间只有一维，比例于 t² − x² − y²。于是钟率 dτ/dt = √(1 − β²) 由光锥定到只差一个常数因子：Null-cone rigidity: a real quadratic form S that vanishes on the Minkowski null cone t² = |w|² is S = S(1, 0)·(t² − |w|²), in every spatial dimension d. The page takes d = 2 and 12 directions on the null cone and solves for the quadratic forms vanishing on all of them: the solutions form one line, proportional to t² − x² − y². So the light cone fixes the rate dτ/dt = √(1 − β²) up to one constant factor: eq_smul_minkowski_of_null

理论接口 · 普适主频与钟慢Theory interface · the universal clock and dilation

这是数值沙盘，不是证明。每个数都由页面当场算出（NUM）；每条 Lean 定理只在依据栏写明的那一个实例上演示。

  1. 主频。两位观察者 A、B 各有一只紫色主飞轮，按同一个主频 ν₀ = 1 转动：时间轴每走一拍，两只飞轮都转一整圈，转过的角度永远相同。
  2. 勾股预算。飞轮的单位转轴 v = (√(1 − η)β, √η, √((1 − η)(1 − β²))) 在竖直的固有时轴上的投影是青色的钟率 dτ/dt = √((1 − η)(1 − β²))；其余是品红的负载：背景纠缠 η 与地址跳跃 (1 − η)β²。三个通道的份额 η + (1 − η)β² + (1 − η)(1 − β²) = 1，正是拉格朗日恒等式在 u = (0, 0, 1) 上的读法：线² + 面积² = 1。
  3. 帧柱。青色硬币是已经完成的固有时帧：主钟走过 ν₀t 拍时，A 完成 t 帧，B 完成 (dτ/dt)·t 帧。β = 0.80 时十拍里 B 只完成 6 帧；η = 0.40 时完成 7.75 帧；η = 1 时一帧也不完成。
  4. 快度。β = tanh r 时 cosh² r − sinh² r = 1 给出 dτ/dt = 1/cosh r = √(1 − β²)。零锥刚性说明：只要知道光锥，计时的二次型就定到只差一个常数因子。
  5. 光锥窗口。观察者在一拍发出雷达脉冲，N 拍后收到回声：能被往返核验的格点只在半径 Rmax = ½cΔτ = N/2 个格距的圆里（c = ℓPν₀，一拍一个格距）。N = 20 时圆里有 317 个格点。按记录计数上界，窗口里至多 N 拍，页面取精度下限 εself,min = 1/N。
  6. 等效。神经、原子、光尺三只子时钟在 A 里的频率是 0.25、2、1 拍；在 B 里全部乘同一个钟率因子，B 内部的比值与 A 内部的比值相同，局部比不出差别。

This is a numerical sandbox, not a proof. Every number is computed on the page (NUM); each Lean theorem is shown only on the one instance named in the evidence panel.

  1. The master clock. Observers A and B each turn a violet master flywheel at the same master frequency ν₀ = 1: with every beat of the rail both wheels turn once round, always through the same angle.
  2. The Pythagorean budget. The unit axis of a wheel, v = (√(1 − η)β, √η, √((1 − η)(1 − β²))), projects onto the upright proper-time axis as the cyan rate dτ/dt = √((1 − η)(1 − β²)); the rest is the magenta load: background entanglement η and address hops (1 − η)β². The three shares η + (1 − η)β² + (1 − η)(1 − β²) add up to 1, which is the Lagrange identity read at u = (0, 0, 1): line² + area² = 1.
  3. Frame stacks. The cyan coins are completed proper-time frames: after ν₀t master beats A has completed t frames and B has completed (dτ/dt)·t. At β = 0.80 B completes 6 frames in ten beats; at η = 0.40 it completes 7.75; at η = 1 it completes none.
  4. Rapidity. With β = tanh r, cosh² r − sinh² r = 1 gives dτ/dt = 1/cosh r = √(1 − β²). Null-cone rigidity says that the light cone alone fixes the quadratic form of timekeeping up to one constant factor.
  5. The light-cone window. The observer sends a radar pulse at one beat and hears its echo N beats later: the lattice nodes that a round trip can verify lie within the circle of radius Rmax = ½cΔτ = N/2 spacings (c = ℓPν₀, one spacing per beat). At N = 20 the circle holds 317 nodes. By the record-count bound a window holds at most N beats, and the page takes the precision floor εself,min = 1/N.
  6. Equivalence. The neural, atomic and photon-ruler sub-clocks run at 0.25, 2 and 1 beats in A; in B every one is multiplied by the same rate factor, so the ratios inside B equal the ratios inside A and no local comparison tells them apart.
