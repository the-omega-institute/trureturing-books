LEAN ✓对每个 2×2 复矩阵 ρ：‖ρ‖²HS = ‖Pρ‖²HS + ‖ρ − Pρ‖²HS，P 是计算基的捏合（不需要正性或迹一）。舞台的指针轴是 z，即计算基；读数里的“非对角 HS 质量”就是最后一项。这是范数平方的恒等式，不是熵公式。已冻结：For every complex 2×2 matrix ρ: ‖ρ‖²HS = ‖Pρ‖²HS + ‖ρ − Pρ‖²HS, with P the computational-basis pinching (no positivity or trace-one hypothesis). The pointer axis on the stage is z, the computational basis; the “off-diagonal HS mass” readout is the last term. A norm-square identity, not an entropy formula. Frozen: pinching_hilbert_schmidt_pythagorean
LEAN ✓若对同一个酉 U 每一步都有 ρ(k + 1) = 基捏合(Uρ(k)U†)，则每一步的熵增等于 D(Uρ(k)U† ‖ 它的捏合) ≥ 0，N 步的总熵增等于这些相对熵之和（自然对数；页面除以 ln 2 用比特）。只在“完全捏合”打开（α = π/2、U = I、轴全为 ±z）时引用；第一步之后增益为零。部分强度的去相位不在它的范围里。已冻结：If ρ(k + 1) = basis pinching of Uρ(k)U† for one fixed unitary U at every step, each entropy increment equals D(Uρ(k)U† ‖ its pinching) ≥ 0, and the total over N steps is the sum of these relative entropies (natural logarithms; the page divides by ln 2 for bits). Cited only with “full pinching” on (α = π/2, U = I, all axes ±z); after the first step the gain is zero. Partial-strength dephasing is outside it. Frozen: entropy_production_coherence_deletion_identity
LEAN ✓对有限的完备投影记录测量 P：未读态 Σₖ PₖρPₖ = ρ，当且仅当每个交叉块 PₖρPₗ（k ≠ l）为零。对 qubit 的两个秩一记录，这就是在指针基里对角：完全捏合（α = π/2）之后的态正是这样的不动点。它不说测量选出了单一结果。已冻结：For a finite complete projective record measurement P: the unread state Σₖ PₖρPₖ equals ρ exactly when every cross block PₖρPₗ (k ≠ l) vanishes. LEAN ✓固定一个非零项 ρᵢⱼ 与一份对该项重叠 ≠ 1 的记录 k；若其余每份记录的重叠都满足 c̄c = 1，则只对其余记录施加反向记录通道，该项仍不能复原（剩下 ckρᵢⱼ）。反向通道是共轭记录振幅，不是任意相互作用的伴随；对 |c| < 1 的记录它只乘以 |c|²，所以舞台上的擦除用的是受控 Uj†（MODEL），不是这条定理。已冻结：Fix a nonzero entry ρᵢⱼ and a record k whose overlap for it differs from 1; if every other record's overlap satisfies c̄c = 1, applying the reverse-record channels to all the other records still cannot restore the entry (ckρᵢⱼ is left). The reverse channel conjugates record amplitudes; it is not the adjoint of an arbitrary interaction, and for a record with |c| < 1 it only multiplies by |c|², so the erasure on the stage uses the controlled Uj† (MODEL), not this theorem. 理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

四个场景回答第 33 章的同一个问题：许多观察者各自丈量同一个系统时，什么留下、什么被删去。本页是数值演示，不是证明；每个数都在页面上实时算出，每种模型的假设写在下面。

  1. 共识张量。每位观察者带一根单位轴 nⱼ 与权重 wⱼ（显式归一化），M = Σ wⱼnⱼnⱼᵀ。(−n)(−n)ᵀ = nnᵀ，所以只有无向的轴算数。M 对称、半正定、迹为 1，M = BᵀB（B[j, a] = √wⱼ·nⱼ[a]）。特征值降序 λ₁ ≥ λ₂ ≥ λ₃，κ = (3λ₁ − 1)/2 ∈ [0, 1] 只量集中度，不单独保证唯一的轴：diag(½, ½, 0) 的 κ = ¼ 却没有唯一主轴。λ₁ − λ₂ < ε = 10⁻⁸ 时页面写“无唯一指针主轴”、不画指针轴，这是显示约定，不是物理常数；λ₁ − λ₂ ≥ ε 时画出的是一条无向的线，箭头方向的选择不是物理。
  2. 一次相遇。Kraus 算子 cos(α/2)·I 与 sin(α/2)·n·σ 给出 Pauli 信道，布洛赫矩阵 K(n, α) = cos α·I + (1 − cos α)·nnᵀ：沿 n 的分量不变，两个横向分量乘以 cos α。页面把 α 限在 [0, π/2]，在这个范围里横向单调衰减；α = π/2 是完全的秩一捏合。
  3. 三种构造，三个名字。按时间顺序的真实乘积 Kk⋯K₁（第一位在最右，一般不对称，也不只由 M 决定）；每次按权重随机选轴的均值信道 K̄ᵏ，K̄ = cos α·I + (1 − cos α)·M，模因子 [λᵢ + (1 − λᵢ)cos α]ᵏ；弱相互作用极限 exp[−t(I − M)]，t = kα²/2，模因子 e−t(1 − λᵢ)。各向同性的雾：均值缩放 [(1 + 2cos α)/3]ᵏ，k = 16、α = 0.4 时为 0.4211，弱极限 e−kα²/3 为 0.4260；共用一轴：有限的横向因子 (cos α)ᵏ = 0.2683，弱极限 e−kα²/2 = 0.2780。两者只在 α → 0 时渐近相同。M 各向同性并不保证每一段有限的历史都各向同性：在“六轴雾”里换成按时间顺序就能看到。
  4. 纵向与横向。在指针基 |±e⟩ 里 ρ₊₋ = (r₂ − i·r₃)/2、ρ₊₊ − ρ₋₋ = r₁：横向的布洛赫分量就是非对角相干的两个正交分量，被删掉的 HS 质量是 ‖ρ − Πρ‖²HS = (r₂² + r₃²)/2。只有 λ₁ = 1（所有轴共线）时纵向才严格保留；λ₁ < 1 时最终都趋向 I/2，最慢衰减的方向只是有限时间里相对优先的方向。
  5. 轴端与相位。翻转一根轴的端点只是交换它的两个结果标签，nnᵀ 逐项不变；它不把布洛赫球上相对的两个纯态（彼此正交）当成同一个态。ket 乘一个公共相位 eiθ 不改变 |ψ⟩⟨ψ|，这是另一种变换，右下角的小图单独画它，θ 随时间轴转动只是为了演示。
  6. 冗余记录。共同指针基，p₀ + p₁ = 1，每份记录 |φj(0)⟩、|φj(1)⟩ 的重叠为 cj，全局态是纯的两分支乘积态。任一片段 F 的约化态秩不超过 2，S(ρF) = h(ΠF)，h(q) = H₂((1 − d)/2)，d = √(1 − 4p₀p₁(1 − |q|²))，于是 I(S : Om) = S(ρS) + S(ρOm) − S(ρON−m)：m = 0 为 0，m = N 为 2S(ρS)。c = 0.3、N = 16 时 m = 3…13 与 S(ρS) 之差小于 0.000526 比特。最后升到 2S 的部分含完整纯化的量子关联，不是“两倍的经典客观信息”。这个闭式只对所设的纯分支模型成立。
  7. 擦除。对能访问的记录施加受控 Uj† 把它们送回空白，剩下的系统相干是 √(p₀p₁)·|Π未控制 cj|；只要有一份 |c| < 1 的记录访问不到，相干就回不来。这是所设受控酉模型里的计算，与 T09 的访问阻碍一致；右下角的相位证书（1/2 → −1/2 → −i/2 → 1/2）是另一件事。
  8. 两个气泡。Z 气泡与 X 气泡的完全捏合在布洛赫坐标里是 diag(0, 0, 1) 与 diag(1, 0, 0)，两种次序都把一切送到 I/2：未读的去相位对易（任意强度也对易），尽管可观测量 X、Z 不对易。两者合起来的张量 ½(xxᵀ + zzᵀ) 谱为 (½, ½, 0)，没有唯一的主轴。真正依赖次序的是选读：从 I/2 出发，先 Z⁺ 后 X⁺ 得 PX+/4，先 X⁺ 后 Z⁺ 得 PZ+/4，迹都是 1/4，HS 距离 1/4。倾斜的轴 m 与 n 之间 [Kn, Km] = (1 − cn)(1 − cm)(n·m)(nmᵀ − mnᵀ)，平行与垂直时为零，45° 时不为零。
  9. 局部友好。两个逻辑实验室，状态 (I ⊗ UB)|Φ⁺⟩，UB = exp(−iθY/2)，θ = atan2(−1, −2)，同时旋转 Bob 的观测量，使 A₁ = Z、B′₁ = X，所有关联不变。P(a, b | x, y) 由 4×4 矩阵实际求迹得到，36 个概率都非负且无信号。白噪声 ρv = v·|ψ′⟩⟨ψ′| + (1 − v)·I/4 下 F = v·7.0838…，在 v > 6/7.0838… ≈ 0.8470 时越界。页面也枚举了这个二元 3 × 3 情形的 96 个 LF 顶点，最大值恰为 6。这里的逻辑实验室不说人类朋友或宏观环境可以被逆转。
  10. 先驱。旧平面 S = span(x, y)，几何投影 PS = diag(1, 1, 0) 与张量 M₀ = diag(0.7, 0.3, 0) 是两样东西（迹分别是 2 与 1）。新轴 n(β) = cos β·x + sin β·z 以权重 η 加入：残差 ‖(I − PS)n‖ = sin β，Mη = (1 − η)M₀ + η·nnᵀ，det Mη = (1 − η)²·0.7·0.3·η·sin²β；0 < η < 1 且 sin β ≠ 0 时秩从 2 变成 3（数值秩阈值 10⁻⁸）。在旧的生成元 I − M₀ 下，z 方向的布洛赫分量按 e−t 衰减，最快：M₀ 的零空间不是免于去相干的方向。

书中：第 33 章 · 共识、去相干与指针轴

Four scenes answer one question of chapter 33: when many observers each measure the same system, what stays and what is deleted. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof; every number is computed live on the page, and each model's assumptions are written below.

  1. The consensus tensor. Each observer carries a unit axis nⱼ and a weight wⱼ (normalized explicitly), M = Σ wⱼnⱼnⱼᵀ. Since (−n)(−n)ᵀ = nnᵀ only the unoriented axis counts. M is symmetric, positive semidefinite, of trace 1, and M = BᵀB (B[j, a] = √wⱼ·nⱼ[a]). With eigenvalues sorted λ₁ ≥ λ₂ ≥ λ₃, κ = (3λ₁ − 1)/2 ∈ [0, 1] measures concentration only and does not by itself certify a unique axis: diag(½, ½, 0) has κ = ¼ and no unique top axis. When λ₁ − λ₂ < ε = 10⁻⁸ the page writes “No unique pointer axis” and draws none: a display convention, not a physical constant. When λ₁ − λ₂ ≥ ε it draws an unoriented line; the choice of arrow direction is not physics.
  2. One encounter. The Kraus operators cos(α/2)·I and sin(α/2)·n·σ give a Pauli channel with Bloch matrix K(n, α) = cos α·I + (1 − cos α)·nnᵀ: the component along n stays, both transverse components are multiplied by cos α. The page keeps α in [0, π/2], where the transverse decay is monotone; α = π/2 is the complete rank-one pinching.
  3. Three constructions, three names. The actual ordered product Kk⋯K₁ (the first observer at the right; in general not symmetric and not determined by M alone); the mean channel K̄ᵏ of axes drawn by weight at every encounter, K̄ = cos α·I + (1 − cos α)·M, with mode factors [λᵢ + (1 − λᵢ)cos α]ᵏ; the weak-interaction limit exp[−t(I − M)], t = kα²/2, with mode factors e−t(1 − λᵢ). Isotropic fog: mean scale [(1 + 2cos α)/3]ᵏ, 0.4211 at k = 16 and α = 0.4, weak limit e−kα²/3 = 0.4260; one shared axis: the finite transverse factor (cos α)ᵏ = 0.2683, weak limit e−kα²/2 = 0.2780. They agree only asymptotically as α → 0. An isotropic M does not make every finite history isotropic: switch the six-axis fog to the ordered product to see it.
  4. Along and across. In the pointer basis |±e⟩, ρ₊₋ = (r₂ − i·r₃)/2 and ρ₊₊ − ρ₋₋ = r₁: the transverse Bloch components are the two quadratures of off-diagonal coherence, and the deleted HS mass is ‖ρ − Πρ‖²HS = (r₂² + r₃²)/2. The longitudinal part is preserved exactly only when λ₁ = 1 (all axes collinear); for λ₁ < 1 everything eventually tends to I/2, and the slowest-damped direction is only preferred for a finite time.
  5. Axis ends and phases. Flipping the end of an axis only swaps its two outcome labels and leaves nnᵀ unchanged entry by entry; it does not identify the two opposite pure states on the Bloch sphere (they are orthogonal). Multiplying a ket by a common phase eiθ leaves |ψ⟩⟨ψ| unchanged: a different transformation, drawn on its own in the inset, where θ follows the rail only for display.
  6. Redundant records. A common pointer basis, p₀ + p₁ = 1, records |φj(0)⟩ and |φj(1)⟩ with overlap cj, and a pure two-branch product state overall. The reduced state of any fragment F has rank at most 2, S(ρF) = h(ΠF) with h(q) = H₂((1 − d)/2), d = √(1 − 4p₀p₁(1 − |q|²)), so I(S : Om) = S(ρS) + S(ρOm) − S(ρON−m): 0 at m = 0 and 2S(ρS) at m = N. With c = 0.3 and N = 16, m = 3…13 stays within 0.000526 bits of S(ρS). The final rise to 2S contains quantum correlations of the complete purification; it is not “twice the classical objective information”. The closed form holds only for the stated pure branching model.
  7. Erasure. Applying the controlled Uj† to accessible records returns them to blank, and the system coherence left is √(p₀p₁)·|Πuncontrolled cj|; one inaccessible record with |c| < 1 is enough to keep the coherence from coming back. This is a calculation in the stated controlled-unitary model, consistent with the access obstruction of T09; the phase certificate at the bottom right (1/2 → −1/2 → −i/2 → 1/2) is a separate matter.
  8. Two bubbles. In Bloch coordinates the full pinchings of the Z bubble and the X bubble are diag(0, 0, 1) and diag(1, 0, 0), and either order sends everything to I/2: the unread dephasings commute (at any strength), although the observables X and Z do not. Their joint tensor ½(xxᵀ + zzᵀ) has spectrum (½, ½, 0) and no unique top axis. What does depend on order is the selected update: from I/2, Z⁺ then X⁺ gives PX+/4 and X⁺ then Z⁺ gives PZ+/4, both of trace 1/4, at HS distance 1/4. For a tilted axis m against n, [Kn, Km] = (1 − cn)(1 − cm)(n·m)(nmᵀ − mnᵀ): zero when parallel or perpendicular, nonzero at 45°.
  9. Local Friendliness. Two logical laboratories in the state (I ⊗ UB)|Φ⁺⟩, UB = exp(−iθY/2), θ = atan2(−1, −2), with Bob's observables rotated along so that A₁ = Z and B′₁ = X and every correlation is unchanged. P(a, b | x, y) comes from actual traces of 4×4 matrices; all 36 probabilities are nonnegative and no-signalling. Under white noise ρv = v·|ψ′⟩⟨ψ′| + (1 − v)·I/4, F = v·7.0838…, which exceeds 6 for v > 6/7.0838… ≈ 0.8470. The page also enumerates the 96 LF vertices of this binary 3 × 3 scenario; their maximum is exactly 6. These logical laboratories say nothing about reversing a human friend or a macroscopic environment.
  10. Pioneer. Old plane S = span(x, y): the geometric projection PS = diag(1, 1, 0) and the tensor M₀ = diag(0.7, 0.3, 0) are different things (traces 2 and 1). A new axis n(β) = cos β·x + sin β·z joins with weight η: the residual ‖(I − PS)n‖ = sin β, Mη = (1 − η)M₀ + η·nnᵀ, det Mη = (1 − η)²·0.7·0.3·η·sin²β; for 0 < η < 1 and sin β ≠ 0 the rank grows from 2 to 3 (numerical rank threshold 10⁻⁸). Under the old generator I − M₀ the z component of the Bloch vector decays as e−t, the fastest of all: the nullspace of M₀ is not a decoherence-free direction.
