理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

终章说：历史遗迹像空间吸引子，同一个观察者忘掉了表层的记事，却一轮又一轮走回熟悉的地方。本页用洛伦兹系统把这句话里能算的部分画出来。本页是数值演示，不是证明；诠释是本书的模型，不是定理。

  1. 两轮。两条轨道从相距很远的两点出发，用同一组参数。短暂的游荡之后它们落到同一对翅膀上，却各走各的步子。琥珀色的圈是吸引子上一处固定的邻域：两条轨道都一次次穿过它，每次穿过的弧段互不重合，方向却几乎平行。两轮各有一次经过、且两点相距 ‖x₁(ta) − x₂(tb)‖ < ε 时，算一次重返：读数给出那里两条单位切向 v̂ = F/‖F‖ 的夹角 δθ 与内积 cos δθ = v̂₁·v̂₂，舞台上画成青色与品红的两支箭头。
  2. 两个非零平衡点。ρ > 1 时，除原点外还有 C₊ = (+√(β(ρ−1)), +√(β(ρ−1)), ρ−1) 与 C₋ = (−√(β(ρ−1)), −√(β(ρ−1)), ρ−1)，x 与 y 同号，舞台上画成两个空心圈。两点的雅可比矩阵有同一个特征多项式 λ³ + (σ + 1 + β)λ² + β(σ + ρ)λ + 2σβ(ρ − 1)。ρ = 28 时它的根约为 −13.85 与 0.094 ± 10.19i：一个负实根加一对实部为正的共轭复根，两点都是不稳定的鞍焦点，轨道绕着它们转，却落不进去。ρ = 14 时三个根的实部都为负，两点稳定，“落进一个点”就是落进其中之一。
  3. 收缩。速度场的散度是常数 −41/3，任何一团点的体积都按 e^(−41t/3) 缩小：t = 1 时只剩约百万分之一。舞台上的 500 个点很快被压到一张薄片上。页面另外沿一条轨道积分切映射，它的行列式与 e^(−41t/3) 相差不到千分之二。
  4. 维数。三个李雅普诺夫指数之和等于散度 −41/3。ρ = 28 时最大的一个为正（相邻轨道分离），中间一个为零（沿轨道方向），最小的约 −14.57；卡普兰—约克维数 2 + λ₁/|λ₃| ≈ 2.06：比一张面多一点。ρ = 14 时三个都为负，轨道落进一个不动点；ρ = 160 时最大的一个为零，轨道绕着一条闭合的环。

书中：终章 · 历史遗迹作为空间吸引子 · 遗忘与内禀倾角 · 似曾相识与维数估计

The coda says that historical ruins are like spatial attractors: one observer forgets the surface record and still walks back, round after round, to familiar places. This page draws the part of that sentence that can be computed, with the Lorenz system. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof; the interpretation is a model of this book, not a theorem.

  1. Rounds. Two orbits start from two distant points with the same parameters. After a short wander they land on the same pair of wings, each at its own pace. The amber circle is one fixed neighbourhood on the attractor: both orbits pass through it again and again, each time along an arc that coincides with no earlier one, yet in nearly the same direction. When a passage of each round has its two points ‖x₁(ta) − x₂(tb)‖ < ε apart, that is a return: the readouts give the angle δθ between the two unit tangents v̂ = F/‖F‖ there and their inner product cos δθ = v̂₁·v̂₂, drawn on the stage as a cyan and a magenta arrow.
  2. The two non-zero equilibria. For ρ > 1, besides the origin there are C₊ = (+√(β(ρ−1)), +√(β(ρ−1)), ρ−1) and C₋ = (−√(β(ρ−1)), −√(β(ρ−1)), ρ−1), with x and y of the same sign, drawn on the stage as two open rings. The Jacobian has the same characteristic polynomial λ³ + (σ + 1 + β)λ² + β(σ + ρ)λ + 2σβ(ρ − 1) at both. For ρ = 28 its roots are about −13.85 and 0.094 ± 10.19i: one negative real root and a complex pair with positive real part, so both points are unstable saddle-foci, and the orbits wind around them without falling in. For ρ = 14 all three roots have negative real part and both points are stable; “falling into a point” means falling into one of them.
  3. Contraction. The divergence of the velocity field is the constant −41/3, so the volume of any cloud shrinks like e^(−41t/3): at t = 1 about one millionth is left. The 500 points on the stage are quickly pressed onto a thin sheet. The page also integrates the tangent map along one orbit, and its determinant differs from e^(−41t/3) by less than two parts in a thousand.
  4. Dimension. The three Lyapunov exponents add up to the divergence −41/3. For ρ = 28 the greatest is positive (nearby orbits separate), the middle one is zero (along the orbit) and the least is about −14.57; the Kaplan–Yorke dimension 2 + λ₁/|λ₃| ≈ 2.06 is a little more than a surface. For ρ = 14 all three are negative and the orbits fall into a fixed point; for ρ = 160 the greatest is zero and the orbits go around one closed loop.
