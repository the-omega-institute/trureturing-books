理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

“反者道之动”：一个概念不是只从一侧长出来的。能到达它的路收向它，到不了它的路在边界折回；两侧在同一道切口相遇，切口越锐，概念越清楚。本页是数值演示，不是证明：每个数都由页面当场算出（NUM），定理只在下面写明的那个有限实例上演示。

  1. 比较阶梯。ℝ⁴ 里第一个锚点是 x = e₁，唯一的自比较 CMP(x, x) = 1（0D 点）。比较信息 Icmp 每越过一个阈值 0.5、1.5、2.5，就多一个锚点 ak+1 = cos ε e₁ + sin ε ek+1，ε = 18°(1 + Icmp)；未越过时它与 x 重叠为 1，就是 x 本身。格拉姆行列式按两锚、三锚与 4 × 4 公式算出：det G₂ = sin²ε、det G₃ = sin⁴ε、det G₄ = sin⁶ε，为正当且仅当锚点线性无关。秩依次为 1、2、3、4：点、线、面、体。
  2. 溢出撑开新轴。新锚点在旧张成之外的残差是 sin ε ek+1，归一化后是一根单位向量：旧投影把它送到 0，它离旧张成的距离恰为 1。舞台画的是各锚点在 x 之外的残差：青色线段长 √det G₂，品红三角形面积 √det G₃ / 2，紫色四面体体积 √det G₄ / 6；品红箭头从旧概念的中心沿新轴伸出。
  3. 相位碰撞与时间轴。从 Icmp = 3.5 起，四面体质心处有一对反相的态 ψ₊ 与 ψ₋ = −ψ₊。有限竞技场是四个顶点上的 ψ₊ 加上这一对；读出是坐标 x、y、z、公平平方 |ψ|² 与相位切口 T。前四条读出留下 2 个有序逃逸对（ψ₊ 与 ψ₋ 互相分不开），加上 T 后为 0：T 降低逃逸率，等价于它使联合核严格变小。把 T 画成沿第四根轴上升的螺旋是模型。
  4. 来路的对角化。K × K 表 T[r][c] ∈ {0, 1, 2, 3} 记录列出的第 r 条来路 Γr → x 在第 c 步的相位象限（0、π/2、π、3π/2）。扭转 twist(y) = y + 2 (mod 4) 没有不动点；对角路径 Γdiag[k] = twist(T[k][k]) 在第 k 步与第 k 行相差 π，所以不等于表中任何一行，最后一步仍汇入 x。加细用单坐标实例：K² 条步记录的 latent 都是“到达 x”，只有 1 类；它们的象限目录覆盖全部四个象限，并入扭转后的对角读出后分成 4 类。
  5. 双无限切口。路径振幅取在 ℝ² 里，M = span((3, 4)/5)：PM = [[9, 12], [12, 16]] / 25，PM⊥ = [[16, −12], [−12, 9]] / 25。分子都是整数，所以 PMPM⊥ = 0、PM + PM⊥ = I、PM² = PM 逐项精确；从 M⊥ = span((−4, 3)) 再取一次正交补，投影回到 PM。模型 Peff(κ₋) = PM + (1 − κ₋)PM⊥：可达权重 Tr PM = 1，不可达权重 1 − κ₋，切口锐度 κ₋，区分信息 1 − H₂(1/(2 − κ₋)) 比特。κ₋ = 0 时 Peff = I，两侧不再有分别，概念化进背景的雾。
  6. 级联。点 → 线：溢出 sin ε = √(det G₂ / det G₁)；线 → 面：√(det G₃ / det G₂)；面 → 体：√(det G₄ / det G₃)；体 → 时间：T 独自捕获的逃逸对。每一级的溢出量与下一级生成元的长度相等，这是残差的定义；把它读成“阻碍变成生成元”的级联，是作者的诠释（MODEL）。
  7. 冻结定理。页面引用九条已冻结的 Lean 定理（依据面板里的 LEAN ✓）：unit_gram_overlap_characterization、two_anchor_gram_det_pos_iff、three_anchor_gram_det_pos_iff、finite_layer_projection_escape、lowersEscape_iff_strict_kernel_novelty、relative_semantic_diagonal_ne_listed_decode、complete_diagonal_strict_topology_refinement、orthogonal_complement_projection_identities、double_orthogonal_complement_eq_closure。它们是一般陈述，页面只演示它们在上面这些有限实例上的样子。

书中：第二十九章 §29.10 · 反者道之动：来路对角化与概念开维级联

“Reversal is the movement of the Dao”: a concept does not grow from one side only. The paths that can reach it converge on it, the paths that cannot are turned back at its boundary; the two sides meet at one cut, and the sharper the cut, the clearer the concept. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof: every number is computed on the spot (NUM), and each theorem is shown only on the finite instance written below.

  1. The comparison ladder. The first anchor in ℝ⁴ is x = e₁, with the one self-comparison CMP(x, x) = 1 (the 0D point). Each time the comparison information Icmp passes a threshold 0.5, 1.5, 2.5, one more anchor ak+1 = cos ε e₁ + sin ε ek+1 joins, with ε = 18°(1 + Icmp); before that it has overlap 1 with x and is x itself. The Gram determinants are computed by the two-anchor, three-anchor and 4 × 4 formulas: det G₂ = sin²ε, det G₃ = sin⁴ε, det G₄ = sin⁶ε, positive exactly when the anchors are linearly independent. The rank runs 1, 2, 3, 4: point, line, surface, volume.
  2. Overflow opens a new axis. The residual of a new anchor off the old span is sin ε ek+1; normalised it is a unit vector that the old projection sends to 0 and that lies at distance exactly 1 from the old span. The stage draws the residual of each anchor off x: the cyan segment has length √det G₂, the magenta triangle area √det G₃ / 2, the violet tetrahedron volume √det G₄ / 6; the magenta arrows leave the centre of the old concept along the new axis.
  3. Phase collision and the time axis. From Icmp = 3.5 the centroid of the tetrahedron holds a pair of opposite-phase states ψ₊ and ψ₋ = −ψ₊. The finite arena is ψ₊ at the four vertices plus this pair; the readouts are the coordinates x, y, z, the fairness square |ψ|² and the phase cut T. The first four readouts leave 2 ordered escape pairs (ψ₊ and ψ₋ cannot be told apart), and with T there are 0: T lowers the escape rate, which is the same as T strictly shrinking the joint kernel. Drawing T as a helix rising along a fourth axis is a model.
  4. Diagonalising the ways in. The K × K table T[r][c] ∈ {0, 1, 2, 3} records the phase quadrant (0, π/2, π, 3π/2) of step c of the listed path Γr → x. The twist twist(y) = y + 2 (mod 4) has no fixed point; the diagonal path Γdiag[k] = twist(T[k][k]) differs from row k by π at step k, so it equals no row of the table, and its last step still runs into x. The refinement uses a one-coordinate instance: all K² step records have the latent “arrives at x”, one class; their quadrant catalog covers all four quadrants, and joining the twisted diagonal readout splits them into 4 classes.
  5. The dual-infinite cut. Path amplitudes live in ℝ², with M = span((3, 4)/5): PM = [[9, 12], [12, 16]] / 25 and PM⊥ = [[16, −12], [−12, 9]] / 25. The numerators are integers, so PMPM⊥ = 0, PM + PM⊥ = I and PM² = PM hold exactly entry by entry; taking the orthogonal complement of M⊥ = span((−4, 3)) once more gives PM back. The model Peff(κ₋) = PM + (1 − κ₋)PM⊥ has reachable weight Tr PM = 1, unreachable weight 1 − κ₋, cut sharpness κ₋ and distinction 1 − H₂(1/(2 − κ₋)) bits. At κ₋ = 0, Peff = I: the two sides are no longer told apart, and the concept melts into the background haze.
  6. The cascade. Point → line: the overflow sin ε = √(det G₂ / det G₁); line → surface: √(det G₃ / det G₂); surface → volume: √(det G₄ / det G₃); volume → time: the escape pairs T alone captures. The overflow of each level equals the length of the next generator, which is what a residual is; reading this as a cascade in which each obstruction becomes a generator is an interpretation by the author (MODEL).
  7. Frozen theorems. The page cites nine frozen Lean theorems (LEAN ✓ in the evidence panel): unit_gram_overlap_characterization, two_anchor_gram_det_pos_iff, three_anchor_gram_det_pos_iff, finite_layer_projection_escape, lowersEscape_iff_strict_kernel_novelty, relative_semantic_diagonal_ne_listed_decode, complete_diagonal_strict_topology_refinement, orthogonal_complement_projection_identities and double_orthogonal_complement_eq_closure. They are general statements; the page only shows what they look like on the finite instances above.
