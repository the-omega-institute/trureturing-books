理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

青色的箭头 A 是已知态 ρA = ½(I + a·σ)，琥珀色的箭头 B 是目标态，快门问的是“是不是 B”：PB = ½(I + b·σ) 记“过”（+1），I − PB 记“不过”（−1）。两支箭头之间只有一个角 θ，不需要第三个坐标架。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 一次只有两种读数。每按一次快门，读数只落在 A 那条直径的两端：+1（过）或 −1（不过）。哪一端是随机的，页面用固定种子的伪随机数抽（MODEL）。
  2. 权重是夹角定的。“过”的权重 p = Tr(ρAPB) = |⟨φB|ψA⟩|²：rank_one_born_pairing_weight（LEAN ✓）。第二章的半角公式把它写成 (1 + cos θ)/2 = cos²(θ/2)；页面用球面点积、旋量内积与 2×2 矩阵的迹三条路各算一遍（NUM）。在球上，B 向 A 的直径作垂线，垂足把直径分成两段，靠 −1 那端的一段占全长的 p：图上青色的那一段。
  3. 两份权重加起来是 1。“过”与“不过”是一套完备记录测量的两支，权重非负、相加为 1：born_probability_skeleton（LEAN ✓）。
  4. 比例趋向权重。按了 N 次，过了 k 次，比例 k/N 在青色虚线 p 附近摆动，N 越大摆得越小。这是大数定律（概率论的经典结果，本书未形式化）；页面只演示一轮抽样，浅色带 p ± 2√(p(1 − p)/N) 是参考尺度，不是保证。90° 时 p(1 − p) 最大，摆得最厉害；0° 与 180° 时带宽为零，每次结果都确定。
  5. 一起转，权重不动。“两支一起转”时，每次快门前把 A 与 B 绕同一根紫色的轴转同一个角。夹角不变，每一次快门的权重都不变；页面逐次用矩阵重算权重，用同一串随机数得到与不转时相同的计数。这对应第二章的话：迹在基变换下不变，Tr(UρU* · UPU*) = Tr(ρP)。
  6. 随机方向上权重均匀。“每次随机方向”让 B 在球面上均匀地随机取（三个高斯数归一化），十段直方图越数越平，每段约占 1/10。这是第二章例题的初等计算：帽盒定理给出高度 cos θ 在 [−1, 1] 上均匀，p = (1 + cos θ)/2 于是在 [0, 1] 上均匀（NUM）。

The cyan arrow A is the known state ρA = ½(I + a·σ), the amber arrow B is the target, and the shutter asks “is it B?”: PB = ½(I + b·σ) records “pass” (+1) and I − PB records “fail” (−1). Between the two arrows there is just one angle θ, and no third frame is needed. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Only two readings per shot. Each shot lands at one of the two ends of A's diameter: +1 (pass) or −1 (fail). Which end is random; the page draws it with a seeded pseudo-random generator (MODEL).
  2. The angle sets the weight. The weight of “pass” is p = Tr(ρAPB) = |⟨φB|ψA⟩|²: rank_one_born_pairing_weight (LEAN ✓). Chapter 2's half-angle formula writes it as (1 + cos θ)/2 = cos²(θ/2); the page computes it by the dot product on the sphere, the spinor overlap and the trace of 2×2 matrices (NUM). On the sphere, drop a perpendicular from B onto A's diameter: its foot cuts the diameter so that the stretch on the −1 side is p of the whole, the cyan stretch in the picture.
  3. The two weights add up to 1. “Pass” and “fail” are the two branches of a complete record measurement; their weights are non-negative and add up to 1: born_probability_skeleton (LEAN ✓).
  4. The share heads for the weight. After N shots with k passes, the share k/N wobbles around the dashed cyan line p, less and less as N grows. That is the law of large numbers (a classical result of probability, not formalized in this book); the page shows one run, and the pale band p ± 2√(p(1 − p)/N) is a reference scale, not a guarantee. At 90° p(1 − p) is largest and the wobble widest; at 0° and 180° the band has zero width and every outcome is certain.
  5. Turning both leaves the weight alone. With “both turn together”, before each shot A and B are turned by the same angle about the same violet axis. The angle between them stays, so the weight of every shot stays; the page recomputes the weight shot by shot with matrices and, with the same random numbers, gets the same counts as without turning. This is Chapter 2's remark that the trace does not change under a change of basis, Tr(UρU* · UPU*) = Tr(ρP).
  6. On random directions the weight is uniform. “A random direction each shot” draws B uniformly on the sphere (three Gaussian numbers, normalized); the ten-bin histogram flattens as it fills, each bin near 1/10. This is the elementary computation of Chapter 2's example: the hat-box theorem makes the height cos θ uniform on [−1, 1], so p = (1 + cos θ)/2 is uniform on [0, 1] (NUM).
