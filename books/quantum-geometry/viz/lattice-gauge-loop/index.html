理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

舞台是一小块格点：每条有向边上有一个单位圆相位 u(e)（边上的指针），每个顶点可以做规范变换 g(v)（紫色指针），边相位随之变为 g(t(e))·u(e)·g(s(e))⁻¹。时间轴从变换前走到变换后：边上的指针都在转，方格中心品红的回路相位却不动。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 依据的章节。第十八章 · 选定生成树上的相位输运与闭合回路相位与锚定规范等价，例题 四顶点单方格。
  2. 已验证的定理。树输运相位的根点归一、树边重建与端点规范协变：selected_tree_transport（LEAN ✓）。有限图上锚定规范下 χ 不变、χ 相同当且仅当规范等价、截面 σ 与 c + a = m + k − n：anchored_phase_classification（LEAN ✓）。
  3. 页面算的是什么。“沿树拧平”取 g(v) = rootPhase(v)⁻¹：树边全变成 1，每条弦上剩下 χ 的回路坐标，这正是截面 σ 的形状（单锚点时）。方格的回路相位是逆时针四条边的乘积；在这里的生成树下，中间一列的弦围住它左边一格，右边一列的弦围住同一行的两格，所以它的回路相位是两格之积。这些等式由页面数值核对（NUM）。
  4. 示意与简化。格子、边的方向、生成树与时间轴上的中间路径是页面的选择（MODEL）。把回路相位读作磁通、连续极限下的场强与非阿贝尔威尔逊圈是文献结果，未在本书形式化（ARGUED）；本页只处理离散阿贝尔 U(1) 相位。

The stage is a small lattice: every directed edge carries a unit-circle phase u(e) (the hand on the edge), and every corner can make a gauge transformation g(v) (the violet hand), which turns the edge phase into g(t(e))·u(e)·g(s(e))⁻¹. The rail runs from before the change to after it: every hand on an edge turns, yet the magenta loop phase at the centre of each square stays still. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The chapter behind it. Chapter 18 · Phase transport on a selected spanning tree and Loop phases and anchored gauge equivalence, with the worked example of a four-vertex plaquette.
  2. Verified theorems. Root normalization, tree-edge reconstruction and endpoint gauge covariance of the tree transport phase: selected_tree_transport (LEAN ✓). On a finite graph, χ is unchanged by anchored gauge transformations, equal χ means gauge equivalent, the section σ, and c + a = m + k − n: anchored_phase_classification (LEAN ✓).
  3. What the page computes. “Iron the tree” takes g(v) = rootPhase(v)⁻¹: every tree edge becomes 1 and each chord keeps its loop coordinate of χ, which is the shape of the section σ (with a single anchor). The loop phase of a square is the product of its four edges taken counter-clockwise; with the tree used here, a chord in the middle column encloses the square to its left and a chord in the right column encloses both squares of its row, so its loop phase is the product of the two. These identities are checked numerically by the page (NUM).
  4. Models and simplifications. The lattice, the edge directions, the tree and the path along the rail are choices of the page (MODEL). Reading a loop phase as a flux, the field strength of the continuum limit and non-abelian Wilson loops are literature results, not formalized in this book (ARGUED); the page handles discrete abelian U(1) phases only.
