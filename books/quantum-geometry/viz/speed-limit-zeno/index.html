三个场景共用一条时间轴，时间以 ħ/E 为单位。页面上的每个数都按公式或迭代实时算出；本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 速度极限。能级 0, E（, 2E）上的叠加态 ψ(0) = Σ √wn |n⟩，重叠 ⟨ψ(0), ψ(t)⟩ = Σ wn e−inEt/ħ，夹角 θFS = arccos |重叠|。左边画重叠：每个能级一支青色箭头，首尾相接，琥珀色的点是它们的和；品红的圆心就是 90° 的墙（重叠为零）。右边画夹角随时间的曲线、速度线 ΔH·t/ħ 与墙。Mandelstam–Tamm 界与 Margolus–Levitin 界是文献结果，未在本书形式化（ARGUED）。冻结定理 record_time_lower_bound（LEAN ✓）把“每段角位移不超过 energy·τ/ħ”当作前提，推出 τ ≥ πħ/(4·energy)；二能级等权一卡按第七章的取法让前提与结论同时取等。
  2. 三种情形。二能级等权：夹角就是速度线，t = π 时撞墙，两条界都取等。三能级等权：t = 2π/3 ≈ 2.094 才撞墙，速度线 1.924 已到墙，ML 刻度在 1.571；曲线弯了，走过的弧长 1.710 弧度长于 90° 的测地线。不等权（权重角 α < 45°）：重叠最小是 |cos 2α|，夹角最多 2α，永远到不了墙。
  3. 芝诺频闪。单比特在 H = (ħω/2)σx 下从北极 |0⟩ 出发，绕 X 轴转；ωT = 180° 内等间隔测 N 次“还在 |0⟩ 吗”。每段转过 180°/N，答“是”的概率 cos²(π/2N)，全程留存 PN = [cos²(π/2N)]N：N = 1, 4, 16, 64 依次是 0、0.5308、0.8569、0.9622。箭头在线上的影子（青色）只损失二阶小量，离开线的那一份（品红）是一阶小量。N → ∞ 的冻结是 Misra–Sudarshan 定理，文献结果，未在本书形式化（ARGUED）。
  4. 首次返回。每步 U = cos(δ/2) I − i sin(δ/2) X，初态 v = |0⟩；每步之后问“回到 v 了吗”，否则投到 v 的正交补。页面迭代这个协议，得到首次返回概率 pn 与留存概率 sn。冻结定理 monitored_return_conservation（LEAN ✓）说 s₀ = 1、pn+1 = sn − sn+1、Σp + sN = 1：每根柱子的青色（已返回）加品红（未返回）恰好是 1。
  5. 平均恒为 2。平均返回时间等于各步 sn 之和，也就是品红的总面积。第七章手算：c²·1 + s²·(1 + 1/s²) = 2，与 δ 无关（c = cos(δ/2)，s = sin(δ/2)）。小图画出整个 δ 范围：第一步没回来时还要等的 1 + 1/s² 随 δ 变小而变长，平均值一直是 2。这是页面的数值核对（NUM），不是引用的定理。

Three scenes share one rail; time is in units of ħ/E. Every number on the page is computed live from a formula or by iteration; the page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Speed limit. A superposition ψ(0) = Σ √wn |n⟩ on the levels 0, E (, 2E) has the overlap ⟨ψ(0), ψ(t)⟩ = Σ wn e−inEt/ħ and the angle θFS = arccos |overlap|. The left panel draws the overlap: one cyan arrow per level, head to tail, with their sum as the amber dot; the magenta centre is the 90° wall (zero overlap). The right panel draws the angle against time, the speed line ΔH·t/ħ and the wall. The Mandelstam–Tamm and Margolus–Levitin bounds are literature results, not formalized in this book (ARGUED). The frozen theorem record_time_lower_bound (LEAN ✓) takes “each angular displacement is at most energy·τ/ħ” as a hypothesis and concludes τ ≥ πħ/(4·energy); the two-even-levels card makes the choices of Chapter 7, for which hypothesis and conclusion both hold with equality.
  2. Three cases. Two even levels: the angle is the speed line and hits the wall at t = π; both bounds are attained. Three even levels: the wall is reached only at t = 2π/3 ≈ 2.094, the speed line reaches it at 1.924 and the ML mark sits at 1.571; the curve bends, and the arc length travelled, 1.710 rad, exceeds the 90° geodesic. Uneven weights (weight angle α < 45°): the smallest overlap is |cos 2α|, the angle reaches at most 2α, and the wall is never reached.
  3. Zeno strobe. A qubit starts at the north pole |0⟩ and turns about X under H = (ħω/2)σx; within ωT = 180° it is asked N times, evenly spaced, “still in |0⟩?”. Each interval turns 180°/N, a yes has probability cos²(π/2N), and the survival over the whole run is PN = [cos²(π/2N)]N: 0, 0.5308, 0.8569 and 0.9622 for N = 1, 4, 16 and 64. The arrow's shadow on the line (cyan) loses only a second-order amount, while the part that leaves the line (magenta) is first order. The freezing as N → ∞ is the Misra–Sudarshan theorem, a literature result, not formalized in this book (ARGUED).
  4. First return. Each step is U = cos(δ/2) I − i sin(δ/2) X, starting from v = |0⟩; after every step ask “back at v?”, and otherwise project onto the orthogonal complement of v. The page iterates this protocol and obtains the first-return probabilities pn and the survival probabilities sn. The frozen theorem monitored_return_conservation (LEAN ✓) states s₀ = 1, pn+1 = sn − sn+1 and Σp + sN = 1: the cyan (returned) and the magenta (not yet) of every column add up to exactly 1.
  5. Always 2 on average. The mean return time is the sum of the sn, which is the total magenta area. By the hand computation of Chapter 7, c²·1 + s²·(1 + 1/s²) = 2 whatever δ (c = cos(δ/2), s = sin(δ/2)). The small plot covers the whole range of δ: the further wait 1 + 1/s² after a first miss grows as δ shrinks, and the mean stays at 2. This is a numerical check by the page (NUM), not a cited theorem.
