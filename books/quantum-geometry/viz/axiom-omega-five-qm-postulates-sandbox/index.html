  1. 旋量体与盲区纤维。ψ = eiχ(cos θ/2, sin θ/2) 在 ℂ² 的正交坐标 y 中是 (eiα, eiβ)/√2，α = χ − u、β = χ + u、u = θ/2；从 y₄ = 1 球极投影得到半径 √2 与 1 的紫色环面。品红纤维是 u 固定的圆，青色细圈是 χ 固定的实截面，两者在投影下以 90° 相交。整条纤维被霍普夫映射送到同一个布洛赫点 r = (sin θ, 0, cos θ)；κray = |⟨ψ₀|ψ⟩|² 在纤维上恒为 1，κS³ = (1 + Re⟨ψ₀|ψ⟩)/2 却随 χ 变化。
  2. 翻转与不确定性。反自伴生成元 X₃ = −iσz 的本征值是 ±i，在虚轴上；乘 eiϑ，ϑ = 90° 时落到实轴，得到厄米的 σz = îX₃。X₁ = −iσx、X₂ = −iσy 翻成 σx、σy，[σx, σy] = 2iσz；在纯量子比特上 G = 0，ΔA·ΔB = |⟨σz⟩|，青色矩形的角恰好落在品红双曲线上。
  3. 往返读数。P(k|ψ) = |⟨φk|ψ⟩|² = (1 + r·rk)/2 = cos²(θk/2)：青色箭头在 z 直径上的影子把直径分成 1 + cos θ 与 1 − cos θ。附录 E 的三叉戟 Ek = (2/3)Prk 上玻恩读数之和为 1，三次标架函数 (1 + rz³)/2 之和为 5/4。
  4. 停机捏合。ρ = ψψ* 的捏合 Φρ 只留对角；‖ρ‖² = ‖Φρ‖² + ‖ρ − Φρ‖²，θ = 60° 时 1 = 0.625 + 0.375。λ 是相位阻尼的强度：留在局部的相干是 (1 − λ)² × 0.375，其余折进环境；等距扩张的联合态 V ρ V* 始终 ‖·‖² = 1。
  5. 张量与 CHSH。单态 Ψ⁻ 的每一边都是 ½I；分析轴取在 xz 平面：Alice 在 θ 与 θ + γ，Bob 在 θ ± γ/2。E(x, y) = −cos(x − y)，|S| = |3cos(γ/2) − cos(3γ/2)|：γ = 0 时对易，|S| = 2；γ = 90° 时 |S| = 2√2。

Every number is computed on the page (NUM); each Lean theorem is shown only on the one instance named in the evidence panel, and where there is none the panel says the page does not show it; the choice of torus, the ledger station and the AXIOM-Ω reading are the interpretation of this book (MODEL).

  1. The spinor body and the blind fibre. In the orthonormal coordinates y of ℂ², ψ = eiχ(cos θ/2, sin θ/2) is (eiα, eiβ)/√2 with α = χ − u, β = χ + u and u = θ/2; projecting stereographically from y₄ = 1 gives the violet torus of radii √2 and 1. The magenta fibre is the circle with u fixed, the thin cyan circle the real section with χ fixed, and they cross at 90° in the projection. The Hopf map sends the whole fibre to one Bloch point r = (sin θ, 0, cos θ); κray = |⟨ψ₀|ψ⟩|² is 1 all along the fibre, while κS³ = (1 + Re⟨ψ₀|ψ⟩)/2 changes with χ.
  2. The turn and the uncertainty. The skew-adjoint generator X₃ = −iσz has eigenvalues ±i, on the imaginary axis; multiplied by eiϑ they reach the real axis at ϑ = 90°, giving the Hermitian σz = îX₃. X₁ = −iσx and X₂ = −iσy turn into σx and σy, with [σx, σy] = 2iσz; on a pure qubit G = 0 and ΔA·ΔB = |⟨σz⟩|, so the corner of the cyan rectangle lies exactly on the magenta hyperbola.
  3. The round trip. P(k|ψ) = |⟨φk|ψ⟩|² = (1 + r·rk)/2 = cos²(θk/2): the shadow of the cyan arrow on the z diameter splits it into 1 + cos θ and 1 − cos θ. On the trine of Appendix E, Ek = (2/3)Prk, the Born readings add to 1 and the cubic frame function (1 + rz³)/2 adds to 5/4.
  4. The halting pinch. The pinching Φρ of ρ = ψψ* keeps the diagonal; ‖ρ‖² = ‖Φρ‖² + ‖ρ − Φρ‖², which is 1 = 0.625 + 0.375 at θ = 60°. λ is the strength of phase damping: the coherence left local is (1 − λ)² × 0.375 and the rest is folded into the environment; the joint state V ρ V* of the isometric dilation always has ‖·‖² = 1.
  5. Tensor and CHSH. Each side of the singlet Ψ⁻ is ½I; the analyzer axes lie in the xz-plane, those of Alice at θ and θ + γ, those of Bob at θ ± γ/2. E(x, y) = −cos(x − y) and |S| = |3cos(γ/2) − cos(3γ/2)|: at γ = 0 the pairs commute and |S| = 2; at γ = 90°, |S| = 2√2.
  6. The rail and the handle. The rail turns only the hidden phase χ: the readings of no card change with it. The handle drags the spinor over the torus; along the fibre it changes χ only, along the real section θ only.
