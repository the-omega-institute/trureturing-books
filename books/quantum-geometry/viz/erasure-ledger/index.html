理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

左边是一个量子比特的布洛赫圆盘（x–z 截面）：琥珀色箭头是态 ρ = ½(I + xσx + zσz)，青色竖线是计算基 Z 的记录线，青色粗线是箭头在线上的影子（对角元），品红是箭头横着的一份（相干元 ρ01 = x/2）。右边是两栏账本：系统的冯·诺依曼熵 S（奈特），与累计付给环境的热，记作无量纲的 βQ = Q/(kBT)，可与熵直接相比（Q 以 kBT 为单位时的数值就是 βQ）。按“操作”给账本记一步，时间轴逐步回放：整数步上的数是实算的，两步之间的动画只是插值示意。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 转动不记账。“转 U”是绕 y 轴转 α 的幺正共轭 ρ ↦ UρU*，本征值不变，熵不变，热为零。
  2. 捏合把横着的一份变成熵。“捏合 Π”是计算基的未读测量 Π(ρ) = Σk PkρPk：箭头落到青色的影子上，品红的一份消失。页面分别算熵增 S(ΠρU) − S(ρ) 与相对熵 D(ρU ‖ ΠρU) = Tr ρU(ln ρU − ln ΠρU)（矩阵对数由特征分解求得）；两者相等是 entropy_production_coherence_deletion_identity（LEAN ✓），连做 N 步时总熵增等于各步 D 之和，熵那一栏就一块一块叠起来。转 90° 再捏合的单比特特例是 dual_account_full（LEAN ✓）的 d = 2：熵增 ln 2。
  3. 复位要付热（阶段 B，文献结果）。把系统擦回 |0⟩ 时，系统的熵减 −ΔS 是兰道尔下界：付给热浴的热 βQ 至少这么多。本书形式化的只是实数层面的账目：若热浴平衡 βQ = ΔSres + D 与幺正平衡 I = ΔSsys + ΔSres 成立，则 βQ = −ΔSsys + I + D（landauer_identity_from_balances，LEAN ✓），且取等当且仅当 I = D = 0（landauer_saturation_iff、landauer_strict_iff，LEAN ✓）。两条平衡式与 I、D 的非负性是文献结果（兰道尔 1961，Reeb–Wolf 2014），未在本书形式化。“理想复位”把 I = D = 0 当作理想极限记账（MODEL）。
  4. 一个热浴比特。“热浴比特复位”是第八章例题的模型：热浴是吉布斯态 diag(p, 1 − p)，βE = ln(p/(1 − p))，联合幺正是交换门。页面实算系统与热浴的熵变、末态互信息 I（由 4×4 联合态求得，等于 0）、热浴的相对熵 D(ρB′ ‖ ρB) 与热 βQ = βE·(末态激发布居 − (1 − p))，并核对两条平衡式。从 ½I 出发（卡 ONE-QUBIT BATH）、p = 0.9 时三项依次约为 0.368、0.511、0.879，与第八章的数值表一致。从别的态出发，符号可以反过来：直接从 |0⟩ 交换时，系统的熵反而增加 0.325，βQ = −0.220（热从热浴流进系统），仍比下界 −0.325 多出 D = 0.105。
  5. 拷贝可以撤回。“拷贝”是受控非门把计算基的记录写进空白的记录比特 E：系统这一侧的约化态只剩对角线（与捏合后相同），局部的熵不减：拷贝前带横着的一份时涨，拷贝前已是对角态时不变；整体（系统 + 记录）的熵与拷贝前相同（受控非门是幺正的）：从纯态出发时整体仍是纯态、熵为 0，从 ½I 出发时仍是 ln 2；热为零。紧接着拷贝的“撤回拷贝”再作用一次受控非门（它就是自己的伴随），联合态精确回到拷贝之前；拷贝之后若又转动过系统，再作用一次受控非门一般不能让记录比特回到空白。受控拷贝的幺正性、可撤回性与约化态重合是 reduced_irreversibility_is_canonical_record_access_defect（LEAN ✓）。系统与记录还连着时，两种复位都不可用：本页不为关联的系统记复位的账。

On the left, the Bloch disc of one qubit (the x–z cross-section): the amber arrow is the state ρ = ½(I + xσx + zσz), the cyan vertical is the record line of the computational basis Z, the thick cyan segment is the arrow's shadow on it (the diagonal entries), and the magenta piece is the arrow's sideways part (the coherence ρ01 = x/2). On the right, a two-column ledger: the system's von Neumann entropy S (in nats) and the heat paid to the environment so far, written as the dimensionless βQ = Q/(kBT) so that it compares directly with S (βQ is the numerical value of Q in units of kBT). Each operation writes one line into the ledger; the rail replays it step by step: the numbers at whole steps are computed, the animation between steps is only an interpolated sketch. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Turning writes nothing. “Turn U” is the unitary conjugation ρ ↦ UρU* by a turn of α about the y axis: the eigenvalues, hence the entropy, stay the same, and no heat is paid.
  2. Pinching turns the sideways part into entropy. “Pinch Π” is the unread measurement in the computational basis, Π(ρ) = Σk PkρPk: the arrow drops onto its cyan shadow and the magenta part is gone. The page computes the entropy rise S(ΠρU) − S(ρ) and the relative entropy D(ρU ‖ ΠρU) = Tr ρU(ln ρU − ln ΠρU) (matrix logarithms by eigendecomposition) separately; that they are equal is entropy_production_coherence_deletion_identity (LEAN ✓), and over N steps the total rise is the sum of the steps' D, so the entropy column stacks up block by block. Turning by 90° and then pinching is the d = 2 case of dual_account_full (LEAN ✓): a rise of ln 2.
  3. Resetting pays heat (stage B, a literature result). When the system is wiped back to |0⟩, its entropy decrease −ΔS is the Landauer floor: the heat βQ paid to the bath is at least that much. What this book formalizes is the bookkeeping in real numbers: if the reservoir balance βQ = ΔSres + D and the unitary balance I = ΔSsys + ΔSres hold, then βQ = −ΔSsys + I + D (landauer_identity_from_balances, LEAN ✓), with equality exactly when I = D = 0 (landauer_saturation_iff, landauer_strict_iff, LEAN ✓). The two balances and the non-negativity of I and D are literature results (Landauer 1961, Reeb–Wolf 2014), not formalized in this book. “Ideal reset” books I = D = 0 as the ideal limit (MODEL).
  4. One bath qubit. “Reset with a bath qubit” is the model of Chapter 8's example: the bath is the Gibbs state diag(p, 1 − p) with βE = ln(p/(1 − p)), and the joint unitary is the swap. The page computes the entropy changes of system and bath, the final mutual information I (from the 4×4 joint state; it is 0), the bath's relative entropy D(ρB′ ‖ ρB) and the heat βQ = βE·(final excited population − (1 − p)), and checks both balances. Starting from ½I (the card ONE-QUBIT BATH) at p = 0.9 the three terms come to about 0.368, 0.511 and 0.879, as in Chapter 8's table. From other states the signs can turn round: swapping straight from |0⟩, the system's entropy rises by 0.325 and βQ = −0.220 (heat flows from the bath into the system), still above the floor −0.325 by D = 0.105.
  5. A copy can be undone. “Copy” is a controlled-NOT that writes the computational-basis record into a blank record bit E: on the system's side only the diagonal is left (the same as after a pinch), and the local entropy does not fall: it rises when there was a sideways part before the copy and stays put when the state was already diagonal; the entropy of the whole (system + record) is what it was before the copy (the controlled-NOT is unitary): still pure, with entropy 0, from a pure state, and still ln 2 from ½I; no heat is paid. “Undo copy” right after the copy applies the controlled-NOT once more (it is its own adjoint) and the joint state is exactly what it was before the copy; if the system was turned after the copy, a second controlled-NOT in general does not leave the record bit blank. That the controlled copy is unitary, can be undone, and leaves coinciding reduced states is reduced_irreversibility_is_canonical_record_access_defect (LEAN ✓). While system and record are still linked, neither reset is offered: the page keeps no reset ledger for a correlated system.
