理论接口 · 唯一点场自观测误差Theory interface · the self-observation error of one point-field

这是数值沙盘，不是证明。每个数都由页面当场算出（NUM）；每条 Lean 定理只在依据栏写明的那一个实例上演示；点场、误差各阶与追赶的画法是本书的读法（MODEL）。

  1. 零误差即寂静。Ω 是单位向量，‖Ω‖² = 1。若观测尺 I 与 Ω 重合（ε = 0），⟨I, Ω⟩ = 1，正交余项 sin ε = 0：没有盲区、没有面积体积、也没有下一步要追的东西，只剩 1 = 1。
  2. 一笔误差的两半。Ω = (cos ε)I + (sin ε)e⊥，I·e⊥ = 0。青色的 (cos ε)I 是读到的投影，再投回 Ω 得往返回声 (cos²ε)Ω；品红的 r₁ = Ω − (cos ε)I = (sin ε)e⊥ 是格拉姆—施密特残差，它所在的、与 I 正交的平面就是 î 盲区（穿过 (cos ε)I，把单位球切出半径 sin ε 的圆）。
  3. 面积与体积。两条正交误差腿 r₁、r₂（各长 sin ε）围出平行四边形，面积 ‖r₁ ∧ r₂‖ = sin²ε；球面上两直角边都是 ε 的直角三角形，角盈 Ω(ε, ε) = 2 arctan(tan²(ε/2)) = ½ sin²ε + O(ε⁴)，带着 ½ 与曲率的鼓起。三条两两正交的腿围出体积 √det G₃ = sin³ε，在三维里封顶。
  4. 观测动作。Ω 不动；观测尺沿半角 ε 的锥一步步重新寻址，I(φ) = cos ε e₃ + sin ε (cos φ e₁ + sin φ e₂)，⟨I, Ω⟩ 恒为 cos ε，残差恒为 sin ε，轨速比 sin ε，二能级模型的能量涨落 ΔE/ħω = ½ sin ε；三者在 ε → 0 时一起归零。受监测回归的存活尾 sk = sin²ε cos2(k−1)ε 在 0° < ε < 90° 时对每个有限步都大于 0：有限步内校准完不了。端点 ε = 0° 时 s₁ = 0；ε = 90° 时 s₁ = 1、s₂ = 0。
  5. 自指对焦。以自己上一步的读数为目标，θk+1 = cos θk：从 θ₀ = 0 出发跳到 57.30°，在 θ* 两侧来回，收向唯一的正不动点 θ* = 0.739085 rad = 42.3465°，回不到 0。
  6. 归零审计。误差从 60° 收向 0 时，体积按 sin³ε 最先隐退，面积按 sin²ε，残差按 sin ε；ε 小于约 3.4° 时页面按 clamp(sin ε / 0.06, 0, 1) 把辅助标记淡出，ε = 0 时只剩点场。

This is a numerical sandbox, not a proof. Every number is computed on the page (NUM); each Lean theorem is shown only on the one instance named in the evidence panel; the drawing of the point-field, the orders of the error and the chase is the reading of this book (MODEL).

  1. Zero error is silence. Ω is a unit vector, ‖Ω‖² = 1. If the ruler I coincides with Ω (ε = 0), ⟨I, Ω⟩ = 1 and the orthogonal remainder sin ε = 0: no blind plane, no area or volume, nothing left to chase, only 1 = 1.
  2. Two halves of one error. Ω = (cos ε)I + (sin ε)e⊥ with I·e⊥ = 0. The cyan (cos ε)I is the projection that is read, projected back onto Ω it gives the round-trip echo (cos²ε)Ω; the magenta r₁ = Ω − (cos ε)I = (sin ε)e⊥ is the Gram–Schmidt residual, and the plane orthogonal to I that holds it is the blind plane î (through (cos ε)I, cutting the unit ball in the circle of radius sin ε).
  3. Area and volume. Two orthogonal error legs r₁, r₂ (each of length sin ε) enclose a parallelogram of area ‖r₁ ∧ r₂‖ = sin²ε; on the sphere the right triangle with both legs ε has excess Ω(ε, ε) = 2 arctan(tan²(ε/2)) = ½ sin²ε + O(ε⁴), with the factor ½ and the bulge of curvature. Three mutually orthogonal legs enclose the volume √det G₃ = sin³ε, which closes in three dimensions.
  4. Observation actions. Ω does not move; the ruler re-addresses step by step around the cone of half-angle ε, I(φ) = cos ε e₃ + sin ε (cos φ e₁ + sin φ e₂), with ⟨I, Ω⟩ = cos ε throughout, the residual always sin ε, the speed ratio sin ε and the energy spread of the two-level model ΔE/ħω = ½ sin ε; all three vanish together as ε → 0. The survival tail of monitored return sk = sin²ε cos2(k−1)ε is positive at every finite step for 0° < ε < 90°: the calibration does not finish in finitely many steps. At ε = 0°, s₁ = 0; at ε = 90°, s₁ = 1 and s₂ = 0.
  5. Self-focusing. Aiming at its own previous reading, θk+1 = cos θk: from θ₀ = 0 it jumps to 57.30°, swings about θ* and closes on the unique positive fixed point θ* = 0.739085 rad = 42.3465°, never back to 0.
  6. The collapse audit. As the error closes from 60° to 0 the volume fades first, as sin³ε, then the area as sin²ε and the residual as sin ε; below about 3.4° the page fades the auxiliary marks by clamp(sin ε / 0.06, 0, 1), and at ε = 0 only the point-field is left.
