ARGUED把它套到这张表上是纸面手算，未在本书形式化：两两正交的是生成 T 的 r 个单位向量 vₐ，不是表的 N 行（秩 1 时每一行都是 (1/N, …, 1/N)，任两行的点积是 1/N）。取 r 个密度态 Pₐ = vₐvₐᵀ 与同样 r 个效应 E₁ = P₁ + (I − T)、Eₐ = Pₐ（a ≥ 2）：它们半正定、加起来是 I，Tr(EₛPₐ) = δₐₛ。定理里态与效应一样多，所以 I − T 并进 E₁，不单列成第 r + 1 个效应。按上面的定理（态的个数取 r，维数 d 取 N）得 r ≤ N。Applying it to this table is a hand calculation, not formalized in this book: what is mutually orthogonal are the r unit vectors vₐ that generate T, not the N rows of the table (at rank 1 every row is (1/N, …, 1/N), and any two rows have dot product 1/N). Take the r density states Pₐ = vₐvₐᵀ and the same number r of effects E₁ = P₁ + (I − T), Eₐ = Pₐ (a ≥ 2): they are positive semidefinite, add up to I, and Tr(EₛPₐ) = δₐₛ. The theorem has as many effects as states, so I − T is merged into E₁ rather than listed as an (r + 1)-th effect. The theorem above (with r states in dimension d = N) gives r ≤ N.
ARGUED文献结果，未在本书形式化：对称矩阵的牛顿—舒尔茨符号迭代 X ↦ 3X² − 2X³ 不改本征向量，只把每个本征值按 x ↦ 3x² − 2x³ 移动；0 与 1 是吸引不动点，在它们附近二次收敛，½ 是排斥不动点。充分条件：扰动的谱范数 ‖Δ‖₂ 严格小于 (√3 − 1)/2 ≈ 0.366 时，迭代渐近收敛到 T₀ = T + Δ 在 ½ 以上本征值的谱投影，秩与 T 相同，但不一定是原表。这个条件之外（包括恰好相等）不作保证，‖Δ‖₂ < ½ 也不够；任何有限的一步都只是逼近，页面报告它实算的幂等误差。Literature result, not formalized in this book: the Newton–Schulz sign iteration X ↦ 3X² − 2X³ on a symmetric matrix keeps the eigenvectors and moves each eigenvalue by x ↦ 3x² − 2x³; 0 and 1 are attracting fixed points with quadratic convergence near them, and ½ is a repelling one. A sufficient condition: if the knock has spectral norm ‖Δ‖₂ strictly below (√3 − 1)/2 ≈ 0.366, the iterates converge asymptotically to the spectral projection of T₀ = T + Δ onto its eigenvalues above ½, of the same rank as T but not necessarily the original table. Outside this condition (equality included) nothing is guaranteed, and ‖Δ‖₂ < ½ is not enough; every finite step is only an approximation, and the page reports its computed idempotency error.

舞台上是一张 N × N 的表 T = Σₐ vₐvₐᵀ（a = 1 … r），vₐ 是两两正交的单位向量。它是实对称的（Tᵀ = T），而且幂等（T² = T）：第 i 行当作尺子去读第 j 列，Σₖ TᵢₖTₖⱼ 正好读回 Tᵢⱼ。页面把这件事画成第三十五章“查表的算法也在表里”“T = T(T)”的一个诠释模型。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 依据的章节。第三十五章 · 乘光而来与自指扩表，特别是定理 thm-ch35-null-pulse-and-record-blocking 第 3 款（N ≤ d）与 thm-ch35-lawvere-diagonal-and-homoiconic-closure。
  2. 四个场景。扩表：时间轴是秩 r，迹 Σ Tₖₖ 始终等于 r，右边 N 个地址槽里 r 个被占。读表：时间轴逐项累加 TᵢₖTₖⱼ；选中对角格时每一项都是平方。自愈：点一格，Tᵢⱼ 与 Tⱼᵢ 各加 ε（保持对称），然后迭代 Tₘ₊₁ = 3Tₘ² − 2Tₘ³，右边是 Tₘ 的 N 个本征值。正交补：r < N 时 ψ(θ) = cos θ·v₁ + sin θ·w，w 与所有 vₐ 正交，读出 Tψ 的长度是 cos θ，转到 90° 归零；r = N 时 T = I，没有非零的正交补，ψ 取 v₁、不随 θ 转，读出 Tψ = ψ，长度是 1。舞台上的玻璃球是一片三维切片，球里是单位球在 T 下的像：扩表时它是杆、圆盘，写满时才是整个球；读表时第 i 行与第 j 列是圆盘里的两支箭头，第 j 列在第 i 行上的影子（琥珀色）乘第 i 行的长度就是 Tᵢⱼ；自愈时椭球的三条半轴是 Tₘ 沿 T₀ 三条本征方向的本征值（取绝对值）；正交补里 ψ 的影子 Tψ 落在圆盘上，品红的一段是 sin θ；r = N 时切片取 v₁、v₂、v₃，像是整个球，ψ = v₁ 与它的读出 Tψ 是同一支箭头，没有品红的一段。
  3. 已验证的定理。只有一条落在舞台上：有限记忆的可区分历史至多 d 个（LEAN ✓，扩表场景）。它讲的是一般的密度矩阵与 POVM；套到这张表上要用生成 T 的 r 个单位向量 vₐ，不是表的 N 行，这一步是手算（见第 4 条）。本章引用的其余冻结定理列在下方声明地图里，页面不画它们。
  4. 不在 Lean 里的。“对称且幂等 ⟺ 正交投影”“迹 = 秩”是线性代数的文献结果（ARGUED）；用 Pₐ = vₐvₐᵀ 与 E₁ = P₁ + (I − T)、Eₐ = Pₐ 把容量定理套到这张表上（r ≤ N）是纸面手算（ARGUED）。牛顿—舒尔茨迭代的收敛条件与二次收敛是文献结果（ARGUED）：‖Δ‖₂ 严格小于 (√3 − 1)/2 是一个充分条件，此时迭代渐近趋向同秩的投影（T₀ 的谱投影，不一定是原表），有限的每一步都只是逼近；0 与 1 附近的二次收敛只是局部性质。这个条件之外页面不作保证，‖Δ‖₂ < ½ 也不够：取 T = I、Δ = aEᵢᵢ、a = (√3 − 1)/2 < ½，本征值 (1 + √3)/2 一步就落到 ½，从此停在那里，幂等误差一直是 ¼。页面每一步只报告实算的幂等误差 ‖Tₘ² − Tₘ‖，不超过 10⁻¹² 时才称“近似幂等”。把这张表读成“生命扩表”“同像自指表 T = T(T)”是本书的诠释（MODEL）。页面上的每个数都是实算（NUM）。

The stage shows an N × N table T = Σₐ vₐvₐᵀ (a = 1 … r), with vₐ mutually orthogonal unit vectors. It is real symmetric (Tᵀ = T) and idempotent (T² = T): row i used as a ruler on column j gives Σₖ TᵢₖTₖⱼ, which is exactly Tᵢⱼ again. The page draws this as one interpretive model of Chapter 35’s “the lookup algorithm is in the table” and “T = T(T)”. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  2. Four scenes. Rank: the rail is the rank r; the trace Σ Tₖₖ always equals r, and r of the N address slots on the right are taken. Read: the rail adds up TᵢₖTₖⱼ term by term; for a diagonal cell every term is a square. Heal: a click adds ε to Tᵢⱼ and Tⱼᵢ (keeping the table symmetric), then Tₘ₊₁ = 3Tₘ² − 2Tₘ³ is iterated; the right shows the N eigenvalues of Tₘ. 90°: for r < N, ψ(θ) = cos θ·v₁ + sin θ·w with w orthogonal to every vₐ; the reading Tψ has length cos θ and vanishes at 90°. For r = N, T = I has no nonzero complement: ψ is v₁ and does not turn with θ, and the reading Tψ = ψ has length 1. The glass ball on the stage is a three-dimensional slice, and inside it is the image of the unit ball under T: while rows are written it is a rod, then a disc, and the whole ball only once the table is full; in reading, row i and column j are two arrows in the disc, and the shadow of column j on row i (amber) times the length of row i is Tᵢⱼ; in healing, the three semi-axes of the ellipsoid are the eigenvalues of Tₘ along three eigen-directions of T₀ (in absolute value); at 90°, the shadow Tψ of ψ lies in the disc and the magenta piece is sin θ; for r = N the slice is v₁, v₂, v₃, the image is the whole ball, ψ = v₁ and its reading Tψ are one and the same arrow, and there is no magenta piece.

The convergence condition and the quadratic convergence of the Newton–Schulz iteration are literature results (ARGUED): ‖Δ‖₂ strictly below (√3 − 1)/2 is a sufficient condition, under which the iterates tend asymptotically to a projection of the same rank (the spectral projection of T₀, not necessarily the original table), and every finite step is only an approximation; the quadratic convergence near 0 and 1 is a local property.
