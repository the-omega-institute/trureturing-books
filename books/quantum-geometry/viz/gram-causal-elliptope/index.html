LEAN ✓拉格朗日—格拉姆恒等式：对有限指标上的实数 u、v，(Σuᵢ²)(Σvᵢ²) − (Σuᵢvᵢ)² = ½ΣᵢΣⱼ(uᵢvⱼ − uⱼvᵢ)²，右边是平方和。取 Q 与 P₀ 两个单位向量，左边就是阶梯第二级 D₂ = 1 − z²：它是一块面积的平方，所以只记一步时窗口是整个 [−1, 1]。更高阶的主子式不在这条定理里。已冻结：The Lagrange–Gram identity: for real u, v over a finite index set, (Σuᵢ²)(Σvᵢ²) − (Σuᵢvᵢ)² = ½ΣᵢΣⱼ(uᵢvⱼ − uⱼvᵢ)², a sum of squares. For the two unit vectors Q and P₀ the left side is the second rung D₂ = 1 − z²: it is the square of an area, so with one step remembered the window is all of [−1, 1]. The higher minors are not part of this theorem. Frozen: lagrange_gram_identity

ARGUED文献结果，未在本书形式化：一张对称表是某组向量的格拉姆矩阵，当且仅当它半正定（向量所需的维数等于它的秩）；正定当且仅当全部顺序主子式为正（西尔维斯特判据），半正定可由带容差的 LDLᵀ / 楚列斯基分解判定；三元情形 G₃ ⪰ 0 ⟺ |x|, |y|, |z| ≤ 1 且 1 − x² − y² − z² + 2xyz ≥ 0，固定 x、y 时 z ∈ [xy − √((1−x²)(1−y²)), xy + √((1−x²)(1−y²))]。ℝ³ 里任取四根以上向量，格拉姆矩阵的秩至多为 3，行列式恒为零。Literature results, not formalized in this book: a symmetric table is the Gram matrix of some vectors if and only if it is positive semidefinite (the dimension the vectors need is its rank); it is positive definite if and only if all leading principal minors are positive (Sylvester's criterion), and semidefiniteness can be decided by an LDLᵀ / Cholesky factorization with a tolerance; for three elements G₃ ⪰ 0 ⟺ |x|, |y|, |z| ≤ 1 and 1 − x² − y² − z² + 2xyz ≥ 0, and with x, y fixed z ∈ [xy − √((1−x²)(1−y²)), xy + √((1−x²)(1−y²))]. Any four or more vectors in ℝ³ have a Gram matrix of rank at most 3, whose determinant is zero.
MODEL把历史内积表 G ⪰ 0 读作“有限观察者的未来因果相容空间”、把记忆深度读作表里的锚点个数，是本书第 29 章的诠释。为画面选定的模型：五步历史与参考步取在 ℝ⁶ = E∥³ ⊕ E⊥³ 的单位球面上，每步 1 弧度、方向按固定种子取；Q 与 P₋₂、P₋₃、P₋₄ 的内积固定在参考步的值上；ℝ³ 对照把每个事件换成它在 E∥ 上的投影再归一。Reading the history table G ⪰ 0 as “the causally compatible future of a finite observer”, and the depth of memory as the number of anchors in the table, is the interpretation of chapter 29 of this book. Chosen for the picture: the five-step history and the reference step lie on the unit sphere of ℝ⁶ = E∥³ ⊕ E⊥³, one radian per step, directions from a fixed seed; the inner products of Q with P₋₂, P₋₃, P₋₄ are held at those of the reference step; the ℝ³ comparison replaces every event by its projection on E∥, normalized.

理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

第 29 章第四层说：有限观察者手握 M 个历史锚点，挑下一个点就是补全一张半正定的格拉姆表，新的内积坐标必须落在这些锚点共同围出的相容泡泡里。本页把这张表一行一行地加上去，看泡泡怎样一圈圈收窄。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 表。按 Q、P₀、P₋₁、…、P₋₍M−1₎ 的顺序排出 (M+1) × (M+1) 的格拉姆表 G。历史之间的内积已定；Q 的那一行是 (1, z, y, b₂, b₃, b₄)，其中 z = Q·P₀ 是“这一步走多远”，y = Q·P₋₁，b₂ 到 b₄ 固定在参考步的值上。
  2. 椭圆体。只记两步（M = 2）时，G 是三阶的，x = P₀·P₋₁，G ⪰ 0 恰好是鼓胀的四面体 1 − x² − y² − z² + 2xyz ≥ 0。历史定下了 x，舞台上把这颗实心的椭圆体在 x 处切开，切面朝前（切掉的一块只留淡淡的轮廓），切面就是它在 x 处的截面：一个椭圆。体表面的网格线是 x 固定与 y 固定的线。竖着穿过琥珀色候选点的青色线段是 z 的窗口。
  3. 多记一步，窄一圈。每多记一个更早的锚点，表就多一行、多一个必须非负的主子式。它的条件仍是 (y, z) 平面里的一个椭圆，套在前一个里面：同一张表的主子式是整张表半正定的必要条件，所以可行的 (y, z) 只会少不会多。舞台上从外到内的品红椭圆就是 M = 2、3、4、5 的边界，窗口随之收窄。
  4. 阶梯。按上面的顺序算顺序主子式 D₁、D₂、…、D_{M+1}。前 k 级只涉及 Q 和最近的 k − 1 个锚点，所以 D_k 第一次变负的位置，恰好说出是第几层记忆先拒绝了这个候选。D₂ = 1 − z² 是一块面积的平方（拉格朗日—格拉姆恒等式）。条带里每一级的高度画的是 D_k / D_{k−1}：前面各级为正时，它就是第 k 根向量在前面那些张成的空间之外还剩下的长度平方，正负号与 D_k 相同。
  5. 为什么要六维。ℝ³ 里四根以上向量的格拉姆矩阵秩至多为 3，行列式恒为零。把事件压回 E∥ 的三维，记到第三层时泡泡只剩边界（里面的点需要第四维才能实现），记到第四、五层只剩孤立的点：窗口没有内部，候选被钉死在边上。取在 ℝ⁶ 里，M 从 1 到 5 的每一层都还有内部。
  6. 诠释。把 G ⪰ 0 读作有限观察者的“因果相容空间”、把记忆深度读作锚点个数，是本书的诠释（MODEL），不是定理。半正定与格拉姆矩阵的对应、西尔维斯特判据与三元椭圆体的边界是文献结果，未在本书形式化。

The fourth layer of chapter 29 says: a finite observer holding M anchors of history chooses the next point by completing a positive semidefinite Gram table, and the new inner-product coordinates must fall inside the compatible bubble those anchors enclose together. This page adds the table one row at a time and shows the bubble narrowing ring by ring. It is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The table. Order Q, P₀, P₋₁, …, P₋₍M−1₎ and form the (M+1) × (M+1) Gram table G. The inner products within the history are settled; the row of Q is (1, z, y, b₂, b₃, b₄), where z = Q·P₀ is “how far this step goes”, y = Q·P₋₁, and b₂ to b₄ are held at the values of a reference step.
  2. The elliptope. With two steps remembered (M = 2) G is 3 × 3, x = P₀·P₋₁, and G ⪰ 0 is exactly the swollen tetrahedron 1 − x² − y² − z² + 2xyz ≥ 0. The history fixes x, so the stage cuts the solid elliptope open at x with the cut facing you (the part cut away is left as faint outlines); the cut face is its slice at x: an ellipse. The grid lines on the surface are the lines of fixed x and of fixed y. The cyan segment running vertically through the amber candidate is the window for z.
  3. One more remembered, one ring narrower. Each older anchor adds a row to the table and one more principal minor that must be nonnegative. Its condition is again an ellipse in the (y, z) plane, nested inside the previous one: the principal minors of one table are necessary for the whole table to be semidefinite, so the feasible (y, z) can only shrink. The magenta ellipses from the outside in bound M = 2, 3, 4, 5, and the window narrows with them.
  4. The ladder. In that order, compute the leading principal minors D₁, D₂, …, D_{M+1}. The first k of them involve only Q and the k − 1 most recent anchors, so the place where D_k first turns negative says exactly which depth of memory refuses the candidate first. D₂ = 1 − z² is the square of an area (the Lagrange–Gram identity). Each bar of the strip shows D_k / D_{k−1}: while the earlier rungs are positive, this is the squared length of the k-th vector left outside the span of those before it, and it has the sign of D_k.
  5. Why six dimensions. Four or more vectors in ℝ³ have a Gram matrix of rank at most 3, so its determinant is zero. Squeeze the events back into the three dimensions of E∥ and at the third depth the bubble keeps only its boundary (points inside it need a fourth dimension), at the fourth and fifth only isolated points: the window has no inside and the candidate is pinned to the edge. In ℝ⁶ every depth from M = 1 to 5 still has an inside.
  6. Interpretation. Reading G ⪰ 0 as a finite observer's “causally compatible space”, and the depth of memory as the number of anchors, is this book's interpretation (MODEL), not a theorem. The correspondence between semidefiniteness and Gram matrices, Sylvester's criterion and the boundary of the three-element elliptope are literature results, not formalized in this book.
