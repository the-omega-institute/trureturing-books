理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

舞台是单位球，琥珀色的点从北极 N = e₃ 出发。每一个转动绕两根紫色转轴之一：a 是竖轴 e₃，b 与 a 成 α 角（α = 90° 时 b = e₂）。一串这样的转动叫旋转字；时间轴一次走一个转动。“覆盖”场景在同一颗球上画出 45° 的对跖开球冠。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 两角寻址。α = 90° 时页面的字就是正文的两步：先绕 e₂ 倾转 θ，到 (sin θ, 0, cos θ)；再绕 e₃ 拧转 φ，高度 cos θ 不变，到 r(θ, φ) = (sin θ cos φ, sin θ sin φ, cos θ)。θ = 0 或 180° 时第二步不动这个点，所以 (θ, φ) 在两极不是坐标卡。这是正文的手算；纯态两角参数化与单比特门要多一个欧拉角，是文献结果，未在本书形式化。
  2. 斜轴的有限字。两轴非零且不共线时，每个行列式为 1 的线性等距都是有限个“固定 a 或固定 b”的因子之积：two_axis_finite_word（LEAN ✓）。定理只说存在，不给字长、不给算法。页面只演示把 N 送到目标点，所用的字由一条贪心规则当场构造：每次先绕 a 把点摆到合适的方位，再绕 b 把它离 N 的角度推远至多 2α，最后绕 a 对准目标。
  3. 为什么夹角小就要多转。绕 b 转动时点到 b 的距离不变，所以它离 a 的角度最多变 2α（球面上的三角不等式，手算）；绕 a 转动不改变这个角度。于是 j 次绕 b 之后点离 N 至多 2jα，青色的台阶画的就是这个界；页面的构造恰好用 ⌈Θ / 2α⌉ 次绕 b 的转动（Θ 是目标离 N 的角度）。两轴共线（α = 0）时这个界一直是 0，N 一步也走不开。
  4. 无正交对的覆盖。第 i 号颜色是 {x : 5 < 2⟨x, aᵢ⟩²}，即围绕 ±aᵢ/√5 的一对 45° 开球冠；六根有理轴 (1, ±2, 0)、(0, 1, ±2)、(±2, 0, 1) 的六种颜色都是开集、同色两点不正交、合起来盖住整颗球：rational_six_axis_open_cover（LEAN ✓）。少于六块不行：sphere_two_open_orthogonality_free_cover_lower_six（LEAN ✓）。舞台上虚线是与当前点正交的大圆，它不进入含当前点的任何球冠。三根坐标轴的球冠在卦限对角线 (±1, ±1, ±1)/√3 附近漏缝（品红的点，网格取样）。

The stage is the unit sphere, and the amber point starts at the north pole N = e₃. Each turn is about one of the two violet axes: a is the vertical axis e₃, and b makes the angle α with a (at α = 90°, b = e₂). A string of such turns is a rotation word; the rail advances one turn at a time. The “Cover” scene draws antipodal open caps of 45° on the same sphere. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Two-angle addressing. At α = 90° the page's word is the text's two steps: tilt by θ about e₂ to (sin θ, 0, cos θ), then twist by φ about e₃, keeping the height cos θ, to r(θ, φ) = (sin θ cos φ, sin θ sin φ, cos θ). At θ = 0 or 180° the second step leaves the point where it is, so (θ, φ) is not a chart at the poles. This is the text's hand computation; the two-angle parametrization of pure states, and the extra Euler angle a qubit gate needs, are literature results, not formalized in this book.
  2. Finite words for skew axes. When the two axes are nonzero and not collinear, every linear isometry of determinant 1 is a product of finitely many factors that each fix a or fix b: two_axis_finite_word (LEAN ✓). The theorem asserts existence only, with no word length and no algorithm. The page only shows N being carried to a target point, by a word built on the spot with a greedy rule: turn about a to set the point at a suitable azimuth, turn about b to push its angle from N out by at most 2α, and finally turn about a onto the target.
  3. Why a small angle needs more turns. A turn about b keeps the distance of the point to b, so its angle from a changes by at most 2α (the triangle inequality on the sphere, by hand); a turn about a does not change that angle. After j turns about b the point is therefore at most 2jα from N, which is the cyan staircase; the page's construction uses exactly ⌈Θ / 2α⌉ turns about b, Θ being the angle of the target from N. For collinear axes (α = 0) the bound stays 0, and N cannot move at all.
  4. A cover without orthogonal pairs. Colour i is {x : 5 < 2⟨x, aᵢ⟩²}, a pair of open caps of 45° around ±aᵢ/√5. For the six rational axes (1, ±2, 0), (0, 1, ±2), (±2, 0, 1) the six colours are open, no colour holds two orthogonal points, and together they cover the whole sphere: rational_six_axis_open_cover (LEAN ✓). Fewer than six cannot do it: sphere_two_open_orthogonality_free_cover_lower_six (LEAN ✓). On the stage the dashed line is the great circle orthogonal to the current point; it enters none of the caps that hold the point. The caps of the three coordinate axes leave gaps around the octant diagonals (±1, ±1, ±1)/√3 (the magenta dots, sampled on a grid).
