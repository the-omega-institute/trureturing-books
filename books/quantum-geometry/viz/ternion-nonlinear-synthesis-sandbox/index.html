理论接口 · 三个数，五种写法Theory interface · three numbers, five grammars

这是数值沙盘，不是证明。每个数都由页面当场算出（NUM）；每条 Lean 定理只在依据栏写明的那个实例上演示，没有实例的写明“页面不演示”或“不构造”；壳的造型、路径与纤维环的画法是本书的读法（MODEL）。

  1. 一对转动。A 绕 ûA 转 θA，B 绕 ûB 转 θB，两轴夹角 χ；q = cos(θ/2) 1 + sin(θ/2) û·(i, j, k)，ij = +k。五张卡读的是同一对 (A, B)，滑块在卡与透镜之间不变。
  2. BCH 指数球。半径 2π 的球里，θC = log(qAqB) 由 cos γ = cos α cos β − (ûA·ûB) sin α sin β 与 sin γ ûC = sin α cos β ûA + cos α sin β ûB + sin α sin β (ûA × ûB) 读出；最后一项把紫色合成抬出平面，品红的平直和 θA + θB 留在平面里。壳上是 ‖θ‖ = π 的半圈转动，即 w = 0；π < ‖θ‖ ≤ 2π 的旋量落在壳外，‖θ‖ = 2π 的整张球面缩成同一个 −1。
  3. 有理双卡。σ = v/(1 + w) 落在单位球里，壳 ‖σ‖ = 1 也是 w = 0；σA ★ σB 的分子是青色的两段平直分量加上品红的 2σA × σB，分母 1 + ‖σA‖²‖σB‖² − 2σA·σB 把它缩回球内。探针转过 180° 时 ‖σ‖ 碰到 1，阴影反演 σ ↦ −σ/‖σ‖² 把它送到对径一侧，旋量叶 ε 翻成 −1。
  4. 霍普夫纤维。球面是可见的布洛赫方向，纤维环是看不见的相位 ψ±。旋量沿最短门搬运时 ⟨ψ|dψ⟩ = 0，北图册的 ψ₊ 按 −A₊ = −½(1 − cos ϑ) dφ 变化：在经线上不变，只在纬向段累积；走完一圈恰是 −Ω/2。
  5. 球面转角。T(P₁↷P₂) = P₁·P₂ + P₁ × P₂ 对应空间转角 2 × 弧长。首尾相接的转角消去中间顶点，所以走过的转角之积就是从 P₁ 到当前点的一段（紫色），回到 P₁ 时为 +1；最短门却带回 (S − iD)/√(S² + D²)。
  6. 流群与李三元系。[x, y, z] = x y⁻¹ z 把从 y 到 x 的那段弧搬到 z 上，不需要原点；y = 1 时它就是乘法 x z。切空间里 {a, b, a} = (a·a) b − (b·a) a，⟨{a, b, a}, b⟩/‖a × b‖² = +1。
  7. 双透镜与对径珠。“无原点”读法不画坐标轴；“几何”读法画出坐标轴与公式标签。两种读法用的是同一组 θA、θB、χ 与 τ。壳外的一对紫色对径珠：在指数球里是 ±π û，即 ±û 这对相反的旋量、同一个转动；在 MRP 球里是 (σ, +1) ∼ (−σ, −1)；在球面上 T(P↷−P) = −1。

  1. A pair of rotations. A turns by θA about ûA, B by θB about ûB, and χ is the angle between the axes; q = cos(θ/2) 1 + sin(θ/2) û·(i, j, k) with ij = +k. All five cards read the same pair (A, B); the sliders stay as they are across cards and lenses.
  2. The BCH exponential ball. In the ball of radius 2π, θC = log(qAqB) comes from cos γ = cos α cos β − (ûA·ûB) sin α sin β and sin γ ûC = sin α cos β ûA + cos α sin β ûB + sin α sin β (ûA × ûB); the last term lifts the violet product out of the plane, while the magenta flat sum θA + θB stays in it. The shell holds the half turns ‖θ‖ = π, that is w = 0; spinors with π < ‖θ‖ ≤ 2π fall outside it, and the whole sphere ‖θ‖ = 2π collapses to the single element −1.
  3. Rational charts. σ = v/(1 + w) lies in the unit ball, and its shell ‖σ‖ = 1 is again w = 0; the numerator of σA ★ σB is the two cyan flat parts plus the magenta 2σA × σB, and the denominator 1 + ‖σA‖²‖σB‖² − 2σA·σB shrinks it back into the ball. When the probe passes 180°, ‖σ‖ reaches 1 and the shadow inversion σ ↦ −σ/‖σ‖² sends it to the antipodal side, with the sheet bit ε turned to −1.
  4. The Hopf fibre. The sphere is the visible Bloch direction, the fibre ring the hidden phase ψ±. Carried by shortest gates, the spinor has ⟨ψ|dψ⟩ = 0, and the north-chart ψ₊ changes by −A₊ = −½(1 − cos ϑ) dφ: not at all along meridians, only where φ changes; after one round it is exactly −Ω/2.
  5. Spherical turns. T(P₁↷P₂) = P₁·P₂ + P₁ × P₂ is a spatial rotation by twice the arc. Turns that meet head to tail cancel the middle vertex, so the product of the turns walked so far is the single arc from P₁ to the current point (violet), +1 back at P₁; the shortest gates instead return (S − iD)/√(S² + D²).
  6. Heap and Lie triple system. [x, y, z] = x y⁻¹ z carries the arc from y to x over to z and needs no origin; at y = 1 it is the product x z. In the tangent space {a, b, a} = (a·a) b − (b·a) a and ⟨{a, b, a}, b⟩/‖a × b‖² = +1.
  7. Two lenses and the antipodal beads. The origin-free reading draws no coordinate axes; the geometric reading draws them with formula labels. Both use the same θA, θB, χ and τ. The pair of violet antipodal beads just outside the shell: in the exponential ball they are ±π û, the opposite spinors ±û of one rotation; in the MRP ball (σ, +1) ∼ (−σ, −1); on the sphere T(P↷−P) = −1.
