理论接口 · 虚平面割弦与阿秒时间双缝Theory interface · the imaginary chord and the attosecond time double slit

这是数值沙盘，不是证明。每个数都由页面当场算出（NUM）；每条 Lean 定理只在依据栏写明的那一个实例上演示。

  1. 两块平面。传播轴 x 向右；竖直的实平面 I 装 Re ψ（向上），水平的盲平面 i 装 Im ψ（朝里），两者沿 x 相交且正交。舞台后墙是 I 的投影屏（青色影子），地面是 i 的投影屏（品红影子）。“实轴平视”沿 i 的方向看，i 成了一条边；“俯瞰虚平面”从上往下看，i 正对读者。
  2. 真实单位的电子。V₀ = 1.00 eV，k = √(2meE)/ℏ。E < V₀ 时墙内 κ = √(2me(V₀ − E))/ℏ，κL 无量纲，T(E) = 1/(1 + V₀² sinh²(κL)/(4E(V₀ − E)))；E = V₀ 时 T = 1/(1 + meV₀L²/(2ℏ²))；E > V₀ 时 q = √(2me(E − V₀))/ℏ，T = 1/(1 + V₀² sin²(qL)/(4E(E − V₀)))，sin(qL) = 0 处是 Ramsauer–Townsend 全透射。
  3. 90° 与割弦。E < V₀ 时墙内动量转进 i：p = îℏκ，exp((î/ℏ)px) = exp(î²κx) = e−κx。舞台画的是定态 ψ(x)e−iEt/ℏ 本身：墙内 ψ = t eikL[cosh κ(L − x) − i(k/κ) sinh κ(L − x)] = C₊e−κx + C₋e+κx：有限厚的墙里两项都在，增长项与衰减项之比是 e−2κ(L − x)，到墙尾才等大，所以 κL 不太小时割弦以 e−κx 为主（κL 很小时两项量级相当，割弦接近下面 E = V₀ 的直线桥）；它的相位在整段墙里总共只转 arctan((k/κ) tanh κL) < 90°，振幅单调下降——不绕圈的割弦；κL ≫ 1 时它贴着 e−κx。墙口与墙尾的紫色四分之一弧是动量方向的 ±90° 转动，只在 E < V₀ 时画。t = 0 时取整体相位，让割弦中点正好落在 i 里，所以平视时墙内几乎什么也看不见，俯瞰时一条桥清清楚楚。E = V₀ 时 κ = q = 0，墙内 p²/2m = 0，没有可转进的 κ，紫色弧不画；墙内是直线桥 ψ = t eikL[1 − ik(L − x)]，相位转 arctan(kL)。E > V₀ 时墙内波数 q 是实数，波不再躲进 i：|ψ(x)|²/|t|² = 1 + V₀/(E − V₀)·sin² q(L − x)，只在 q(L − x) 为 π/2 的整数倍处转向。qL ≤ π/2 时墙里还不到四分之一个周期，|ψ| 仍单调降到墙尾的 |t|（E/V₀ = 1.01、L = 0.60 nm 时 |ψ|/|t| 从墙口的 3.19 经中点的 1.83 降到 1.00）；qL 更大时 |ψ| 随法布里–珀罗驻波起伏，相位转过 qL 量级、可以超过 90°，qL = nπ 处 T = 1。
  4. 时间双缝。两道快门（宽 σt = 120 as）在 t₁ 与 t₂ = t₁ + Δt 打开，每道放出振幅 √w 的一支（w 是透过墙的概率）。|Aj(E)|² = w·exp(−2σt²(E − Ec)²/ℏ²)，P(E) = |A₁|² + |A₂|² + 2V|A₁A₂| cos(EΔt/ℏ + ΔγB)；相邻暗纹（相位 (2n + 1)π）相隔 ΔE = h/Δt，V = 1 时暗纹处两支 100% 相消。地面上的相量盘画两支在 E* 处的相量与它们之间的相位 E*Δt/ℏ。
  5. 中途探针与芝诺。探针强度 p 把分支比特 ρ 捏合成 (1 − p)ρ + pP(ρ)，相干 V = 1 − p；p = 1 时条纹塌成 |A₁|² + |A₂|²。墙内的芝诺模型：两态 |L⟩、|R⟩，整段相干转角 Θ（sin²Θ = T），n = round(12p) 次中途检查把它切成 n + 1 段，Tmon = (1 − cosn+1(2Θ/(n + 1)))/2，n 越大越小。这是模型，芝诺定理本身是文献结果。

This is a numerical sandbox, not a proof. Every number is computed on the page (NUM); each Lean theorem is shown only on the one instance named in the evidence panel.

  1. Two planes. The propagation axis x points right; the vertical real plane I carries Re ψ (up) and the horizontal blind plane i carries Im ψ (inwards); they meet at right angles along x. The back wall of the stage is the projection screen of I (cyan shadow), the floor that of i (magenta shadow). The side real view looks along i, which becomes an edge; the top blind view looks straight down, with i facing the reader.
  2. An electron in real units. V₀ = 1.00 eV and k = √(2meE)/ℏ. For E < V₀, κ = √(2me(V₀ − E))/ℏ inside, κL is dimensionless and T(E) = 1/(1 + V₀² sinh²(κL)/(4E(V₀ − E))); at E = V₀, T = 1/(1 + meV₀L²/(2ℏ²)); for E > V₀, q = √(2me(E − V₀))/ℏ and T = 1/(1 + V₀² sin²(qL)/(4E(E − V₀))), with the Ramsauer–Townsend full transmission where sin(qL) = 0.
  3. 90° and the chord. For E < V₀ the momentum inside the wall turns into i: p = îℏκ and exp((î/ℏ)px) = exp(î²κx) = e−κx. The stage draws the stationary state ψ(x)e−iEt/ℏ itself: inside, ψ = t eikL[cosh κ(L − x) − i(k/κ) sinh κ(L − x)] = C₊e−κx + C₋e+κx: in a wall of finite width both terms are present, the growing one being e−2κ(L − x) of the decaying one and equal only at the exit, so unless κL is small the chord is dominated by e−κx (for small κL the two are comparable and the chord approaches the linear bridge of E = V₀ below). Its phase turns by only arctan((k/κ) tanh κL) < 90° across the whole wall while its amplitude falls monotonically — a chord that does not wind; for κL ≫ 1 it hugs e−κx. The violet quarter arcs at the entrance and exit are the ±90° turns of the momentum, drawn only for E < V₀. At t = 0 the overall phase puts the midpoint of the chord exactly in i, so the side view shows almost nothing inside the wall and the top view shows a clear bridge. At E = V₀, κ = q = 0 and p²/2m = 0 inside: there is no κ to turn into and no violet arc is drawn; the field inside is the linear bridge ψ = t eikL[1 − ik(L − x)], whose phase turns by arctan(kL). For E > V₀ the wavenumber q inside is real and the wave no longer hides in i: |ψ(x)|²/|t|² = 1 + V₀/(E − V₀)·sin² q(L − x), which turns only where q(L − x) is a multiple of π/2. For qL ≤ π/2 the wall holds less than a quarter period and |ψ| still falls monotonically to |t| at the exit (at E/V₀ = 1.01, L = 0.60 nm, |ψ|/|t| goes from 3.19 at the entrance through 1.83 at the midpoint to 1.00); for larger qL, |ψ| rises and falls as a Fabry–Pérot standing wave, its phase turns by an amount of order qL that can exceed 90°, and T = 1 where qL = nπ.
  4. The time double slit. Two gates (width σt = 120 as) open at t₁ and t₂ = t₁ + Δt, each releasing a branch of amplitude √w (w the probability to pass the wall). |Aj(E)|² = w·exp(−2σt²(E − Ec)²/ℏ²) and P(E) = |A₁|² + |A₂|² + 2V|A₁A₂| cos(EΔt/ℏ + ΔγB); neighbouring dark nodes (phase (2n + 1)π) lie ΔE = h/Δt apart, and at V = 1 the two branches cancel 100% there. The phasor dial on the floor shows the two branch phasors at E* and the phase E*Δt/ℏ between them.
  5. The midway probe and Zeno. A probe of strength p pinches the branch qubit ρ to (1 − p)ρ + pP(ρ), leaving coherence V = 1 − p; at p = 1 the fringes flatten to |A₁|² + |A₂|². The Zeno model inside the wall: two states |L⟩, |R⟩ and a whole coherent turn Θ (sin²Θ = T) cut into n + 1 pieces by n = round(12p) midway checks, Tmon = (1 − cosn+1(2Θ/(n + 1)))/2, smaller as n grows. This is a model; the Zeno theorem itself is a literature result.
