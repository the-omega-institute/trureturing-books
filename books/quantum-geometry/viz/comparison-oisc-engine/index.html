理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

四个场景回答第 32 章 §32.1–§32.2 的同一个问题：不预设任何坐标、只做两两比较时，几何从哪里来，装不下的东西又去哪里。本页是数值演示，不是证明；每个数都在页面上实时算出，模型与诠释分开标注。

  1. 原语：一次两两比较。wA = σA/√8、wB = σB/√8 ∈ {±1}⁸/√8（槽符号 σA,j, σB,j ∈ {±1}）是单位向量，逐槽相乘：相同的槽（XNOR）m 个，不同的槽（XOR）d 个，s = ⟨wA, wB⟩ = (m − d)/8 = cos θ，a = √(1 − s²) = sin θ。同时 ‖wA + wB‖² = m/2、‖wA − wB‖² = d/2，两者之和恒为 4（平行四边形律）。时间轴上，圆画的是前 k 槽的比较：两个截断的字各自归一之后的夹角，cos θk = (1/k)Σj<kσA,jσB,j = (8/k)Σj<kwA[j]wB[j]；k = 8 时就是两个字本身。
  2. 两向的公平。在 (wA, e⊥) 平面里 wB = s·wA + a·e⊥，记 CMP(A, B) = s + aÎ、CMP(B, A) = s − aÎ。若纯差方向自乘 Î² = κ，两向之积是 s² − κa²，而 Î(−Î) = −κ。a ≠ 0 时乘积等于 1 当且仅当 κ = −1：这是页面的代数。Î = [[0, −1], [1, 0]] 是斜伴随的酉矩阵，已冻结的定理给出它的平方是 −1；κ 旋钮只是对照，不是物理参数。
  3. 显影：相似表成为几何。M 个字的格拉姆表 G = WWᵀ 对称半正定，Gᵢᵢ = 1。雅可比法求 G = VΛVᵀ，取前三个特征值得 Xᵢ = (√λ₁vᵢ₁, √λ₂vᵢ₂, √λ₃vᵢ₃)；秩不超过 3 时 G = XXᵀ，每一对都有 d²ᵢⱼ = ‖Xᵢ − Xⱼ‖² = 2(1 − Gᵢⱼ)。嵌入只确定到一个正交变换，页面用两对字的差把它转正。八个不同的 ±1 八槽单位字不可能落在立方体的八个角上（那要三个方向分到同样多的槽，而 8 不被 3 整除），所以默认的是 3、3、2 个槽的八角盒；正八面体是 ± 三条沃尔什行；“翻一位”把一个字的一个槽翻过来，秩升到 4，只取前三维时距离不再全对。k = 0 与 k = 28 之间节点的位置是显示用的插值，只有表填满时的位置是谱嵌入；小图里的谱把未揭开的项按 0 读，这张半张表不一定半正定。
  4. 折痕：公平平方与楔。把 u = (s₁, a₁)、v = (s₂, a₂) 读成复数，比较 ū v = (s₁s₂ + a₁a₂) + i(s₁a₂ − a₁s₂)，|ū v|² = |u|²|v|²，即 (s₁² + a₁²)(s₂² + a₂²) = (s₁s₂ + a₁a₂)² + (a₁s₂ − s₁a₂)²，这是拉格朗日恒等式在两个分量时的形状。镜像 ū = (s₁, −a₁) 与 u 相乘只剩 |u|²（手性折叠）；v 与 u 共线时楔为零（u ∧ u = 0）。楔不为零时 |u||v| 不能小于 |u ∧ v|：罗伯逊–薛定谔恒等式里，交换子那一项正是中心化向量内积的虚部，这里的楔扮演它的角色；页面没有画算子。
  5. 预算与光锥。单位比较预算 (dτ/dt)² + (dx/(c dt))² = cos²θ + sin²θ = 1 移项即 (c dτ)² = (c dt)² − dx²：同一条代数恒等式，把它读成闵可夫斯基间隔是本书的诠释。θ = 90° 时 dx = c dt、dτ = 0，世界线贴上光锥。已冻结的定理说：在零锥上为零的二次型只能是闵可夫斯基形式的倍数。
  6. 开维：装不下的残差。旧的表张成 𝒱₂ = span(e₁, e₂)。新态 w(β) = cos β(cos φ e₁ + sin φ e₂) + sin β e₃ 在表里的投影长 cos β，残差 r = (I − P)w = sin β e₃ 与 e₁、e₂ 的比较都为零；{e₁, e₂, w} 的格拉姆行列式是 sin²β，β > 0 时秩从 2 升到 3。这一切都在固定的 ℝ³ 里：页面不说物理空间长出了新维度。
  7. 查表：有限的位宽。十六个四位态只读前 b 位：同一前缀的态落进一个桶，桶里分不开的有序对就是逃逸对，共 16(24−b − 1) 个（无序的是 C(24−b, 2)·2b）。去掉第 b 位，逃逸对等于原来的逃逸对并上只有第 b 位能分开的对：16(25−b − 1) = 16(24−b − 1) + 16·24−b（页面枚举计数）。深度为 b 的前缀树做一次查找只比较 b 次。
  8. 自比较。xₙ₊₁ = cos xₙ 从 x₀ = 0 出发，收向满足 cos θ* = θ* 的 θ* ≈ 0.7390851332；每步误差大约乘 sin θ* ≈ 0.674（页面数值）。已冻结的定理给出 θ* 在 (0, 1) 中存在、在正实数中唯一，并由一个有理系数幂级数在 π 处求和得到；它不陈述蛛网迭代的收敛。
  9. 宇宙单指令比较引擎与零背景公理 A₀。把每一次比较读成宇宙唯一的一条指令（类比单指令计算机的 SUBLEQ），把“比较之前没有坐标”读成零背景比较公理 A₀，是本书的诠释。单指令计算机的通用性、平行四边形律刻画内积、距离表的欧氏可实现性与格里森定理是文献结果，未在本书形式化；P ≠ NP 与物理希尔伯特空间的维数增长是开放问题。

书中：第 32 章 · 宇宙单指令比较引擎与零背景比较公理

Four scenes answer one question of §32.1–§32.2 in chapter 32: with no preset coordinates and nothing but pairwise comparison, where does geometry come from, and where does what does not fit go? The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof; every number is computed live on the page, and models and interpretations are labelled apart.

  1. Primitive: one pairwise comparison. wA = σA/√8, wB = σB/√8 ∈ {±1}⁸/√8 (slot signs σA,j, σB,j ∈ {±1}) are unit vectors multiplied slot by slot: m equal slots (XNOR), d unequal slots (XOR), s = ⟨wA, wB⟩ = (m − d)/8 = cos θ and a = √(1 − s²) = sin θ. At the same time ‖wA + wB‖² = m/2 and ‖wA − wB‖² = d/2 always add up to 4 (the parallelogram law). Along the rail the circle shows the comparison of the first k slots: the angle between the two truncated words, each renormalised, cos θk = (1/k)Σj<kσA,jσB,j = (8/k)Σj<kwA[j]wB[j]; at k = 8 it is the two words themselves.
  2. Two-way fairness. In the (wA, e⊥) plane wB = s·wA + a·e⊥; write CMP(A, B) = s + aÎ and CMP(B, A) = s − aÎ. If the pure difference squares to Î² = κ, the two-way product is s² − κa², and Î(−Î) = −κ. For a ≠ 0 the product equals 1 iff κ = −1: this is the page's algebra. Î = [[0, −1], [1, 0]] is a skew-adjoint unitary, and the frozen theorem gives its square as −1; the κ dial is only a control, not a physical parameter.
  3. Geometry: the similarity table develops. The Gram table G = WWᵀ of M words is symmetric positive semidefinite with Gᵢᵢ = 1. Jacobi's method gives G = VΛVᵀ, and the top three eigenvalues give Xᵢ = (√λ₁vᵢ₁, √λ₂vᵢ₂, √λ₃vᵢ₃); when the rank is at most 3, G = XXᵀ and every pair has d²ᵢⱼ = ‖Xᵢ − Xⱼ‖² = 2(1 − Gᵢⱼ). The embedding is fixed only up to an orthogonal map, which the page straightens with the differences of two pairs of words. Eight distinct unit ±1 words of eight slots cannot sit on the corners of a cube (the three directions would need equally many slots, and 3 does not divide 8), so the default is the box of 3, 3 and 2 slots; the octahedron is ± three Walsh rows; “one bit flipped” flips one slot of one word, the rank rises to 4, and the top three dimensions no longer get every distance right. Node positions between k = 0 and k = 28 are a display interpolation; only the positions of the full table are the spectral embedding. The spectrum in the small plot reads unrevealed entries as 0, and that half-filled table need not be positive semidefinite.
  4. Folding: fair squares and the wedge. Reading u = (s₁, a₁) and v = (s₂, a₂) as complex numbers, the comparison ū v = (s₁s₂ + a₁a₂) + i(s₁a₂ − a₁s₂) has |ū v|² = |u|²|v|², that is (s₁² + a₁²)(s₂² + a₂²) = (s₁s₂ + a₁a₂)² + (a₁s₂ − s₁a₂)², the shape of Lagrange's identity for two components. The mirror ū = (s₁, −a₁) times u leaves only |u|² (chirality folding); when v lies along u the wedge vanishes (u ∧ u = 0). Otherwise |u||v| cannot fall below |u ∧ v|: in the Robertson–Schrödinger identity the commutator term is exactly the imaginary part of the inner product of the centred vectors, and the wedge plays that role here; the page draws no operators.
  5. Budget and light cone. The unit comparison budget (dτ/dt)² + (dx/(c dt))² = cos²θ + sin²θ = 1 rearranges into (c dτ)² = (c dt)² − dx²: the same algebraic identity, and reading it as the Minkowski interval is the book's interpretation. At θ = 90° dx = c dt and dτ = 0: the worldline meets the light cone. The frozen theorem says that a quadratic form vanishing on the null cone can only be a multiple of the Minkowski form.
  6. Expansion: the residual that does not fit. The old table spans 𝒱₂ = span(e₁, e₂). A new state w(β) = cos β(cos φ e₁ + sin φ e₂) + sin β e₃ projects into the table with length cos β; the residual r = (I − P)w = sin β e₃ compares to zero with both e₁ and e₂; the Gram determinant of {e₁, e₂, w} is sin²β, and for β > 0 the rank rises from 2 to 3. All of this happens inside a fixed ℝ³: the page does not say that physical space grows a new dimension.
  7. Lookup: finite width. Sixteen four-bit states read only through their first b bits: states with the same prefix share a bucket, and the ordered pairs a bucket cannot separate are the escape pairs, 16(24−b − 1) of them (C(24−b, 2)·2b unordered). Dropping bit b, the escape pairs are the old escape pairs together with the pairs only bit b separates: 16(25−b − 1) = 16(24−b − 1) + 16·24−b (counted by enumeration on the page). A prefix trie of depth b answers a lookup with b comparisons.
  8. Self-comparison. xₙ₊₁ = cos xₙ from x₀ = 0 closes in on the θ* ≈ 0.7390851332 with cos θ* = θ*; each step multiplies the error by roughly sin θ* ≈ 0.674 (page numerics). The frozen theorem gives θ* in (0, 1), unique among positive reals, as the sum at π of a power series with rational coefficients; it says nothing about the convergence of the cobweb.
  9. The cosmic comparison engine and the zero-background axiom A₀. Reading every comparison as the universe's one instruction (by analogy with the SUBLEQ of one-instruction computers), and “no coordinates before comparison” as the zero-background comparison axiom A₀, is the book's interpretation. The universality of one-instruction computers, the parallelogram characterisation of inner products, the Euclidean realisability of distance tables and Gleason's theorem are literature results, not formalized in this book; P ≠ NP and the growth of a physical Hilbert space's dimension are open questions.
