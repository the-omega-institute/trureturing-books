理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

左边是微波脉冲的包络 Ω(t)/2π（单位 MHz，横轴是纳秒），右边是布洛赫球。时间轴走到哪里，左边就把那之前的包络下的面积涂成品红，右边就把态转到那一刻。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 旋转系。比特的固有哈密顿量 H₀ = ½ω₀Z（ω₀/2π = 5 GHz）。在实验室系里，赤道上的态每 0.2 ns 绕竖轴转一圈；两极是 H₀ 的本征态，纹丝不动（energy_eigenstate_stationary，LEAN ✓；它依赖本征线上的标量指数化 exp_mulVec_of_eigenvector，LEAN ✓）。跟着驱动频率 ω_d 一起转的参考系把载频消掉，这是文献结果，未在本书形式化。页面默认画旋转系；切到“实验室系”时，把旋转系里的解绕 z 轴转 ω_d t。
  2. 面积律。共振、相位 φ 固定、旋波近似下，旋转系哈密顿量是 ½Ω(t)(cos φ X + sin φ Y)，各时刻彼此对易，传播子是绕赤道轴 (cos φ, sin φ, 0) 转 θ = ∫Ω dt 的 R_φ(θ) = exp[−(i/2)θ(cos φ X + sin φ Y)]。所以方波和高斯波只要面积相同就转到同一处：面积 π/2 是四分之一转，π 是半转。文献结果，未在本书形式化。
  3. 失谐。加上 Δ = ω₀ − ω_d，生成元是 ½(Ω(t)cos φ X + Ω(t)sin φ Y + ΔZ)。包络在变时，各时刻的生成元不再对易，转轴随时间晃动，要按时间次序相乘；页面把脉冲切成至少 1200 小段，每段用该段的平均振幅做精确指数。
  4. 次序。两个绕不同轴的转动一般不对易：先 X(π/2) 后 Y(π/2) 把北极送到 −y，先 Y 后 X 送到 +x。页面只是数这两个终点，不涉及第十一章冻结的三脉冲积分核误差界——那条命题讲的是 pulse0 核的迹，不是幺正门。
  5. 虚拟 Z。由 R_φ(θ)R_z(λ) = R_z(λ)R_{φ−λ}(θ)：电路里先做 R_z(λ) 再做 R_φ(θ)，等于把后面的脉冲改成相位 φ − λ，再在最后补一个 R_z(λ)；最后那个 R_z 与 Z 基投影对易，不改读数，于是可以不发。舞台上的实线是硬件真正做的（只发相位 φ − λ 的脉冲，0 ns 的 Z），白色虚线是电路字面上的那条路（先绕竖轴转 λ，再按 φ 发脉冲）：两条路终点的高度相同，所以 P(0) 相同，方位差 λ。符号取反（φ + λ）时终点高度一般不同：从 |+⟩ 出发、λ = 90°、X(π/2) 时，正确的符号到北极 P(0) = 1，错误的符号到南极 P(0) = 0。这些是第十一章文献注记的内容，文献结果，未在本书形式化。读数不随参考系更新而变，在算子代数层面对应状态前推与效应拉回的迹对偶：若信道与完全正映射满足迹对偶前提，则 t 步前推后的读数等于效应拉回 t 次后的读数（observer_pullback_trace_identity，LEAN ✓；t 是离散步数，定理不涉及脉冲时长）。

On the left is the envelope Ω(t)/2π of the microwave pulse (MHz against nanoseconds), on the right the Bloch sphere. Wherever the rail stands, the left side paints the area under the envelope up to that moment in magenta, and the right side turns the state to that moment. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The rotating frame. The qubit's own Hamiltonian is H₀ = ½ω₀Z (ω₀/2π = 5 GHz). In the lab frame a state on the equator goes once round the vertical axis every 0.2 ns; the poles are eigenstates of H₀ and do not move (energy_eigenstate_stationary, LEAN ✓; it rests on the scalar exponential along an eigenline, exp_mulVec_of_eigenvector, LEAN ✓). A frame turning along at the drive frequency ω_d removes the carrier; that is a literature result, not formalized in this book. The page draws the rotating frame by default; in “Lab” it turns the rotating-frame solution about z by ω_d t.
  2. The area law. At resonance, with fixed phase φ and the rotating-wave approximation, the rotating-frame Hamiltonian is ½Ω(t)(cos φ X + sin φ Y); its values at different times commute, and the propagator turns about the equatorial axis (cos φ, sin φ, 0) by θ = ∫Ω dt: R_φ(θ) = exp[−(i/2)θ(cos φ X + sin φ Y)]. So a square and a Gaussian pulse of the same area end at the same point: area π/2 is a quarter turn, π a half turn. Literature result, not formalized in this book.
  3. Detuning. With Δ = ω₀ − ω_d the generator is ½(Ω(t)cos φ X + Ω(t)sin φ Y + ΔZ). While the envelope changes, its values at different times no longer commute, the axis sways, and the slices must be multiplied in time order; the page cuts the pulse into at least 1200 slices, each an exact exponential at the slice's mean amplitude.
  4. Order. Two turns about different axes do not commute in general: X(π/2) then Y(π/2) takes the north pole to −y, Y then X takes it to +x. The page only counts these two end points; it has nothing to do with the frozen three-pulse kernel bound of Chapter 11, which is about the trace of pulse0 kernels, not unitary gates.
  5. The virtual Z. From R_φ(θ)R_z(λ) = R_z(λ)R_{φ−λ}(θ): doing R_z(λ) and then R_φ(θ) is the same as sending the later pulse at phase φ − λ and appending R_z(λ) at the end; that last R_z commutes with the Z-basis projectors and does not change the readout, so it need not be sent. On the stage the solid path is what the hardware does (only a pulse at phase φ − λ, a Z of 0 ns), and the white dashed path is the circuit taken literally (first turn by λ about the vertical axis, then a pulse at φ): the two end points have the same height, hence the same P(0), and azimuths λ apart. With the sign reversed (φ + λ) the heights differ in general: from |+⟩ with λ = 90° and X(π/2), the right sign reaches the north pole, P(0) = 1, the wrong sign the south pole, P(0) = 0. These are the literature remarks of Chapter 11, literature results not formalized in this book. That the readout survives a frame update matches, at the level of operator algebras, the trace duality of pushing states forward and pulling effects back: when a channel and a completely positive map satisfy the duality premise, the readout after t steps forward equals the readout of the effect pulled back t times (observer_pullback_trace_identity, LEAN ✓; t counts discrete steps, and the theorem says nothing about pulse durations).
