理论接口 · 玻恩往返与虚部盲区Theory interface · the Born round trip and the imaginary blind spot

这是数值沙盘，不是证明。每个数都由页面当场算出（NUM）；每条 Lean 定理只在依据栏写明的那一个实例上演示。

  1. 舞台。观察者是琥珀色的轴 I（|0⟩）。态 ψ = cos θ|0⟩ + eiφ sin θ|1⟩ 画成单位向量 V = cos θ I + sin θ (cos φ e₁ + sin φ e₂)，θ ∈ [0°, 90°]：|0⟩ 的振幅取成非负实数，|1⟩ 的复振幅 sin θ eiφ 落在与 I 垂直的品红盲平面 span{e₁, e₂} 上。θ 固定、φ 走一圈，V 扫出紫色的锥；锥口在盲平面上的影子是半径 sin θ、周长 2π sin θ 的盲圆，锥围住的球冠立体角是 2π(1 − cos θ)。下半球的碗是 −V，只差一个负号，是同一组态，只用来盛住画面。
  2. 单程与往返。V 投到 I 上只得影子 PIV = cos θ I（青色）。雷达式的往返：从 I 的尖端垂直落到 V 的线上，得 cos θ V；再垂直落回 I，得 cos²θ I（琥珀色回声）。cos²θ = |⟨I|V⟩|² = Tr(ρPI) 正是玻恩权重；剩下的 sin²θ = |I ∧ V|² 是品红的亏量，二者相加为 1。
  3. 盲平面。PI 与 P⊥ = 1 − PI 互相抵消：盲平面里的任何方向 e⊥ 在 I 上都读 0。φ 只住在盲平面里，所以单基 Z 读出对整条锥给同一个数（θ = 45° 时恰为 0.500000）。把读出基转 90°（布洛赫球上的 Z → X、Y；在舞台的希尔伯特夹角里是 45°），PX(+) = (1 + sin 2θ cos φ)/2 与 PY(+i) = (1 + sin 2θ sin φ)/2 才读得到 φ；罗伯逊—薛定谔的交换子项 |⟨[σz, σx]⟩|/2 = |sin 2θ sin φ| 在 θ = 45°、φ = 90° 达到 1。“沿 I 轴”的视角从侧面看，盲平面成一条边，V 只剩高度 cos θ 可读；“转 90° 的基”把镜头再转 90°，顺着 I 向下看，盲平面正对镜头，φ 成了看得见的角。
  4. i 的两次转身。在 I 与 e⊥ 张成的平面里，转 90° 的旋子 R = [[0, −1], [1, 0]] 满足 Rᴴ = −R、RRᴴ = 1，于是 R² = −1。第一次转身 I ↦ e⊥，I·e⊥ = 0：落进 I 的盲区，I 读不到；第二次转身 I ↦ −I，回到 I 看得见的轴上，振幅 −1，回声 |−1|² = 1；第三、四次依次到 −e⊥ 与 I。把这个“垂直于 I、I 看不见”的方向叫作虚的（imaginary），是本书的读法。
  5. 有 i 与没有 i。取 H = ω|0⟩⟨0|：U(t)|0⟩ = e−iωt|0⟩，I 的振幅在单位圆上顺时针走（转盘），速度 −iωz 与 z 垂直，|z| = 1 永远不变；相对相位 φ(t) = φ₀ + ωt，V 绕锥逆时针转，Z 读出不动（[H, PI] = 0）。把 −iω 换成实数 −γ，得到假想的无 i 演化 ψ(t) = e−γtψ₀：速度与 ψ 反向，|ψ(t)|² = e−2γt，ω = γ = 1 时 t = 1 只剩 0.135335，t = 2 只剩 0.018316。
  6. θ*。θ* = 0.739085133215161 rad = 42.346459° 是 cos θ = θ 的唯一实根：cos²θ* = 0.546247，sin²θ* = 0.453753，2π sin θ* = 4.232429，2π(1 − cos θ*) = 1.639376 sr。

书中：§34.19 往返投影与虚部盲区

This is a numerical sandbox, not a proof. Every number is computed on the page (NUM); each Lean theorem is shown only on the one instance named in the evidence panel.

  1. The stage. The observer is the amber axis I (|0⟩). The state ψ = cos θ|0⟩ + eiφ sin θ|1⟩ is drawn as the unit vector V = cos θ I + sin θ (cos φ e₁ + sin φ e₂), θ ∈ [0°, 90°]: the amplitude of |0⟩ is taken real and non-negative, and the complex amplitude sin θ eiφ of |1⟩ lies in the magenta blind plane span{e₁, e₂} at 90° to I. With θ fixed and φ going once round, V sweeps the violet cone; the shadow of its rim on the blind plane is the blind circle of radius sin θ and circumference 2π sin θ, and the cap inside the cone has solid angle 2π(1 − cos θ). The bowl of the lower hemisphere is −V, the same states up to a sign, and only holds the picture.
  2. One way and round trip. Projecting V on I gives only the shadow PIV = cos θ I (cyan). The radar round trip: drop from the tip of I perpendicularly onto the line of V to get cos θ V, then perpendicularly back onto I to get cos²θ I (the amber echo). cos²θ = |⟨I|V⟩|² = Tr(ρPI) is exactly the Born weight; what is left, sin²θ = |I ∧ V|², is the magenta deficit, and the two add up to 1.
  3. The blind plane. PI and P⊥ = 1 − PI annihilate each other: every direction e⊥ of the blind plane reads 0 on I. φ lives only in the blind plane, so the single-basis Z readout gives one number for the whole cone (exactly 0.500000 at θ = 45°). Turn the readout basis by 90° (Z → X, Y on the Bloch sphere; 45° in the Hilbert-space angle of the stage) and PX(+) = (1 + sin 2θ cos φ)/2 and PY(+i) = (1 + sin 2θ sin φ)/2 read φ; the Robertson–Schrödinger commutator term |⟨[σz, σx]⟩|/2 = |sin 2θ sin φ| reaches 1 at θ = 45°, φ = 90°. The “Along I axis” vantage looks from the side: the blind plane becomes an edge and V keeps only its height cos θ to be read; the “90° turned basis” turns the camera by another 90° to look down I, the blind plane faces the camera and φ becomes a visible angle.
  4. The two quarter turns of i. In the plane of I and e⊥ the 90° rotor R = [[0, −1], [1, 0]] has Rᴴ = −R and RRᴴ = 1, hence R² = −1. The first turn I ↦ e⊥ gives I·e⊥ = 0: it lands in the blind spot of I, which cannot read it; the second turn I ↦ −I lands back on the axis I can see, with amplitude −1 and echo |−1|² = 1; the third and fourth reach −e⊥ and I. Calling this direction “at 90° to I, invisible to I” imaginary is how this book reads it.
  5. With i and without. Take H = ω|0⟩⟨0|: U(t)|0⟩ = e−iωt|0⟩, the amplitude of I runs clockwise on the unit circle (the dial), its velocity −iωz is perpendicular to z and |z| = 1 for ever; the relative phase is φ(t) = φ₀ + ωt, V turns anticlockwise round the cone, and the Z readout stays put ([H, PI] = 0). Replace −iω by a real −γ and the hypothetical i-free evolution ψ(t) = e−γtψ₀ has its velocity against ψ, |ψ(t)|² = e−2γt: with ω = γ = 1 only 0.135335 is left at t = 1 and 0.018316 at t = 2.
  6. θ*. θ* = 0.739085133215161 rad = 42.346459° is the only real root of cos θ = θ: cos²θ* = 0.546247, sin²θ* = 0.453753, 2π sin θ* = 4.232429 and 2π(1 − cos θ*) = 1.639376 sr.
