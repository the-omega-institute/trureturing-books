ARGUED文献结果，未在本书形式化：Ryu–Takayanagi 的两区间极小曲面竞争（Ryu & Takayanagi 2006、Headrick 2010）。舞台取最简单的情形——静态 AdS₃ 真空、一条直线边界上的两段区间——测地线长给出 S_分开 = ln(l₁/ε) + ln(l₂/ε)、S_连通 = ln(d/ε) + ln((l₁ + l₂ + d)/ε)（以 c/3 为单位），取两者较小者；连通的更短当且仅当领先阶互信息 I_RT > 0，交点满足 d(l₁ + l₂ + d) = l₁l₂。不连通相里只是领先阶的 I_RT 为零，精确的量子互信息仍可有次领先贡献。Literature result, not formalized in this book: the competition of minimal surfaces for two intervals (Ryu & Takayanagi 2006, Headrick 2010). The stage takes the simplest case, static AdS₃ vacuum with two intervals on a straight boundary, where geodesic lengths give S_separate = ln(l₁/ε) + ln(l₂/ε) and S_connected = ln(d/ε) + ln((l₁ + l₂ + d)/ε) (in units of c/3), and the smaller one counts; the connected one is shorter exactly when the leading-order mutual information I_RT is positive, and the crossing satisfies d(l₁ + l₂ + d) = l₁l₂. In the disconnected phase only the leading-order I_RT vanishes; the exact quantum mutual information can still have subleading contributions.

两个场景回答第 33 章的同一个问题：两个观察者互相丈量时，会发生什么。“互指”画的是两人读数的平面，“极小割”画的是两段边界区间与它们的极小割。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 互指。横轴是 A 的读数 x，纵轴是 B 的读数 y。两条青色曲线是两人的读法：A 读 B 得 x = cos y，B 读 A 得 y = cos x。每一拍两人同时更新，(x, y) ↦ (cos y, cos x)；琥珀色的矩形是这一拍（灰色的是走过的各拍）：A 横着走到自己的曲线，B 竖着走到自己的曲线，新的点在对角。两条曲线只在 (θ*, θ*) 相交（既有结果的应用：g = cos∘cos 在全实轴上压缩，没有二周期解，见例第 5–9 项）。
  2. 逐拍与逐轮。两人起点相同时，这对读数始终在对角线上，每拍都越过 θ*，与孤立的余弦迭代一模一样。两拍一轮取样（空心的环），每个坐标做一次 g，只从一侧单调逼近。每轮误差约乘 sin²θ* ≈ 0.4538，正是每拍 −sin θ* ≈ −0.6736 的平方：一轮含两次余弦求值，按求值次数算并没有更快。
  3. 极小割。灰线是边界，琥珀色是两段区间 A、B。候选的割是半圆形的测地线：各自的两条（A 上、B 上），或连通的两条（间隙上一条小的，整段上一条大的）。品红是较短的那一组，青色是它围出的纠缠楔；虚线是落选的一组。在 AdS₃ 真空里测地线长换成 S = ln(l/ε)（以 c/3 为单位），交点由 d(l₁ + l₂ + d) = l₁l₂ 给出，d_crit = [√((l₁ + l₂)² + 4l₁l₂) − (l₁ + l₂)]/2；l₁ = l₂ = 1 时 d_crit = √2 − 1 ≈ 0.414。互信息 I = max(0, S_分开 − S_连通) 与 ε 无关。这些是 Ryu–Takayanagi 与 Headrick 的文献结果，未在本书形式化；不连通相里为零的只是领先阶的 I_RT。
  4. 已验证的部分。绿色框是本章引用的宽度三桥图最大流最小割命题：一个离散网络里最小割从桥内落到外边界。它不覆盖舞台上的连续半平面，两者只是同一类问题。
  5. 诠释。“观察者”“互为故乡”“锁进同一个不动点”是本书的诠释模型（MODEL）。页面不声称这些并排的事实互相推出。

Two scenes answer one question of chapter 33: what happens when two observers measure each other. “Mirrors” draws the plane of their two readings; “Min cut” draws two boundary intervals and their minimal cuts. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Mirrors. The horizontal axis is A's reading x, the vertical axis B's reading y. The two cyan curves are how each reads the other: A reading B gives x = cos y, B reading A gives y = cos x. At every beat both update at once, (x, y) ↦ (cos y, cos x); the amber rectangle is this beat (grey ones are the beats already walked): A moves across to its curve, B moves up or down to its curve, and the new point is the opposite corner. The two curves meet only at (θ*, θ*) (an application of existing results: g = cos∘cos is a contraction on the whole real line, so there is no two-cycle; clauses 5–9 of the example).
  2. Beats and rounds. When both start alike, the pair stays on the diagonal and crosses θ* at every beat, exactly like the lone cosine iteration. Sampled every two beats (the hollow rings), each coordinate takes one step of g and closes in monotonically from one side. Each round multiplies the error by about sin²θ* ≈ 0.4538, the square of the −sin θ* ≈ −0.6736 of one beat: a round costs two cosine evaluations, so counted per evaluation it is no faster.
  3. Min cut. The grey line is the boundary, the amber segments are the two intervals A and B. The candidate cuts are semicircular geodesics: the separate pair (over A and over B) or the connected pair (a small one over the gap and a large one over the whole span). Magenta is the shorter pair, cyan the entanglement wedge it encloses; dashed is the pair that loses. In AdS₃ vacuum geodesic lengths turn into S = ln(l/ε) (in units of c/3), the crossing is d(l₁ + l₂ + d) = l₁l₂, so d_crit = [√((l₁ + l₂)² + 4l₁l₂) − (l₁ + l₂)]/2; for l₁ = l₂ = 1, d_crit = √2 − 1 ≈ 0.414. The mutual information I = max(0, S_separate − S_connected) does not depend on ε. These are literature results of Ryu–Takayanagi and Headrick, not formalized in this book; what vanishes in the disconnected phase is only the leading-order I_RT.
  4. What is verified. The green box is the max-flow min-cut proposition for the bridge graph of width three cited in this chapter: in a discrete network the minimal cut moves from inside the bridge to the outer boundaries. It does not cover the continuous half-plane on the stage; the two are only problems of the same kind.
  5. Interpretation. “Observers”, “each other's home” and “locking onto one fixed point” are this book's interpretive model (MODEL). The page does not claim that the facts shown side by side imply one another.
