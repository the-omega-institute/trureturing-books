一弧生三相与回环One arc, three phases and the loop

这是数值演示，不是证明：每个数都由页面当场算出，定理只在下面写明的实例上演示。

  1. 本源圆弧。A(ε) = (sin ε, cos ε, 0)，B = e₂，C(ε) = (0, cos ε, sin ε)：A → B 是赤道面里一段角为 ε 的极小圆弧，C 在第二面坐标墙里。三点都在单位球上，球被挖去 x、y、z ≥ 0 的八分角，原胞就在缺口里。
  2. 一阶 Λ¹：线与粒子。A 处朝向 B 的单位切向 T = (−cos ε, sin ε, 0)。M = span(A) 的投影 P 把 B 送到 cos ε · A，补投影 Q = I − P 把 B 送到 sin ε · T：青色半弦 sin ε 是 B 离开直线 OA 的正交分量，也是切线世界线上 A 到 Q₁ = A + sin ε · T 的一段。
  3. 二阶 Λ²：面与波。Q₁ 到 B 的品红矢高长 1 − cos ε，把弧从切线上拉弯；球面帽 ABC 的角超 Ω = α + β + γ − π 由三个球面角算出，此例 Ω = 2 arctan(tan²(ε/2))。
  4. 三阶 Λ³：体与场。以 O 为顶、帽 ABC 为底的紫色扇锥体积为 Ω/3；格拉姆行列式 det G₃ = (A·(B × C))² = sin⁴ε；巴格曼迹 Δ₃ = Tr(ρAρBρC) 的虚部是 A·(B × C)/4 = sin²ε/4，辐角是 Ω/2。
  5. 回环与相消。回环是赤道上半径 1.26 的十二格，格点 x 位于方位角 90° − 30°x（x = 0 对着 B）。每格画三个相位 χ₀、χ₄、χ₈：它们在 H = {0, 3, 6, 9} 上恒为 1，ann H = {0, 4, 8}。缠绕 W 从 0 到 1 把相位从同向转到特征相位 e2πiW·(kx mod 12)/12；W = 1、κ = 1 时壳外和为 0，壳上和为 3。阶段 s 从 3 到 4 时 W = s′ · τ，s′ 是 s − 3 的平滑过渡。
  6. “阶”的意思。这里的阶指外代数的次数：线 Λ¹、面 Λ²、体 Λ³，三维里 Λ⁴ = 0。按 ε 的幂次，sin ε ≈ ε，1 − cos ε ≈ ε²/2，Ω ≈ ε²/2，Ω/3 ≈ ε²/6，Im Δ₃ ≈ ε²/4，det G₃ ≈ ε⁴：ε → 0 时面与体比线更快熄灭，只剩那条切线。

This is a numerical illustration, not a proof: every number is computed on the spot by the page, and each theorem is shown only on the instance written below.

  1. The seed arc. A(ε) = (sin ε, cos ε, 0), B = e₂, C(ε) = (0, cos ε, sin ε): A → B is a minimal arc of angle ε in the equatorial plane, and C lies in the second coordinate wall. All three lie on the unit sphere, from which the octant x, y, z ≥ 0 is cut away; the cell sits in the notch.
  2. Grade one Λ¹: line and particle. The unit tangent at A towards B is T = (−cos ε, sin ε, 0). The projection P onto M = span(A) sends B to cos ε · A, and the complementary projection Q = I − P sends B to sin ε · T: the cyan half-chord sin ε is the part of B orthogonal to the line OA, and also the stretch of the tangent worldline from A to Q₁ = A + sin ε · T.
  3. Grade two Λ²: surface and wave. The magenta sagitta from Q₁ to B has length 1 − cos ε and bends the arc off the tangent; the excess of the cap ABC, Ω = α + β + γ − π, is computed from the three spherical angles, and here Ω = 2 arctan(tan²(ε/2)).
  4. Grade three Λ³: volume and field. The violet sector with apex O over the cap ABC has volume Ω/3; the Gram determinant is det G₃ = (A·(B × C))² = sin⁴ε; the Bargmann trace Δ₃ = Tr(ρAρBρC) has imaginary part A·(B × C)/4 = sin²ε/4 and argument Ω/2.
  5. The loop and the cancellation. The loop is a ring of twelve sites of radius 1.26 on the equator, site x at azimuth 90° − 30°x (x = 0 faces B). Each site carries three phases χ₀, χ₄, χ₈: they equal 1 on H = {0, 3, 6, 9}, and ann H = {0, 4, 8}. The winding W from 0 to 1 turns them from aligned to the character phases e2πiW·(kx mod 12)/12; at W = 1 and κ = 1 the sum is 0 off shell and 3 on shell. From s = 3 to s = 4 the winding is W = s′ · τ, where s′ is a smooth ramp of s − 3.
  6. What “grade” means. Grade is the degree of the exterior algebra: line Λ¹, surface Λ², volume Λ³, and in three dimensions Λ⁴ = 0. In powers of ε, sin ε ≈ ε, 1 − cos ε ≈ ε²/2, Ω ≈ ε²/2, Ω/3 ≈ ε²/6, Im Δ₃ ≈ ε²/4 and det G₃ ≈ ε⁴: as ε → 0 the surface and the volume go out faster than the line, and only the tangent remains.
