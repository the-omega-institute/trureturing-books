点体八度与粒子—场层级Point–volume octave and the particle–field hierarchy

这是模型与数值的沙盘，不是证明。

八度。三维外代数按阶分成 1 + 3 + 3 + 1 = 8 维：一个点（标量）、三条线 e₁, e₂, e₃、三块面 e₂₃, e₃₁, e₁₂、一块体 e₁₂₃。霍奇星把线 eᵢ 对到与它正交的面，把点对到体，所以 k 阶与 3 − k 阶的方向自由度相同。页面把八度坐标 z ∈ [−1, 3] 放在螺旋上，s = (z + 1)/4：z = −1 是上一层的场体 Λ³ℓ−1，z = 0 是点，z = 1、2 是线与面，z = 3 是体，它同时就是下一层的点 Λ⁰ℓ+1。走满一圈，傅里叶读出 χ = e2πis 回到原值，层级 ℓ 加一；这是 Lean 中黄金螺旋一步的两条定理在本页的读法。标架随 s 绕螺旋轴转一整圈，体积读数 det G 按三向量格拉姆展开式计算，在所有旋转下保持为 1。

场。琥珀核心 x₀ 是 (ℤ/9ℤ)³ 上的一个格点。格林函数 G(x, x₀) = (1/729) Σₖ eik·(x−x₀)/(λ(k) + m²eff)，λ(k) = Σᵢ (2 − 2 cos(2πkᵢ/9))，m²eff = m² + 1/81（有限环面的红外底，模型）。页面逐格点核对 (−Δ + m²eff)G = δx₀ 的残差。21 根场丝沿黄金角方向从核心伸出，亮度按 G 沿丝的比值衰减；拖动核心，整片场丝一起平移。

缀饰与对偶。缀饰核心是半径 a 的高斯云，a = max(1/2, 2.5(1 − p))，自能 E(a) = (1/1458) Σₖ e−a²λ(k)/(λ(k) + m²eff)。底 1/2 取自黄金模型集的堆积半径，这一取法是模型；傅里叶透镜把核心附近放大两倍来画，虚线圆是探针要求、却低于下限的半径。ℤ/12ℤ 上取 f 为一维格点格林函数，H 由探针 p 选定，页面同时核对泊松求和两边与零化子特征和。

This is a sandbox of models and numbers, not a proof.

Octave. The exterior algebra of three dimensions splits by grade into 1 + 3 + 3 + 1 = 8 dimensions: one point (the scalar), three lines e₁, e₂, e₃, three planes e₂₃, e₃₁, e₁₂ and one volume e₁₂₃. The Hodge star sends a line eᵢ to the plane orthogonal to it and the point to the volume, so grades k and 3 − k carry the same number of directions. The page puts the octave coordinate z ∈ [−1, 3] on a helix, s = (z + 1)/4: z = −1 is the field body Λ³ℓ−1 of the previous level, z = 0 the point, z = 1 and 2 the lines and the planes, z = 3 the volume, which is at the same time the point Λ⁰ℓ+1 of the next level. After a full turn the Fourier readout χ = e2πis is back at its value and the level ℓ rises by one; this is how the page reads the two Lean theorems on one golden helix step. The frame turns once about the helix axis as s runs, and the volume readout det G, computed with the three-vector Gram expansion, stays 1 under every rotation.

Field. The amber core x₀ is a site of (ℤ/9ℤ)³. The Green function is G(x, x₀) = (1/729) Σₖ eik·(x−x₀)/(λ(k) + m²eff) with λ(k) = Σᵢ (2 − 2 cos(2πkᵢ/9)) and m²eff = m² + 1/81 (the infrared floor of the finite torus, a model). The page checks the residual of (−Δ + m²eff)G = δx₀ at every site. Twenty-one filaments leave the core along golden-angle directions, their brightness following the ratio of G along each filament; drag the core and the whole web moves with it.

Dressing and duality. The dressed core is a Gaussian cloud of radius a = max(1/2, 2.5(1 − p)), with self-energy E(a) = (1/1458) Σₖ e−a²λ(k)/(λ(k) + m²eff). The floor 1/2 is taken from the packing radius of the golden model set; this choice is a model. The Fourier lens draws the neighbourhood of the core at twice the scale, and the dashed circle is the radius the probe asks for when it falls below the floor. On ℤ/12ℤ the function f is the one-dimensional lattice Green function, H is chosen by the probe p, and the page checks both sides of Poisson summation and the character sums over the annihilator.
