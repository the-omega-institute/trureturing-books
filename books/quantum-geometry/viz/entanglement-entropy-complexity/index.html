理论接口 · 量子纠缠、P=NP 与熵的正交三位一体Theory interface · orthogonal triad of entanglement, P=NP & entropy

为什么把“量子纠缠、P=NP、熵”放进同一座克利福德正交坐标系，三者会严丝合缝地咬合成同一个投影几何的不同切面？本页在 16 比特张量网络（观察者区 A 八比特、对偶区 Ā 八比特）上实时联立偏迹密度矩阵、勾股守恒与跨割键维模型。本页是交互数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 双体纯态的精确勾股守恒与偏迹盲区。对正交施密特角 θ ∈ [0, π/2] 参数化的典范双比特纯态 |ψ(θ)⟩ = cos(θ/2)|00⟩ + sin(θ/2)|11⟩，对 Ā 求偏迹得约化态 ρ_A = diag((1 + cos θ)/2, (1 − cos θ)/2) = ½(I₂ + cos θ · σ_z)。局部布洛赫向量长度平方为 ‖r_A‖² = cos²θ，而二体纠缠度（2-tangle）τ(A:Ā) = C² = sin²θ 恰等于归一化线性熵 S_L(ρ_A) = 2(1 − Tr(ρ_A²)) = sin²θ，故局部确定性与跨割纠缠严格服从勾股守恒 ‖r_A‖² + τ(A:Ā) = cos²θ + sin²θ = 1；二元冯·诺依曼纠缠熵为 S_vN(ρ_A) = H_bin((1 + cos θ)/2)。当 θ = 90° 时，全局仍为秩一纯态（S_global = 0），局部却缩为球心最大混合态 ½I₂（LEAN ✓：bell_pure_state_has_maximally_mixed_marginal、local_marginal_correlation_blind_spot）。
  2. 面积律（Area Law）对照体积律（Volume Law）的张量割键维分水岭。在由 k_cut 对独立跨割纠缠对构成的 16 比特示范网络中，局部偏迹截断总熵为 S_cut = k_cut · S_vN(θ)，有效键维为 χ_eff = 2^{S_cut}（θ > 0 时精确施密特秩为 χ_exact = rank(ρ_A) = 2^{k_cut}，0° < θ < 90° 时 χ_eff 严格小于它，例如 θ = 60°、k_cut = 8 时 χ_eff ≈ 89.9 而 χ_exact = 256）。面积律模式（k_cut = 2，类比一维有能隙基态与 2-SAT 局部传递链）保持 S_cut = O(1) 与多项式键维（所引一维文献结果要求基态唯一、局部维数固定、局部作用有限程且强度有界、能隙有与 n 无关的正常数下界；在此条件下基态满足面积律，并有多项式键维 MPS 近似，属 ARGUED，不是精确的有限秩等式）；体积律模式（k_cut = 8，类比 3-SAT 扩展图阻挫或晚期霍金辐射）令 S_cut = Θ(n)，使跨越该割的有效键维 χ_eff 与精确施密特秩 χ_exact 都按 2^{Θ(n)} 指数增长（文献背景：Hastings 2007；Page 1993）。
  3. 纠缠的双刃剑：局部单配性防火墙 vs 全局相干读取。对场内有限观察者而言，CKW 单配性不等式 τ(A:B) + τ(A:C) ≤ τ(A:BC) 叠加被偏迹遮蔽的环境相干删除（对照 LEAN ✓：entropy_production_coherence_deletion_identity）与 Harlow–Hayden 的复杂度归约（在 QSZK ⊄ BQP 的假设下排除多项式时间解码；指数规模 2^{Ω(S_BH)} 只是他们的猜想，OPEN），使局部视角面临复杂度高墙；而在抬升偏迹刀片的全局无时间纯态视角下，S_global ≡ 0，额外赋予稀有事件后选择（PostBQP = PP ⊇ NP）或非局域共享纠缠多证明者协议（MIP* = RE，Ji 等 2020）则呈现高阶验证能力。上述定理各有严格资源前提，标准统一图灵机下的 P ≠ NP 仍为开放问题（OPEN）。
  4. 余弦不动点 θ* ≈ 42.35° 的活体观察者平衡（MODEL）。在唯一正余弦不动点 cos θ* = θ* ≈ 0.7391 rad（42.346°，LEAN ✓：rational_inverse_endpoint）处，局部向量长度平方 ‖r_A‖² ≈ 0.5462 与跨割纠缠度 τ = S_L ≈ 0.4538、并发度 C = sin θ* ≈ 0.6736、冯·诺依曼熵 S_vN ≈ 0.559 bits 达到黄金平衡——既保有稳定局部指向，又维持跨界纠缠带宽。

Why do “quantum entanglement, P=NP, and entropy” lock together as three orthogonal projections of one geometric structure? On a 16-qubit tensor network (8 observer qubits in A, 8 partner qubits in Ā), this lab couples partial-trace density matrices, exact Pythagorean conservation, and cross-cut bond dimensions in real time. It is an interactive numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Exact two-body Pythagorean conservation and the partial-trace blind spot. For the canonical two-qubit pure state |ψ(θ)⟩ = cos(θ/2)|00⟩ + sin(θ/2)|11⟩ with Schmidt angle θ ∈ [0, π/2], tracing out Ā gives ρ_A = diag((1 + cos θ)/2, (1 − cos θ)/2) = ½(I₂ + cos θ · σ_z). The squared local Bloch length is ‖r_A‖² = cos²θ, while the 2-tangle τ(A:Ā) = C² = sin²θ equals the normalized linear entropy S_L(ρ_A) = 2(1 − Tr(ρ_A²)) = sin²θ, yielding the exact Pythagorean identity ‖r_A‖² + τ(A:Ā) = cos²θ + sin²θ = 1 alongside von Neumann entropy S_vN(ρ_A) = H_bin((1 + cos θ)/2). At θ = 90°, the global state remains rank-1 pure (S_global = 0) while the local marginal collapses to ½I₂ at the sphere center (LEAN ✓: bell_pure_state_has_maximally_mixed_marginal, local_marginal_correlation_blind_spot).
  2. Area Law vs Volume Law across the tensor cut. Across k_cut independent entangled pairs bridging A and Ā in the 16-qubit benchmark network, local partial-trace cut entropy is S_cut = k_cut · S_vN(θ) with effective bond dimension χ_eff = 2^{S_cut} (the exact Schmidt rank is χ_exact = rank(ρ_A) = 2^{k_cut} for θ > 0, and χ_eff stays strictly below it for 0° < θ < 90° — e.g. χ_eff ≈ 89.9 vs χ_exact = 256 at θ = 60°, k_cut = 8). Area Law (k_cut = 2, modeling 1D gapped chains and 2-SAT implication rails) keeps S_cut = O(1) and χ_eff polynomial (the cited 1D results assume a unique ground state, fixed local dimension, bounded finite-range interactions and a spectral gap bounded below by a positive constant independent of n; in that regime the ground state obeys an area law and has a polynomial-bond MPS approximation, ARGUED, not an exact finite-rank identity); Volume Law (k_cut = 8, modeling frustrated 3-SAT expanders or old Hawking radiation) drives S_cut = Θ(n), so both χ_eff and the exact Schmidt rank χ_exact grow as 2^{Θ(n)} (literature: Hastings 2007; Page 1993).
  3. Double-edged sword of entanglement: local monogamy firewall vs global joint readout. For a finite local observer, CKW monogamy τ(A:B) + τ(A:C) ≤ τ(A:BC), coherence deletion into traced-out sectors (cf. LEAN ✓: entropy_production_coherence_deletion_identity), and the Harlow–Hayden complexity reduction (excluding polynomial-time decoding assuming QSZK ⊄ BQP; the exponential size 2^{Ω(S_BH)} is only their conjecture, OPEN) erect a complexity wall; lifting the partial-trace blade restores S_global ≡ 0, where extra resources such as postselection (PostBQP = PP ⊇ NP) or shared multi-prover entanglement (MIP* = RE, Ji et al. 2020) unlock higher verification classes. Each theorem carries strict resource hypotheses, and standard Turing-machine P ≠ NP remains open (OPEN).
  4. Living observer balance at Dottie's constant θ* ≈ 42.35° (MODEL). At the unique positive root cos θ* = θ* ≈ 0.7391 rad (42.346°, LEAN ✓: rational_inverse_endpoint), local vector weight ‖r_A‖² ≈ 0.5462 and cross-cut tangle τ = S_L ≈ 0.4538 (concurrence C = sin θ* ≈ 0.6736, S_vN ≈ 0.559 bits) strike a poised balance between local identity and cross-boundary entanglement bandwidth.
