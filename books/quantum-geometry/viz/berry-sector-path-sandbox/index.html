面积、纤维与选路Area, fiber and path selection

这是数值演示，不是证明。六条 Lean 验证只支持各自的冻结陈述；球面面积、三维 Stokes 推导、Hopf 联络与驻相解释分别标注。

球面三角形取 A = (sin ε, cos ε, 0)、B = (0, 1, 0)、C = (0, cos ε, sin ε)。品红弧顶的面积等于立体角 Ω，球心围出的实心扇锥体积为 Ω/3；青色弦四面体体积为 sin²ε/6，等于三点 Bargmann 迹虚部的三分之二。正向回路的几何相位取 γ_B = −Ω/2。

纤维卡的紫色柱只是 Hopf 相位纤维的局部示意，柱高展开相位，并非额外空间坐标。和乐卡只在顶点 B 作规范变换：两条树边相位此消彼长，闭合回路的 arg W 不变。扇形卡直接计算 Π_N 的模长与连续分支相位；费曼卡是 41 条等权路径的有限模型，不是完整传播子，也不含贝里相位项。舞台下方的曲线与读数面板都是这些公式的实时数值；费曼卡在球下方另立一块复平面幕，画出两束相量的首尾累加。

书中：§34.6 贝里扇锥体积与路径积分

This is a numerical illustration, not a proof. The six Lean results support their own frozen statements; spherical area, the three-dimensional Stokes derivation, Hopf connection and stationary-phase interpretation are labelled separately.

The spherical triangle has A = (sin ε, cos ε, 0), B = (0, 1, 0) and C = (0, cos ε, sin ε). The magenta cap has area equal to the solid angle Ω, and the solid sector it bounds from the center has volume Ω/3; the cyan chord tetrahedron has volume sin²ε/6, two thirds of the imaginary part of the triple Bargmann trace. The geometric phase of the positive loop is γ_B = −Ω/2.

On the fiber card the violet column is only a local schematic of a Hopf phase fiber; its height unwraps phase and is not an extra spatial coordinate. The holonomy card changes the gauge only at vertex B: the two tree-edge phases trade turns while the closed-loop arg W stays fixed. The fan card computes the modulus and continuous-branch phase of Π_N directly; the Feynman card is a finite model of 41 equally weighted paths, not the full propagator, and includes no Berry-phase term. The curves under the stage and the readout panel are live values of these formulas; on the Feynman card a complex-plane screen under the sphere shows the head-to-tail sum of the phasors of both bundles.
