理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

两个场景共用一条时间轴：电路走过的层数。页面上的每个数都实时算出；本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 增长。8 个量子比特排成一条开放的链，从 |00000000⟩ 出发。奇数层在 (1,2)(3,4)(5,6) 上各放一道两比特门，偶数层在 (0,1)(2,3)(4,5)(6,7) 上各放一道，每道门是固定种子的哈尔随机 4 × 4 酉矩阵（复高斯矩阵做格拉姆—施密特正交化）。割线在第 3 与第 4 个比特之间，只有奇数层的 (3,4) 门跨过它。每层之后算左半 4 个比特的约化密度矩阵 ρA（16 × 16）的本征值 λi，熵 S = −Σ λi log₂ λi（青色）。白线是已经用掉的门数，第 t 层之后是 3⌈t/2⌉ + 4⌊t/2⌋。
  2. 两道天花板。熵不超过 log₂ 16 = 4 比特，也不超过跨割门数的 2 倍（每道跨割门至多把施密特秩乘 4）：这是灰色的台阶。品红的“预期复杂度”是模型 min(门数, 2^N)：门数只是态复杂度的上界；文献预期复杂度在热化之后近似跟着门数线性增长，直到 exp(O(N)) 量级的天花板，更久以后饱和并回归——页面不画回归。
  3. CV 的读法。复杂度等于体积猜想把品红那段上升的线对应到黑洞内部最大切片的体积。这是开放猜想（第二十章 [conj-complexity-equals-volume]），页面只标出读法。
  4. 深度。第二十章的开放边界链：N 个格点，N + 1 道门 K1、U(1)…U(N − 1)、KN 按下标 k = 0…N 排成一条路径，相邻两道门共用一个格点。切点集 S ⊆ {1…N} 决定每一步的先后：k + 1 ∈ S 时门 k 先于门 k + 1，否则门 k + 1 先于门 k。每道门放在它前面最长依赖链的长度那一层（最早可放的层），层数 = 最长同向段 + 1。κ = |S| 个折返点把 N − κ 个向下的步切成至多 κ + 1 段，所以至少 ⌊N/(κ + 1)⌋ + 1 层；按证明里的递归构造放切点恰好取到。灰色虚线是两条旧公式的预测：奇数 N 为 (N + 3)/2 − κ，偶数 N 为 (N + 4)/2 − κ。

Both scenes share one rail: the number of layers the circuit has run. Every number on the page is computed live; the page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Growth. Eight qubits in an open chain start from |00000000⟩. Odd layers put a two-qubit gate on each of (1,2)(3,4)(5,6), even layers on each of (0,1)(2,3)(4,5)(6,7); every gate is a fixed-seed Haar-random 4 × 4 unitary (a complex Gaussian matrix put through Gram–Schmidt). The cut lies between qubits 3 and 4, and only the (3,4) gates of the odd layers cross it. After each layer the page takes the eigenvalues λi of the reduced density matrix ρA (16 × 16) of the left four qubits, and the entropy S = −Σ λi log₂ λi (cyan). The white line is the number of gates used so far, 3⌈t/2⌉ + 4⌊t/2⌋ after layer t.
  2. Two ceilings. The entropy is at most log₂ 16 = 4 bits, and at most twice the number of gates across the cut (each such gate multiplies the Schmidt rank by at most 4): the grey staircase. The magenta “expected complexity” is the model min(gate count, 2^N): the gate count is only an upper bound on the complexity of the state; the literature expects the complexity to follow it roughly linearly after thermalization up to a ceiling of order exp(O(N)), and to saturate and recur much later — the page does not draw the recurrence.
  3. The CV reading. The complexity = volume conjecture matches the rising part of the magenta line with the volume of the maximal slice through the black-hole interior. It is an open conjecture (Chapter 20, [conj-complexity-equals-volume]); the page only marks the reading.
  4. Depth. The open chain of Chapter 20: N sites and N + 1 gates K1, U(1)…U(N − 1), KN lined up by index k = 0…N into a path in which neighbouring gates share a site. The set of cuts S ⊆ {1…N} sets each step's order: if k + 1 ∈ S gate k runs before gate k + 1, otherwise gate k + 1 runs before gate k. Each gate sits in the layer given by the longest chain of gates it waits for (the earliest layer it can take), so the number of layers is the longest same-direction run plus 1. The κ = |S| turning points cut the N − κ downward steps into at most κ + 1 runs, hence at least ⌊N/(κ + 1)⌋ + 1 layers; placing the cuts by the recursion in the proof reaches it. The grey dashed line is what the two old formulas predict: (N + 3)/2 − κ for odd N, (N + 4)/2 − κ for even N.
