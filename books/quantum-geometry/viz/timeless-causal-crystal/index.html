LEAN ✓对有限指标集上的实值 u、v：(Σuᵢ²)(Σvᵢ²) − (Σuᵢvᵢ)² = ½ΣᵢΣⱼ(uᵢvⱼ − uⱼvᵢ)²。取两个单位态，左边是 1 − c²，右边是楔积面积的平方 sin²θ：格拉姆矩阵的每个 2 × 2 主子式都是面积，因而非负；格拉姆窗口的半宽 sin θ₁ sin θ₂ 就是两块面积之积的平方根。已冻结：For real u, v on a finite index set: (Σuᵢ²)(Σvᵢ²) − (Σuᵢvᵢ)² = ½ΣᵢΣⱼ(uᵢvⱼ − uⱼvᵢ)². For two unit states the left side is 1 − c² and the right side the squared wedge area sin²θ: every 2 × 2 principal minor of the Gram matrix is an area, hence nonnegative; the half-width sin θ₁ sin θ₂ of the Gram window is the square root of the product of two such areas. Frozen: lagrange_gram_identity

“顺序无关的本质就是根本不存在前后的时间……因果就是这样必然。”“因为观察者是有限的，所以经历的因果是有限的，观察者的选择是在因果的相容空间中做选择，不断在时间场中选点。”“全局静态时间场 P = NP，但有限观察者 P ≠ NP。”“P 是场，NP 是线。”本页把这四句话收进一本账：左边拨三个角，中间看账本一步步记下去，右边看整张因果相图。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 格拉姆窗口。令 B 在北极，A、C 与 B 的夹角为 θ₁、θ₂，二面角为 φ，则 cAC = c₁c₂ + s₁s₂ cos φ，只能落在窗口 [cos(θ₁ + θ₂), cos(θ₁ − θ₂)] 内；det G = 1 − cAB² − cBC² − cAC² + 2cABcBCcAC = s₁²s₂² sin²φ ≥ 0。φ 是有限观察者的当下一念：φ ∈ (0, π) 时 det G > 0，三点张开一块体积；φ = 0 或 π 时 det G = 0，两条余弦当场锁死第三条；把 cAC 直接放到窗口外（相图上点窗口外，或“越界禁区”），det G < 0，没有三个单位向量能实现这组余弦。半宽 s₁s₂ 来自两块面积 1 − c² 之积，见 lagrange_gram_identity；窗口顶端 c = 1 恰是 C = A，见 unit_gram_overlap_characterization。三阶恒等式本身与“半正定 ⇔ 可实现”是标准线性代数，未在本书形式化。
  2. 去序账本。cos θ = Σₖ (−1)ᵏθ²ᵏ/(2k)!：2k 次跃迁的 (2k)! 种先后排序被当作同一块几何体积。账本第 k 行把这一项同时加到三个余弦 cAB、cBC、cAC = cos θ₃ 上，再用截断后的三个数算 det G⁽ᵏ⁾。低阶截断不是真正的余弦（可能超出 [−1, 1]），det G⁽ᵏ⁾ 可以为负——符号带上的品红格；阶数一高，截断余项 |θ|²ᵏ⁺²/(2k + 2)! 降到 10⁻¹⁶ 以下，det G⁽ᵏ⁾ 收敛到精确值，符号回到真实的一侧。读数里的 cos²θ + sin²θ = 1 用同一阶数的正弦级数核对。
  3. 晶体书册。时钟 C 有 N = 12 页 τⱼ = 2πj/12，系统是单比特，HS = diag(0, 1)，ψ₀ = |+⟩。全局态 Ψ = (1/√N) Σⱼ |τⱼ⟩ ⊗ U(τⱼ)ψ₀ 满足 (HC ⊗ I + I ⊗ HS)Ψ = 0；切片 ψ(τ) = √N⟨c(τ)|Ψ⟩ = U(τ)ψ₀，⟨σx⟩ = cos τ。表盘上 12 页同时点亮，页间的细线是彼此不为零的页间块；三个夹角 θ₁、θ₂、θ₃ 是在这本书上翻到的三页，右侧曲线是 cos τ 与当前阶的泰勒部分和。按页条件化得到合法的条件态（conditionalState_isState），页间块不为零所以捏合改变晶体（unreadState_fixed_iff），记录权重是秩一记录的玻恩权重（rank_one_born_pairing_weight）。
  4. P 场 vs NP 线。左：二维 Klee–Minty 多边形上的对数势垒场 −∇V 处处有定义，从 (1, 1) 出发的梯度流满足耗散恒等式 V(x₀) − V(xT) = ∫‖ẋ‖² dt（S2_dissipation）。右：在 N = 2ⁿ 维状态球上，均匀初态与隐藏解的内积只有 2−n/2，振幅放大 Q = (2UU* − 1)(1 − 2P) 每一步只把指针转 2θ，sin θ = 2−n/2：Qᵗ U = sin((2t + 1)θ) W + cos((2t + 1)θ) B（uniform_operator_rotation）。这一视图的账本逐步记下这根线，页面同时逐分量模拟并核对；走到解约需 (π/4)√N 步，没有算法能更快是文献结果（BBBV 1997）。
  5. 无信号。取两个量子比特的纠缠态 cos(θ₁/2)|00⟩ + sin(θ₁/2)|11⟩，C 侧沿 m = (sin θ₂ cos φ, sin θ₂ sin φ, cos θ₂) 测量而不读结果：B 的约化态一点不变，ΔρB = 0；只有读出结果时 B 的条件态才移动，两种结果平均后又相消。观察者的当下一念不能向 B 发信号。
  6. 边界。P ≠ NP 与量子引力中的时间问题都是开放问题；本页的画面与数值不推出它们的答案。

“Order-independence means that there is, at bottom, no before and after … and that is exactly why causality is necessary.” “Because the observer is finite, the causality it lives through is finite; its choices are made inside the compatible space of causes, picking point after point in the time field.” “The global static time field has P = NP, but the finite observer has P ≠ NP.” “P is a field, NP is a line.” This page puts these four sentences into one ledger: set three angles on the left, watch the ledger record step by step in the middle, and see the whole causal phase map on the right. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The Gram window. Put B at the north pole, A and C at angles θ₁, θ₂ from B with dihedral angle φ. Then cAC = c₁c₂ + s₁s₂ cos φ can only lie in the window [cos(θ₁ + θ₂), cos(θ₁ − θ₂)], and det G = 1 − cAB² − cBC² − cAC² + 2cABcBCcAC = s₁²s₂² sin²φ ≥ 0. φ is the finite observer's present choice: for φ ∈ (0, π), det G > 0 and the three points span a volume; at φ = 0 or π, det G = 0 and two cosines lock the third on the spot; put cAC directly outside the window (click outside on the phase map, or “Forbidden breach”) and det G < 0: no three unit vectors realize these cosines. The half-width s₁s₂ comes from the product of two areas 1 − c², see lagrange_gram_identity; the top of the window, c = 1, is exactly C = A, see unit_gram_overlap_characterization. The third-order identity itself and “positive semidefinite ⇔ realizable” are standard linear algebra, not formalized in this book.
  2. The de-ordered ledger. cos θ = Σₖ (−1)ᵏθ²ᵏ/(2k)!: the (2k)! orders of 2k steps count as one geometric volume. Row k of the ledger adds this term to all three cosines cAB, cBC, cAC = cos θ₃ at once and computes det G⁽ᵏ⁾ from the three truncated numbers. Low-order truncations are not true cosines (they can leave [−1, 1]) and det G⁽ᵏ⁾ can be negative — the magenta squares of the sign tape; as the order grows, the remainder |θ|²ᵏ⁺²/(2k + 2)! falls below 10⁻¹⁶, det G⁽ᵏ⁾ converges to the exact value, and the sign returns to its true side. The readout cos²θ + sin²θ = 1 checks the sine series to the same order.
  3. The crystal book. The clock C has N = 12 pages τⱼ = 2πj/12; the system is a qubit with HS = diag(0, 1) and ψ₀ = |+⟩. The global state Ψ = (1/√N) Σⱼ |τⱼ⟩ ⊗ U(τⱼ)ψ₀ obeys (HC ⊗ I + I ⊗ HS)Ψ = 0; the slice ψ(τ) = √N⟨c(τ)|Ψ⟩ = U(τ)ψ₀ has ⟨σx⟩ = cos τ. The dial lights all 12 pages at once, and the thin chords are the inter-page blocks, none of them zero; the three angles θ₁, θ₂, θ₃ are three pages turned to in this book, and the curve on the right is cos τ with the Taylor partial sum of the current order. Conditioning on a page gives a valid conditional state (conditionalState_isState); the inter-page blocks are nonzero, so pinching changes the crystal (unreadState_fixed_iff); the record weights are the Born weights of rank-one records (rank_one_born_pairing_weight).
  4. P field vs NP thread. Left: the log-barrier field −∇V on the two-dimensional Klee–Minty polygon is defined everywhere, and the gradient flow from (1, 1) obeys the dissipation identity V(x₀) − V(xT) = ∫‖ẋ‖² dt (S2_dissipation). Right: on the state sphere of dimension N = 2ⁿ the uniform start and the hidden solution have inner product only 2−n/2, and amplitude amplification Q = (2UU* − 1)(1 − 2P) turns the pointer by only 2θ per step, sin θ = 2−n/2: Qᵗ U = sin((2t + 1)θ) W + cos((2t + 1)θ) B (uniform_operator_rotation). In this view the ledger records that thread step by step while the page simulates it entry by entry and checks; reaching the solution takes about (π/4)√N steps, and that no algorithm does better is a literature result (BBBV 1997).
  5. No signalling. Take the entangled two-qubit state cos(θ₁/2)|00⟩ + sin(θ₁/2)|11⟩ and let side C measure along m = (sin θ₂ cos φ, sin θ₂ sin φ, cos θ₂) without reading the result: B's reduced state does not move at all, ΔρB = 0; only a read result moves B's conditional state, and the two outcomes cancel on average. The observer's present choice cannot signal to B.
  6. Limits. P ≠ NP and the problem of time in quantum gravity are open; nothing in the pictures or numbers of this page settles them.
