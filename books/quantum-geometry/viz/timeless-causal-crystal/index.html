第 29 章问：如果整体没有时间，必然性从哪里来，观察者的选择又在哪里。它的回答是几何的：三角形的三条边不是谁先谁后推出来的，而是同时并存、彼此约束。本页把这个回答画成四个场景。数学事实与本书的诠释分开标注；本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 窗口。B 立在北极，A 与它夹角 θ₁，C 与它夹角 θ₂。C 只能在绕 B 的一个圆上，转角记作 φ，于是 A·C = cos θ₁ cos θ₂ + sin θ₁ sin θ₂ cos φ，只能落在 [cos(θ₁ + θ₂), cos(θ₁ − θ₂)] 里。det G = (sin θ₁ sin θ₂ sin φ)² 是三根向量撑开的体积的平方：窗口内部体积为正，贴上窗边时三根向量共面、体积为零，窗口之外 det G 为负，没有向量能实现。给定两条余弦之后，第三条的范围不由谁决定，也不随时间变化。
  2. 书页。取一个 N 格的时钟和一个量子比特，整体态是 N 页的等权叠加，第 j 页是初态绕翻页轴转 j·360°/N。把时钟拨一格、同时把系统转一格，整体态不变：它没有“现在”。对“时钟指着第 j 页”条件化，才得到一个具体的态。页与页之间的重叠只依赖页码之差。
  3. 场。球面上取势能 V = 1 − cos α（α 是到目标的角度）。负梯度处处指向目标，从任何地方出发都只要顺着脚下的坡度走；沿途势能的下降恰好等于速度平方的积分。场在目标的对面必须为零，那里是一个不稳定的平衡点。
  4. 线。在 N = 2ⁿ 个候选里找一个目标，振幅放大每步把态向目标转 2θ，sin θ = 1/√N，大约 (π/4)√N 步到达；逐个试则平均要 N/2 次。这根线只有一个方向可走，走错方向没有坡度可以纠正。

Chapter 29 asks where necessity comes from if the whole has no time, and where an observer's choice lives. Its answer is geometric: the three sides of a triangle are not derived one after another; they exist together and constrain each other. The page draws that answer as four scenes. Mathematical facts and this book's interpretations are labelled separately; the page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The window. B stands at the north pole, A makes the angle θ₁ with it and C the angle θ₂. C can only lie on a circle about B, with turning angle φ, so A·C = cos θ₁ cos θ₂ + sin θ₁ sin θ₂ cos φ can only fall in [cos(θ₁ + θ₂), cos(θ₁ − θ₂)]. det G = (sin θ₁ sin θ₂ sin φ)² is the square of the volume the three vectors span: positive inside the window, zero at its edges where the three vectors are coplanar, and negative outside, where no vectors exist. Once two cosines are given, the range of the third is decided by no one and does not change in time.
  2. The pages. Take a clock with N positions and one qubit; the global state is an equal superposition of N pages, page j being the initial state turned by j·360°/N about the paging axis. Advance the clock by one position and turn the system by one step, and the global state is unchanged: it has no “now”. Only conditioning on “the clock reads page j” gives a definite state. The overlap of two pages depends only on the difference of their numbers.
  3. The field. Take the potential V = 1 − cos α on the sphere (α is the angle to the target). The negative gradient points at the target everywhere, so from anywhere it is enough to follow the slope underfoot; along the way the drop of the potential equals the integral of the squared speed. The field must vanish opposite the target, at an unstable equilibrium.
  4. The thread. To find one target among N = 2ⁿ candidates, amplitude amplification turns the state by 2θ per step, sin θ = 1/√N, and arrives after about (π/4)√N steps; trying candidates one by one takes N/2 on average. The thread has a single direction to follow, and no slope corrects a wrong one.

LEAN ✓对有限指标集上的实值 u、v：(Σuᵢ²)(Σvᵢ²) − (Σuᵢvᵢ)² = ½ΣᵢΣⱼ(uᵢvⱼ − uⱼvᵢ)²。取两个单位态，左边是 1 − c²，右边是楔积面积的平方 sin²θ：格拉姆矩阵的每个 2 × 2 主子式都是面积平方，因而非负；格拉姆窗口的半宽 sin θ₁ sin θ₂ 是两个主子式乘积的平方根，亦即两块楔积面积的乘积。已冻结：For real u, v on a finite index set: (Σuᵢ²)(Σvᵢ²) − (Σuᵢvᵢ)² = ½ΣᵢΣⱼ(uᵢvⱼ − uⱼvᵢ)². For two unit states the left side is 1 − c² and the right side the squared wedge area sin²θ: every 2 × 2 principal minor of the Gram matrix is a squared area, hence nonnegative; the half-width sin θ₁ sin θ₂ of the Gram window is the square root of the product of the two minors, equivalently the product of the wedge areas.
