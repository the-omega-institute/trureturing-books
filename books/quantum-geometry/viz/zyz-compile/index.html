理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

舞台是单位球。琥珀色的箭头 v 依次转三段：先绕 z 转 γ，再绕 y 转 β，最后绕 z 转 α，对应旋子 R(α, β, γ) = Rz(α) Ry(β) Rz(γ) 里从右到左的三个因子；时间轴一单位走一段。白色三脚架是 e₁、e₂、e₃ 被同一串转动带到的地方。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 基本旋子与夹心积。绕 u 轴右手转 θ 的旋子是 Ru(θ) = cos(θ/2) − sin(θ/2) Bu，Bz = e₁e₂、By = e₃e₁ = −e₁e₃、Bx = e₂e₃。夹心积 v ↦ R v R̃ 中，与轴平行的分量 v∥ 与 Bu 可交换，留在原地（青色）；平面里的分量 v⊥ 与 Bu 反对易，左右两个半角合成整角（品红的扇形，“θ/2 + θ/2”）。这是第二十六章的手算。
  2. 三段乘成一枚旋子。页面把三个因子在八个刀片掩码上按异或乘起来，得到只在掩码 0、3、5、6 上有分量的 R = s + b₁₂e₁₂ + b₁₃e₁₃ + b₂₃e₂₃，并核对 RR̃ = 1。三列 Re₁、Re₂、Re₃ 就是正文的 zyz_matrix。条带小图是乘到此刻的旋子：s² 与 b₁₂² + b₁₃² + b₂₃² 此消彼长，和恒为 1。
  3. 一根轴、一次转。把 R 写成 cos(φ/2) − sin(φ/2)(n₁e₂e₃ + n₂e₃e₁ + n₃e₁e₂)，就读出转轴 n = (−b₂₃, b₁₃, −b₁₂)/|B| 与转角 φ = 2·atan2(|B|, s)；绕 n 一次转 φ 与三段走到同一点。单位旋子就是一次绕轴转动、R 与 −R 给同一个转动、任意单比特门都有 ZYZ 欧拉角，是文献结果，未在本书形式化。页面只做正文的正向编译：给定 (α, β, γ)，算出 R。
  4. 箭头就是纯态。v 对应 2 × 2 矩阵 ρ = blochMatrix(1, v)。页面当场核对 Tr ρ = 1、bloch(ρ) = v、ρ² = ρ、‖bloch ρ‖ = 1。

The stage is the unit sphere. The amber arrow v makes three turns: first by γ about z, then by β about y, and last by α about z, the three factors of the rotor R(α, β, γ) = Rz(α) Ry(β) Rz(γ) read from right to left; the rail advances one turn per unit. The white tripod is where e₁, e₂, e₃ are carried by the same turns. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Elementary rotors and the sandwich. The rotor of a right-handed turn by θ about the axis u is Ru(θ) = cos(θ/2) − sin(θ/2) Bu, with Bz = e₁e₂, By = e₃e₁ = −e₁e₃, Bx = e₂e₃. In the sandwich v ↦ R v R̃ the part v∥ along the axis commutes with Bu and stays put (cyan); the part v⊥ in the plane anticommutes with Bu, and the two half-angles on the left and on the right add up to the whole angle (the magenta fan, “θ/2 + θ/2”). This is the hand computation of Chapter 26.
  2. Three turns, one rotor. The page multiplies the three factors on the eight blade masks by XOR and obtains R = s + b₁₂e₁₂ + b₁₃e₁₃ + b₂₃e₂₃, supported on masks 0, 3, 5, 6, and checks RR̃ = 1. The columns Re₁, Re₂, Re₃ are the text's zyz_matrix. The strip shows the rotor multiplied so far: s² and b₁₂² + b₁₃² + b₂₃² trade off against each other, and their sum stays 1.
  3. One axis, one turn. Writing R as cos(φ/2) − sin(φ/2)(n₁e₂e₃ + n₂e₃e₁ + n₃e₁e₂) gives the axis n = (−b₂₃, b₁₃, −b₁₂)/|B| and the angle φ = 2·atan2(|B|, s); one turn by φ about n arrives where the three turns arrive. That a unit rotor is one turn about an axis, that R and −R give the same turn, and that every single-qubit gate has ZYZ Euler angles are literature results, not formalized in this book. The page only runs the text's forward compiler: given (α, β, γ), compute R.
  4. The arrow is a pure state. v corresponds to the 2 × 2 matrix ρ = blochMatrix(1, v). The page checks Tr ρ = 1, bloch(ρ) = v, ρ² = ρ and ‖bloch ρ‖ = 1 on the spot.

Claim map. The frozen declarations Chapter 26 cites with lean= (all in ActualPureQubitGeometry, all of kind theorem):

  • blochMatrix_trace (LEAN ✓): Tr blochMatrix(a, r) = a.
  • bloch_blochMatrix (LEAN ✓): bloch(blochMatrix(a, r)) = r.
  • bloch_geometry_of_isHermitian (LEAN ✓): Hermitian reconstruction, and ‖bloch M‖ = 1 for trace-one idempotents.
  • norm_bloch_le_trace_of_posSemidef (LEAN ✓): ‖bloch M‖ ≤ Re Tr M for positive-semidefinite M.

These four concern the state side (matrices and the ball); the rotor compiler itself has no frozen theorem, and on the page it is labelled NUM and ARGUED only.
