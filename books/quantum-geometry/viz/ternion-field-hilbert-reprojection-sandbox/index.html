LEAN ✓在 m × n 双体系统（m,n ≥ 1 且 mn > 1）中，无迹厄米空间正交分解为局部扇区之和与关联扇区，关联扇区的维数为 (m²−1)(n²−1)。双比特（m = n = 2）时，bellDensity 与 classicalCorrelatedDensity 是两个不同的态，它们在两侧的边缘态分别相同。For a bipartite m × n system with m,n ≥ 1 and mn > 1, the traceless Hermitian space splits orthogonally into the sum of the local sectors and the correlation sector. The latter has dimension (m²−1)(n²−1). For two qubits (m = n = 2), the distinct states bellDensity and classicalCorrelatedDensity have equal marginals on both sides. local_marginal_correlation_blind_spot
LEAN ✓在完备实内积空间中，阶段与壳层都取闭子空间序列。若每个新阶段都是上一阶段与一个新壳层的正交和，则第 n 阶段等于初始子空间与前 n 个壳层之和。第 n 阶段与其正交补之和是整个空间。In a complete real inner-product space, let the stages and the shells be sequences of closed subspaces. If each new stage is the orthogonal sum of the previous stage and one shell, stage n is the sum of the initial subspace and the first n shells. Stage n and its orthogonal complement span the whole space. finite_stage_expansion
LEAN ✓若非负可和特征值之和为 Rμ/π，则超过或等于正阈值 t 的模态有限，个数至多 Rμ/(πt)。For nonnegative summable eigenvalues of total trace Rμ/π, finitely many meet a positive threshold t. Their count is at most Rμ/(πt). finite_rank_concentration_mode_bound
LEAN ✓每个阶段若都是真子空间且有正交投影 P，则各阶段 ‖I−P‖ = 1，沿非平凡滤子不能一致趋零。If every stage is a proper subspace admitting an orthogonal projection P, each has ‖I−P‖ = 1. These norms cannot tend to zero along a nontrivial filter. uniform_completion_obstruction

A generic pure ray has 2ᴺ⁺¹−2 real parameters, and typical Haar states have volume-law entanglement. These counts give no general efficient classical simulation.

如何读这两层Reading the two layers

这是数值沙盘，不是证明。上层显示场的组合，下层把选定对象写成平直基坐标。每张卡的下层柱高都按本帧最大模长缩放以便辨认，再乘展平强度 λ。实时读数给出未经缩放的准确范数与维数。拖琥珀手柄调整投影强度，拖背景旋转视角。This numerical sandbox is not a proof. The upper layer composes fields. The lower layer writes the chosen object in a linear basis. On every card, lower pillar heights are scaled to the largest magnitude in the current frame for legibility, then multiplied by the lens flattening λ. The live readouts give the exact, unscaled norms and dimensions. Drag the amber handle to adjust the projection, or drag the background to orbit.

  1. 单场的三个轴两两形成三个旋量平面。取单位轴 n，四元数的四个实坐标为 (cos(θ/2), n sin(θ/2))，平方和为 1。Three axes determine three pairs and hence three bivector planes. For unit n, the four real quaternion coordinates (cos(θ/2), n sin(θ/2)) have squared sum one.
  2. Spin(4) 的左右 SU(2) 因子各有三个参数。四维向量有四个坐标，反对称二阶张量有六个；左侧 2 × 2 根柱显示 qᴸ · 1 · q̄ᴿ，右侧两排各三根柱显示一个示例二形式 qᴸ ∧ qᴿ 的电、磁坐标。欧氏 Spin(4) 与洛伦兹时空有不同的度规。Each chiral SU(2) factor of Spin(4) has three parameters. A four-vector has four coordinates and a two-form has six. The left 2 × 2 block represents qᴸ · 1 · q̄ᴿ. The right two rows carry electric and magnetic coordinates of the sample two-form qᴸ ∧ qᴿ. Euclidean Spin(4) and Lorentzian spacetime have different metrics.
  3. 有向线 (124) (235) (346) (457) (561) (672) (713) 中，前两基元之积等于第三个。反向变号。结合子 [a,b,c] = (ab)c − a(bc) 在线上为零，其余 28 个无序三元组的范数均为 2。On each oriented line (124) (235) (346) (457) (561) (672) (713), the first two basis elements multiply to the third. Reversing them changes the sign. The associator [a,b,c] = (ab)c − a(bc) vanishes on a line and has norm two on all 28 other unordered triples.
  4. 十六元数取 S₁₆ = 𝕆 ⊕ 𝕆e₈。(a,b)(c,d) = (ac − d̄b, da + bc̄)。切换两个输入的组合，可逐项检验范数平方积为 4，输出的十六个坐标全为零。Use S₁₆ = 𝕆 ⊕ 𝕆e₈ with (a,b)(c,d) = (ac − d̄b, da + bc̄). Switch input pairs to see two factors of norm √2 cancel in every output coordinate.
  5. 多体卡的柱高取乘积态样本的振幅绝对值。台前的青色与品红线段按单体与多体泡利槽位数分段。N 比特有 2ᴺ 个复振幅，去掉归一化与整体相位后纯态射线的实维数为 2·2ᴺ−2。4ᴺ−1 个非恒等泡利字中有 3N 个单体字。其余是无迹算子空间的关联方向，密度矩阵还须满足正性。The many-body pillars show absolute amplitudes of a product-state sample. The cyan and magenta front edge divides one-site from multi-site Pauli slots in proportion to their counts. N qubits have 2ᴺ complex amplitudes. Removing normalization and global phase leaves 2·2ᴺ−2 real pure-ray parameters. Exactly 3N of the 4ᴺ−1 nonidentity Pauli words have weight one. The remainder counts correlation directions in traceless operator space. Physical density matrices also obey positivity.
  6. 在开边界 MPS 一般满秩层，令 rₖ = min(χ,2ᵏ,2ᴺ⁻ᵏ)。原始复参数有 ∑2rₖ₋₁rₖ 个，内部换基占 ∑rₖ² 个复维数（只计内部键）。取差 D 后，纯态射线实维数为 2D−2。较低秩层的维数可更小。On the generic full-rank stratum of an open-boundary MPS, let rₖ = min(χ,2ᵏ,2ᴺ⁻ᵏ). Subtract the internal bond gauge dimension ∑rₖ² from the raw complex entry count ∑2rₖ₋₁rₖ to get D. The ray has 2D−2 real parameters. Lower-rank strata can be smaller.
