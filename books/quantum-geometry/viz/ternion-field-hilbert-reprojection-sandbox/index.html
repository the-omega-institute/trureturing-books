MODEL本书的读法与舞台的造型：场体、透镜与基柱是几何读法；场体的球面八分体形状、透镜的位置与键的粗细都是示意。局部旋转群 SU(2)ᴺ 有 3N 个实参数，乘积纯态射线有 2N 个；键维受控的 MPS 描述低纠缠的态族，参数量为 O(Nχ²)；一般纯态需要 2ᴺ⁺¹ − 2 个实参数，典型的哈尔态具有体积律纠缠。这些数目没有给出通用的高效经典模拟算法。The reading of this book and the shapes on the stage: the field bodies, the lens and the pillars are a geometric reading; the spherical-octant shape of the bodies, the place of the lens and the width of the bonds are schematic. Local rotations in SU(2)ᴺ have 3N real parameters and product pure rays have 2N; MPS of bounded bond dimension describe low-entanglement families with O(Nχ²) parameters; a generic pure ray has 2ᴺ⁺¹ − 2 real parameters, and typical Haar states have volume-law entanglement. These counts give no general efficient classical simulation.

理论接口 · 多场再投影Theory interface · many-field reprojection

这是数值沙盘，不是证明。每个数都由页面当场算出（NUM）；每条 Lean 定理只在依据栏写明的那一个实例上演示，没有实例的写明“页面不演示”；场体、透镜与基柱的画法是本书的读法（MODEL）。每张卡的下层柱高都按本帧最大模长缩放以便辨认，再乘展平强度 λ；实时读数给出未经缩放的准确范数与维数。

  1. 单场与四元数。一个场的三条轴两两张成三个旋量平面。取单位轴 n，四元数的四个实坐标为 (cos(θ/2), n sin(θ/2))，平方和为 1：银白的是标量柱 w，青色的是三根向量柱。
  2. 左右手征场。Spin(4) 的左右 SU(2) 因子各有三个参数。四维向量有四个坐标，反对称二阶张量有六个：左侧 2 × 2 根柱显示 qᴸ · 1 · q̄ᴿ，右侧两排各三根柱显示示例二形式 qᴸ ∧ qᴿ 的电、磁坐标，可分解的二形式满足 E·B = 0。欧氏 Spin(4) 与洛伦兹时空的度规不同。
  3. 法诺七场。有向线 (124) (235) (346) (457) (561) (672) (713) 上，前两个基元之积等于第三个，反向变号。结合子 [a, b, c] = (ab)c − a(bc) 在线上为零，其余 28 个无序三元组的范数都是 2；品红折痕画出 [e₁, e₂, e₃] = −2e₆。
  4. 十六元数零因子。取 𝕊₁₆ = 𝕆 ⊕ 𝕆e₈，(a, b)(c, d) = (ac − d̄b, da + bc̄)。切换三组输入：两个范数为 √2 的因子沿品红路径两两相消，十六个输出坐标全为零。
  5. 多场再投影。多场卡的柱高取乘积态样本的振幅绝对值。N 比特有 2ᴺ 个复振幅，去掉归一化与整体相位后纯态射线的实维数为 2ᴺ⁺¹ − 2。4ᴺ − 1 个非恒等泡利字中 3N 个是单体字，台前的青色与品红线段按单体与多体的个数分段；其余是无迹算子空间的关联方向，密度矩阵还须满足正性。
  6. 矩阵乘积态。在开边界 MPS 的一般满秩层，令 rₖ = min(χ, 2ᵏ, 2ᴺ⁻ᵏ)。原始复参数有 ∑ 2rₖ₋₁rₖ 个，内部换基占 ∑ rₖ² 个复维数（只计内部键），差为 D，纯态射线的实维数为 2D − 2；秩更低的层维数可更小。条带小图画出 N = 1 至 300 的 3N、2D − 2、2ᴺ 与 4ᴺ − 1。
  7. 双透镜。“多场再投影”读法把上层读成场、下层读成被摊平的基坐标；“凯莱—迪克森与张量网络”读法把同一组系数读成 ℍ、Spin(4)、𝕆、𝕊₁₆ 与 SU(2)ᴺ 的代数。两种读法用的是同一组 N、λ 与 χ。

This is a numerical sandbox, not a proof. Every number is computed on the page (NUM); each Lean theorem is shown only on the one instance named in the evidence panel, and where there is none the panel says the page does not show it; the drawing of the field bodies, the lens and the pillars is the reading of this book (MODEL). On every card the lower pillar heights are scaled to the largest magnitude in the current frame for legibility and then multiplied by the flattening λ; the live readouts give the exact, unscaled norms and dimensions.

  1. One field and the quaternions. The three axes of a field span three bivector planes in pairs. For a unit axis n, the four real quaternion coordinates (cos(θ/2), n sin(θ/2)) have squared sum 1: the silver pillar is the scalar w and the three cyan pillars are the vector part.
  2. Left and right chiral fields. Each chiral SU(2) factor of Spin(4) has three parameters. A four-vector has four coordinates and a two-form has six: the left 2 × 2 block shows qᴸ · 1 · q̄ᴿ, and the two rows of three on the right carry the electric and magnetic coordinates of the sample two-form qᴸ ∧ qᴿ, which as a decomposable two-form has E·B = 0. Euclidean Spin(4) and Lorentzian spacetime have different metrics.
  3. The seven Fano fields. On each oriented line (124) (235) (346) (457) (561) (672) (713) the first two basis elements multiply to the third, and reversing them changes the sign. The associator [a, b, c] = (ab)c − a(bc) vanishes on a line and has norm 2 on all 28 other unordered triples; the magenta crease draws [e₁, e₂, e₃] = −2e₆.
  4. Sedenion zero divisors. Take 𝕊₁₆ = 𝕆 ⊕ 𝕆e₈ with (a, b)(c, d) = (ac − d̄b, da + bc̄). Switch between three input pairs: two factors of norm √2 cancel in pairs along the magenta paths and all sixteen output coordinates vanish.
  5. Many-field reprojection. On the many-field card the pillars show the absolute amplitudes of a product-state sample. N qubits have 2ᴺ complex amplitudes; removing the normalization and the global phase leaves 2ᴺ⁺¹ − 2 real parameters for a pure ray. Exactly 3N of the 4ᴺ − 1 non-identity Pauli words have weight one, and the cyan and magenta segments of the front edge are split by the one-site and multi-site counts; the rest are correlation directions of the traceless operators, and a density matrix must also be positive.
  6. Matrix product states. On the generic full-rank stratum of an open-boundary MPS, let rₖ = min(χ, 2ᵏ, 2ᴺ⁻ᵏ). Subtracting the internal gauge dimension ∑ rₖ² from the raw complex entry count ∑ 2rₖ₋₁rₖ gives D, and the ray has 2D − 2 real parameters; strata of lower rank can be smaller. The strip draws 3N, 2D − 2, 2ᴺ and 4ᴺ − 1 for N = 1 to 300.
  7. Two lenses. The many-field reading reads the upper layer as fields and the lower one as their flattened basis coordinates; the Cayley–Dickson and tensor-network reading reads the same coefficients as the algebra of ℍ, Spin(4), 𝕆, 𝕊₁₆ and SU(2)ᴺ. Both readings use the same N, λ and χ.
