理论接口 · 多面体割圆、毛球角亏与余弦不动点Theory interface · Polyhedral approximation, hairy ball defect & cosine fixed points

球永远只是近似：有限多面体要合拢成球，就必须把 4π 的总曲率集中成离散顶点上的角亏接缝；有限阶多项式要逼近 cos θ，就必须留下非零的截断余项；有限精度的读数要逼近自指不动点 θ* = cos θ*，就必须保留非零残差。本页把这三道裂缝与第二十一章的对角线逃逸、第二十四章的三类自指不动点放在同一块屏幕上。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 测地多面体与 720° 角亏（第二十一章 §21.4）。V = 12、42、162、642 由正二十面体逐次中点细分并投影到单位球得到；V = 60 是 60 个点经库仑排斥弛豫后的凸包。页面实时数出 E 与 F，验证 E = 3V − 6、F = 2V − 4 与 V − E + F = 2，并统计顶点度：每张网格恰有 12 个五度顶点，其余全是六度。在等边模型里五度顶点角亏 (6 − 5)·60° = 60°、六度顶点为 0°，合计 12 × 60° = 720° = 4π。在真实的内接多面体上，凸性使每个顶点的实际角亏都严格为正：六度顶点同样分到角亏，网格越密，曲率摊得越均匀，读数里五度顶点所占的份额随 V 增大而迅速下降；但总和始终精确等于 720°（笛卡尔定理，文献结果）。只有在等边模型里，720° 才全部集中在 12 个五度顶点上。
  2. 泰勒截断裂缝（第二十一章 §21.3）。滑块 N ∈ [1, 6] 取 cos₂N(θ) = ∑ⱼ₌₀ᴺ⁻¹ (−1)ʲ θ²ʲ/(2j)!（次数 2N − 2）。页面直接对尾项求和得到余项 R₂N(θ₀)，并与拉格朗日界 θ₀²ᴺ/(2N)! 比较；对 θ₀ ∈ (0, π] 余项严格非零。
  3. 开口螺旋与圆形影子（第二十一章 §21.3，示意模型）。螺旋 (cos t, sin t, p·t/2π) 沿 z 轴投下的影子是闭合的单位圆；螺距 p 为零时螺旋退化为这个圆，p > 0 时绕一圈回到 (1, 0, p) 而不是 (1, 0, 0)。这是把“并入相对语义对角线 conceptJoin(latent, g) 使观测拓扑严格加细”想象成沿新法向抬升的意象；对应的 Lean 结论是 complete_diagonal_not_continuous 与 complete_diagonal_topological_settlement（LEAN ✓），它们讲的是划分拓扑与分离亏损，螺旋本身不是定理的内容。
  4. Dottie 余弦蛛网（第二十四章 §24.4）。从任一初值 θ₀ ∈ [0, π] 出发迭代 θₖ₊₁ = cos θₖ，蛛网在对角线 y = θ 与余弦曲线之间螺旋收向唯一实不动点 θ* ≈ 0.7390851332，此处 cos θ* = θ*，正交双矢量 sin θ* = √(1 − θ*²) ≈ 0.6736120292。对紧不变区间内任意异于 θ* 的初值 θ₀ ∈ [0, 1] \ {θ*}（或前向轨道避开 θ* 的任意初值，例如由林德曼—魏尔斯特拉斯定理保证的 [0, 1] 内一切有理/代数弧度初值），每一有限步 k ≥ 0 的残差 εₖ = |cos θₖ − θₖ|（及 |θₖ − θ*|）均严格大于 0；cos 在 [0, 1]（及 [−1, 1]）上的压缩率为 sin 1 ≈ 0.8414709848 < 1。巴拿赫压缩与 θ* 的超越性是文献结果，未在本书形式化。
  5. 黄金倒数迭代（第二十四章 §24.3）。从 x₀ = max(0.2, θ₀) 迭代 xₖ₊₁ = 1 + 1/xₖ，残差 |1 + 1/xₖ − xₖ| 迅速下降，迭代值趋向 φ = (1 + √5)/2 ≈ 1.6180339887。golden_fixed_point_unique（LEAN ✓）保证 φ 是 1 + 1/x = x 在正实数上的唯一解；迭代的收敛只在页面上数值展示。
  6. 有限收缩集合（第二十四章 §24.1）。有限读数网格 X = {j/16 : 0 ≤ j ≤ 50} ⊂ [0, π]。初始集 S₀ 取 X 中落在 θ₀ 与 cos θ₀ 之间（两端各放宽 1/16）的读数；更新 U(A) 保留 A 中残差 |cos q − q| 较小的一半（至少保留一个），故 U(A) ⊆ A。finite_contracting_updates_stabilize（LEAN ✓）保证序列在 N ≤ |S₀| 步内稳定、严格变化次数不超过 |S₀|。稳定后剩下的读数 q 仍有 |cos q − q| > 0：有限精度的机器停在一个自洽的集合上，却停不到 θ*。
  7. 模型边界。V = 60 的网格坐标取五位小数后重新归一化，其余网格由公式生成；角亏、余项、迭代与集合全部在双精度浮点下计算。θ* 与 π 是否代数无关是开放问题（可由沙努埃尔猜想推出）。

A sphere is only ever approximated: a finite polyhedron can close up into a sphere only by concentrating the total curvature 4π into angular-defect seams at its vertices; a finite polynomial can approach cos θ only by leaving a nonzero truncation remainder; a finite-precision reading can approach the self-referential fixed point θ* = cos θ* only by keeping a nonzero residual. This page puts these three seams on one screen, next to the diagonal escape of Chapter 21 and the three kinds of self-referential fixed points of Chapter 24. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Geodesic polyhedra and the 720° defect (Chapter 21 §21.4). V = 12, 42, 162, 642 come from repeated midpoint subdivision of the icosahedron projected onto the unit sphere; V = 60 is the convex hull of 60 points relaxed under Coulomb repulsion. The page counts E and F live, checks E = 3V − 6, F = 2V − 4 and V − E + F = 2, and tallies vertex degrees: every mesh has exactly 12 degree-5 vertices and all others have degree 6. In the equilateral model a degree-5 vertex carries a defect of (6 − 5)·60° = 60° and a degree-6 vertex 0°, for a total of 12 × 60° = 720° = 4π. On the actual inscribed polyhedron convexity makes every metric defect strictly positive: degree-6 vertices carry defect as well, the curvature spreads more evenly as the mesh gets finer, and the readouts show the share of the degree-5 vertices falling quickly as V grows; yet the total is always exactly 720° (Descartes's theorem, a literature result). Only in the equilateral model is all of the 720° concentrated at the 12 degree-5 vertices.
  2. The Taylor truncation seam (Chapter 21 §21.3). The slider N ∈ [1, 6] selects cos₂N(θ) = ∑ⱼ₌₀ᴺ⁻¹ (−1)ʲ θ²ʲ/(2j)! (degree 2N − 2). The page sums the tail directly to obtain the remainder R₂N(θ₀) and compares it with the Lagrange bound θ₀²ᴺ/(2N)!; for θ₀ ∈ (0, π] the remainder is strictly nonzero.
  3. The open helix and its circular shadow (Chapter 21 §21.3, schematic model). The helix (cos t, sin t, p·t/2π) casts a closed unit circle as its shadow along the z axis; at pitch p = 0 it collapses onto that circle, while for p > 0 one turn brings it to (1, 0, p) rather than back to (1, 0, 0). This pictures “adjoining the relative semantic diagonal conceptJoin(latent, g) strictly refines the observation topology” as a lift along a new normal; the corresponding Lean results are complete_diagonal_not_continuous and complete_diagonal_topological_settlement (LEAN ✓), which concern partition topologies and separation deficits; the helix itself is not part of any theorem.
  4. The Dottie cosine cobweb (Chapter 24 §24.4). Starting from any seed θ₀ ∈ [0, π], the iteration θₖ₊₁ = cos θₖ spirals between the diagonal y = θ and the cosine curve towards the unique real fixed point θ* ≈ 0.7390851332, where cos θ* = θ* and the orthogonal bivector is sin θ* = √(1 − θ*²) ≈ 0.6736120292. For every seed θ₀ ∈ [0, 1] \ {θ*} (or any seed whose forward orbit avoids θ*, including all rational and algebraic angles in [0, 1] by the Lindemann–Weierstrass theorem), every finite step k ≥ 0 keeps a strictly positive residual εₖ = |cos θₖ − θₖ| > 0 (and |θₖ − θ*| > 0); on [0, 1] (and [−1, 1]) cos contracts with rate sin 1 ≈ 0.8414709848 < 1. Banach contraction and the transcendence of θ* are literature results, not formalized in this book.
  5. The golden reciprocal iteration (Chapter 24 §24.3). From x₀ = max(0.2, θ₀), the iteration xₖ₊₁ = 1 + 1/xₖ drives the residual |1 + 1/xₖ − xₖ| down quickly and approaches φ = (1 + √5)/2 ≈ 1.6180339887. golden_fixed_point_unique (LEAN ✓) guarantees that φ is the only positive solution of 1 + 1/x = x; convergence of the iteration is only shown numerically.
  6. Finite contracting sets (Chapter 24 §24.1). The finite reading grid is X = {j/16 : 0 ≤ j ≤ 50} ⊂ [0, π]. The initial set S₀ holds the readings of X between θ₀ and cos θ₀ (widened by 1/16 at both ends); the update U(A) keeps the better half of A by residual |cos q − q| (at least one element), so U(A) ⊆ A. finite_contracting_updates_stabilize (LEAN ✓) guarantees stabilization within N ≤ |S₀| steps with at most |S₀| strict changes. The surviving reading q still has |cos q − q| > 0: a finite-precision machine settles on a self-consistent set, but not on θ*.
  7. Model limits. The V = 60 mesh coordinates are stored to five decimals and renormalized; the other meshes are generated by formula; defects, remainders, iterations and sets are all computed in double-precision floating point. Whether θ* and π are algebraically independent is an open problem (it would follow from Schanuel's conjecture).
