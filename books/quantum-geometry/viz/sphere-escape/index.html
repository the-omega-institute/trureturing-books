理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

第二十一章的主张是：站在一个系统内部，用有限的手段去穷尽它，总会有东西落在外面；想把它收进来，就得换到更大的系统里，而那里又有新的东西落在外面。本页用六个能亲手算的例子给这句话配上画面。每个场景下面的图都画同一件事：留在外面的量随步数怎样变化，并且从不为零。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 对角线。一张清单有 N 行，每行 N 位。读出第 k 行的第 k 位再翻转，得到 d 的第 k 位。于是 d 与第 k 行在第 k 位不同，d 不等于任何一行。把 d 收进清单并补上一列，新的对角线又给出一个不在清单里的串。右侧的点阵画出全部 2ᴺ 个串，清单只占其中至多 N 个。
  2. 纤维。第二十一章的四地址例：解码器是常零、恒等、取反、常一，目录满射。每个地址读自己的潜坐标、喂自己的解码器、再翻转，得到 g。潜坐标相同的地址组成一根纤维；无论怎样排潜坐标（共 16 种），总有一根纤维里的两个地址被 g 分开（分离亏损），把 g 立成第二根轴后块数严格增加。
  3. 割圆。单位圆的内接正 n 边形，每条边的中点离圆弧还有 1 − cos(π/n) = 2 sin²(π/2n)。刘徽从 6 边割到 192 边（用 192 边形的面积得 3.1410），祖冲之算到 24576 边；边数每翻一倍，缝大约缩到四分之一，但对任何有限的 n 都大于零。余弦的泰勒部分和也一样：截到有限项总有余项。
  4. 指针。一台 N + 1 格的记录指针，振幅 c(0), …, c(N) 的平方和为 1。平移 ℓ 格后它与自己的重叠 |γ(ℓ)| 不超过正 (⌊N/ℓ⌋ + 2) 边形的边心距 cos(π/(⌊N/ℓ⌋ + 2))，严格小于 1；由此，任何恢复操作的误差至少是弓高的一半。正弦形 c(k) ∝ sin(π(k + 1)/(N + 2)) 在 ℓ = 1 时恰好取到这个界（页面实算，不是定理的一部分）。
  5. 余弦。从任意实数出发反复取余弦，数列交替地从两侧逼近 x* ≈ 0.739085，每步误差大约乘以 sin x* ≈ 0.674。误差按等比缩小，但有限步之后仍大于零。镜头跟着放大时，蛛网图看起来总是同一个螺旋。
  6. 素数。把清单里的前 k 个素数乘起来加一得到 Q。Q 除以清单里每一个素数都余 1（每只钟的指针都指着 1），所以 Q 的素因子都不在清单里。Q 自己不一定是素数：2·3·5·7·11·13 + 1 = 30031 = 59 × 509。有限个素数的欧拉乘积 ∏(1 − p⁻²)⁻¹ 始终小于 π²/6。

Chapter 21 claims that from inside a system, exhausting it by finite means always leaves something outside; to take that in you must move to a larger system, where something new is left outside again. The page gives the sentence six pictures you can compute by hand. The plot under each scene draws the same thing: how what is left outside changes with the step count, and that it is never zero. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The diagonal. A list has N rows of N bits. Read bit k of row k and flip it to get bit k of d. Then d differs from row k in place k, so d equals no row. Put d on the list, add a column, and the new diagonal again gives a string that is not on the list. The dot grid on the right shows all 2ᴺ strings; the list holds at most N of them.
  2. The fibres. Chapter 21's four-address example: the decoders are constant 0, identity, negation and constant 1, so the catalog is surjective. Each address reads its own latent coordinate, feeds its own decoder and flips the result to give g. Addresses with the same latent form a fibre; however the latents are arranged (16 ways), some fibre holds two addresses that g separates (the separation deficit), and raising g as a second axis strictly increases the number of blocks.
  3. The polygon. For the regular n-gon inscribed in the unit circle, the midpoint of each side is still 1 − cos(π/n) = 2 sin²(π/2n) short of the arc. Liu Hui went from 6 sides to 192 (and got 3.1410 from the area of the 192-gon) and Zu Chongzhi to 24576; each doubling shrinks the gap to about a quarter, yet it is positive for every finite n. The Taylor partial sums of the cosine behave the same way: cut after finitely many terms, a remainder is left.
  4. The pointer. A record pointer on N + 1 cells has amplitudes c(0), …, c(N) whose squares sum to 1. Shifted by ℓ cells, its overlap |γ(ℓ)| with itself is at most the apothem cos(π/(⌊N/ℓ⌋ + 2)) of the regular (⌊N/ℓ⌋ + 2)-gon, strictly below 1; hence every recovery leaves an error of at least half the sagitta. The sine shape c(k) ∝ sin(π(k + 1)/(N + 2)) meets the bound exactly at ℓ = 1 (computed on the page, not part of the theorem).
  5. The cosine. Take the cosine again and again from any real start: the sequence closes in on x* ≈ 0.739085 from alternating sides, the error shrinking by about sin x* ≈ 0.674 per step. It shrinks geometrically but is still positive after finitely many steps. With the view zooming in, the cobweb always looks like the same spiral.
  6. The primes. Multiply the first k primes on the list and add one to get Q. Q leaves remainder 1 on division by every listed prime (every dial's hand points at 1), so no prime factor of Q is on the list. Q itself need not be prime: 2·3·5·7·11·13 + 1 = 30031 = 59 × 509. The Euler product ∏(1 − p⁻²)⁻¹ over finitely many primes always stays below π²/6.
