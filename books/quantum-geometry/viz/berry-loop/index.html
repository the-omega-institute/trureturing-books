ARGUED贝里相位与巴格曼不变量（Berry 1984、Simon 1983、Bargmann 1964、Pancharatnam 1956）：大圆弧围成的回路，相邻内积连乘积的相位等于所围立体角的一半，arg Δ = Ω/2；自旋朝上支的贝里相位按约定 γB = −Ω/2。文献结果，未在本书形式化。Berry phase and the Bargmann invariant (Berry 1984, Simon 1983, Bargmann 1964, Pancharatnam 1956): for a loop of great-circle arcs, the phase of the product of neighbouring inner products is half the enclosed solid angle, arg Δ = Ω/2; by convention the spin-up Berry phase is γB = −Ω/2. Literature result, not formalized in this book.

理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

舞台是布洛赫球面。球面上一点 p = (sin θ cos φ, sin θ sin φ, cos θ) 对应二维复旋量 ψ(p) = (cos θ/2, eiφ sin θ/2)。青色的回路由大圆弧连成；琥珀色的箭头是贴在球面上的切向量，沿回路“不扭转地”搬运（沿大圆弧就是绕这条弧的转轴旋转）。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 两种转序。从北极出发，先绕 Y 转 90° 再绕 X 转 90°，停在 +X；先绕 X 再绕 Y，停在 −Y（第十章 §10.1）。第二步都不挪动点，却把贴在点上的箭头转了 90°。页面用旋转实算（NUM）。
  2. 面积。回路圈住的有向立体角 Ω：从回路内部一点 c 向回路连扇形，把每个小球面三角形的有向面积相加（NUM）。逆着从外面看的方向走为正。八分之一球是 π/2，赤道圈住的半球是 2π。
  3. 转角。把箭头从起点沿回路搬到当前点，再与“沿 起点 → c → 当前点 两段大圆弧搬过来”的虚线参照比较，两者的夹角随时等于品红扇形已扫过的面积；走回起点时参照就是出发时的箭头。这是高斯—博内定理在球面上的一例：文献结果，未在本书形式化（ARGUED）；页面量出的两数一致（NUM）。
  4. 相位。相邻顶点复内积的连乘积 Δ = ⟨ψ₀, ψ₁⟩⟨ψ₁, ψ₂⟩⋯⟨ψn−1, ψ₀⟩ 不依赖每个旋量各自的相位选择。大圆弧回路的 arg Δ = Ω/2；自旋朝上支的贝里相位按约定是 γB = −Ω/2（Berry 1984、Simon 1983、Bargmann 1964、Pancharatnam 1956；文献结果，未在本书形式化，ARGUED）。八分球三角形 N → +X → +Y 的 Δ = (1 + i)/4，arg Δ = π/4。
  5. 同纬度的内积。赤道上相邻两点的内积由冻结的推论给出：⟨latitude(t, z), latitude(t, w)⟩ = (1 − t) + t·z̄w，latitude_dot（LEAN ✓）。t = ½、z、w 相差 i 时它是 (1 + i)/2；赤道四点连乘得 −1/4，相位 π，正是半球 2π 的一半。推论本身只给内积公式，不声明和乐定理。
  6. 反着走。同一个三角形反向走，Ω、转角与 arg Δ 都变号，而各内积的模平方 |⟨ψi, ψj⟩|² 一个也不变：只看模平方分不出手性。
  7. 梳不平。切向量场 v(p) = e₃ − (p·e₃)p 是竖直方向在切平面上的影子，在 +X 处是 e₃，长度 sin θ，只在两极为零，这两个孤立零点的指标各为 +1，和为 χ(S²) = 2。任何连续切向量场在 S² 上都有零点（毛球定理）；零点都孤立时，指标之和总是 χ(S²) = 2（庞加莱—霍普夫）。两者都是文献结果，未在本书形式化（ARGUED）。

The stage is the Bloch sphere. A point p = (sin θ cos φ, sin θ sin φ, cos θ) corresponds to the two-component complex spinor ψ(p) = (cos θ/2, eiφ sin θ/2). The cyan loop is made of great-circle arcs; the amber arrow is a tangent vector on the sphere carried along the loop without twisting (along a great-circle arc that is a rotation about the arc's axis). The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Two orders. Starting at the North Pole, a 90° turn about Y followed by a 90° turn about X ends at +X; about X first and then about Y ends at −Y (Chapter 10, §10.1). Neither second turn moves the point, but each turns the arrow attached to it by 90°. The page computes the rotations (NUM).
  2. Area. The oriented solid angle Ω enclosed by the loop: a fan from a point c inside the loop, adding the oriented areas of its small spherical triangles (NUM). Going anticlockwise as seen from outside counts as positive. An eighth of the sphere is π/2; the hemisphere enclosed by the equator is 2π.
  3. Turn. The arrow carried from the start along the loop to the current point is compared with the dashed reference carried there along the two arcs start → c → current point. Their angle equals, at every moment, the magenta area the fan has swept; back at the start the reference is the arrow as it set out. This is one case of the Gauss–Bonnet theorem on the sphere: a literature result, not formalized in this book (ARGUED); the two numbers the page measures agree (NUM).
  4. Phase. The product of the complex inner products of neighbouring corners, Δ = ⟨ψ₀, ψ₁⟩⟨ψ₁, ψ₂⟩⋯⟨ψn−1, ψ₀⟩, does not depend on the phase chosen for each spinor. For a loop of great-circle arcs arg Δ = Ω/2; by convention the spin-up Berry phase is γB = −Ω/2 (Berry 1984, Simon 1983, Bargmann 1964, Pancharatnam 1956; a literature result, not formalized in this book, ARGUED). For the octant triangle N → +X → +Y, Δ = (1 + i)/4 and arg Δ = π/4.
  5. Inner products on one latitude. The inner product of neighbouring points on the equator comes from a frozen corollary: ⟨latitude(t, z), latitude(t, w)⟩ = (1 − t) + t·z̄w, latitude_dot (LEAN ✓). With t = ½ and w = i·z it is (1 + i)/2; the product round the four equator points is −1/4, of phase π, half the hemisphere's 2π. The corollary itself gives the inner product only; it does not state a holonomy theorem.
  6. The other way round. Walking the same triangle backwards flips the sign of Ω, of the turn and of arg Δ, while not one squared modulus |⟨ψi, ψj⟩|² changes: squared moduli alone cannot tell the handedness.
  7. Cannot be combed. The tangent field v(p) = e₃ − (p·e₃)p is the shadow of the vertical direction on the tangent plane: e₃ at +X, of length sin θ, zero only at the two poles, two isolated zeros of index +1 each, adding up to χ(S²) = 2. Every continuous tangent field on S² has a zero (hairy ball theorem); when its zeros are isolated, their indices always add up to χ(S²) = 2 (Poincaré–Hopf). Both are literature results, not formalized in this book (ARGUED).
