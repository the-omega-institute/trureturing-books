第 30 章把纳维—斯托克斯方程与 P 对 NP 并排来看：光滑的“场”会不会被非线性项拉成一根“线”。本页只画流体这一侧的三件事，每一件都可以亲手验证。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 对流的两半。(u·∇)u = ∇(|u|²/2) + ω × u。舞台上的流场在驻点流（s = 0，无旋）与刚体旋转（s = 1）之间变化，涡度 ω = 2s；探针处白色箭头是对流项，青色是梯度那一半，品红是兰姆项。无旋时品红箭头消失：整个对流项是梯度，并入压强。s 越过 ½ 时流线从双曲线合拢成椭圆。
  2. 涡管。对方程取旋度，梯度全部消失，剩下涡度方程，其中 (ω·∇)u 是拉伸项。平面流里 ω 垂直于平面而 u 不随那个方向变化，这一项恒为零。三维里涡管被轴向拉伸：体积不变，截面 A 变小；无粘时环量 Γ = ωA 不变，所以 ω = Γ/A 变大。粘性让涡核向外扩散：高斯涡核的半径满足 dR²/dt = 4ν − αR²，恒定拉伸率 α 下按精确解 R(t)² = R∞² + (R₀² − R∞²)e^(−αt) 光滑地趋向 R∞ = √(4ν/α)，截面停在 4πν/α（伯格斯涡）；无粘时 R = R₀e^(−αt/2)，核心涡度 ω = ω₀e^(αt) 无界增长。R∞ ∝ ν^½ ∝ Re^(−½) 只属于这根涡管；小图另画柯尔莫哥洛夫的均匀湍流标度 η/L ∼ Re^(−¾)、N ∼ Re^(9/4) 与模型能谱，那是湍流的统计规律，两个指数不由一个推出另一个。如果拉伸率本身正比于涡度（示意模型 α = κω），截面按 dA/dt = 4πν − κΓ 线性变化：κΓ > 4πν 时在有限时间 A₀/(κΓ − 4πν) 夹断。
  3. 闭合。只看得见 x 的观察者想用 x 预测 x 的加速度。把状态拆成看得见的 a 与看不见的 b，看见的加速度之差是 P(L b) − P(B(a, b) + B(b, a)) − P(B(b, b))。三项都为零时，舞台上每条曲线都是水平线：下一步只由 x 决定。任何一项不为零，同一个 x 就对应不同的下一步。Lean 证明了这是充要条件，并在一个五模态傅里叶—伽辽金实例里算出混合项等于 i/4，所以那里不存在精确闭合。

Chapter 30 sets the Navier–Stokes equations beside P versus NP: can a smooth “field” be drawn out into a “line” by the nonlinear term? This page draws three things on the fluid side only, each of which can be checked by hand. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The two halves of advection. (u·∇)u = ∇(|u|²/2) + ω × u. The flow on the stage goes from the stagnation flow (s = 0, irrotational) to rigid rotation (s = 1), with vorticity ω = 2s; at the probe the white arrow is the advection term, the cyan arrow its gradient half and the magenta arrow the Lamb term. Without vorticity the magenta arrow is gone: the whole advection term is a gradient and joins the pressure. As s passes ½ the streamlines close from hyperbolas into ellipses.
  2. The vortex tube. Taking the curl of the equation removes every gradient and leaves the vorticity equation, in which (ω·∇)u is the stretching term. In a plane flow ω is normal to the plane and u does not vary in that direction, so the term is identically zero. In three dimensions the tube is stretched along its axis: the volume stays, the cross-section A shrinks; without viscosity the circulation Γ = ωA stays, so ω = Γ/A grows. Viscosity spreads the core: the radius of a Gaussian core obeys dR²/dt = 4ν − αR², and under a steady strain α it follows the exact solution R(t)² = R∞² + (R₀² − R∞²)e^(−αt) smoothly down to R∞ = √(4ν/α), the cross-section settling at 4πν/α (the Burgers vortex); without viscosity R = R₀e^(−αt/2) and the core vorticity ω = ω₀e^(αt) grows without bound. The scaling R∞ ∝ ν^½ ∝ Re^(−½) belongs to this tube alone; the small plot draws, separately, Kolmogorov’s scaling of homogeneous turbulence, η/L ∼ Re^(−¾) and N ∼ Re^(9/4), with a model spectrum: a statistical law of turbulence, and neither exponent follows from the other. If the strain itself is proportional to the vorticity (the schematic model α = κω), the cross-section changes linearly, dA/dt = 4πν − κΓ: for κΓ > 4πν it pinches at the finite time A₀/(κΓ − 4πν).
  3. Closure. An observer who sees only x wants to predict the acceleration of x from x. Split the state into a seen part a and an unseen part b; the difference of the seen accelerations is P(L b) − P(B(a, b) + B(b, a)) − P(B(b, b)). When all three terms vanish every curve on the stage is a horizontal line: the next step depends on x alone. If any of them is nonzero, one x has different next steps. Lean proves that this is necessary and sufficient, and computes the mixed term in a five-mode Fourier–Galerkin instance: it equals i/4, so no exact closure exists there.
