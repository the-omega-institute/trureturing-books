  1. Bowl and trough. The bowl on the stage is the part V ≤ Vmax of the potential V(Φ) = λ(|Φ|² − η²)², λ = 1, Vmax = max(1.15 η⁴, 0.4), with heights drawn as V/Vmax (so the hilltop V(0) = λη⁴ always lies inside the bowl). For η = ηSSB > 0 its lowest points form a circular trough of radius η (a slice of the vacuum manifold G/H), and the hilltop Φ = 0 is symmetric but unstable; at η = 0, V = λ|Φ|⁴ and only one symmetric vacuum is left at the centre. The amber anchor v₀ is the vacuum that has been chosen, and the rail is its azimuth φ₀: turning along the trough costs no energy, and every φ₀ gives the same readouts.
  2. Pythagorean split. Take ε = (π/2) η (a model choice). For the unit vector v₀ and the vacuum v displaced by ε, the inner-product share cos²ε and the wedge share |v₀ ∧ v|² = sin²ε come from the two sides of Lagrange's identity and add up to 1. The trihedron ΔTε has its right angle at v₀ and legs of length ε along e¹ and e²; its excess Ω = 2 arctan(tan²(ε/2)) is the integral over this triangle of the curvature F = −e¹∧e² of the H-connection on SU(2)/U(1) = S², and the flux through the whole polar cap is 2π(1 − cos ε). From matrices the page also computes the Maurer–Cartan form U⁻¹dU = eᵃXₐ + ωT and checks dθ + θ∧θ = 0 and F + e¹∧e² = 0.
  3. Goldstone and Higgs. With global breaking (g = 0) the phase wave along the trough is massless, runs all the way round and is never screened. With a local gauge symmetry (g > 0) the tree-level mass of the Abelian Higgs model is mV = g η v₀ (v₀ = 1) and the screening length λV = 1/mV: 2 transverse polarizations swallow 1 Goldstone mode into a massive vector with 3 polarizations, and the total count is kept (2 + 2 = 3 + 1). Electroweak: 4 massless gauge bosons × 2 + 4 real Higgs scalars = 12 = W⁺W⁻Z 3 × 3 + photon 2 + Higgs 1.
  4. Skyrmion. The vacuum manifold of SU(2)L × SU(2)R → SU(2)V is S³. The hedgehog U = exp(i F(r) r̂·σ) has F(0) = π and F(∞) = 0; the page takes F = 2 arctan(R²/r²) and integrates B = (2/π)∫ sin²F (−F′) dr numerically to 1. The baryon number depends only on the two end values: it is the winding number of π₃(S³) = ℤ. Along any radial line (σ, π) runs over half the chiral circle, and the stage draws that half on the trough. The baryon density of B = 1 is spherically symmetric; a toroidal shape only appears at B = 2.
  5. Superconductor and crystal. Cooper pairs carry charge 2e, so U(1) breaks to ℤ₂; the phase turns by 2π once round the vortex core and the enclosed flux is one flux quantum Φ₀ = h/2e = 2.067833848 × 10⁻¹⁵ Wb. In the core |Φ| = 0: the hilltop. The ratio κ of penetration depth λ to coherence length ξ is set to √2/g on the page (schematic). The lattice ring breaks continuous translations and leaves phonons; the Bogoliubov transformation keeps u² − v² = 1.
  6. Relay of five towers. Each tower keeps the books dim G = dim H + dim G/H: gravity GL(4,ℝ) → SO(3,1): 16 = 6 + 10; electroweak SU(2) × U(1) → U(1)em: 4 = 1 + 3 (all three eaten); strong SU(2) × SU(2) → SU(2): 6 = 3 + 3 (pions); superconductor U(1) → ℤ₂: 1 = 0 + 1; consensus SO(2) → 1: 1 = 0 + 1, with fixed point θ* = cos θ* = 0.7390851. The relay of scales: v = 246.22 GeV → 2mu + md ≈ 8.99 MeV → mp = 938.27 MeV → Φ₀ → θ*. Here mu = 2.16 MeV and md = 4.67 MeV are renormalized running parameters in the MS-bar scheme at μ = 2 GeV; 1 − (2mu + md)/mp = 99.04% is only a naive comparison of two scales, not a fraction of any decomposition of the proton mass. The quark-mass term is the vacuum-subtracted, normalized expectation value ⟨N|Hm|N⟩/⟨N|N⟩ in the nucleon at rest of the operator Hm = Σf ∫ d³x mf q̄f qf (Ji, hep-ph/9410274, Eqs. (27)–(30); Yang et al., arXiv:1808.08677, Eqs. (1)–(6)), not the valence count times a mass parameter, and the page gives no number for it; that QCD generates most of the nucleon mass is a literature result (ARGUED in the evidence panel). Reading gravity and consensus as coset towers is the book's model.
