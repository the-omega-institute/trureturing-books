理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

舞台是一个有 p 个点的圈 ZMod p，画成一条线（默认 p = 121，60 步之内绕不回来）。两种游走都从原点出发：上方青色是量子游走，下方灰色是经典随机游走，时间轴是步数 n。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 单步规则。按正文的 hadamardStep：新的 (0, x) 振幅是 (ψ(0, x + 1) + ψ(1, x + 1))/√2，新的 (1, x) 振幅是 (ψ(0, x − 1) − ψ(1, x − 1))/√2。所以 |0⟩ 那一份向左走、|1⟩ 那一份向右走，两份在每一步都按 Hadamard 混合、相互干涉。初态是原点上的硬币 c = (√w, eiφ√(1 − w))。位置 x 的概率 P(x) = |ψ(0, x)|² + |ψ(1, x)|²。
  2. 经典对照。经典随机游走每步以 ½ 的概率向左或向右，n 步后是二项分布，方差恰为 n，宽度 √n。量子游走的宽度在页面上随 n 线性增长（σ ÷ n 停在 0.54 附近）；这一渐近行为是文献结果，未在本书形式化。两者的差距是平方关系：在一条线上是二次的，不是指数的。
  3. 对称硬币。正文证明梗概里的一步：镜像对 (0, x) 与 (1, −x) 的概率之和与硬币无关，c* 把每一对均分。页面数出每一对的最大差：c*（以及它的共轭 (1, −i)/√2）时为零，于是 P(x) = P(−x)；换一枚硬币，例如 (1, 1)/√2，分布就偏向一边，品红是它比镜像多出的部分。冻结的结论是关于几何纠缠的：SymmetricCoinMaximalWalkEntanglement.result（LEAN ✓）：对一切 p ≥ 1、n 与单位硬币 c，geometricEntanglement(walkState(p, n, c)) ≤ geometricEntanglement(walkState(p, n, c*))。页面不计算这个纠缠量，只画分布。
  4. 适用边界。这个引理不说量子游走在任意图上都有指数加速，也不说相消干涉能把任意图上的回头路清零。黏合树上的指数分离与一般图通常至多二次加速是文献结果，未在本书形式化（第十六章文献注记）。

The stage is a cycle ZMod p of p points, drawn as a line (p = 121 by default, so no walk of 60 steps wraps round). Both walks start at the origin: the cyan one above is the quantum walk, the grey one below the classical random walk, and the rail counts the steps n. The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The one-step rule. As in the text's hadamardStep: the new amplitude at (0, x) is (ψ(0, x + 1) + ψ(1, x + 1))/√2 and the new amplitude at (1, x) is (ψ(0, x − 1) − ψ(1, x − 1))/√2. The |0⟩ part steps left, the |1⟩ part steps right, and at every step the Hadamard mixes the two so that they interfere. The walk starts at the origin with the coin c = (√w, eiφ√(1 − w)). The probability at x is P(x) = |ψ(0, x)|² + |ψ(1, x)|².
  2. The classical comparison. The classical random walk steps left or right with probability ½ each; after n steps it is binomial with variance exactly n, width √n. On the page the width of the quantum walk grows linearly in n (σ ÷ n settles near 0.54); that asymptotic behaviour is a literature result, not formalized in this book. The gap between the two is a square: on a line the gain is quadratic, not exponential.
  3. The symmetric coin. A step of the proof sketch in the text: the sum of the probabilities of a mirror pair (0, x) and (1, −x) does not depend on the coin, and c* splits every pair evenly. The page reports the largest gap within a pair: zero for c* (and for its conjugate (1, −i)/√2), and then P(x) = P(−x); with another coin, such as (1, 1)/√2, the distribution tilts to one side, and magenta is what it has beyond its mirror image. The frozen result is about geometric entanglement: SymmetricCoinMaximalWalkEntanglement.result (LEAN ✓): for every p ≥ 1, n and unit coin c, geometricEntanglement(walkState(p, n, c)) ≤ geometricEntanglement(walkState(p, n, c*)). The page does not compute this entanglement; it draws the distributions.
  4. Where it stops. The lemma does not say that quantum walks give an exponential speedup on every graph, nor that destructive interference clears every way back on any graph. The exponential separation on glued trees, and at most a quadratic speedup on general graphs as a rule, are literature results not formalized in this book (the literature remark of Chapter 16).
