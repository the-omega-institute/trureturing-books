寄存器有 Q = 2q 格。输入是梳子 {x₀, x₀ + r, x₀ + 2r, …} 上 n 格的等幅叠加；正指数单位化 QFT 之后，频率 k 的振幅是 αk = Σx e2πikx/Q / √(Qn)。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 齿轮。每只齿轮对应 k 的一个二进制位 2j：时间轴每走一格 x，它转 2j/Q 圈。最左边那只每格转半圈，只停两个位置；往右每只慢一半，停的位置多一倍。频率 k 在格 x 的相位 2πkx/Q 是 k 的二进制位选中的那几只（亮的）齿轮的转角之和。Q = 8、x = 5 时三只齿轮停在 π、π/2、5π/4，与第十五章第二步一致（文献结果，未在本书形式化）。
  2. 链。时间轴扫过梳子上的一格，就给选中的频率 k 接上一支长为 1、方向 2πkx/Q 的青色箭头。墨色的合成箭头就是 Σ e2πikx/Q；它的长度平方除以 Qn，就是谱上第 k 根柱子的高度。
  3. 相消。r 整除 Q、起点为 0 时，k 不是 Q/r 的倍数，箭头均匀绕一圈，链首尾相接、合成为 0；k 是 Q/r 的倍数时箭头全部同向，合成长 n。Q = 16、r = 4、k = 1 正是第十五章第四步的 1 + i + (−1) + (−i) = 0。扫描途中不共振的柱子会先长出来，再缩回 0。
  4. 峰。品红的小三角在 sQ/r（s = 0, 1, …, r − 1）。r 整除 Q 时它们都落在整数上，每根峰高 1/r；不整除时它们落在两个整数之间，峰摊到相邻几格，离 sQ/r 最近的那些格合计占大部分概率。
  5. 读出阶。对每个峰把 k/Q 展开成连分数，取分母不超过 √Q 的最后一个渐近分数 s/d。Q = 16、r = 4 时，k = 4、12 读出 1/4、3/4，得到 4；k = 8 读出 1/2，只得到 2（阶的真因子），要再检验；k = 0 不含信息。Q = 64、r = 6 时，k = 11 读出 1/6。

The register has Q = 2q cells. The input is the equal superposition of the n cells of the comb {x₀, x₀ + r, x₀ + 2r, …}; after the positive-exponent unitary QFT the amplitude of frequency k is αk = Σx e2πikx/Q / √(Qn). The page is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. The gears. Each gear stands for one binary digit 2j of k: every cell x along the rail turns it by 2j/Q of a turn. The leftmost gear turns half a turn per cell and rests in only two positions; each gear to the right is half as fast and rests in twice as many. The phase 2πkx/Q of frequency k at cell x is the sum of the angles of the gears picked (lit) by the binary digits of k. At Q = 8 and x = 5 the three gears rest at π, π/2 and 5π/4, as in Step 2 of Chapter 15 (a literature result, not formalized in this book).
  2. The chain. Each time the rail passes a cell of the comb, the chosen frequency k gains a cyan arrow of length 1 pointing at 2πkx/Q. The ink resultant is Σ e2πikx/Q; its squared length divided by Qn is the height of bar k of the spectrum.
  3. Cancellation. When r divides Q and the offset is 0, a k that is not a multiple of Q/r turns the arrows evenly round, the chain closes on itself and sums to 0; when k is a multiple of Q/r all arrows point the same way and the sum has length n. Q = 16, r = 4, k = 1 is exactly the 1 + i + (−1) + (−i) = 0 of Step 4 of Chapter 15. During the sweep the off-resonant bars first grow and then shrink back to 0.
  4. Peaks. The small magenta triangles sit at sQ/r (s = 0, 1, …, r − 1). When r divides Q they all fall on integers and each peak has height 1/r; otherwise they fall between two integers, each peak spreads over neighbouring cells, and the cells nearest to sQ/r together carry most of the probability.
  5. Reading the order. For each peak the page expands k/Q as a continued fraction and keeps the last convergent s/d with denominator at most √Q. At Q = 16 and r = 4, k = 4 and 12 read 1/4 and 3/4, giving 4; k = 8 reads 1/2 and only gives 2 (a proper divisor of the order), which must be checked again; k = 0 carries no information. At Q = 64 and r = 6, k = 11 reads 1/6.
