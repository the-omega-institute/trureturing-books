舞台从左到右是全书的阅读顺序（序曲、三十九章、终章、附录 A 与 B），每一格是 trureturing.lock.json 里的一条冻结声明，落在中文正文第一次引用它的那一章的柱子里。附录 C 是逐条的总索引，引用了每一条，所以不单列一柱，只在读数里给出它的序号。时间轴是阅读位置：读到哪一章，那一章的格子就落定。本页是这本书引用关系的一份清点，不是证明；每一条声明成立与否由 Lean 内核负责，页面只数它们在哪里。

  1. 依据的章节。第二十七章 · 第一至三部冻结声明全景审计表、第二十八章 · 第四至六部冻结声明全景审计表 与 附录 C · 冻结定理总索引。
  2. 格子从哪里来。生成脚本 gen-data.py 读锁文件的每一条（GID、种类、源码行），再读中文章节：::: 行上的 lean="…" 属性记作“环境引用”（实心），(lean:…) 行内链接记作“行内引用”；两者都没有、只靠行内链接出现的画成空心。附录 C 的序号（T、D、R 三段）取自那张表的第一列。
  3. 绿色。绿色只说锁文件里这一条的种类是 theorem，即 Lean 内核检查过的定理；灰色是定义（def 与 inductive）。依据栏里两组 LEAN ✓ 是第二十七、二十八章逐行解剖的八条，正文已写出它们的陈述。
  4. 不在页面上的。页面不读 Lean 源码、不重新检查证明，也不判断一条声明“重要”与否；柱子的高低只表示那一章第一次引入了多少条。

From left to right the stage follows the reading order of the book (the prelude, thirty-nine chapters, the coda, Appendices A and B). Each cell is one frozen declaration of trureturing.lock.json, placed in the column of the chapter whose Chinese text cites it first. Appendix C is the entry-by-entry index and cites every one of them, so it has no column of its own; the readouts give its entry number instead. The rail is the reading position: as you read a chapter, its cells land. The page is a count of how the book cites, not a proof; whether each declaration holds is the Lean kernel's business, and the page only counts where they are.

  1. The chapters behind it. Chapter 27 · the audit table of Parts I–III, Chapter 28 · the audit table of Parts IV–VI and Appendix C · the index of frozen theorems.
  2. Where the cells come from. The script gen-data.py reads every entry of the lock (GID, kind, source line), then the Chinese chapters: a lean="…" attribute on a ::: line counts as an environment citation (filled), a (lean:…) link as an inline citation; a declaration that only inline links cite is drawn hollow. The Appendix C entry numbers (sections T, D, R) are read from the first column of that table.
  3. Green. Green says only that the lock records the kind theorem for the entry, a theorem checked by the Lean kernel; grey is a definition (def or inductive). The two LEAN ✓ boxes in the evidence panel are the eight theorems Chapters 27 and 28 dissect line by line, whose statements the text writes out.
  4. Not on the page. The page does not read the Lean sources, does not recheck any proof and does not judge whether a declaration is “important”; the height of a column only says how many declarations that chapter introduces first.
