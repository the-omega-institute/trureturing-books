ARGUED既有结果的应用（第 33 章例 ex-ch33-mutual-cosine-monotonic-lock 把上面这条端点定理与现成实分析结果接起来，没有新增 Lean 定理）：余弦迭代从任何实初值出发都收敛到 θ*；初值在 [0, 1] 内且不是 θ* 时每步严格越过 θ*，误差比趋于 −sin θ* = −√(1 − θ*²)。文献结果，未在本书形式化：余弦在 [0, 1] 上是常数 sin 1 的压缩映射；牛顿迭代在不动点邻域内局部二次收敛（记 F(x) = x − f(x)，在 |x − x*| ≤ ρ 上 |δₖ₊₁| ≤ C·δₖ²，C = max|F″|/(2 min|F′|)；本页取 ρ = 0.5，余弦 C ≈ 0.393、1 + 1/x 的 C ≈ 0.585，都满足 C·ρ < 1；离不动点更远时，例如余弦从靠近 −π/2 的 x₀ 出发、1 + sin x₀ 接近 0，一步可能跳得很远，本页不对这样的步声称二次律）；对 q ∈ [0, 1] 有 |q − θ*| ≤ |cos q − q| ≤ (1 + sin 1)|q − θ*|；θ* 是无理数，所以每个渐近分数 a/b 的残差满足 0 < |cos(a/b) − a/b| < (1 + sin 1)/b²。1 + 1/x 收敛到 φ、每步约乘以 −1/φ² 同属经典分析。Application of existing results (Example ex-ch33-mutual-cosine-monotonic-lock of chapter 33 joins the endpoint theorem above to existing real-analysis results, with no new Lean theorem): the cosine iteration converges to θ* from every real start; from a start in [0, 1] other than θ* it crosses θ* at every step and the error ratio tends to −sin θ* = −√(1 − θ*²). Literature results, not formalized in this book: on [0, 1] the cosine is a contraction with constant sin 1; Newton's method is locally quadratic near the fixed point (with F(x) = x − f(x), on |x − x*| ≤ ρ one has |δₖ₊₁| ≤ C·δₖ², C = max|F″|/(2 min|F′|); the page takes ρ = 0.5, where C ≈ 0.393 for the cosine and C ≈ 0.585 for 1 + 1/x, both with C·ρ < 1; farther away, for instance the cosine started from x₀ near −π/2, where 1 + sin x₀ is close to 0, a single step can jump far, and the page claims no quadratic law for such a step); for q in [0, 1], |q − θ*| ≤ |cos q − q| ≤ (1 + sin 1)|q − θ*|; θ* is irrational, so every convergent a/b has residual 0 < |cos(a/b) − a/b| < (1 + sin 1)/b². That 1 + 1/x converges to φ, about −1/φ² per step, is classical analysis too.

理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

舞台是蛛网图：青色曲线 y = f(x)、灰色直线 y = x，两者的交点就是不动点。从起点竖直走到曲线，再水平走回直线，就是按一次 f。本页看的是这条折线怎样收进不动点、收得多快。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 迭代。按一次 f，误差 δₖ = xₖ − x* 变成 δₖ₊₁ ≈ f′(x*)·δₖ。余弦在 θ* 处的斜率是 −sin θ* = −λ ≈ −0.6736；1 + 1/x 在 φ 处的斜率是 −1/φ² ≈ −0.382。负号让折线一左一右地夹住不动点，模长就是每步缩小的比例。舞台上品红的虚线是不动点处的切线；对余弦，灰色虚线是 [0, 1] 上最陡的斜率 −sin 1 ≈ −0.8415，即压缩映射原理的全局常数（文献结果，未在本书形式化；余弦迭代的定性收敛是既有结果的应用，见第 33 章例 ex-ch33-mutual-cosine-monotonic-lock）。
  2. 单位圆。点 P = (cos θ*, sin θ*) = (θ*, λ) 在单位圆上：青色的横坐标 θ* 是闭环停下的位置，品红的纵坐标 λ 是它合拢的速率，(θ*)² + λ² = 1。这是第二十四章 §24.5 的读法。
  3. 牛顿。在 xₖ 处沿曲线的切线走到直线 y = x，落点就是 xₖ − (xₖ − cos xₖ)/(1 + sin xₖ)：比单纯取余弦多读了一个量 sin xₖ。进入 θ* 邻域后，误差满足 δₖ₊₁ ≈ θ*/(2(1 + λ))·δₖ²（文献结果，未在本书形式化）。页面把 |xₖ − θ*| ≤ 0.5 当作这个邻域：在那里 |δₖ₊₁| ≤ C·δₖ²，C = cos(θ* − 0.5)/(2(1 + sin(θ* − 0.5))) ≈ 0.393，且 0.5·C < 1，所以轨道一旦进入就留在里面；读数只在上一步已在邻域内时才拿比值对照 θ*/(2(1 + λ))，邻域外的步在右下图里是灰点。起点靠近 −π/2 时 1 + sin x₀ 接近 0，第一步会跳得很远，五步之内未必进入邻域。本页每个起点的每一步 1 + sin xₖ 都不为零，上面的落点公式处处有定义（数值观察）。读数里的“迭代要走几步”是页面把两条轨道逐步比较数出来的。
  4. 读数。θ* 的连分数是 [0; 1, 2, 1, 4, 1, 40, 1, 9, 4, 2, 1, …]（数值观察）。对每个渐近分数 q = a/b，品红竖线是残差 |cos q − q|。其中从直线到水平线 y = θ* 的那一段恰好是 |q − θ*|；竖线在余弦曲线上的端点停在两条灰色虚线张成的楔形里，因为曲线在 [0, 1] 上的斜率介于 0 与 −sin 1 之间。b² × 残差始终低于 1 + sin 1。尺子 D 取离 θ* 最近的 a/D：D = 1000 时是 0.739，残差约为 17/23 的两倍。
  5. 已验证的部分。θ* 的存在、唯一与 0 < θ* < 1，以及 φ 是 1 + 1/x 唯一的正不动点，是两条冻结的 Lean 定理（见绿色框）。余弦迭代的收敛、严格交替的条件与误差比的极限是这条端点定理与现成实分析结果的应用（第 33 章例 ex-ch33-mutual-cosine-monotonic-lock，没有新增 Lean 定理）；1 + 1/x 的收敛速率、牛顿在不动点邻域内的局部二次收敛与残差的双边界是文献结果，未在本书形式化；小数是数值。

The stage is the cobweb plane: the cyan curve y = f(x) and the grey line y = x meet at the fixed point. Going up or down to the curve from the start and then across to the line is one application of f. The page shows how that path closes in on the fixed point, and how fast. It is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Iteration. One application of f turns the error δₖ = xₖ − x* into δₖ₊₁ ≈ f′(x*)·δₖ. The slope of the cosine at θ* is −sin θ* = −λ ≈ −0.6736; the slope of 1 + 1/x at φ is −1/φ² ≈ −0.382. The minus sign makes the path close in from alternating sides, and the size is the ratio by which the error shrinks per step. The dashed magenta line on the stage is the tangent at the fixed point; for the cosine, the dashed grey line has the steepest slope on [0, 1], −sin 1 ≈ −0.8415, the global constant of the contraction principle (a literature result, not formalized in this book; the qualitative convergence of the cosine iteration is an application of existing results, see Example ex-ch33-mutual-cosine-monotonic-lock of chapter 33).
  2. The unit circle. The point P = (cos θ*, sin θ*) = (θ*, λ) lies on the unit circle: its cyan abscissa θ* is where the loop stops, its magenta ordinate λ is how fast it closes, and (θ*)² + λ² = 1. This is the reading of chapter 24, §24.5.
  3. Newton. At xₖ, follow the tangent of the curve to the line y = x; it lands at xₖ − (xₖ − cos xₖ)/(1 + sin xₖ), which reads one quantity more than the plain cosine: sin xₖ. Once xₖ is near θ*, the error obeys δₖ₊₁ ≈ θ*/(2(1 + λ))·δₖ² (a literature result, not formalized in this book). The page takes |xₖ − θ*| ≤ 0.5 as that neighbourhood: there |δₖ₊₁| ≤ C·δₖ² with C = cos(θ* − 0.5)/(2(1 + sin(θ* − 0.5))) ≈ 0.393, and 0.5·C < 1, so an orbit that enters it stays; the readout compares the ratio with θ*/(2(1 + λ)) only when the previous step was already inside, and steps from outside are grey dots in the lower right plot. From a start near −π/2, 1 + sin x₀ is close to 0, the first step jumps far, and five steps need not reach the neighbourhood. For every start on the page, 1 + sin xₖ is nonzero at every step, so the landing formula above is always defined (a numerical observation). The readout “steps the iteration needs” is counted by the page, comparing the two orbits step by step.
  4. Readings. The continued fraction of θ* is [0; 1, 2, 1, 4, 1, 40, 1, 9, 4, 2, 1, …] (a numerical observation). For each convergent q = a/b the magenta upright is the residual |cos q − q|. The part of it from the line to the level y = θ* is exactly |q − θ*|; the endpoint of the upright on the cosine curve stays inside the wedge of the two dashed grey lines, because on [0, 1] the slope of the curve lies between 0 and −sin 1. b² times the residual always stays below 1 + sin 1. The ruler D takes the a/D nearest θ*: with D = 1000 it reads 0.739, whose residual is about twice that of 17/23.
  5. What is verified. The existence and uniqueness of θ* with 0 < θ* < 1, and φ as the only positive fixed point of 1 + 1/x, are two frozen Lean theorems (the green boxes). The convergence of the cosine iteration, the condition for strict alternation and the limit of the error ratio are applications of that endpoint theorem and existing real-analysis results (Example ex-ch33-mutual-cosine-monotonic-lock of chapter 33, with no new Lean theorem); the rate for 1 + 1/x, Newton's local quadratic convergence near the fixed point and the two-sided residual bound are literature results, not formalized in this book; the decimals are numerical.
