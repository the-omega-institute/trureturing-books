理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

舞台是蛛网图：青色曲线 y = f(x)、灰色直线 y = x，两者的交点就是不动点。从起点竖直走到曲线，再水平走回直线，就是按一次 f。本页看的是这条折线怎样收进不动点、收得多快。本页是数值演示，不是证明。

  1. 迭代。按一次 f，误差 δₖ = xₖ − x* 变成 δₖ₊₁ ≈ f′(x*)·δₖ。余弦在 θ* 处的斜率是 −sin θ* = −λ ≈ −0.6736；1 + 1/x 在 φ 处的斜率是 −1/φ² ≈ −0.382。负号让折线一左一右地夹住不动点，模长就是每步缩小的比例。舞台上品红的虚线是不动点处的切线；对余弦，灰色虚线是 [0, 1] 上最陡的斜率 −sin 1 ≈ −0.8415，即压缩映射原理的全局常数（文献结果，未在本书形式化）。
  2. 单位圆。点 P = (cos θ*, sin θ*) = (θ*, λ) 在单位圆上：青色的横坐标 θ* 是闭环停下的位置，品红的纵坐标 λ 是它合拢的速率，(θ*)² + λ² = 1。这是第二十四章 §24.5 的读法。
  3. 牛顿。在 xₖ 处沿曲线的切线走到直线 y = x，落点就是 xₖ − (xₖ − cos xₖ)/(1 + sin xₖ)：比单纯取余弦多读了一个量 sin xₖ。误差满足 δₖ₊₁ ≈ θ*/(2(1 + λ))·δₖ²（文献结果，未在本书形式化）。读数里的“迭代要走几步”是页面把两条轨道逐步比较数出来的。
  4. 读数。θ* 的连分数是 [0; 1, 2, 1, 4, 1, 40, 1, 9, 4, 2, 1, …]（数值观察）。对每个渐近分数 q = a/b，品红竖线是残差 |cos q − q|。其中从直线到水平线 y = θ* 的那一段恰好是 |q − θ*|；整条竖线停在两条灰色虚线张成的楔形里，因为曲线在 [0, 1] 上的斜率介于 0 与 −sin 1 之间。b² × 残差始终低于 1 + sin 1。尺子 D 取离 θ* 最近的 a/D：D = 1000 时是 0.739，残差约为 17/23 的两倍。
  5. 已验证的部分。θ* 的存在、唯一与 0 < θ* < 1，以及 φ 是 1 + 1/x 唯一的正不动点，是两条冻结的 Lean 定理（见绿色框）。收敛本身、收敛的速率、牛顿的二次收敛与残差的双边界都是文献结果，未在本书形式化。

The stage is the cobweb plane: the cyan curve y = f(x) and the grey line y = x meet at the fixed point. Going up or down to the curve from the start and then across to the line is one application of f. The page shows how that path closes in on the fixed point, and how fast. It is a numerical illustration, not a proof.

  1. Iteration. One application of f turns the error δₖ = xₖ − x* into δₖ₊₁ ≈ f′(x*)·δₖ. The slope of the cosine at θ* is −sin θ* = −λ ≈ −0.6736; the slope of 1 + 1/x at φ is −1/φ² ≈ −0.382. The minus sign makes the path close in from alternating sides, and the size is the ratio by which the error shrinks per step. The dashed magenta line on the stage is the tangent at the fixed point; for the cosine, the dashed grey line has the steepest slope on [0, 1], −sin 1 ≈ −0.8415, the global constant of the contraction principle (a literature result, not formalized in this book).
  2. The unit circle. The point P = (cos θ*, sin θ*) = (θ*, λ) lies on the unit circle: its cyan abscissa θ* is where the loop stops, its magenta ordinate λ is how fast it closes, and (θ*)² + λ² = 1. This is the reading of chapter 24, §24.5.
  3. Newton. At xₖ, follow the tangent of the curve to the line y = x; it lands at xₖ − (xₖ − cos xₖ)/(1 + sin xₖ), which reads one quantity more than the plain cosine: sin xₖ. The error obeys δₖ₊₁ ≈ θ*/(2(1 + λ))·δₖ² (a literature result, not formalized in this book). The readout “steps the iteration needs” is counted by the page, comparing the two orbits step by step.
  4. Readings. The continued fraction of θ* is [0; 1, 2, 1, 4, 1, 40, 1, 9, 4, 2, 1, …] (a numerical observation). For each convergent q = a/b the magenta upright is the residual |cos q − q|. The part of it from the line to the level y = θ* is exactly |q − θ*|; the whole upright stays inside the wedge of the two dashed grey lines, because on [0, 1] the slope of the curve lies between 0 and −sin 1. b² times the residual always stays below 1 + sin 1. The ruler D takes the a/D nearest θ*: with D = 1000 it reads 0.739, whose residual is about twice that of 17/23.
  5. What is verified. The existence and uniqueness of θ* with 0 < θ* < 1, and φ as the only positive fixed point of 1 + 1/x, are two frozen Lean theorems (the green boxes). Convergence itself, its rate, Newton's quadratic convergence and the two-sided residual bound are literature results, not formalized in this book.
