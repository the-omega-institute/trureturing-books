There are two ways to write a book about grand unification. The first is the mythology of triumph: sketch an ambitious blueprint, sweep discrepant mass thresholds under "higher-order corrections," dress hand-tuned couplings as "natural scales," and declare the universe sealed inside a single equation. The second is the anatomy of the cartographer: push one concrete discrete geometry under the unforgiving glare of exact rational arithmetic, certified interval enclosures, and a formal proof assistant, measure how far it can actually walk on its own legs, and mark the **exact numerical cliff** where it stops.

*Omega Unification* chooses the second path. Centered on the **four open questions of grand unification** laid down in Section 33.9 of *The Geometric Intuition of Quantum Computing* (tagged `ch39` in the companion research archive), this monograph uses the 19-point Behrend shell $x_1^2 + x_2^2 + x_3^2 = 867$, dual quaternion clocks, and the spectrum of nontrivial Riemann zeta zeros as a single shared laboratory. Across fifteen chapters, it maps the complete mathematical boundary—from integer shell arithmetic, gauge unification and Higgs portals, four-dimensional lattice chiral generations and Yukawa hierarchy obstructions, baryon-triality proton protection, and simplicial Regge gravity with four-layer vacuum cancellation, to the 529-triangle dual-clock winding ladder, the 624 internal channels, and the final four-question balance sheet.

## 0.1 Origin of the Four Questions and the Scientific Weight of "0/4" {#sec-ou-0-1}

Everything begins with a question as seductive as it is ruthless: if the microscopic universe contains no extra spatial dimensions and no bloated simple gauge matrix packed with proton-destroying $(X, Y)$ leptoquarks, but instead houses three-dimensional space, hypercharge, weak isospin, and strong color as parallel bounded concept fields on a single discrete minimal-arc complex, **can one master connection—with zero post-hoc curve fitting—quantitatively derive the defining numbers of the Standard Model and gravity?**

That challenge splits into four concrete benchmarks investigated across Volumes II through V:

1. **Question 1 (Gauge unification, Higgs mass, and the electroweak scale):** Unthresholded one-loop Standard Model running misses exact gauge convergence between $10^{13}$ and $10^{17}\,\mathrm{GeV}$ by an inverse-coupling gap $\Delta\alpha^{-1} > 3.66$ (precisely about $3.664$). Can the discrete partition function of the shell derive heavy thresholds that close two-loop unification while simultaneously fixing $m_H \approx 125.2\,\mathrm{GeV}$ and the hierarchy $v / M_P \sim 10^{-17}$?
2. **Question 2 (Three chiral generations on a 4D lattice):** Without violating the Nielsen–Ninomiya fermion-doubling theorem, can shell phases and an intrinsic spectral cut produce exactly $N_{\mathrm{gen}} = 3$ chiral Weyl generations in an interacting four-dimensional lattice theory?
3. **Question 3 (Flavor hierarchy, quark mixing, and proton stability):** Can one cross-concept coupling generate five orders of magnitude in fermion masses, realistic CKM mixing angles, and the Jarlskog CP invariant $J$, while protecting the proton via exact discrete selection rules such as baryon triality?
4. **Question 4 (Four-dimensional spin-2 gravity and the cosmological constant):** Can discrete Regge deficit angles and four-layer heat-kernel cancellation yield a massless helicity-$\pm 2$ graviton propagator, the normalized Newton constant $G_N$, and a non-circular derivation of the dark-energy ratio $\Lambda_{\mathrm{cos}}\ell_P^2 \sim 10^{-122}$ without inputting the Hubble radius?

## 0.4 Notation, Reading Tracks, and Replay Conventions {#sec-ou-0-4}
