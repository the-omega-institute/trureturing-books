## 15.2 Five Structural Tensions Across the Replay Archive

Any patch that relieves one ridge without addressing the other four simply pushes the strain into a neighboring sector.

### 15.2.1 The Arithmetic-Clock vs Flavor-Weight Tension

In [Chapter 12](ch12-shared-arithmetic-clock.md), forcing arithmetic times and reference quark flavor to share identical probabilities works cleanly through the fourth time and collapses globally at the fifth (31-bit dyadic exclusion, minimal max error $\approx 2.5597 \times 10^{-10}$). Rescuing the fifth time via one neutral-shell resolvent pass (clearing the $18.49684\ldots$ efficiency floor at $A \approx 2.9036 \times 10^9$) forces a sharp sixth-time shift $\ge 1.3301556 \times 10^{-8}$ and pins the held-out 35-bit seventh-time code to `32071996606` against reference `32071992476` (requiring at least 44-bit input precision).

At the common scale $A = 2.9036 \times 10^9$ that rigid chain compresses $> 99.931\%$ of spectral mass into three isolated height bands; the constructed eight-zero solution leaves a held-out $\mathrm{CP}$ deficit of $6.0833\%$ in $J$ ($5.8282\%$–$7.6012\%$ on the eleven-zero list; $[5.57\%, 9.34\%]$ on 23 zeros once the sixth time and the $|V_{cb}|^2$ offset are also fixed; a full-spectrum supremum of about $16.568\%$ when only the mean $|V_{ub}|^2$ is fixed). This deficit is not forced: it can be made arbitrarily small at sufficiently large scale, another set of eight zeros already brings it below $9.4 \times 10^{-10}$ at the same scale (where it is forced above $3.9 \times 10^{-11}$), and when the mean $|V_{ub}|^2$ is exact its ratio to the $|V_{cb}|^2$ offset lies between $1.919 \times 10^{10}$ and $6.387 \times 10^{10}$. Meanwhile, power laws ($s \ge 1.00001485$), Gaussian determinants, Gibbs triangle weights, short coherent walks, and single-shot physical selectors (pass rate $< 4.4287 \times 10^{-5}$) are all ruled out one by one.

Yet reaching $105$ windings still leaves a **$100$-dimensional continuous feasible family** of weight vectors (subject to normalization, nonnegativity, and the stated lower bounds on $w_{104}$ and $w_{105}$). Standard variational selection rules do not close this family either: at the flagship phase, the optimal weights of maximum entropy, minimum two-norm, and the analytic center on the feasible family are all marginal vacua, where the reference phase is degenerate in energy with another phase or loses positive-definite curvature.
