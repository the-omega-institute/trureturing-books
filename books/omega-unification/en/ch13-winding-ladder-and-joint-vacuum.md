# Chapter 13 The 529-Triangle Dual-Clock Action, the Winding Ladder, and the Joint Zero Witness {#sec-ch13}

[Chapter 12](ch12-shared-arithmetic-clock.md) drove external probability reweighting into a cul-de-sac: smooth priors, monotone efficiencies, Gaussian determinants, Gibbs factors, and short walks all break against the shared arithmetic clock. Faced with such a wall, physics knows only one honorable pivot—**stop cherry-picking backgrounds from outside, write down an intrinsic action inside the shell, and let energy minimization pull the vacuum into place**.

The 19-point Behrend shell naturally spans **$529$ triangular plaquettes**, each enclosing a nontrivial holonomy built from the dual clock phases $(\theta_1, \theta_2)$—a discrete non-abelian Wilson lattice gauge action on a finite 19-site sphere, where higher winding orders $k$ act as progressively sharper Fourier harmonics carving narrow interference pockets across the two-torus. Summing multi-winding Wilson contributions over all $529$ triangles with nonnegative weights $w_k \ge 0$ produces not vague heuristics, but an extraordinarily sharp **winding-number staircase** down to the exact integer.

Along that staircase, inside the three-window domain, no critical point exists up to $18$ windings, $19$ yields the first saddle, and $21$ yields the first strict local minimum; $27$ achieves the first global three-window vacuum while crashing quark flavor; $31$ and $36$ unlock local and height-dependent global three-window vacua with exact reference flavor; and precisely **$105$ windings** achieves a height-independent global three-window, exact-reference-flavor vacuum using one fixed weight vector and phase across all finite heights and the unit-spoke limit—culminating in a parts-per-million joint witness at real Riemann zero **`#2,306,888,100,085`**. ## 13.1 The 529-Triangle Dual-Clock Action, Noncommutative Curvature, and Global Angle Bounds

We first specify the multi-winding dual-clock variational problem and anchor in the Lean kernel why triangular shell holonomies carry irreducible directional curvature rather than trivial abelian phase cancellations.

::: {.definition #def-ou-ch13-wilson-winding-action title="529-Triangle Multi-Winding Dual-Clock Wilson Action and Three-Window Acceptance"}
On the $529$ undirected triangular plaquettes of the 19-point Behrend shell at zero height $\gamma$ and dual clock phases $(\theta_1, \theta_2) \in (\mathbb{R} / 2\pi\mathbb{Z})^2$, assign each edge $(i, j)$ its edge stiffness $s_{ij}(\gamma)$ ($1$ on ordinary shell edges and $1 - \delta(\gamma)$ on the three height-softened radial spokes), define each triangle's stiffness product $s_\triangle(\gamma) = s_{ij}(\gamma) s_{jl}(\gamma) s_{li}(\gamma)$ (since every triangle carries at most one softened radial spoke, $s_\triangle(\gamma) \in \{1,\, 1 - \delta(\gamma)\}$, reducing to $s_\triangle \equiv 1$ in the unit-spoke limit $\delta \to 0$), and let $\phi_\triangle(\theta_1, \theta_2)$ be its oriented loop holonomy angle. For maximum winding order $K \ge 1$ and normalized nonnegative weights $w = (w_1, \dots, w_K)$ ($w_k \ge 0$, $\sum_{k=1}^K w_k = 1$), the multi-winding Wilson action is

$$
S_w(\theta_1, \theta_2; \gamma)
\;=\; \sum_{k=1}^K w_k \sum_{\triangle = \{i, j, l\}} s_\triangle(\gamma)\,\Bigl(1 - \cos\bigl(k\,\phi_\triangle(\theta_1, \theta_2)\bigr)\Bigr).
$$

At unit winding, this stiffness-weighted sum equals the third spectral moment difference $6 S_{(1)} = \operatorname{tr}(L_1^3) - \operatorname{tr}(L_0^3)$; by contrast, the live heat map $W_K(\phi_1, \phi_2) = \frac{1}{529}\sum_{\triangle} \cos(K\,\phi_\triangle)$ rendered in the interactive card below visualizes the unweighted arithmetic mean of the $K$-th harmonic terms at unit stiffness rather than the stiffness-weighted certified action $S_w$. A background $(\theta_1, \theta_2; \gamma)$ satisfies **three-window acceptance `[1]+[2]+[4]`** if it simultaneously passes the five-charge three-light-mode cut `[2]`, the two-loop strong-coupling window `[1]`, and the $0.2\%$ four-layer curvature window `[4]`. Its quark flavor readout is governed by the six-face holonomy coordinate $c(\theta_1, \theta_2)$, which yields $|V_{ub}|^2$ and the signed Jarlskog invariant $J$.
:::

Reversing order breaks projective ray equivalence, while the orthogonal-axis half-step triad carries an exact rational Bargmann–Gram spherical excess of $1/4$. Both anchors are frozen directly in Lean 4 (with the second carrying an empty axiom list `axioms: []`):

Why does an impenetrable no-critical-point wall stand inside the three-window domain all the way up to $K = 18$? At low windings, the fundamental cosines of all $529$ triangles pull coherently toward zero phase, leaving the narrow three-window corridor stranded on a steep slope where no convex combination can cancel the gradient ($\|\nabla S_w\| > 3.00565$). Only at $K = 19$ do higher harmonics create enough local counter-slope to produce a saddle, and only at $K = 27$ can the pocket sink deeper than zero phase to become the **global minimum**.

At $K = 27$, a remarkably clean two-term ratio such as $100:13$ or $8:1$ pulls the global minimum inside `[1]+[2]+[4]` for every height $\gamma \ge 10^{12}$, the three softened radial spokes break mirror degeneracy cleanly, and real zero `#4,224,080,297,279` lands within $2.26473 \times 10^{-7}$ of the free global minimum. Yet sending that vacuum into the quark flavor readout exposes a chasm: across the entire viable ratio interval, $|V_{ub}|^2$ stalls at $26.909\%$–$26.922\%$ of reference and signed $J$ stalls at $31.337\%$–$31.362\%$. Forcing reference flavor back onto the $100:13$ action costs at least $1.25008749932365$ across the torus and $(1.3266175,\, 1.32661933)$ while keeping all three windows intact.

Unifying three-window acceptance and reference quark mixing inside a single global minimum first becomes possible at $K = 36$ if coefficients may track height. Remarkably, six small integers already suffice across every finite height (see [`ch39-small-integer-flavor-action.py`](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing-experiments/blob/ba0ac9539cdb641744054bf3a0398494f4cba1c8/experiments/ch39-omega-research/replay/ch39-small-integer-flavor-action.py)):

$$
w_{\text{26}} \;=\; \frac{1}{26}\bigl(\delta_1 + \delta_2 + 2\,\delta_{30} + \delta_{32} + 15\,\delta_{33} + 6\,\delta_{36}\bigr),
$$

which passes all three windows with residuals $+0.4210\%$ / $+0.2567\%$ and exact 8-bit dyadic integer code `252` (decimal); keeping the same six winding orders and splitting **`6899`** integer parts drives $|V_{ub}|^2$ and $J$ errors below **$0.47\;\mathrm{ppm}$** and **$0.29\;\mathrm{ppm}$** across all finite heights!

Removing height dependence completely—locking one fixed weight vector and phase across all finite heights and the unit-spoke limit—eliminates all four candidate branches up to $104$ windings and pins the exact floor at **$105$ windings**, forcing heavy high-frequency weight on the top two modes:

$$
w_{104} \;>\; 0.6589\%, \qquad w_{105} \;>\; 6.8674\%, \qquad -0.01280 \;\le\; x \;\le\; -0.00815.

Yet just when $105$ windings seems to pin down the action, dimension counting delivers a sobering twist: fixing the global vacuum, all three windows, and all reference flavor observables still leaves a **$100$-dimensional continuous feasible family** of weight vectors (subject to normalization, nonnegativity, and the stated lower bounds on $w_{104}$ and $w_{105}$).

## 13.3 Monotone-Weight and Native Autocorrelation Exclusions

The forced high-frequency spikes $w_{104} > 0.6589\%$ and $w_{105} > 6.8674\%$ already hint that any naturally damped action where higher windings carry less weight must fail.
