## Closing Synthesis: Proof Returned to Computation, Geometry Returned to Relation {#sec-om-coda-philosophical-synthesis}

For a century, foundational orthodoxy assumed that surrendering completed infinity and the Axiom of Choice would cripple modern mathematics, leaving no viable calculus, rotation geometry, harmonic analysis, or spectral theory. These six volumes point to the opposite conclusion: **calculus, spherical geometry, harmonic analysis, and Ramanujan spectral gaps appeared inseparable from $\infty$, $\pi$, $e$, and `noncomputable` reals only because curved finite rational relations were first flattened onto Cartesian coordinates, creating an unending approximation debt.**

Once that flat scaffolding and the external third point are withdrawn in favor of homogeneous projective pairs, integer quaternion spinors, nilpotent jet quotients, and primitive prime loops, three unifications arrive together:

1. **Infinity becomes finite closure**: the classical point at infinity becomes an ordinary antipodal anchor $[1 : 0]$ with finite readings;
2. **Approximation becomes exact algebraic identity**: derivatives, Haar moments, and spectral margins shed limit filters and decimal truncation for exact integer and rational identities;
3. **Classical analysis assumes its rightful boundary**: $\mathbf{Math}_\Omega$ builds no isolated enclave; it keeps the executable zero-axiom core in the strict libraries whose selected closures undergo empty-kernel replay, while classical continuum statements live cleanly beyond the one-way shadow functor $\Pi_{\mathbb{R}}$, achieving full interoperability without axiom contagion.
