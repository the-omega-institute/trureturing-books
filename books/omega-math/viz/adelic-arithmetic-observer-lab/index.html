LEAN ✓欧几里得素数逃逸：对任意乘积为正的有限自然数列表 s，存在自然数 p，它是素数、整除 s 的乘积加一，并且不在 s 中：Euclid prime escape: for every finite list s of natural numbers whose product is positive, there is a natural number p that is prime, divides the product of s plus one, and is not a member of s: euclid_prime_escape
LEAN ✓连分数渐近行列式：对任意有限自然数部分商列表，从 (1, 0) 与 (0, 1) 出发、每读一个系数 a 就把 (当前, 前一项) 换成 (a·当前 + 前一项, 当前) 所得的渐近对，其行列式 当前分子·前一项分母 − 前一项分子·当前分母 等于 (−1) 的系数个数次方：Convergent determinant: for every finite list of natural partial quotients, the convergent pair obtained from (1, 0) and (0, 1) by replacing (current, previous) with (a·current + previous, current) for each coefficient a has determinant current numerator · previous denominator − previous numerator · current denominator equal to (−1) to the number of coefficients: cf_convergent_determinant
LEAN ✓四分深度整数预算：对自然数 c、d、p、q、N，若 d > 0、p > 0，且 quarterDepth(c, d, p, q)（从 4dp 起每次乘 4、直到超过 cq 为止的次数）≤ N，则 c·q < (d·p)·4N+1：Integer quarter-depth budget: for natural numbers c, d, p, q, N with d > 0, p > 0 and quarterDepth(c, d, p, q) ≤ N, where quarterDepth counts how often 4dp is multiplied by 4 before it exceeds cq, c·q < (d·p)·4N+1: quarterDepth_integer_sound
LEAN ✓模 5 秩一分裂：对六个模方 5 的生成元 g（(1, ±2, 0, 0)、(1, 0, ±2, 0)、(1, 0, 0, ±2)）中的每一个，分裂矩阵 [[w + 2x, y + 2z], [2z − y, w − 2x]] 模 5 等于 g 所列余数因子的外积 (u, v)ᵀ(s, t)，且不是零矩阵：Rank one mod 5: for each of the six generators g of squared norm 5 ((1, ±2, 0, 0), (1, 0, ±2, 0), (1, 0, 0, ±2)), the split matrix [[w + 2x, y + 2z], [2z − y, w − 2x]] reduced mod 5 equals the outer product (u, v)ᵀ(s, t) of the residue factors listed for g, and is not the zero matrix: mod_five_rank_one
LEAN ✓FRACTRAN 确定性：对任意分数程序与自然数 n、m₁、m₂，若 n 经“第一条分母整除 n 的规则”既能转到 m₁ 也能转到 m₂，则 m₁ = m₂：FRACTRAN is deterministic: for every fraction program and natural numbers n, m₁, m₂, if n steps to m₁ and to m₂ by the first rule whose denominator divides n, then m₁ = m₂: fractran_deterministic
LEAN ✓凯莱—哈密顿：对每个二维整数矩阵 A = [[a, b], [c, d]]，A·A − tr(A)·A + det(A)·I 是零矩阵，其中 tr(A) = a + d，det(A) = ad − bc：Cayley–Hamilton: for every 2 × 2 integer matrix A = [[a, b], [c, d]], A·A − tr(A)·A + det(A)·I is the zero matrix, where tr(A) = a + d and det(A) = ad − bc: cayley_hamilton
